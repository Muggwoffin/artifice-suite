From Alexander Kinn, Luxembourg.

To Dr. Max Hodann, c/o SKUSE, 63 Haverstock Hill, N.W.3.

Date of Letter: April 19, 1935.

Date of Postmark: April 20, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that he had to leave Brussels a week prior due to his declining health.

He had already contacted several organizations which will be of use to the recipient in his lectures.

Note: An earlier communication (not filed) dated April 5, 1935, listed this man's address as 22 Avenue, Parc Neger (?), Brussels. The name then appeared as KIUN.

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

HODANN, Max Julius Alexander

August 31st, 1928.
Writing to S.I.S., from Scotland House, regarding Leopold MARESCH and the "International Committee of the Friends of Soviet Russia," he states that their correspondence is signed "fraternally, Dr. Max HODANN" on behalf of L. MARESCH, but elsewhere, Dr. Max HODANN is referred to as Hon. Secretary or signs for "the Committee."

September 11th, 1928.
S.I.S. indicates that Dr. Max HODANN appears to be identical with the Berlin city physician, whose publications, "Geschlecht und Liebe" and "Bringt uns Wirklich der Klapperstorch?" were banned by the Public Prosecutor at Rudolstadt for being contrary to public morality. HODANN successfully appealed this decision.

October 2nd, 1928.
S.I.S. reports that Dr. Max HODANN is a Berlin city councillor (Stadtrat).

October 1st, 1928.
In a letter to I.P.I., Dr. Max HODANN is described as Secretary of the International Committee of the Friends of Soviet Russia, Friedrichstrasse 24, Berlin, and "a medical practitioner whose works have incurred censorship on grounds of sexual content."

January 3rd, 1928.
The Reichskongress of the Society of Friends of Russia took place on November 4th, 1928, in the former Prussian Upper House, Berlin. Dr. HODANN spoke of the difficulties which Russia had to overcome after the Revolution and stated that it was the duty of all workers to defend the Soviet Union, now the home of Socialism.

January 8th, 1929.
Sent a report on the Congress of the Friends of Soviet Russia held in Brussels on December 2nd, 1928, at Café Cornet, 16, rue du Marché aux Fromages, concerning the formation of a Belgian section. The meeting was sponsored, among others, by Dr. HODANN, Secretary of the German Section and International President of the Society. A report of the proceedings appeared in the "Drapeau Rouge" on December 4th, 1928, with articles written by HODANN, etc., in issues of December 5 & 6, 1928, under reference SZ/98/3.

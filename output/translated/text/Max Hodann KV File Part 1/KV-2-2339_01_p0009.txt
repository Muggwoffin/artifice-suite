S.9.a.
23.4.35.

The investigation into Max HODANN and Lisa LINDBAEK has yielded an interesting letter from one Molla ORDING in Oslo.

We know that the governess who cared for Mrs. MARTIN's son was a Miss ORDING, and the intercepted correspondence at 52a clearly indicates that this was indeed Molla ORDING, and furthermore, that she possesses more knowledge of the Martin case than initially believed.

The letter refers to one Arne, Professor Arne ORDING of 1 Britannia Street, W.C.1, who is also corresponding with HODANN.

If you agree, I believe an investigation into this man's correspondence would be justified. The investigation report is submitted herewith for your signature if approved.

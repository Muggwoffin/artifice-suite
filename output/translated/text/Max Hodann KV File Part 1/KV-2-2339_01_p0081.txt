Regarding FINKELSTEIN, the author of one of the letters forwarded to you on April 11th, 1935, we have determined that this man is a German chemist, born on January 25th, 1877, in Leipzig. He arrived in this country on August 21st, 1935. We are hoping to obtain further information about him and have obtained a warrant for his correspondence.

Regarding HARYOLL or HARE, also referred to in our letter of April 11th, 1935, we have received a further letter from this individual in which he states that he has asked Karen to come to Oslo, which he hopes will be a safe haven for her. He asks HODANN to keep him informed if he hears anything.

Particularly interesting, I believe, is his connection with Professor Arne ORDING, who arrived in this country on March 7th, 1935, to study for three months. You will recall that Miss ORDING was the governess to whom Mrs. HARTIN's son George was entrusted.

I wonder if you have come across this Professor ORDING during any investigations, or whether you have further details of Miss ORDING's family which would help us to connect the two.

Yours sincerely,

G. H. L.

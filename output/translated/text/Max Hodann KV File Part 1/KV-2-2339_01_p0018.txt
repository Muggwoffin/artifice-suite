Home Office.

Regarding Lise LINDBAEK, we do not object to an extension of her stay.

With regard to Max HODANN, we believe that his role as assistant to Lise LINDBAEK is merely a pretext for him to remain in the country.

As a refugee who may prove to be highly undesirable, we are not concerned with his permanent settlement here. However, we would not object to granting him a short extension, after which we would be pleased to submit further observations.

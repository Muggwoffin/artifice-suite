Lutembach, December 19, 1955

Dear Mr. Hadas,

I had intended to send this letter some time ago, due to personal matters.

You will also find that the various organizations involved in beautification are already taking action, and you should be observing their efforts today.

On the 23rd of this month, I will be back in Brussels. I will inform you of further developments regarding your plans when possible. I will write to you again between now and then.

You will receive the relevant documents by the end of this month, or they may need to be delivered to you in Brussels.

Sincerely,
Herrnacle Lévis
Mr. Alexandre Lévis

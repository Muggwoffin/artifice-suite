To Dr. M. Hodann, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

From Alexis Finkelstein, 13 Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter: April 14, 1935.
Date of Postmark: April 14, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Disappointed not to have seen Dr. Hodann.

"I will be in town on Thursday afternoon. I would very much like to see you and [Mrs. Lindbeck]. Will you please arrange a definite appointment? My last train departs from Liverpool Street at 8:22."

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

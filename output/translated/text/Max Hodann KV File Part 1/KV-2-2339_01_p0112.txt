To Dr. M. Hodann, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

From Alexis S. Finkelstein, 13 Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter: April 12, 1935.

Date of Postmark: April 12, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Hopes to meet with Hodann and [Frau Lindbaek] on Sunday.

Suggests sightseeing, etc. States that if the proposed itinerary does not meet with the recipient's approval, he will telephone him at Trumpington 233 before noon, as he has no telephone at his residence.

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

COMMENT:

(a) No action required. P.A.

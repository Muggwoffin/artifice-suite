From Miss Dora Russell, Beacon Hill School, Boyles Court, South Weald, near Brentwood, Essex.

To Mr. Hodann, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road.

Date of Letter: April 18, 1935.

Date of Postmark: April 18, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that she believes the recipient (Frau Lindbaek) indicated an ability to pay £2.10 per week. She is willing to reduce this amount if only for a short period. Asks whether she should expect Janka to attend the following term at a rate of £1.10 per week.

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

(a) No action required. P.A.

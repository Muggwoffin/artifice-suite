13 Shellford Park, Great Shelford

Dear Mr. Hodann,

It was a great disappointment to us that you did not attend. Should I be held responsible for failing to warn you about the summer weather? Or did your letter and telegram arrive too late?

Regardless, I will be in town on Thursday afternoon. I am very keen to see you and Mrs. Lindbergh. Please let me know if you are available for an appointment! My last train departs from Liverpool Street at 8:22.

Yours affectionately,
Alexis S. Finkelstein

Dr. M. Hodann
Kynance Court
Gloucester Road
SW7

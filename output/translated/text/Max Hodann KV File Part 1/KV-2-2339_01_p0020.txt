63, Haverstock Hill, NW 3.

London, April 20, 1935.

To the Under-Secretary of State, Home Office.

Sir,

I am writing to request an extension of my stay in this country, as my current permit expires on April 29th. I am currently engaged in a study of the life and work of the late Mr. Nansen. This research is being conducted at the Royal Institute of International Affairs, the School of Economics, and the League of Nations Union, among other locations. However, I find that this undertaking requires more time than initially anticipated. Furthermore, Professor Philip Noel Baker, whose assistance is essential to my work, is currently abroad and will not return until mid-June.

In addition to my research, I am also working as a correspondent for several newspapers, writing articles on English politics. Therefore, I wish to remain in this country for an extended period to fulfill these journalistic obligations.

I would therefore request that the permit of stay granted to Dr. Max Hodann be extended for the same duration, so that he may assist me as my amanuensis. Dr. Hodann has been invited to lecture at the Academy of Letters and Science in Oslo from April 29th to May 5th, and it is essential that he can return to England without difficulty.

The Academy of Letters and Science of Oslo will provide the necessary funds for the completion of my research.

I have the honour to remain,

Your most obedient servant,

Lise Lindbæk.
(Lise Lindbæk-Neander-Nilsson.)

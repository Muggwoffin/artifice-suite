From K. Ceul ROUNG? KEUNZ? Kusnetzki, Most 9, Quartier 9, Moscow.

To Dr. D. FINKELSTEIN, 14 Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter: April 22, 1935.

Date of Postmark: April 25, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that if Karin is a member of the Party, she must obtain permission from the Party before traveling to Moscow.

She will be required to work as a Russian and will not receive the privileges afforded to foreign specialists. They will endeavor to assist her in this matter.

Requests confirmation of the address for Professor NATHANSON.

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

COMMENT:

(a) No action required. P.A.

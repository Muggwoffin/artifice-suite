From Jacques Goldstein, President of "Prokor", Brussels.

To Dr. Max Hodann, c/o Skuse, 63 Haverstock Hill, N.W.3.

Date of Letter: April 19, 1935.

Date of Postmark: April 19, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

This letter refers to correspondence sent by SonnTag to the recipient and the exchanges between Kinn and himself, and invites Hodann on behalf of the "Prokor" organization to a conference to be held in Brussels and Antwerp.

It suggests that the recipient's lectures should be given in German, with a fee of 1000 French Francs for each.

The letter states that "Prokor" is a Belgian organization equivalent to the French "Geserd," and closely allied to the "Ozet" of Moscow.

The principal aims of "Prokor" are as follows:

1. To promote Soviet interests and the results of Jewish colonization in the U.S.S.R.

2. To discourage the return of Jews to Palestine, which is considered an imperialist viewpoint.

3. To improve the living conditions of Jews in accordance with those of the U.S.S.R.

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

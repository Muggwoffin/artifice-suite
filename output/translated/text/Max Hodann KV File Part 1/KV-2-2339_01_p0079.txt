PF.43817/D.S.9.a.

April 16th, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

Regarding my telephone conversation with [redacted] this morning concerning Max HODANN, the following CX references from earlier records are noted on our files: - CX/12906/V. of September 11, 1928, October 2, 1928, November 13, 1928, January 28, 1929, and CX/6561/V. of April 29, 1931, and CX/9955/V. of March 4, 1935.

Several points of particular interest have emerged since his arrival here, regarding which I provided you with a brief note in our PF.43817/D.S.9.a. of April 11, 1935.

The following further details may be of interest to you:

Max Julius Alexander HODANN was born in Neisse on August 30, 1894. He possesses a German passport No. 48/Rev/28/31, issued in Berlin on February 24, 1931. His description is as follows: - Face oval; eyes brown; hair darkish blonde; build medium. I am also enclosing a copy of his photograph.

HODANN is believed to have spent some time during 1934 in Palestine collaborating with [Mrs. LINDBAEK-NELSON] in writing a book entitled "Jews are Coming Home."

He holds a medical degree, but apparently no longer practices medicine, instead earning a living by writing articles for newspapers on social issues. However, his sympathies appear to be communist rather than socialist. He is a member of the Danish P.E.N. Club and an authorized correspondent for the Danish newspaper "Arbeiderbladet."

Since his arrival in this country, he has been residing at Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, with Mrs. LINDBAEK-NELSON, with whom he is purportedly collaborating on writing the biography of Dr. Nansen. However, observation of his movements suggests that he does not spend much time on this project.

He has a divorced wife, Maria HODANN née SARAN, who resides in London and whom he occasionally visits. We have seen correspondence from one [redacted] urging HODANN to return to Copenhagen where she, he, and Sonja can live more cheaply together as she cannot manage on the money he sends her. She also refers to the question of separation.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

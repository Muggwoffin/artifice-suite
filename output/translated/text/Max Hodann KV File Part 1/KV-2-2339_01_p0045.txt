PF.43817/D.S.9.a.

23rd April, 1935.

Dear Mr. Cooper,

Regarding the cases of Max HODANN and Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON, who arrived on 18th March, 1935, for a period of six weeks, I would be most grateful if you could ensure that we are consulted should the matter of extensions arise at any time.

Yours sincerely,

(SD) K.W.W.S.

N. Cooper Esq., O.B.E.,
Home Office,
S.W.1.

Oslo, April 23, 1935

From the Editorial Staff

Mr. Max Hodann,
London NW 3,
63 a Haverstock Hill.

Dear Comrade Hodann!

Thank you very much for your letter. I had to include several articles about the Workers' Government in the April issue of "20 de Århundre," so now the May issue is already full with what remains. I hope to be able to print your article on sexual morality in the August issue. However, I understand that under these circumstances it will be impossible to find space for an article on the refugee question. Nevertheless, I believe that such an article would be very suitable for "Arbeiderbladet." Write a relatively short piece about it, and we will print it if we have room. It is much easier for us if the article is written in Norwegian.

The book by Braatoey was discussed in detail by me in August or September of last year.

Your article on unemployment has now been published, and I will transfer the honorarium to Kreditbanken.

With best regards

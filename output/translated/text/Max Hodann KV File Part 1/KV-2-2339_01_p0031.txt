25.8.33. Applied for a twelve-month extension to continue industrial research.

Cellulose Compositions Co. Ltd. provided supporting documentation for this application.

13.10.33. Extension granted until 21.9.34.

19.10.33. FINKELSTEIN wrote to the Head Office requesting permission for his wife and child (aged 11) to travel to the United Kingdom. He was informed that advance permission to land could not be granted, and that this matter would need to be addressed by the Immigration Officer.

11.1.34. Martha FINKELSTEIN (Jewish), born on 5.9.79 at Lubeck, arrived in Harwich. Passport No. 1 was issued on 3.1.34 at Berhberg, accompanied by her daughter Hildamarie.

22.8.34. Applied for an extension for himself, his wife and child.

30.8.34. Cellulose Compositions Co. Ltd. wrote to the Head Office stating that FINKELSTEIN had invested in the company and recommending his application for an extension.

20.9.34. Extension granted until 21.9.35.

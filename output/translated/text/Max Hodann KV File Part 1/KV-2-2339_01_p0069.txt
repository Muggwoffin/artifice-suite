BEACON HILL SCHOOL

BOYLE'S COURT,
SOUTH WEALD,
Near Brentwood,
Essex.
April 18th, 193 [Year]

Dear Mr. Hedann,

I am sorry if I have misunderstood your letter. I believed that Frau Lindback was able to pay £2½ per week. Unfortunately, the original letter is not with me. As you can appreciate, it would be exceedingly difficult for me to continue running the school, especially if I were to accept all children at a very low rate. However, given that this arrangement will only be temporary, I am willing to proceed. Please ask Frau Lindback to inform us whether Janka will be joining us next term.

My address is:

Carn Veel
Porthcurno
Cornwall.

Yours sincerely,

Dr. Russell

To Max Hodann, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

From Bertold Hawolff c/o Carl Pande, Tostrupsgate 22, Oslo.

Date of Letter: April 12, 1935.

Date of Postmark: April 13, 1935.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that he has written to Karen, requesting her presence in Oslo, which he hopes will provide her with safety and refuge.

Requests Hodann to keep him informed should he receive any relevant information.

For translation, please refer to the attached sheet.

(a) No action required. P.A.

**CROSS-REFERENCE.**

Subject: Dr. Max HODANN.

20.4.35. Miss Lise LINDBAEK-NILSSON wrote from her address at 63, Haverstock Hill, N.W.3., to the Under-Secretary of State requesting an extension of her visa and that of Dr. Max HODANN, her assistant, for a further six months. She stated that HODANN had been requested to travel to Brussels to deliver lectures during the period from April 29th to May 5th. (Extract from H.O. file L.1675.)

Minute to H.O.:

"Regarding Dr. Max HODANN, we believe his employment as assistant to Miss LINDBAEK is merely a pretext for remaining in this country.

As a refugee who may prove to be highly undesirable, we have no wish for him to settle permanently here. However, we would not object to granting him a short extension, after which we would be pleased to submit further observations.

(Sd.) O.A.H."

29.4.31. S.I.S. reported that Max HODANN arranged a meeting in Berlin in 1928 between the chief of the Russian Espionage service and Dr. GUMBEL of Heidelberg, who was then offered the position of Russian agent in Germany and neighbouring states, which he accepted. (Note: The summary regarding HODANN mentions his presence at a Reichs Foundation Congress of the F.O.S.U. in Berlin on 4.11.28.)

6.6.32. A press cutting from the "Daily Worker" mentions that Dr. Marx HODANN is one of the members of a preparatory committee formed in Germany to assist in the organisation of an International Congress against War, scheduled to take place in Geneva in July.

(This information is also contained in intercepted correspondence from Berlin W.I.R. Headquarters, addressed to Isabel BROWN), dated 8.6.32.

16.8.33. At a meeting of the German Relief Committee held by BRIDGEMAN, Isabel BROWN, Ellen WILKINSON, etc., the question of the Doctors' Committee was raised, as Dr. Louis, who was in charge of this, was launching a strong protest campaign against the solitary confinement imposed by the Nazis. The name of one of two eminent doctors suggested for inclusion in this campaign was apparently that of HODANN.

4.2.35. S.I.S. reported that Dr. Max HODANN was one of a group of German immigrants in Palestine, known to have belonged to "some radical party or other" before they left Germany. The following information about HODANN is appended:
"He was a member of the Social Democratic Party of Berlin and played an active role in politics. He has written several books, including one on travel in Soviet Russia. Following his travels in Russia, he became involved in Communist organisations such as the Red Aid Association and the League against Imperialism. He recently published articles on travel in Syria and Egypt in the German immigrant press."

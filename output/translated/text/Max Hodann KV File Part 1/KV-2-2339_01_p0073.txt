**PF.43817/D.S.9.a.**

Most Confidential and Personal.

April 17th, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

Following my PF.43817/D.S.9.a. of April 16th, 1935, regarding Max HODANN, I am writing to bring your attention to the following additional matters.

We have obtained a letter from Max LICHTENSTEIN in Copenhagen, addressed to HODANN, in which he states: "You may thank your stars and Lisa that you have been permitted to enter England. My emigration was terribly difficult."

The writer also mentions Maurice (?)RAPAPORT of 131 Regent Street, W.1. (whose existence we have not confirmed). He adds: "If you should speak with RAPAPORT, do not mention my political views nor my current lack of funds. He need not be informed."

The letter continues: "That indolent fellow, Heinz, should take an interest in the Englishman. If he possesses financial resources, he might establish a business in Holland and amass considerable wealth."

It appears that LICHTENSTEIN may be identical to a man of the same name about whom we wrote to you in September 1934 – your CX/6561/V. of October 13th, 1934, refers.

Furthermore, I omitted from my letter of yesterday a crucial detail: During observation of HODANN, he was seen visiting Flat 37, Hartington Court, Lansdowne Road, which is the address of [Alick WEST], whose name appears in your Most Confidential communications and with whom you have corresponded with Miss Saunders under CX/12650/3043/V.

Yours sincerely,

(SD) K.H.H.S.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

**To Mr. Biro-Bidjan and others in Jewish communities of Crimea and White Russia:**

I would be grateful if you could inform me whether you are able to fulfill our requests.

You may address your conference in German, and I am pleased to offer a fee of 500 French Francs (five hundred francs) per conference, or 1000 francs for both; travel expenses will be at your own cost. It is important that you know that you will receive a discount on transportation from May 1st onwards, following the International Exhibition held in Brussels.

Furthermore, it may be possible to organize further events of this kind during your stay in Brussels, but this would be without any commitment on our part.

I eagerly await your response and extend to you, Mr., my best regards.

The President,
Jacques Goldstein

34, Rue d'Arenberg
Brussels.

Mr. Boxall of the G.P.O. contacted me to report that letters for HODANN were experiencing a double delay due to their redirection from 24, Cockspur Street – an address added to the check on 27.3.35.

I agreed that, as the letters were being redirected to Kynance Court, it would be safe to cancel the 24, Cockspur Street address.

I spoke with Mr. Cumming who will advise the G.P.O. accordingly.

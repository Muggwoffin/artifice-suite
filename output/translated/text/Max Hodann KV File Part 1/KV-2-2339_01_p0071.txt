Photostat filed in: PF.43824. LINDBAEK-NILSSON.

From [Molla ORDING, Erling Skjalgsonns gate 23e, Oslo.]

To [Mrs. Lise LINDBAEK, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7]

Date of letter postmark: 9 April 1935.

Summary of Contents:
States that she has been remiss in writing "but now that there is a quiet moment at the Institute I can use it to advantage."

Reference to Jankung and a journey.

"I have received a long letter from Mrs. MARTIN urging me to join her in a mothers' congress and sending you her love. You probably know, she said, that the letter you had sent to Tavastehus was [incomplete], and there the rest of the sentence was scratched out. Anyhow, please write to her before the 15th from London, and I shall do the same. Ruben is staying with Ragna and Carl VIGGO at Trondhjem." Also news of George, Stang and Mons.

"There has been no further attack since you went away except that N.S. had an article about 'More Hodannism at our University' in reference to the talk about Professor MOHR as dean. None of our names was mentioned in connection with George but the Tidens Tegn had a long article by Eines about Mrs. MARTIN, which anyhow was full of inaccuracies, and he had told GJESDAHL that we were both spies." Mentions Karl JCHAN. States that Lisbeth BROCH will be going to England soon and asks addressee to look after her; says she will do Arne good. "I don't feel free until the situation is cleared up. It was fine that you were together and he was impressed by the way you got yourself and others into the country........... To think it is a year since Geneva. It leaves room for much philosophising, but that we had something, and something concrete, out of it, is certain..........."

PF. 43824/D.S. 9.a.

23rd April, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

Following my telephone conversation with Mr. Mills on Thursday and in continuation of my PF. 43817/D.S. 9.a. of 16.4.35., I am enclosing a translation of a letter from Molla ORDING to Lisa LINDBAEK, written in Norwegian.

Based on the contents, I believe we can be satisfied that Molla ORDING is indeed the governess who cared for George MARTIN.

This letter, along with what we already know about Max HODANN and Lisa LINDBAEK, suggests that both individuals likely possess significant knowledge regarding the Martin case, more than we initially suspected.

"Traute" presumably refers to Traute NEUMANN, mentioned in my letter of 16.4.35.

"George" must be George MARTIN, and "STANG" – Emil STANG, Mrs. MARTIN's lawyer.

"Arne" is Professor Arne ORDING, now residing at 1, Britannia Street, W.C.1.

According to the Traffic Index, Lisbeth BROCH has not yet arrived in this country, but I am taking steps to ensure that we will be informed if and when she does.

Regarding GJESDAHL, who is mentioned in the enclosed translation, we have a record of one Nils Severin GJESDAL, a Norwegian ship's fireman, who was deported from this country in January 1931 after being convicted of failing to register with the police. I am obtaining Home Office records for this man in order to obtain further information.

Yours sincerely,

(©) K.H. H.S.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

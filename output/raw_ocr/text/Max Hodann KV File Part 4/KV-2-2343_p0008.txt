SECRET

Alexis FRIELESTEN

A German chemist born 25.1.1877 at Leipzig, who arrived in the U.K. as a refugee in 1935 and was still resident in this country in 1945. He is on record here because of his connection with HODANN and Franz LINDBAEK, but we have no further adverse information about him.

It is impossible on current information to assess whether these associations of ROSENTHAL are of any significance. Perhaps the phrase "involved in the MARTIN Case" used in our previous letter was too strong, in that these individuals are themselves mainly suspect on grounds of association. Nevertheless there is some reason to believe that HODANN at any rate has had dealings with the R.I.S. in the past.

31 January, 1955.

C.P.C. de Wesselow.

(e) LINDBAEK-NILSON/HODANN.

In March 1935 Elise LINDBAEK-NILSON arrived in the U.K. in company with Max HODANN, with whom she was collaborating on a book on Palestine.

Max HODANN was suspected by [illegible] of being involved in Soviet Espionage. On arriving in the U.K. he made contact with Communist circles and visited a known Comintern courier named Alick WEST. HODANN later served eighteen months in the Army Sanitary Service in Spain.

Shortly after arriving in the U.K. HODANN received an anxious letter from Alexis S. FINKELSTEIN who had told KAREN that HODANN was "the Uncle Max HANSEN I spoke to in London".

(H) HODANN/HINKS(I) of S.O.E.

In December 1941 Roger HINKS of the B.B.C. was vetted for employment by S.O.E. and became their representative in Stockholm (responsible for P.W.E. matters) until August 1945, during which time he employed Max HODANN and Kurt STEIN, who furnished HINKS and S.O.E./12 with occasional reports on German activities in Sweden and spread P.W.E. propaganda, at a monthly salary of 1,100 Kroner plus rent & expenses.

Extract from letter from M.I.6. re Roger HINKS, suspected of espionage for Russia, ment. Max HODANN.

Another contact of Roger HINKS which the have also commented is that with Helmut GOTTSCHALK, with whom HINKS was in touch in Sweden in June 1944. (GOTTSCHALK is, I think, known to you for his connection with the case of Mrs. Mary Louise MARTIN). View was that HINKS maintained his contact with GOTTSCHALK on instructions from Max HODANN, who they thought was HINKS's chief. (S.O.E. records shew, however, that it was HODANN who worked for HINKS).

A note on the relationship of various personalities mentioned above and others connected has been prepared from our existing records here. As agreed during your visit to this office, I am attaching a copy of this for your information.

(d) MARTIN/LINDBAEK-NILSON.

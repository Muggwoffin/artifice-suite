Extract from S.I.S. letter re Roger Packman HINKS mentioning HODANN.

We know little of HINKS' activities when he was with S.O.E. in Stockholm. I find that among the people he employed were Dr. Max HODANN and Dr. Kurt STEIN. Dr. HODANN probably figures in your records. An interesting point about him is that he comes up in the MARTIN case which I am studying at the moment and about which I shall produce a paper in due course.

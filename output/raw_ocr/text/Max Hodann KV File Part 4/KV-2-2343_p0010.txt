SECRET

Dr. Fritz ROSENTHAL

Thank you for your letter of 15 December 1954 about the above named individual.

We know from a confidential and reliable source that in 1935 ROSENTHAL was given an introduction by Dr. Max HODANN to a certain Alexis PINKELSTEIN. ROSENTHAL had been recommended to HODANN by Frau LINDBANK through a mutual friend.

The MARTIN Case

Mrs. Maris MARTIN @ SCHUL @ PAQUETTE @ STREH @ STORF @ NEHERBACK, was accused of running a large Soviet espionage network in Finland in 1933 and was sentenced to eight years' imprisonment.

Mrs. MARTIN is well known to the F.B.I. as the wife of Janis Alfred TILFEN.

Dr. Max HODANN

A German, born 30.8.94 at Niesse. He has written a number of works on sexual problems. HODANN has a lengthy connection with Communism. In 1928 he was prominently associated with the German section of the F.B.I. and in 1929 apparently became International Secretary of the organisation.

According to one report, HODANN arranged a meeting in Berlin in 1928 between the "chief of the Russian espionage service" and a Dr. GUBAI of Heidelberg, who was then enrolled as a Soviet agent in Germany and neighbouring states.

HODANN associated with communists both in the U.K. and later in Sweden.

His connection with "the MARTIN Case" consists of his close association with Frau LINDBANK and the fact that he was in contact with a Professor Arne GRIND, said to be a relation of Nolla GRIND, who acted as nurse to Mrs. MARTIN's child.

Frau Elise LINDBANK @ Ida LINDBANK-MILSON

A Norwegian formerly married to a Dane. She was resident in Geneva in 1954 and there looked after Mrs. MARTIN's son, until he was fetched away presumably by the father, A. TILFEN @ MARTIN. An associate of Dr. Max HODANN and a circle of Norwegian communists, she was a member of the Norwegian Cultural Delegation to Moscow in 1950.

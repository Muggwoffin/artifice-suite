(k) HODANN/HINKS(2) of S.O.E.

In 1943 it was reported that the Swedish C.P. had secretly elected a Free German Committee for Sweden (modelled after that of Moscow), of which HODANN was chosen one of the members.

In 1945 S.O.E./12 noted in his records that HODANN had worked directly for HINKS and only indirectly for S.O.E./12. HODANN, who had maintained contact with German deserters and refugees interested in such matters as the re-education of Germany, was now no longer being employed by S.O.E./12 and there were good reasons for supposing that he was collaborating with the Russians.

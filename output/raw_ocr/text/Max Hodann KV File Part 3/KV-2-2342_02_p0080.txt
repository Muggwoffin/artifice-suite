From Ernst Toller, 9 Lambolle Road.

To Dr Max HODANN.

Date of Letter -

Date of Postmark 15.11.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Writer would like to help HODANN if he could. Asks whether he knows the people at Woburn House or the Quakers. He thinks one of the more influential Quakers should speak for HODANN or else advises him to go himself to the H.O. The people there are in general understanding. Writer does not believe there has been any dirty work but just a bureaucratic blunder. Tells HODANN to ring him up when he returns to London and to come and see him.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT
(a) No action required. P.A.

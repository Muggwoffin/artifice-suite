From Kurt HILLER, Flat 5, 2308, Prague, XII.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, South Weald, Brentwood.

Date of Letter 16.10.35.

Date of Postmark 16.10.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

"In answer to your questions:"

States that F. MATYAS, Bratislava, Vzecho-Slovakia, Hontianska 10, is in possession of first edition, 1896. "Sappho und Sokrates". "Write to him by all means...........a man given up to the good cause: and 'Akribiker'.

"Peter ZIMANN: Bln(?) W 50, Prager Strasse, 17, GH. You can write him sealed letters, with very cautious text, e.g. on no account mention "Saved" or "materials". Also instead of 'M.H.' "papa" should be said, or 'the old professor' or 'the ? councillor'"

"Moll? No idea. BLOCH: non plus. In 1920 I had to leave my library behind in Germany."

"As far as health goes I am getting on well, but financially catastrophic."

Encloses obituary notice for Magnus HIRSCHFELD. States that he tried to publish it in England and Denmark. Havelock ELLIS and Norman HAIRE refused. "LEUNBACH behaved disgustingly. He has quite changed over to the impossible Herr W. REICH.

For translation see attached sheet. Refers to Peter. Mentions address Columbia House, Oranienberg, Brauenburg. Gives his address from 1.11.35. as Premyslowska 23 IV (Suchy). Prague XII.

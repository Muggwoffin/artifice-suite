13.11.44. Vetting form from S.O.E. re employment of Max HODANN.

252.

F.2.b. Miss Bagot.

S.O.E. are proposing to make use of Max HODANN as "a contact in Sweden". (Main traces are 161a, 220a, 230a, 239a and 247a).

In view of our information I should be grateful for your comments on the S.O.E. proposal.

C.2 (London)
22 Nov 44.

253

C.2 (London)
Major Stone

I think HODANN's suitability depends upon how far S.O.E propose to confide in him. From his long connection with Communism & Soviet espionage it is obvious that there is a strong probability that he might be rather a leaky vessel. But S.O.E may have some specific work in which they can use him without telling him too much. It also seems possible that S.O.E work in conjunction with the Russians in Sweden.

CROSS REFERENCE.

JECT:-- FINKELSTEIN, Alexis Samuel.

.39. Source:- Report from C.C. Cambridge re. Henry Peter BAYON.

Report mentions that BAYON used to associate with Alexis Samuel FINKELSTEIN, a German alien, who resided at Great Shelford but removed to Girton believed during 1936.

Footnote to report says that FINKELSTEIN removed on 30.9.38. from Pepys Way, Girton, to C/o Mrs. Miles, 44c Denning Rd., London, N.W.3. He was a professor of chemistry at Cambridge University.

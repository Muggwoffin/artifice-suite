New Southgate N.11

Dear Mrs Palmer,

Enclosed please find copy of a letter received from Dr Hodann. I am writing to Dr Hodann to say that I cannot at present pay him anything for the article. As you know Miss Stella Brown well, can you get her to do the translation of Hodann's article for us gratis. I am so sorry that "Marriage Hygiene" is not yet in a position to pay for contributions but I expect the time will come when we shall be able to recompense all those who have been helpful to us.

Hodann's articles are generally good and I am hoping that this one also will come up to his usual standard. It is enough if I get it about the middle of May. That means Miss Brown will have to take up the translation immediately and the copy sent to me by air mail.

I have decided to appoint Hodann as Editor for Soviet Russia provided he will not expect any payment.

I hope you are better and on your feet again.

With best wishes and many thanks,

yours sincerely,

A. L. Fisher

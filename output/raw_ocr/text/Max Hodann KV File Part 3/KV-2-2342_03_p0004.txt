From B. Fl. (Dr. Bernard FLEURSCHEIM) Rushmoor, Fleet, Hants.

To Dr. Alexis FINKELSTEIN, Great Shelford, Cambridgeshire.

Date of Letter 4.11.35.

Date of Postmark 4.11.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges letter from addressee whom, he says, sounds depressed.

Is glad addressee's index, although anonymous, has received recognition.

Has to go to London next week and suggests that he might meet addressee.

Has just returned from a long stay in France and Switzerland where they have been for his wife's health. She apparently took a cure at Evian and saw a specialist at Geneva.

Hopes addressee's wife and little girl are keeping well.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

From Babett, Paris.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Beacon Hill School, South Weald, Essex.

Date of Letter 13.11.35.

Date of Postmark -

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

"We ask you urgently for the following information.

would like to know as quickly as possible about FERBER, who is living with him at the moment in Lyons. FERBER has been introduced to him through He gives to GUMBEL as reference the name of a certain Egon BURKERT a German emigrant from the circle of SCHERINGER to which FERBER says he also belongs. Egon BURKERT is now believed to be residing in England. (Sussex.)

I should be extremely grateful if you could get into touch with BURKERT and communicate to us his opinion about FERBER. Perhaps you also know FERBER personally and can give us information about him.

The matter is extremely urgent."

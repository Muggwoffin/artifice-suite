From Bernard Fl. Rushmoor, Fleet, Hants.

To Dr. Alexis FINKELSTEIN, Great Shelford, Cambridgeshire.

Date of Letter 8.11.35.

Date of Postmark

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges letter.

Is unable to meet addressee on Thursday as it is Election Day.

Has been asked at a Conservative meeting to help on that day.

Will meet addressee Thursday 21st. at any time in the Patent Office Library, ground floor.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT
(a) No action required. P.A.

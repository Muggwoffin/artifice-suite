PF.43817/DS9.  30th April 1936.

Dear Major Vivian,

After your CX.12650/3189 of 24.1.36 we had no indication of Max HODANN's whereabouts, but to-day we have seen a letter from him from Bygdo Alle 70, Oslo (a new address to us) where he apparently is with Lise LINDBAEK. He hopes to go to the U.S.S.R. in May, and it seems possible that Lisa LINDBAEK may go with him.

Yours sincerely,

Kours.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

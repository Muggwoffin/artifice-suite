Maes-yr-haf itself, being the Headquarters, interests its members in the same manner, if anything, on a far more advanced scale from an educational and social standpoint.

Since its inception a Handicraft Centre has been formed. A two storey weaving house has been erected, where a staff of about 20 is regularly employed in spinning and weaving, dressmaking, embroidery and quilting. The articles made are mostly sent away and sold by the Settlement management.

The "Malt House" at Wick is under their management and used as a holiday centre for men.

"Boverton Camp" Wick is also under command and is used as a holiday centre for women and girls. Only a nominal charge is made in both instances for a weeks holiday.

Each winter, commencing in October, educational classes and social functions are held at Maesyrhaf.

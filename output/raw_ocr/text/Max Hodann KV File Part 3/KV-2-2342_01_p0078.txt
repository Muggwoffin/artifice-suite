[Max Lichtenstein.]

Reference your PF.43817/D.S.9a, dated 22.10.35.:

I enclose herewith a photograph of Lichtenstein received.

The latter state that Lichtenstein had a "Permis de Sejour" valid up to 31.1.36. In connexion with his recent application to have this Permit extended, he was interrogated and the following was elicited from him:-

He is a German subject and a political refugee. He arrived in Denmark on 31.10.34. and is at present residing at Graekenlandsvey 120, Copenhagen. He is a merchant and has a business in Berlin with a capital of 80,000 Marks. This business is now being looked after by a friend who remits him 100 Marks every month.

On arrival in Denmark Lichtenstein was in possession of a German passport valid up to 3.11.35. When this passport expired he approached the German Consul in Copenhagen for a new one, but his request was flatly refused.

He is married, but his wife lives in Holland.

I should be grateful if you would let me know, in due course, the result of the comparison of this photograph with that of Lichtenstein alias Ornsteyn, forwarded with my CX/12650/3189 of 11.1.36.

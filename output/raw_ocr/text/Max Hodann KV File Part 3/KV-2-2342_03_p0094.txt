Conclusions:

Generally speaking HODANN seems to be connected with Left Wing German Political refugees, both here and abroad. In addition he seems to have certain very definite Communist connections.

He came here to assist Lise LINDBAEK - with whom he is undoubtedly on very intimate terms - in compiling her biography of Nansen. Actually it is doubtful whether he is engaged on this work. On the other hand he seems to be in touch with doctors, chemists and scientists, possibly connected with his work on sex problems in which he is undoubtedly interested.

It seems possible that HODANN may be actively associated with the work of the German Relief Committee and the assistance of Political refugees both at home and abroad. Max LICHSTENSTEIN in Copenhagen seems to control certain funds in this connection and may even be running some kind of organisation into Germany.

From [Dr. Felix TIETZE,] Wahringerstrasse 5-7, Vienna IX/2.

To Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, South Weald, Brentwood.

Date of Letter 19.10.35.

Date of Postmark 19.10.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that he has just received a letter from Dr. PILLAY from Bombay "in which he enclosed a copy of your letter of the 2nd October, to him."

States that he has been asked, on the recommendation of Dr. C.P. BLACKER and Major Leonard DARWIN, by Dr. PILLAY, to take on the position of editor for the European Continent for the journal "Marriage Hygiene".

States that Dr. PILLAY has informed him of addressee's ideas of founding a society - does not think it would be successful, partly on account of the language difficulty.

"I am, as you see, a children's doctor. I am also on the way to the field of work of M.H."

For translation see attached sheet.

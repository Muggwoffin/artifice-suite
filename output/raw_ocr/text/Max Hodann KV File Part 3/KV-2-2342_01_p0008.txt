Captain Liddell.

You will probably like to see that in accordance with Home Office instructions HODANN left the U.K. on 1.1.36 (213a), and that three days later he was followed by Lise LINDBAEK (file attached).

I have therefore cancelled the H.O.W. and informed Major Vivian: I imagine that now they are out of this country there is nothing more to be done? (Green cards have been inserted in the Traffic Index.)

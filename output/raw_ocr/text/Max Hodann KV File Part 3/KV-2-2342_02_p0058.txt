From Lionel PENDLEBURY, Canon Hall, Bridge of Earn, Perthshire.

To Dr. FINKELSTEIN, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter 10.12.35.

Date of Postmark -

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges letter and states that he has heard from Karen, who wrote from Motystr.

Refers to BELLERBY who "is up a pole". "People who say we ought to be more friendly to Adolf in the interests of peace overlook the fact that our opposition to his policy has been purely Platonic humbug designed to give us an excuse to arm to the teeth while we really do all we can for him."

Continues in the same strain as above.

Will possibly call on addressee during the Christmas holiday.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

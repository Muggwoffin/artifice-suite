PF.43817/DS9.  14th January 1936.

Dear Major Vivian,

Reference our correspondence about Max HODANN, You will like to know that the Home Office have refused permission for this man to remain longer in the U.K. and he accordingly departed on 1st January for Ostend.

I have arranged for us to be notified if he should land again in this country.

Yours sincerely,

[handwritten initials]

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E., S.I.S.

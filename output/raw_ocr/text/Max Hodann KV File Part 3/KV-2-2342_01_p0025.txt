Cross Ref. to S.I.S. report re FREE GERMAN CTE in SWEDEN

A Swedish Communist Arnold L JUNGDAHL issued invitations to a meeting to elect a Free German Cte modelled after that of Moscow.

The Movement's Sponsors decided to wait for official Swedish reactions before making public announcement of the names of those who were chosen to serve on local Free German Cte but it is reported that Max HODANN (Pres) is one of four secretly chosen members of Cte. Nothing is known of the Cte's functions. Some Communists are reported to be dissatisfied with its composition.

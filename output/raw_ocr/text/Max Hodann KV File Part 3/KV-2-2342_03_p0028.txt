From Professor FREUD, Berggasse 19, Vienna IX.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, Brentwood, Essex.

Date of Letter 20.10.35.

Date of Postmark 20.10.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Has no one there whom he can request to get together the PSA(?) literature for addressee but suggested that HODANN could get it from Dr. J. RICKMAN, 11, Kent Terrace, N.W.1 "who owns (or has written) a valuable publication 'Index Psycho-analysis 1893-1926.'"

Says that addressee must surely know that there is a PSA(?) Society in England under the leadership of Dr. E. JONES.

For translation see attached sheet.

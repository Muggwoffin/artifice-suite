PF.43817/D.S.9.a.  24th October, 1935.

Dear Captain Lindsay,

In connection with certain investigations I am at present making the name of the following settlement has come to notice: - Maes-Yr-Haf, Educational Settlement, Trealaw, Rhondda.

I should be most grateful for any information you may be able to let me have regarding this Settlement and the nature of the classes and lectures given.

The manner in which it has come to notice in my investigations would seem to indicate certain Communistic leanings.

Yours sincerely,

(G.W.L.)

Colonel Sir Vernon Kell.

Captain L.A. Lindsay, O.B.E., M.V.O.,
Chief Constable,
Glamorganshire County Constabulary,
Canton, CARDIFF.

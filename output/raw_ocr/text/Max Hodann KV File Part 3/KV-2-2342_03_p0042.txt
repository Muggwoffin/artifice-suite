HOME OFFICE,
WHITEHALL, S.W.1.

H: 2807. 21st October, 1935.

Dear Mrs. Graham Murray,

You wrote to me on the 10th October on behalf of Dr. Max Hodann, who is desirous of prolonging his stay in this country. I have had enquiries made into the case and I find that Dr. Hodann has been allowed to enter and prolong his stay in this country until the 29th October solely for the purpose of acting as secretary to Mrs. Lindbaek. I am unable to agree to his establishing himself in this country or remaining here after the 29th October with a view to carrying out the projects outlined in the enclosure to your letter, which I am now returning.

Yours very sincerely,

(signed) John Simon.

The Hon. Mrs. Graham Murray.

S.F.420/Germany/F.2b/WJBB.

OXFORD.

13th January, 1944.

Dear Mr. Curry,

Would you please refer to your C.X.12535/36/IX dated 12.12.43 enclosing an O.S.S. report about the Free German Committee in Sweden.

For record purposes you may care to have the following notes of our information about the individuals mentioned:-

JUNGDAHL, Arnold L. No trace.

SEYDEMITZ, Max First came to notice in February 1933 when, as a representative of the German Socialist Workers Party, he addressed the National Congress of the French Party of Proletarian Unity in Paris. As a member of the S.A.P he was one of the signatories to an appeal for a German Popular Front published by 'Humanité' on 9.1.37 and on 10.9.39 in your C.X.12535/V you stated that he had lost all influence in the S.A.P.

HODANN, Max This man is well known and has been the subject of correspondence ending with your of 9.10.41.

PESCHKE (? PESCHE), Paul This man is probably identical with Professor PESCHKE who was mentioned in a letter dated 24.8.42 from Albert THEILE, Santiago, Chile to Senorita Prof. Dra. Marietta BLAH. c/o Free Germany, Mexico. PESCHKE was said to have escaped from Europe and to be actively engaged in anti-Nazi propaganda in the University of Uppsala, where he was a guest professor.

It seems unlikely that the President of the Free German Committee in Sweden is identical with Paul PESCHKE who first came to notice in 1929 when he contributed articles to "International Press Correspondence" about German industrial strikes. This man was subsequently connected with the International Anti-War Congress and has been mentioned in correspondence ending with your C.X.7652/V dated 1.2.33.

PEYSER, Alfred Probably identical with Dr. A. PEYSER, Valhallvagen 159, Stockholm, who on 8.12.42 wrote about friends to Professor Gerhard LEIBHOLZ. LEIBHOLZ, a German Jewish refugee, came to notice in 1940 when it was learnt that he was living at 33 Beechcroft Road, Oxford and was obtaining books for a bookseller by passing orders on to his brother Hans

/LEIBHOLZ

Curry, Esq.,
S.I.S.

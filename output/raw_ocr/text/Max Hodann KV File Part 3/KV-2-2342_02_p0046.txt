Translation of Int. F.M. 8085.

From Kurt JACOBY, Cumberland Hotel, London, W 1.

To Dr. [illegible] Finkelstein.

27.12.35.

"I would like to confirm our detailed discussion of yesterday shortly as follows:

You say that you have agreed to take on for us parts of the translation of the 'Richter-Anschütz' for the London edition. The fee is a sum of £4 per 16 pages. In addition it is intended to interest you in other literary works of aid. The work is to begin about the middle of January.

I hope our cooperation in work will have very good results.

With every good wish."

AW 6/1/36.

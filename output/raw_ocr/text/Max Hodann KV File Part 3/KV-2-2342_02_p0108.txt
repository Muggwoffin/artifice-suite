I wonder if Karen is still at Motysto. Her last letter had no address, & something she said seemed to suggest she might have moved, though otherwise I should have assumed the address was still the same. Her removals are a bit confusing & as often as not I write to Clara's.

Best wishes to Mrs Finkelstein, and to Hilde-Marie—especially if she has gone to her new school.

Yours sincerely,

L. F. Pendlebury

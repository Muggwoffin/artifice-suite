Professor Emil GUMBEL.

Reference your PF.43817, dated 20.11.35., regarding a certain GUMBEL, resident at Lyons, who was referred to in a letter from "BABETTE" to Max HODANN:

Information obtained

is to the effect that this man is identical with Professor Emil GUMBEL, born at Munich on 18.7.91. This man, they say, drew attention to himself in September 1934 as a member of the "Comité International d'Aide aux Victimes du Fascisme Hitlerien", 65 Boulevard Arago, Paris.

From this, I think we can take it that Professor Emil GUMBEL is identical with Dr. E.J. GUMBEL, subject of previous correspondence between us.

for V.V.

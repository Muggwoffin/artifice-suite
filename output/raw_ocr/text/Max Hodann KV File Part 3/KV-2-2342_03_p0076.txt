Gist of Int. B 540 K.

From Dr. Felix TIETZE, Vienna IX/2, Währingerstrasse 5 - 7.

To Dr. Max HODANN, South Weald, Brentwood, Essex.

"I have just received a letter from Dr. PILLAY from Bombay, in which he enclosed a copy of your letter of the 2nd October, to him.

I have been asked, on the recommendation of Dr. C.P. BLACKER and Major Leonard DARWIN, by Dr. PILLAY, to take on the position of editor for the European Continent for the journal "Marriage Hygiene", and have accepted it, although in many states on the continent, especially in Middle Europe, where there is nothing doing. Dr. PILLAY has also informed me of his ideas on the founding of a society and I have already told him that I do not think it will be successful, partly on account of the language difficulty. As you also have written him about this, even if from the standpoint of Great Britain I should like to write me something for 'Marriage Hygiene'. I should also be grateful for other suggestions for the magazine. I am, as you see, a children's doctor. I am also on the way to the field of work of M.H."

AW 2/10/35

Dr. Max Julius Alexander HODANN.

Born 30.8.94. Niesse. German citizen, doctor and City Counsellor. Has written a number of works on sexual problems.

This man has a certain revolutionary past which dates back in our records to 1928 when he was prominently associated with the German Section of the F.S.U. In 1929 he appears to have become International Secretary of that organisation.

In 1928, according to an S.I.S. report, HODANN arranged a meeting in Berlin between the chief of the Russian espionage service and Dr. GUBEL of Heidelberg who was then offered and accepted the post of Russian agent in Germany and the neighbouring States.

In August 1933, HODANN seemed to be connected with the Doctors' Committee of the German Relief Committee.

In 1935 he visited Palestine with Lisa LINDBAEK who at one time is known to have been in charge of the child of Mrs. MARTIN, now undergoing a term of imprisonment in Finland for Soviet espionage. He arrived here in March 1935 in company with Lisa LINDBAEK and stated that his business was to assist that lady in compiling a biography of Nansen and to study social conditions.

It transpired later that he was a correspondent of the Arbeiderbladet in Copenhagen.

1. He has been in touch with SAKLANTVALA.

2. He is connected with the headquarters of Rundschau in Basle.

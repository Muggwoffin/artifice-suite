The latest information regarding HODANN was received in S.I.S. in December 1943 in connection with their report on the Free German Committee in Sweden. It was reported that a Swedish Communist had issued invitations to a meeting to elect a Free German Committee modelled after that of Moscow. The Movement's sponsors decided to wait for official Swedish reactions before disclosing the names of those chosen to serve on the Committee. It was reported that HODANN was one of four secretly chosen members of the Committee. Since leaving Germany HODANN does not appear to have practised as a doctor but is a contributor to the Press on social and cultural matters.

Generally speaking it can be said that HODANN is a political refugee who has very definite Communist connections. His, together with his alleged connection with Soviet espionage in Berlin in 1928 points to the distinct possibility that he might prove to be rather a "leaky vessel".

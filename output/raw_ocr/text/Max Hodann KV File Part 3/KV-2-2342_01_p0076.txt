COPY in List 451 (167)

PF.43817/DS9. 5th March 1936.

Dear Major Vivian,

We have compared the photograph of [Max LICHTENSTEIN] forwarded in your CX.12650/3189 of 28.2.36 with that of Samuel ORNSTEYN alias LIECHTENSTEIN, and I think it is quite certain they are not identical.

In view of this and the fact that there is only a slight difference in month between their dates of birth, it looks as though the LICHTENSTEIN who was denounced to the Immigration Officer is the same as the contact of HODANN now in Copenhagen. It is perhaps also worth noticing that ORNSTEYN is of Polish origin while LICHTENSTEIN is in both instances stated to be a German.

Yours sincerely,

[handwritten] RE: DC

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E., S.I.S.

Exhibitions of paintings and interesting posters are held periodically and when these are held, an arrangement is made with the Rhondda Education Committee for teachers to bring their classes to view the exhibits.

There is a large library on the premises, some of the books are the gift of Her Majesty the Queen.

A tutorial class on Biology has been held during the last few winters and has included visits to the Laboratories at the Cardiff University. Full length courses on Social History, Economic & Current Problems, English Literature, Philosophy and History.

The courses have been held in co-operation with the Worker's Education Association & Joint Committee of Educational Bodies for Adult Education in South Wales. This includes the University of Cardiff. The lecturers in most instances have been men holding Degrees.

From my observations I am able to state that the class of persons attending and connected with the settlement courses and activities are the better class people of the district, a large number being school teachers and others in good positions.

A good number of studious employed and unemployed ordinary working class also attend, wives and daughters also being catered for in the sewing groups, knitting classes, social evenings etc.

I have not on any occasion known a person holding communist views have any connection whatsoever with the Headquarters of the Settlement.

Some of their unemployed members of clubs have on a few occasions been known to attend, as representatives of a club, at Unemployed Demonstrations and to have carried banners bearing slogans but not communist slogans. As far as I am aware this was not encouraged by the wardens of the Settlement.

Up to the present the wardens have not allowed Maesyrhaf to be used by any party for political purposes. The wardens on behalf of the Society of Friends appear to be inclined towards Labour but do not express themselves. In my opinion the wardens would not tolerate any drift or leaning towards Communism.

I am, Sir,
Your obedient servant
William F. Gully

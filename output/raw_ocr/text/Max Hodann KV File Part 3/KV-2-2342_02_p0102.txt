For Finkelstein
(Cambridge)

RUSHMOOR.
FLEET.
HANTS.
Nov. 8th 35.

Dear Alexis,

Thank you for your letter. It is too bad, but Thursday is Election Day; it was understood that there would be no election in our constituency, as there was only one candidate, but at the last moment, a third party candidate was put up, and last night, at a local Conservative meeting, I have been asked to help on Election Day. I have done this for more than 25 years, & shall do it this time, too. Also, I must add.

Having left you the chiro of day, and being one of those old-fashioned people who are punctilious about keeping appointments, I feel very bad about it, but hope that you will understand. But since Thursday is obviously your preferred day, I could meet you on the following Thursday (21-1) instead. If you will be in town on that day, I can be at the Post Office library, ground floor, at any time in the afternoon.

Yours sincerely,

R. M. 7724

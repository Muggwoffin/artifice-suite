Photostat filed in: L.312(1740)

From Edward R. ELKAN,
14a Manchester Square,
London, W.1.

To Dr. Alexis FINKELSTEIN, The Cellulose Composition Co.,
Trumpington, Great Shelford, Cambridge.

Date of letter 5.11.35.

Summary of Contents

"For the first time in our relations I shall try to write you a letter concerning technique and business."

Letter relates to cervical caps etc.

"I am glad to hear that Karin is comparatively safe. Please convey my greetings to her if you write."

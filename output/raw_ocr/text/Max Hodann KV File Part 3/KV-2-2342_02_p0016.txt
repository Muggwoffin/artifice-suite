Dear Sir,

Replying to your recent letter we would inform you that there is no flash point for "Xylonite", but it fumes off at temperatures over 300° F.

The material we supply from stock can be considered as an ordinary commercial quality.

As regards price, this is governed by the thickness. The thicker gauges are cheaper than the thinner ones, but if you will let us know the thicknesses that interest you, we shall be pleased to give you prices.

Yours faithfully,

EXTRACT.

Referring to Alexis FINKENSTEIN.

Extracted from R.F. 44989. ROSENTHAL, 8a. No. .............................................

Author of original M.I.S. to S.I.S. ........ Place and date of origin. 25.5.36

Extract made by... C. THOMAS .................. on (date) 16.4.40 .............

With reference to correspondence in the past about A. FINKENSTEIN and his connection with Max HODANN, in October 1935 we saw a letter from a certain Dr. Fritz ROSENTHAL to FINKELSTEIN in which the writer stated that he was an organic chemist who had studied in Berne and was now looking for a post in England. He said he had been advised by Dr. Max HODANN to approach FINKELSTEIN, and enclosed a letter from HODANN which said that Rosenthal was of German Jewish extraction and had been recommended to him (HODANN) by a mutual friend in Paris through Frau LINDBAEK.

Sir,

Re. The MAES-YR-HAF EDUCATIONAL SETTLEMENT
at TREALAW.

I respectfully beg to report that this Educational Settlement was founded in this district by the "QUAKERS" in 1926.

The person responsible for its inauguration, is Mrs Emma NOBLE. She came to South Wales in May 1926 together with some members of the Society of Friends who sought opportunities to help the prevailing distress in the district at that time. They got in touch with the local Council, Guardians, Miner's Federation, Labour Party, School Teachers and groups of men and women organized for mutual help, and thereby got in close touch with the life and needs of the district.

Almost from the commencement it has been under the Wardenship of: - Mr & Mrs William & Emma Noble, but is at present in the charge of Mr & Mrs Miles Davies as Acting Wardens.

PF.43817/D.S.9.a.  12th November, 1935.

Dear Mr. Rawson,

The name [Lionel F. PENDLEBURY] has recently come to my notice in the course of certain investigations I am making at the present time. My only previous record of this man shows that he had definite Communist leanings whilst at Cambridge University.

I understand that in July of this year his address was 21, Marriner's Drive, Heaton, Bradford.

I should be most grateful for any information you may be able to let me have regarding him, particularly as to whether he associates himself with Communist activities locally. I am of course anxious that he should not become aware that he is the subject of enquiry.

Yours sincerely,

(Sd) G. H. H.

Colonel Sir Vernon Kell.

Thomas Rawson Esq.,
Chief Constable,
City Constabulary,
BRADFORD.

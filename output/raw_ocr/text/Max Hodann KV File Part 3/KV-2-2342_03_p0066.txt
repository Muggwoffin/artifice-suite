5. Finally, I must know how long they want me to stay there.

I hope they are not frightened by so many questions, but the answers will make it easier for me to make my arrangements.

Then I will give some facts upon myself:
I am 25 years old, graduated Bachelor 1923. Then I passed a training school of cooking (6 months). Afterwards I took alone care of the household at home (3/4 year). Then I went to Paris (1 year). There I passed a school for foreigners "Alliance Française". At the same time I took care of the education of a little girl (2 years). Besides this I partly took care of the family's household. Arriving from France I commenced my library perfection (1 1/2 year). Then I was engaged as librarian at "Studentenamfundet Bibliotek" Oslo, where I have now been working for 2 years. These 2 years I have been busy with other work, too, as teaching (Norwegian and French), translation, office-work, a.s.o. Further I have taken active part in our social student work.

As I said before I should very much enjoy to pass some time in the settlement. Naturally I should do my best, whatever the work might be. And I want to say that I am not afraid of working.

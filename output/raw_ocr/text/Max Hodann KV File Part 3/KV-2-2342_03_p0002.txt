Gist of Int. C 445 C.

From J.H. LEUNBACH, Upsalagade, Copenhagen.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, Brentwood.

4.11.35.

Thinks it a pity that the addressee's work has had to suffer such an unexpected injury. It is also a pity that they have just changed and the new house is not nearly finished. Mia and the writer are living at present in a one-roomed house which he is to use alone in the future. The rest of the family is living in in a homeless dwelling till the house is ready. They will have no guest rooms in future and unfortunately cannot receive the addressee. Will try to find accommodation for him elsewhere. Tells him that if he arrives on Sunday evening to go to a hotel and ring up next morning. His telephone number remains unchanged, Obro 5586 or 5573. His new address is: Upsalagade 3. In the meantime they will find somewhere for him, probably a cheap hotel. The addressee will probably meet Lisa JENSEN here too. She is to speak in Copenhagen on Nov. 14th. They will probably be able to find material for him here, which will be easier when he himself is present.

AW 7/11/35.

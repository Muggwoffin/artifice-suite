From Gina PHILIPSBORN, Bolshavn, Denmark.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Beaconhill School, Brentwood.

Date of Letter 16.10.35.

Date of Postmark 17.10.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Apologises for delay in replying to his letter. Thanks him for his report. Her plans all went to pieces owing to the fact that she did not dare to procure the necessary identity papers for her daughter, as she would have had to send in her own passport.

Refers to having stayed in a house tainted with bourgeois and zionist tendencies, which was very undesirable.

Encloses letter from ? illegible: V. Philipson, Bolshavn/Bornholm.

Asks whether addressee has come across the address of a company in Paris which enables doctors to go to Russia. He would be interested to see the conditions there for himself.

Asks whether HODANN advises him to go there if not absolutely necessary. Does not mind work and living simply but is too old to stand many privations. Actually there is no immediate need for them to leave Denmark.

For translation see attached sheet.

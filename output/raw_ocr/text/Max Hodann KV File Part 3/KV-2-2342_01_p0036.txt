GLOUCESTER CONSTABULARY

FINKELSTEIN, Alexis Samuel,

Address: Buckingham House, Wellington Street, Cheltenham.

Occupation: None, Formerly chemist.

Born: 25.1.77. Leipzig.

We have traces that in 1935 and 1936 this enemy alien was an associate of a German political refugee, who in turn had been connected with the Soviet espionage system and was also connected with a woman who had received the maximum sentence for espionage in Finland.

We have, however, no recent trace of FINKELSTEIN.

R. K. D. Preston

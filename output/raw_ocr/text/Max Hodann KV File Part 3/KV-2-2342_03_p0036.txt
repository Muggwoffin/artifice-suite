14.10.35. The Hon. Mrs. Graham-Murray forwards application for extension.

This man is trying to establish himself here, and os is Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON who, it appears, has a daughter at school in the U.K. (see E.C. report on N.904/2).

In the first place:

Index to C.of P. (S.B.) for enquiry as to how long Dr. H. wishes to stay, what he proposes to do, what his financial position is, and whether he proposes to go to Norway at any future date for residence, as has so often been stated. What are Mrs. L's intentions? (N.904.)

(sd) T.B.W. 16.10.35.

It is extremely doubtful if Dr. HODANN should be allowed to remain in U.K. He was lucky to get in at all and would not have done so but for the intervention of the Norwegian Legation who were primarily concerned with Mrs. LINDBAEK for whom Dr. HODANN is said to have been acting as secretary although it is open to doubt whether his activities in that direction have been very serious. He is known to be an active and prominent Communist whose spiritual home is in Russia and he is likely to be a stormy petrel if he is allowed to settle here. He is connected with the Berlin Institut von sexuelle Torsetung was very intimate and it is known the outlook of the Institut on sex matters was extremely advanced and likely to offend the susceptibilities of ordinary people. It seems desirable to bring this man's stay to an end before we are stuck with him.

?Say that Dr. Max HODANN has been allowed to prolong his stay in this country solely for the purpose of acting as secretary to Mrs. LINDBAEK and S.of S. is not prepared to agree to his establishing himself in this country or remaining here after 29/10. Return letter, keeping copy. Copy to C.of P.

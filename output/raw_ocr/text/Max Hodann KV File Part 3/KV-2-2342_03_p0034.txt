Gist of Int. B 613 K.

From Professor FREUD, Vienna IX, Berggasse 19.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, Brentwood, Essex.

20.10.35.

Has no one there whom he can request to get together the PSA(?) literature for the addressee. But he can get it nearer home. In London, 11 Kent Terrace, N.W. 1, there lives an anylist, Dr. J. RICKMAN, who owns (or, has written) a valuable publication 'Index Psycho-analysis 1893 - 1926'. It is quite possible that his interest did not cease with the date 1926.

The addressee must surely know that there is a PSA(?) Society in England under the leadership of Dr. E. JONES.

AW 26/10/35.

Any back Pd?
K. SW
26/10.

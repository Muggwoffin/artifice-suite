Date of letter submitted
27.9.39.

M.1.5. (Original)
Copies to M.C.3.

It may be thought advisable to enquire into writer's activities.

Writer apparently an enemy alien who expects "to be granted a certain amount of liberty" by the Tribunals, asks addressee not to write to him at the address he used before the war but to him, or "still" better to his wife ANNA L. FRANK, at his new address. He appears to be engaged on journalistic work and is anxious for news and press cuttings from SWEDEN and GERMANY. He states that he has transferred the publication of the "Informations-brief" to NEW YORK. His political sympathies are not revealed.

P.A. in PF 43,817 HOBANN & MARTENS.

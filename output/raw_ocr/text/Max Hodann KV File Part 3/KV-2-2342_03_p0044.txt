HOME OFFICE,

and the following number quoted:—
H: 2807.

HOME OFFICE,
WHITEHALL.

23rd October, 1935.

The Under Secretary of State is directed to return the passport of Dr. Max HODANN and to say that the date on or before which the holder is required to leave the United Kingdom has been postponed to the 29th October, 1935.

The new endorsement on the passport should be shown to the Aliens Registration Officer at the Police Station where the holder is registered.

Mrs. Lise Lindbaek-Nilson,
Beaconhill School,
SOUTH WEALD,
Essex.

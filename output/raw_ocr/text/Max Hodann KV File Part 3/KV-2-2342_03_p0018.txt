Dear Sir,

Maes-yr-Haf Educational Settlement, Trealaw.

In reply to your letter of the 24th instant:
I beg to forward herewith a good report which has been submitted to me by the local Police officer in the district where this Settlement is situated, and which, I think, will give you the information you require.

As far as we are aware, this Settlement has always been very well conducted, and we have received no hint of any Communistic leanings in connection with their activities.

Yours faithfully,

Lionel Lindsay
Chief Constable.

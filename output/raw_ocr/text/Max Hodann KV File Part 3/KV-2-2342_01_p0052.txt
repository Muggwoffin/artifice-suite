Oslo Hotel Parkheimen
Drammensveien 2
8th dec. 1938

Dear comrade,

I came just back from Spain after 1 1/2 years sanitary service in the army. Now, I published here in Norway a children-novel concerning the history of a half-jewish schoolboy in Germany who has to emigrate after being maltreated by the Nazis. It is an experiment to write a political novel for children between 12-15 years. The children who read it were very satisfied. Do you think that we could interest Gollancz to start an english translation? He has, of course, a lector who is reading norwegian?

Hoping to hear soon from you, with the best regards to the West's

Yours sincerely

Max Hodann
(Max Hodann)

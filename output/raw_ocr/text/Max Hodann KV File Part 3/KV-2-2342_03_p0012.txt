From [Janet CHANCE, Kitelands.]

To Dr. HODANN and [Mrs. LINDBAEK,] Beacon Hill School, Boyle's Court, South Weald.

Date of Letter -

Date of Postmark 4.11.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Writes to condole with them on the Home Office decision regarding HODANN with "regret and disgust at this uncivilised treatment."

Hopes to see HODANN on Monday.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT
(a) No action required. P.A.

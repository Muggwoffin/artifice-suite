CROSS-REFERENCE.

HODANN Max.

36. Gordon GRACE is still employed as Secretary at Beacon Hill School, Brentwood.

On the 19th July, 1935, two aliens viz: Max HODANN, a German subject, Reg: Cert: No. 568684 issued at Bow Street on the 1.5.35. and Elise Neander NILSSON came to reside at Beacon Hill School, Brentwood from 63 Haverstock Hill, London, N.W.3.

HODANN was the subject of considerable correspondence, "reference" his departure from this country and during a conversation I had with him he informed me that should he return to Germany he would be shot owing to his Political views.

He departed from Dover for Belgium on the 1.1.36. and was shortly afterwards followed by NILSSON.

C.C. Essex. 28.

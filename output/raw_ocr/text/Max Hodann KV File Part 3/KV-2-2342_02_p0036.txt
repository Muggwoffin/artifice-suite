13. SHELFORD PARK.
GREAT SHELFORD.
CAMBRIDGE

Akademische Verlagsgesellschaft M.B.H.,
Translations.

6th December, 1935.

Dear Doctor,

The transfer of the fee due to you has been, unfortunately, refused by the exchange office. We are obliged to apply once more, giving more details. Please state from your correspondence at what time we have agreed about the General Index 151-175.

(Signed) Jacoby.

Cumberland Hotel, London. 27th December, 1935.

Dear Doctor,

Confirming yesterday's interview, I beg to state that you agree, in principle, to translate on our resp. the London Company's account parts of Richter Anschlag, the fee to be £4 for the 16 pages sheet. In addition, we hope for your collaboration with other literary work, to begin by the middle of January.

I hope for a close and successful collaboration.

Yours sincerely,

(Signed) Kurt Jacoby.

.43817/DS8.

20th November 1935.

Dear V.V.,

With reference to my PF.43817/DS9a of 22.10.35 attached is a translation of a letter addressed to Max HODANN signed "Babette", who is presumably Babette GROSS, Willi MUNZENBERG's mistress. GUMBEL I imagine to be Dr. E.J. GUMBEL, subject of previous correspondence between us showing him as connected with the League against Imperialism and other kindred organisations. He is also presumably identical with the GUMBEL mentioned in your CX.6561/V dated 29.4.31 for whom Max HODANN arranged a meeting with the Chief of the Russian Espionage Service. It was also stated that GUMBEL accepted the post of Russian agent in Germany and the neighbouring states as a result of this meeting. GUMBEL is now evidently situated at Lyons where there is also resident an individual named FERBER whom GUMBEL got to know through the introduction of Traute NEUMANN, HODANN's ex-wife. He has given as a reference to GUMBEL a certain Egon BURKERT who is now living in England. We have a trace of an Egon BURCHER or BURCHARDT who is living at Bexhill-on-Sea. He appeared to have been in touch with Communists and to be generally interested in Left Wing Refugees.

Babette GROSS wants Max HODANN to get into touch with BURKERT and find out about FERBER. I think it might be worth while trying to get some information out of the French about Dr. GUMBEL's present activities in Lyons.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E., S.I.S.

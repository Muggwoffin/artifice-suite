Karen appears to have gone to Oslo but on 11.8.35. was back in Berlin.

On 5.4.35. a certain Berthold HASTOLL, c/o Carl PANDE, Tostrupsgate 22, 111 Oslo, was writing to HODANN about the arrest of Karen and the difficulties her friends were having with the Police. In a later letter he told HODANN that he had asked Karen to come to Oslo.

On 22.4.35. K. Ceul ROUNG or KEUNZ, Kusnetski Most 9, Quartier 9, Moscow, wrote to FINKELSTEIN stating that if his daughter Karen was a member of the Party she should get permission from the Party before going to Moscow where she would have to work as a Russian and not as a specialist.

On 3.4.35. correspondence showed that Dr. Arne ORDING, a Norwegian professor, aged 36, who came here in March 1935, was in touch with HODANN.

ORDING is friendly with the Swedish revolutionary VILBO who is now associated with the International Bureau for Revolutionary Socialist Unity in London.

ORDING is evidently a relation of Molla ORDING of Erling Skjalgsonns gate 23e. Oslo, who on 2.4.36. wrote to Lisa LINDBAEK about Mrs. MARTIN, her son George and Emil STANG, the Norwegian lawyer who acted on Mrs. MARTIN's behalf. Molla ORDING at one time acted as nurse to Mrs. MARTIN's child. She is evidently au fait with revolutionary activities in Norway.

One Hans VOOT of Oslo is in touch with ORDING. He seems to be au fait with revolutionary activities in Norway and interested in espionage matters. He is evidently closely connected with German political refugees in Norway.

Dear Sir,

We have your letter of the 30th ultimo but regret we are unable to look upon 750 shares of the Cellulose Compositions Company as sufficient security for an overdraft and we should require full approved cover either by guarantee or easily negotiable stock or shares.

We shall be glad to have your proposals and will be only too pleased to render what assistance we can.

Yours faithfully,

E.H. Lee Warner
Manager.

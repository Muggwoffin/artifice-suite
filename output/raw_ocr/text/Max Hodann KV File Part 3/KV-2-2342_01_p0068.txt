Oslo Bygdo Alle 70
Copy 7-4-36

Dear Dr Pilldy,

Just yesterday I received the new copy of M.H. - excellent! But the difficulties with the USA Customs are a real scandal!

Now, I have an article for you, "80 years 'White Salve' Traffic". It is published only in Norwegian, in "Frit Ord", April 1936. I send the manuscript today to Miss Stella Browne, 16 King's Mansions, Lawrence Street, SW 3, Chelsea, London. I am going to take this article, wire to Browne and give order to translate my German Mskt. Can you, of course, pay something to Miss Browne if she is translating? I suppose it is very necessary and fair.

On this occasion, I have no idea - do you pay honorar for the articles published in M.H.? You know, I am refugee from Germany and have to live only by my writing as we refugees do not obtain the permit to work in the countries where we live. That's why also I would be very glad to hear that you can pay. But in the case of Miss Browne, it is in my opinion a conditio sine qua non...

In the meantime, Dr Wilhelm Reich has founded a Institute for Sex-Economic research (Institut fur sexual-okonomische Forschung) here in Oslo. I think it would be early enough to report about it in the M.H. copy of August 1936. I have organized the archives for the new Institution and hope to suggest something more for the development of the new work before I am going - I hope, of course, - to Sowjet Russia in May. I propose to correspond via Oslo, this address C/o Mrs Lindbaek. If Mrs Lindbaek comes to Russia, we can correspond via Tietze. I have new information that it is not very convenient indeed to receive letters from Sowjet Territory in India.

But nevertheless we have to maintain our communications, and it would be very favourable for me to act officially as your permanent correspondent in the Sowjet countries. Can you send me such a confirmation?

Can you send me copies from M.H. Volume I No. 1 & 2 and Vol. II No. 1 - it was impossible for me to get these numbers in London!

Yours sincerely,

Sd/- Max Hodann

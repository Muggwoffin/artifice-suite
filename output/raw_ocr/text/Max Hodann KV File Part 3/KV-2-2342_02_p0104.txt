From L.F. PENDLEBURY, c/o Mrs. Hamilton, Canon Hall, Bridge of Earn, Perthshire.

To Dr. A.S. FINKELSTEIN, 13, Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter 6.11.35.

Date of Postmark 7.11.36.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges letter. Doubts whether the C.D.N. will take a letter from him at this distance.

Has had one or two specimens of Karen's journalism and has congratulated her. "I think we'll see her in the Spring."

"I think regular work is improving my character. I thought it would".

"I wonder if Karen is still at Motystr. Her last letter had no address and something else she said seemed to suggest she might have moved, though otherwise I should have assumed the address was still the same. Her removals are a bit confusing and as often as not I write to Clara's."

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

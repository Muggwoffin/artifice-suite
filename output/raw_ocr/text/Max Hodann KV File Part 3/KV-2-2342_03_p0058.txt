From Aase DORUMSGAARD, Bjerregaarfsgrt 29, opg. G. I. Oslo.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Beacon Hill School near Brentwood.

Date of Letter 15.10.35.

Date of Postmark 17.10.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that she has been asked by Molla ORDING if she was willing to undertake some work in a school for coal workers in England. Is writing for further information as to the nature of the work and asks addressee to pass her letter to Frau LINDBAEK or to the School, the name of which she gives as Maes-Yr-Haf, Educational Settlement, Treallaw, Rhondda.

Gives particulars of herself.

Sends greetings from Andreassen, her husband.

"If you will be so kind as to take charge of this matter I should be very grateful to you. Molla told me that you have been successful in some of your plans. I am glad to hear that and hope that you will succeed in your efforts."

For translation see attached sheet.

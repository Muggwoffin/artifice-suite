From J.H. LEUNBACH, Upsalagade, Copenhagen.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, South Weald, Brentwood.

Date of Letter 4.11.35.

Date of Postmark 4.11.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Thinks it a pity that addressee's work had had to suffer such an unexpected injury.

Writer is just moving and cannot receive addressee but will try to find him accommodation elsewhere.

If he arrives on Sunday tells him to ring up the next morning - Obro 5586 or 5573. His new address is Upsalagade 3.

States that addressee will probably meet Lisa JENSEN as she is to speak in Copenhagen on 14.11.35.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

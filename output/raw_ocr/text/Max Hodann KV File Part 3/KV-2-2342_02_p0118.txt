From Karen FINKELSTEIN, Berlin-Charlottenburg.

To Dr. Alexis FINKELSTEIN, 13, Shelford Park, Cambridge.

Date of Letter -

Date of Postmark 4.11.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that "it is O.K. with G." but she left the grain merchant as he was away longer than he had said and now he wishes to take legal steps against her. She had put herself out to help him till he found someone, then he explained that in the interval till she could legally leave he would not let her go and would give her no money - so she left.

Refers to the South American journalist for whom she is working.

Stated that she had to go to the aerodrome for photographs that day, "a very interesting but tiring job."

Is now starting to work for a solicitor who represents important chemists. Hopes the South American will behave when he returns.

For translation see attached sheet.

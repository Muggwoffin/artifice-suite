Extracted from L.154/262, v.14, 496a

Special Branch,
New Scotland Yard, S.W.1.

1st August, 1936.

The undermentioned are applying for certificates of naturalisation. Will you please say if anything is known or recorded of the following names and addresses. Lists should be returned to this office by the 10th August, 1936.

HODANN, Renate @ SARAN, scholar,
Age 15, German,
first arr'd in UK in 1933
Kitelands, Micheldever Station, Hants
St. Swithun's School

Result of Search:-

Renate HODANN @ SARAN, about whom we have nothing recorded against, is the daughter of Max HADANN, the subject of your report No. 320/FGN/530 of the 22nd March, 1935, who is believed to have been associated with Mrs. Marie Louise MARTIN who received the maximum sentence for Soviet military espionage in Finland.

At the end of 1935, the Home Office refused to extend permission for Max Hodann to remain in this country, and he left for Ostend on 1.1.36.

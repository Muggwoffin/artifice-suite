Gist of Int. B 534 K.

From Gina PHILIPSBORN, Bølshavn, Denmark.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Beaconhill School, Brentwood.

16.10.35.

Apologizes for the delay in replying to his letter. Thanks him for his report. Her plans all went to pieces, due to the fact that she did not dare to procure the necessary identity papers for her daughter, as she would have had to send in her own passport. She, unfortunately has stayed in a home in the country, which is tainted with bourgeois and Zionist tendencies, which is very undesirable. There was, however, no choice for her. Sonja was a great pleasure to them and has made a great impression on her youngest daughter. She is soon going to Copenhagen and hopes to see Sonja again.

Also enclosed from?

Has by chance come across the address of a company/which enables doctors to go to Russia. He would be interested to see the conditions there for himself. He believes that the food conditions have considerably improved there lately, but he would like to have some more detailed information. Asks if HODANN knows anything about it, and if he advises him to go there at all, if not absolutely necessary. He does not mind the fact that one has to work and live simply, but he is too old to stand many privations. Asks if HODANN intends going there or if he has colleagues who were or are there. Thinks it would be very attractive to live in a country and work where one was really needed and not only tolerated. Actually there is no immediate necessity for them to leave Denmark, and for that reason he would like him to supply him with the most reliable information possible.

AW 23/10/35.

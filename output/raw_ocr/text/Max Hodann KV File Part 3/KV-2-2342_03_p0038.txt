10.10.35. The Hon. Mrs. Graham-Murray wrote to the H.O. applying for extension for HODANN. She stated that his work had been very helpful to the Eugenics Society and Dr. Blacker would be ready to act as his guarantor. Enclosed letter from Dr. C.P. Blacker:

"I rang you up this morning to give you a message about Dr. HODANN for whom, last week, I arranged a small meeting of people who could give him advice on his future plans. I am of the opinion that the work projected by Dr. HODANN would be of great interest and value. I understand that his permis de séjour in this country expires in a few weeks. I do not know if you can do anything to have this renewed. If you can, it would I think, be an excellent thing, more especially as negotiations are in progress to find him a job where his knowledge and experience would be put to good purpose. I understand that he has come to an arrangement with a publisher to write a book and that they are helping him financially until the book is ready."

21.10.35. Letter from Sir John Simon to The Hon. Mrs. Graham-Murray stating that he was unable to agree to HODANN establishing himself here.

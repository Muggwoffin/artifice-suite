1. On 24.7.35. Hans and Ella RICHTER of 3, Claremont Gardens, Surbiton, wrote to Arne ORDING, telling him that he was invited to LAHR's, 242, Tufnell Park Road, Tufnell Park, - "It is a definite group evening."

2. On 27.8.35. Karen FINKELSTEIN wrote to Dr. Alexis FINKELSTEIN, asking him to get in touch with Helen THILO, 17, Hurstwood Road, N.W.11., and ask her, on behalf of Dr. Franz LOEVENSTEIN, Berlin, "if she cannot bring the stuff for me."

We have had a fortnight's return of correspondence on the two addresses underlined above. It has been ascertained that a Mrs. E. LAHR and a Mrs. VASCO are receiving letters at both addresses. At 242, Tufnell Park Road, Tufnell Park, one Eugene LAHR is also receiving letters.

Note. In 1931 a bookshop at 68, Red Lion Street (in the name of E. LAHR) was managed by Charles LAHR - a German, who arrived in this country in 1905 and in 1920 married Esther ARGEBAHN. The bookshop was found to have some connection with a bookshop at 28, Red Lion Street where Communist literature was sold and where SCHODEL (a Communist agent arrested for failing to register and finally deported) received correspondence.

SCHODEL was known to be in touch with Paddy AYRISS who on one occasion was seen to hand him some papers.

According to the Telephone Directory the bookshop at 68, Red Lion Street is still under the name of E. LAHR.

There seems just a bare possibility that he may be the Eugene LAHR of the HODANN case.

Gist of Int. E64K.

From Babett GROSS, Editions du Carrefour, 83 Bd. du Montparnasse, Paris VI.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, Brentwood, Essex.

4.12.35.

Thanks him for his note of the 21st November.

Meanwhile they have sold the "Yellow Stain" to Gollancz. Therefore they are being very careful about sending prospectuses to the English addresses named by him but they have sent prospectuses to all the Scandinavian and Belgian addresses. A copy of the book is being sent to the addressee with the same post.

They have a new favour to ask of him. They have taken over the delivery of a great biological work which appeared in the U.S.S.R. and they want a prospectus in English and German prepared for it. They have no one who could do a correct English translation and knows the corresponding biological technical expressions. Could he find someone who could do this?

AW 9/12/35.

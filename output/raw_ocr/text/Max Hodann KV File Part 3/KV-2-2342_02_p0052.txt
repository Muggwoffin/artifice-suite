Gist of Int. E 774 C.

From Traute NEUMANN, Vedbaek, Larsensvej 18 Nordsjaelland.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, Brentwood, Essex.

28.12.35.

Thanks him for a sum of money received for Sonja and tells of the uses to which it has been put. She has also received the Christmas letter from Renate. Gives an account of Sonja's presents and by whom they were given. They were at a meeting of the Red Aid, and Sonja was delighted with the many comrades. With regard to his last letter, his plans for the next few months do not seem quite clear. It seems to be merely a possibility that he will send money from Russia and nothing more concrete. She is probably going to produce a film-manuscript with Kurt St. in May for a stage-manager. At the moment they are working on three Danish books for him. Cannot get any money before May. Things look promising however and she wishes to stay until May. She cannot take on the financial responsibility for Sonja and unless he lets her know at once his plans for the latter, the writer will be obliged to travel with the addressee. She is far more interested in her profession, but there seems to be no alternative.

AW 1/1/36.

From Babette GROSS, Editions du Carrefour, 83, Bd. de Montparnasse, Paris, VI.

To Dr. Max HODANN, Boyle's Court, Brentwood, Essex.

Date of Letter 4.12.35.

Date of Postmark 4.12.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges note of 21.11.35. Refers to "Yellow Stain" which has been sold to Gollancz. Therefore they are being very careful about sending prospectuses to the English addresses named by writer but have sent prospectuses to all the Scandinavian and Belgian addresses.

Asks addressee whether he will find someone to do an English translation of a biological work which appeared in the U.S.S.R.

For translation see attached sheet.

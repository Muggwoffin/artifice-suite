RUSHMOOR,
FLEET,
HANTS.
Nov. 4 35.

Dear Alex,

To judge by your letter, you seem to be very depressed. I hope this may prove a passing phase, and that everything will come out all right.

I am glad to hear that your index, though anonymous, has received recognition; it must have been done very well indeed, as the average index is not usually commented on, I think, unless it can be criticised.

Thanks for calling my attention to a passage in "Nature" dealing with my note; I must have a look at it when next in town.

Next week I shall have occasion to go to London & can choose my day; if therefore you should intend to go up too, I could fix arrange if so as to meet you in which case please fix day, time & place, writing me this week.

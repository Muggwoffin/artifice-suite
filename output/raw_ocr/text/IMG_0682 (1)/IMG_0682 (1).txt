you for the books and those glorious cigarettes. The Australian yours. I found excellent reading in bed. I liked them immensely, except when they tended to moralise. But they were full of vigour. The 'France' I haven't yet tackled - I'm not sure if my French is up to it. As for the city, - well, imagine what you would feel like if you'd been hampered in for a decent top, & stood over a box of these Coates turned up. That's exactly what I felt like. Also I must admit I received some from Rose some time ago, & some how fairly arrived the same time at yours, so I have a peaceful time in front of me.

In your last letter you ask if there are any 'light' books I might like. Please don't trouble Hig. I'm really fairly well supplied at the moment, & it will be an awful lot for sending parcels through.

If I'm really desperately bad up for something to read, I'll drop you a hint.

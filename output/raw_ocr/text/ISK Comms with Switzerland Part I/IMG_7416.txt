LETTER NO. 5. FROM SWITZERLAND.

COPY.

L. Willi;
Dieser Bericht stammt nicht aus meinen Informationen sondern von einem Menschen

With reference to our telephone conversation to-day:-

1. As you know, S.O.2. receive considerable help from the International Transport Workers Federation and they are most anxious to help them in return in any way they can.

2. The I.T.F. have reported that Erhardt KONOPKA, August HAAS and the latter's son were on board the French steamer, "WINNIPEG", which was intercepted by a Dutch naval vessel on her way to Martinique.

They state that these three individuals are regarded by them as being absolutely trustworthy and anti-Hitler and all they ask is that the appropriate Authorities should be informed that they are alright and should be treated kindly. It is understood that these people are now in Trinidad.

3. I would be very grateful if you could send a message to your representative in Trinidad to the above effect.

D/T
26.6.41.

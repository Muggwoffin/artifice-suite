TO: AD/X.
FROM: X
4th March, 1943.

The following is the gist of a message from ROBERT for transmission to ECLAIR:-

1. Slight mishap caused one week's arrest in Switzerland. Probably no resulting trouble but may cause slowing up in my work for you and increase danger of communications.

2. Report from Breslau beginning of February describes men on leave showing open contempt of S.A. and party officials.

3. Siemsen trying to form group including publisher Oprecht and Book Guild who are interested to put out cheap editions of popular books on economics, his story, etc. for post-defeat Germany. Swiss would help finance preliminary work. We hesitate to join in actively owing to ideological difficulties and E.N.I. If you consider E.N.I. should prepare anything similar you may obtain release of Gustav and Erich with our friend's help.

ROBERT asks for a reply to be sent through the same channels.

We shall be grateful if you will have this passed on to ECLAIR, in a suitable form.

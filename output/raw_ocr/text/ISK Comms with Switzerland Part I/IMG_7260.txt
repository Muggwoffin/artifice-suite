Copy.
Letter No. "from Switzerland."
FROM: ROBERT
TO: WILLI.
21.10.1942.

Lieber Freund!

Gestern sah ich unseren Gewaehrsmann fuer die Flugblatt-Uebermittlung nach Dld. Er sagte mir folgendes:

27th November 1942.
FROM: AD/X.

TO: X. / To see.
Copy to D/R.

You will have seen from recent correspondence that ROBERT asked YVON to see WILLI. I thought it would be better for me to bring them together than to leave them to themselves. They met today and had a general talk about conditions in France. YVON had nothing to say which we did not know already, but it was obvious that ROBERT had been most anxious for him to see WILLI. It is evident that ROBERT relies very much on WILLI's judgment and support.

J.P.

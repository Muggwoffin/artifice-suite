Switzerland.

TO: W. Eichler,
24 Mandeville Rise,
Welwyn Garden City.

7.11.41.
FROM: X

I discussed the question of propitiating WILLI with X/U this morning and we decided that all recent evidence goes to prove that WILLI is very jealous of AUERBACH's position and the present interrelationship between ROBERT, WILLI and the I.T.F. is unsatisfactory from our point of view.

Our ultimate objectives are:-

1. to propitiate EICHLER and arrange with him that ROBERT in Switzerland should give us his full co-operation.

2. patch up any outstanding differences between WILLI and the I.T.F.

3. arrange for all communications between WILLI and ROBERT to pass through our channels and not through the open mail.

With this in view we have agreed that X/U will approach WILLI, informing him that in addition to his post at M.E.W., he also has other contacts and that he in fact passes ROBERT's communications to AUERBACH, who in turn has been passing them on to WILLI.

X/U will then ask WILLI whether this system works satisfactorily or whether he would prefer to have the stuff passed to him direct. This will gradually lead the discussion to the whole system of communication between here and Switzerland and X/U will inform WILLI that he does not know the exact details, but he can try to arrange a meeting with one of MR. CAMBELL's friends, who is the "real thing".

When we have got in touch with WILLI by these means, we shall be able to explain to him that we hope to get ROBERT to do some valuable work for his "British friends" in Switzerland and that ROBERT's proposed trip to Lisbon, London or U.S.A. would gravely prejudice his chances of accomplishing the work in view.

If this goes smoothly, we could then suggest to WILLI that he might draft a telegram, which we could send to ROBERT through our channels, telling him that WILLI is in touch with ROBERT's "British friends" in London, thoroughly understands the situation and advises him to remain at his post in Switzerland and co-operate with us.

If we can get WILLI's confidence, the ideal of handling communications with ROBERT would be for us to split ROBERT's correspondence, passing the I.T.F. part direct to the I.T.F. and the I.S.K. stuff direct to WILLI. (without the I.T.F. seeing it).

P.T.O.

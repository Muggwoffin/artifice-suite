Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942. 15B.

Extracts:-

LOCAL NO. 375
CIPHER TELEGRAM FROM BERNE
DESP. 1800 4.2.43.
RECD. 0240 5.2.43.

MR. 1023

ROBERT TO ECLAIR.

1. I WAN OFF POST NOT YET CENSORED. BUT STILL NO CONFIDENCE IN EMIL AS SUDDEN CHANGE MIGHT UPSET WORK THERE.

2. GERARD'S POSSIBLE ADDRESS: MADAM DIEULOULARD. REPEAT DIEULOULARD, 28 RUE D'ISLY, ALGIERS. NO NEWS FOR MONTHS. PERHAPS INTERVENTION NECESSARY. IF YOU CAN TELEGRAPH SAYING HANNI IS WELL AND SENDS GREETINGS.

3. DITTO FROM SIMONNE TO HANS. HE COULD COMMUNICATE WITH HER THROUGH US BY TELEGRAM AND LETTER. SHE IS WORKING WELL IN SPITE OF MORE DIFFICULT CONDITIONS.

4. ILLEGAL PERSONAL CONTACT BRUSSELS AMSTERDAM IMPOSSIBLE. CAN ARRANGE ILLEGAL FINANCIAL AID AND OPEN POST CORRESPONDENCE. IF YOU WANT LATTER, GIVE US KEYWORD.

5. COULD HELP LUCY'S SON FINANCIALLY BUT NO POSSIBILITY OF GETTING HIM ACROSS. WE TRUST MARIE'S MOTHER TO GIVE NECESSARY GUARANTEES HERE FOR VISE LUCY'S SISTER. NO DECISION YET. CAN LUCY PAY FOR UPKEEP?

6. TRYING TO MAKE DESIRED ARRANGEMENTS FOR KREYSSIG'S FRIEND K.B.

7. THANKS FOR LETTER OF DECEMBER 31ST. CENSORED BY GERMANS SO RECOMMEND USE OF HAIDA'S OR OTHER HARMLESS SENDER ADDRESS. ALSO DADDY AND JEANNE TO BE REFERRED TO AS ALBERT AND VRE? - I ENDS.

TP AT 1615 5.2.43. MET

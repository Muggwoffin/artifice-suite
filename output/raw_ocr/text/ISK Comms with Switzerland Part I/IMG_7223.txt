Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942.

Extracts:-

12th February 1943.

TO: X.
Copy to J.

FROM: AD/X.1.

Re Berne telegram No.1023, paragraph 3 contains a reference to SIMONNE. An I.T.F. contact has given me some information about this person which may be of interest.

SIMONE (correct spelling according to my informant) is the nom de guerre of a certain KARL MOLT who was a T.U. leader for the Württemberg district with headquarters at Stuttgart until 1933. He was head of a detachment of the Reichsbanner and in 1933 he had to flee to Saarbrücken when it became known that he was preparing an armed rising against Hitler.

In 1934 he fled to Rorschach (Switzerland) where he took the name of WOERNLI. He was imprisoned by the Federal authorities, but in 1940 was discharged and went to Arosa. Here it was given out that he had died, though actually he had taken on a new identity as SIMONE.

He is said to be under 50 and fanatically anti-Nazi.

CONFIDENTIAL.

FROM: H.
Zurich.
Switzerland.

TO: W. Eichler,
24 Mandeville Rise,
Welwyn Garden City.

TO: X/G

14.11.41.
FROM: X

X.U.'s preliminary interview with WILLI was apparently very successful, and as a result of it, WILLI freely admits that ROBERT is of common interest to the British, the I.T.F. and the I.S.K.

He also is willing to dispence with ROBERT's trip if we will give him a little assistance in getting ROBERT's wife to England and thence to America. There is no reason why we should not do this, but we cannot guarantee the attitude of the Swiss, who may not want ROBERT's wife to go.

WILLI also agreed that incoming correspondence could be split by us, his part being handed to him direct and the I.T.F's part being handed to them direct.

X.U. then said he would try to arrange a meeting with one of his friends, i.e. you, to go into the questions a little more fully.

When X.U. introduces you to WILLI on Tuesday, I think the following points should be made:-

1. You should confirm from past experience that a trip of ROBERT's to Lisbon or London would gravely prejudice his chances of organising successful work in Switzerland.

2. You could agree to help, as far as possible, in getting ROBERT's wife to England.

3. With regard to communications, you could point out, again from past experience and "inside" reports, that letters to or from Switzerland by open mail may very easily cause embarrassment to the writers or recipients of the letters.

4. You could offer WILLI a definite safe channel of communication between him and ROBERT, if he will pass all his communications to you or X.U., whichever we prefer.

5. You might get him to agree to draft a telegram for safe delivery to ROBERT, indicating that he, WILLI, is now in touch with the British and agrees that it is advisable for ROBERT to stay at his post and co-operate fully with us. He could also indicate in the telegram, if he likes, that we shall help to get ROBERT's wife to England. He could presumably insert something in the telegram to show that it really was from him to ROBERT. We could then have the telegram sent to Switzerland and everybody would be satisfied.

Please discuss the question with X.U. before the meeting.

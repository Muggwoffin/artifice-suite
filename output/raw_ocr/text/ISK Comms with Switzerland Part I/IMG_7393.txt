14.11.41.
FROM: X

X.U.'s preliminary interview with WILLI was apparently very successful, and as a result of it, WILLI freely admits that ROBERT is of common interest to the British, the I.T.F. and the I.S.K.

18.11.41.

On 18.11.41. X/U introduced X/G to WILLI. At this meeting X/G impressed upon WILLI all the points mentioned in X's memo of 14.11.41. and WILLI agreed all of them. X/G in fact found WILLI most responsive and apparently very pleased at establishing close contact and co-operation with us. Furthermore, it is evident that WILLI himself is most desirous that ROBERT should proceed to a more active stage of his work. In this connection WILLI says that ROBERT can achieve results in Germany, Alsace, France and Northern Italy, but cannot do anything in Austria.

WILLI concurred that a trip of ROBERT's to this country is highly undesirable and is going to instruct him to stay put. He will hand X/U the draft of a telegram containing instructions and will henceforth communicate with ROBERT through us - and will tell ROBERT to do the same from his end.

It was agreed that X/U would be the intermediary between WILLI and us, but X/G also gave him the means of communicating direct with him, in case of necessity.

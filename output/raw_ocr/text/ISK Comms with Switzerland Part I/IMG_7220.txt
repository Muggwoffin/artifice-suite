Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942. 15B.

Extracts:-

As we see it, it is essential for the maintenance of some independence of the Labor movement, to have those possibilities, and R. also insisted on our trying to get financial assistance from the American Labor movement, which we did successfully several times. Since you never objected against this attitude, and since R's letter of October 20th, which you forwarded to us, without any contrary comment, expressed exactly our attitude, we feel that you cannot possibly disagree with our general attitude and the spirit of the agreement that was made here, such as I explained it.

For the time being, there is no mail being received from R. here, but occasionally a few cabled notes reached us. It would of course be very helpful if you could let us have whatever material you should receive from him, for our and our friends' information. I am enclosing, for your information, a message that was just received here (this is only the content, not quotation, of the message).

I do not enter today into the problem of French politics as such and our attitude towards it, because we thought we would first want to wait and see how deep you see our difference of opinion on this point, after my letter of November 8. This letter was only meant to tell you once again how the problem of relations with R. was handled.

Only this for today. Best greetings from all friends, from Ernagespecially. Warm wishes to all of you,

Yours,

ROBERT, DARLAN, BOUSSIN

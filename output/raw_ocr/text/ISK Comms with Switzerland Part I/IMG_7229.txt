FOLLOWING IS RESUME OF LONG TELEGRAM FROM ROBERT AND LACOSTE TO ECLAIR, PHILLIPPE HAUCK.

1. IN SPITE OF CONSIDERABLE EFFORTS AND SOME PROGRESS SYNDICALISTS ARE NOT KEEPING PACE WITH EVENTS AND ARE NOT YET CORNER STONES OF RESISTANCE OR ORGANISED FOR DIRECT ACTION WHEN NECESSARY.

2. THEREFORE (A) CONFERENCE BETWEEN VARIOUS GROUPS PLANNED TO EXAMINE POSSIBILITY OF CO-ORDINATION.

(B) CLANDESTINE PRESS MUST GIVE MORE ATTENTION TO ACTION BY WORKING CLASSES

(C) B.B.C. SHOULD GIVE MORE DEFINITE PAROLES FOR MASS ACTION E.G. "THEY HAVE TAKEN STALINGRAD, TRIPOLI. WHAT ABOUT YOU? YOU ARE WAITING FOR LIBERATION. YOU WILL NEVER GET IT WITHOUT FIGHTING FOR IT. FORGE YOUR ARMS. FORM GROUPS. UNITE ETC"

3. BUT AVOID SINGLE IRRESPONSIBLE ACTS AS AT MARSEILLES LYONS.

4. HAVE OPPORTUNITY OF SEEING MILITANT SYNDICALISTS SHORTLY.

AM PREPARED TO FORM LIAISON.

TP 0645 6.2.43 MFB

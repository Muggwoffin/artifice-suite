Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI

Extracts:-

Some notes from R., received on January 25, 1943 (no quotation).

With the exception of PEBREL, who acts as an observer for the Legion, all members of the CATMALA group have resigned from Vichy (end of December).

The Police judiciaire headed by one BUFFETT (friend of Laval) is preparing the round-up of those who resisted. Lists of all resitants (Communists, Free-masons and Jews) have been asked by the police judiciaire from the regular police. It is reported that the police is using dilatory methods in complying with this request.

ROBERT LE FONCTIONNAIRE had a conversation with one H.P., journalist, friend of Laval. According to this conversation, Laval is convinced of the impossibility of a German victory. 99% of the French population are reported to desire an American victory, because they are confident that America will not allow the bolchevization of France. Because of this belief that security is afforded by the Americans, the French resistance movement has gained new and influential supports.

R. thanks E. for three thousand from American labor movement. He has received 12,900. He can give assistance to Joseph's family.

E. should cable whether Ansze will get her visa. In that case she could proceed either to Switzerland or to Portugal. Danger of deportation. Warm greetings to Theo.

The December issue of "Liberation" reports that 280,000 tins of American milk deposited with Raymond in Perpignan had been taken over by the Germans. They were destined to French children.

NOGUES, DARLAN, BOUSSON have been named by public Proclamation of resistance movements as Trojan horses to the Allies.

Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942.
Extracts:-
No. 65. from Switzerland.

TO: X.
FROM: X/G..
6th. January, 1943.

Telegram 0841 from Berne.

It has come to my notice through D/T ringing us up to enquire what value we attach to the information, that this telegram was published in the D.R.I. (Daily Record of Intelligence) No. 234 of 31.12.42. (Germany, para.1) by L/B's Department, specifically by L/B.2.

I was naturally amazed that L/B's Department should include any telegram in the summary destined for 'C' without consulting us and I let L/B know my views without mincing matters.

I also informed D/T of the facts and told him we attach no value to the information. You will no doubt want to take this matter up to ensure that no repetition of such gaffes happen again.

N.B. Have ascertained that the D.R.I., besides being sent to 'C', has an internal circulation to AD/P and L. - There is no further circulation so far as I could find out.

X/Sec.

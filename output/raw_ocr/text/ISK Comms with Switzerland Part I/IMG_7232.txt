Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942. 15B.

Extracts:-

No. 65. from Switzerland.

The German Newspaper for the East is the source of the following quotations:-

From the 18th December issue:

Owing to the unwarranted political conclusions drawn by the Eastern Workers from the 15% wages equalisation tax, this tax is withdrawn as from the beginning of 1943.

From the 29th December issue:

As the sugar beet cultivators in Lettland failed to reach the quota agreed upon for delivery, the sugar premium has been withdrawn and heavy punishments for fraudulent conduct and breach of contract threatened.

Production Boards for milk have been set up in Lettonia because of the great extension in the underground traffic in butter.

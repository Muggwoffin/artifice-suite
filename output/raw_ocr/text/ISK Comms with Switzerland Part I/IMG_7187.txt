TO: AD/X
FROM: X/G
4th May, 1943.

The following messages have been received from ROBERT:

1. "ECLAIR for OLDENBROEK.
GOMEZ relatively safe in Italian occupied zone. Unwilling to come to Switzerland as he sees no useful activity here. He is prepared to join you but only if he can work. Am in regular contact with him and providing funds. If you want him to leave you can always give him necessary instructions."

N.B. We think it desirable that the above mentioned message should not be transmitted via ECLAIR, but direct from you to OLDENBROEK.

2. "For ECLAIR.
Are you receiving stuff sent via Sweden? Is it useful? Can you send us via Sweden documentation on social development in England and U.S.A? Would like to receive Socialist Commentary, New Statesman and Nation, interesting T.U. papers. Also some American publications. Could use for work with French Syndicalists. Cover address Family BAUMER, Rosenweg 8, THUN."

N.B. We understand you have passed to ECLAIR the few cuttings which have reached us from Switzerland via Sweden on two or three occasions. (One such envelope has just been received and we enclose it herewith for transmission.) As to the return traffic suggested, ECLAIR would have to send them by his own means.

3. "For ECLAIR.
Have you news of GERARD and SCHALMEI?"

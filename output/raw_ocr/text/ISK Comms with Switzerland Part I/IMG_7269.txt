CIPHER TELEGRAM RECEIVED FROM BERNE DESP. 1745 20/10/42 RECD. 2300

0546

FOLLOWING FOR WILLI FROM ROBERT

1. GENERAL UNREST AMONG RAILWAY WORKERS AT LYONS FOLLOWING LAVAL POLICY OF SAVE LABOUR FOR GERMANY. SINCE OCTOBER 15TH MEN AT LA MOUCHE SIDING AT REPAIR SHOP AT OULINS AND AT VAISE STATION HAVE REFUSED TO MARSHAL TRAINS DESTINED FOR GERMANY OR DO OTHER WORK FAVOURING ENEMY.

2. AS A RESULT OF FOREGOING FULLY ARMED FRENCH SENTRY HAVE BEEN POSTED AT ALL VITAL POINTS ON RAILWAYS AND HAVE ON OCCASION ASSUMED THREATENING ATTITUDE TOWARD WORKERS.

3. AT BERLIET WORKS AT LYONS SOME MEN INCLUDING FOREMAN AND HIS GANG SCHEDULED TO PROCEED TO GERMANY REFUSED TO GO. INCIDENTS WITH POLICE RESULTED AND 8 MEN WERE ARRESTED.

4. SPREADING OF SUCH INCIDENTS TO A LARGE NUMBER OF FACTORIES IN Z.N.O. IS REPORTED. MEN ARE TEARING UP THEIR INSTRUCTIONS TO PROCEED TO GERMANY. B.S.C. MIGHT USEFULLY URGE EXTENSION OF THIS PRACTICE TO STIFFEN RESISTANCE.

5. ABOVE SUMMARISES POSITION UP TO MORNING OF OCTOBER 17TH. PRESENT STAGE ONE OF UNREST ONLY BUT WITH POSSIBILITY OF ASSUMING MORE SERIOUS PROPORTIONS.

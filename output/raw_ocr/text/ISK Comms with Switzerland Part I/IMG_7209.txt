12th. March, 1943.
From: AD/X.1.

WILLI has asked me if the following telegram can be sent from him to ROBERT. This is in connection with X's memorandum of 4th. March regarding message from ROBERT to WILLI.

"Have spoken GUIGUI and agree entirely Stop If OPRECHTS cheap editions are only reprints not interested otherwise do not appreciate difficulties with ENI Stop Did you get Russland und die Komintern? Perhaps OPRECHT can publish Towards European Unity from Renaissance Publishing Company being two speeches by Hauck and Eichler Stop Can we have France Combattante Bulletin urgent Stop Can you use Marie-Louise more or Elizabeth Martin? Received papers via Sweden and MASCHAS economic notes and letter to JEF have you heard from Ernst Friedrich address LA CASTELIE BARRE-DE-CEVENNES LOZERE"

Para. 1, WILLI suggests that it is very important to get the full reports referred to by ROBERT and wonders whether ROBERT could be encouraged to send them in a safe manner.

If you would like any more particulars about the people referred to in the wire, I will try to get them.

F.D.

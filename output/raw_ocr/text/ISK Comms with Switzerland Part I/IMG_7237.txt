No. 65. from Switzerland

LOCAL 212
CYPER TELEGRAM RECD. FROM BERNE
DESP. 1206 23.1.43
RECD. 1500 23.1.43
0965
?FOLLOWING FROM ROBERT FOR ECLAIR I.T.F.
PARA 1.
REFERENCE YOUR TELEGRAM 3767 FOR KRAMER. REGRET FRIEDEL MARION, IDA NOT IN SWITZERLAND. BRATSCHI'S TELEGRAM WAS MISLEADING. HIS SECRETARY RECEIVED LETTER FROM IDA ON DECEMBER 20TH SAYING THAT MARION IS WELL AND WITH GRANDPARENTS AND THAT FRIEDEL WOULD SOON SEE HER: BUT WITHOUT STATING WHERE FRIEDEL IS. ALSO ASKS NEWS OF YOU? IDA'S LETTER POSSIBLY RESULT OF VISIT FROM A FRIEND OF OURS. WE ARE REPLYING AND TRYING TO ESTABLISH REGULAR LIAISON.

PARA 2.
FOR OLDENBRUCK.
?DEATH OF GOMEZ'S WIFE REMOVES OBJECTION TO PREVIOUS CLANDESTINE DEPARTURE. PLEASE SOUND LONDON WHETHER BRITISH WOULD NOW ASSIST ESCAPE AND INSTRUCT ME.
COULD YOU INSTRUCT ARGENTINE CONSUL TO GIVE HIM ?PASSPORT WHICH WOULD GREATLY FACILITATE JOURNEY VIA SPAIN.
SEE MY IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING TELEGRAM.

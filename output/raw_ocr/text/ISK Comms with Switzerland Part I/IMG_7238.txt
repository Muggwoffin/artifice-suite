Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942. 15B.

Extracts:-

January 26th, 1943.

To: AD/X.
From: X.

Reference J's memo. J/SW/4192 of 25.1.43, and our conversation this afternoon, I explained to you the delicate situation in which we are placed.

6th February 1943.

TO: X.
FROM: AD/X.

With reference to your note of January 26th, I have seen WILLI and passed on the paraphrased messages to him on the lines of the draft, informing him that the reports were received through very delicate channels and impressing upon him that he should not publish any of this material or divulge it to anyone else.

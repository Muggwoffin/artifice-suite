CONFIDENTIAL.

FROM: H.
Zurich.
Switzerland.

TO: W. Eichler,
24 Mandeville Rise,
Welwyn Garden City.

To: X/G/116/1A

I talked with London Colleagues of your Friends at Berne about your journey. They told me facts which convinced me that you should not leave the Continent as long as you have the intention of returning there. Jeanne can apply to go to England. They promised to help her to go later to USA. From our point of view and for improvement of anti-nazi activities in North and South America I think she should take that opportunity, unless you cannot find any substitute for her work. Any correspondence including names should go through the way which you occasionally used unless you can disguise names in air mail letters.

ECLAIR.

19 Nov. 1941.

FOR ROBERT TO ECLAIR. RESUME OF END OF YEAR
"STIMMUNGSBILDER" FROM FRANKFURT
KASSEL AND SOUTH GERMAN S.P. ERS.

1. MASSES TIRED OF WAR BUT APATHETIC EVEN TOWARDS LOSSES IN EAST. NAZIS QUIETER BUT WANT VICTORY AT COSTS. SOCIALISTS AND TRADE UNIONISTS WANT NAZI DEFEAT BUT FEAR SECOND VERSAILLES. BENES, SIKORSKI FRONTIER DEMANDS TURNED DOWN AS SEED OF NEW WARS DITTO ALL LOSS OF TERRITORY. NAZI PROPAGANDA MAKES UTMOST OF EVERY SUCH STATEMENT. ANNOUNCEMENT OF PUNISHMENT FOR S.S.-ERS CAUSES NAZIS TO TRANSFER SONS OF EX SOCIALISTS AND TRADE UNIONISTS FROM OTHER MILITARY UNITS INTO S.S.

2. ANTI NAZI'S PAROLE: WORK SLOW SABOTAGE WHERE POSSIBLE (GRP MUT) BE DONE AT PRESENT MORE ONLY WHEN MILITARY SITUATION ACUTE. OTHERWISE POINTLESS SUICIDE. MUCH SABOTAGE IN WAR WORKS RAILWAYS GAS ELECTRICITY WORKS. MORE PROPAGANDA MATERIAL CIRCULATING, OURS FROM OUTSIDE VERY WELCOME. WORKERS BROADCASTS LISTENED TO AND DISCUSSED REGULARLY IN SMALL GROUPS. COMMUNISTS EXCLUDED AS THEY HAVE LEARNT NOTHING.

3. 340 DEAD IN KASSEL BOMBARDMENT HENSCHEL SEVERELY DAMAGING STILL ALL CLOSED OFF.

4. SAUCKEL'S "LEISTUNGSLOHN" FAILURE MADE RIDICULOUS. PRODUCTION DECREASING SLOWLY BUT STEADILY.

5. APPROVE PUNISHMENT FOR TERROR IN OCCUPIED COUNTRIES. READY FOR HONEST CONSTRUCTIVE WORK BUT NOT UNDER FOREIGN PRESSURE ONLY IN FREE SOCIALIST GERMANY IN SOCIALIST EUROPE.

TP 1148 23.1.43 MFB

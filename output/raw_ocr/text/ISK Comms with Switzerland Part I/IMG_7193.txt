ROBERT TO ECLAIR

1. Liberation Franc-Tireur and combat for March all call themselves "Organ of United Resistance Movements" thus expressing realization of resistance unity.

2. Resumes of (A) Franc Tireur number 16 of March 20th (B) Liberation number 25th of March 1st (C) Combat number 42 (D) Populaire number 10 (Issue 30,000 repeat 30,000) if requested by you.

3. Great number combat leaflets entitled "Debout Against Slavory" give paroles for resistance against deportation.

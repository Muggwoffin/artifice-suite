82. From AD/X reporting on a meeting between Yvon and Willi, from which it was made evident that Robert relies very much on Willi's judgment and support. Enc:82A. 27.11.42.

83. Telegram 0795 from ROBERT for ECLAIR giving information on France. Enc:83A. 15.12.42.

84. Telegram 0841 from Berne, from Robert for Eclair, giving various reports on Germany. Enc:84A. 30.12.42.

85. From X. to X/G. saying how the telegram is to be handled as far as AD/X and WILLI are concerned. Enc:85A. 2.1.43.

86. To AD/X passing on some of the information. Enc:86A. 5.1.43.

87. To X from X/G informing him that the information contained in telegram 0841 had been published in the D.R.I. of 31.12.42. and commenting thereon. Enc:87A. 6.1.43.

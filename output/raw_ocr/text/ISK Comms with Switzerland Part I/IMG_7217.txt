22nd February, 1943.

We have received the attached through our friends in New York for delivery by safe hand to WILLI. No doubt it has come through our channels as it concerns ROBERT's relations with the Americans and would be rather delicate matter to forward by other means.

If WILLI wishes to answer we would be willing to transmit the reply.

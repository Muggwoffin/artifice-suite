TO: J
FROM: X
XF/G/237.
23rd April, 1943.

Reference telegram 1370. This is certainly an awkward telegram to answer.

Naturally we must leave paras. 1 and 2 to D/R Section to answer, as that is entirely their affair and I don't know how they have handled the various messages from ROBERT for de GAULLE.

I am really only concerned with the first sentence of para. 3 and paras. 5 and 6., and I suggest that they should be answered on the following lines:-

1) For your personal information ECLAIR has to be handled with the utmost discretion, as he is a German emigre with certain Nationalist tendencies and he has many contacts with other emigre groups.

2) He knows ROBERT is working for us but knows no operational details of any kind.

3) Subject to above we pass the gist of ROBERT's intelligence to him.

4) Sole purpose of humouring ECLAIR is to keep ROBERT sweet.

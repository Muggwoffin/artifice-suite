FOLLOWING FROM ROBERT #ECLAIR FROM LACOSTE FOR THE GENERAL DATED MARCH 21ST. FROM THONON.

1. SOME DISPERSION BUT NOT SURRENDER OF MOUNTAIN GROUPS. REFUSAL TO GO TO GERMANY MAINTAINED. POPULAR SUPPORT EXCELLENT. DISAPPOINTMENT AT LACK OF BRITISH MILITARY OPERATIONS ON FRENCH SOIL.

2. LOCAL AUTHORITIES ADMIT THEY DID NOT WIN FIGHT IN SPITE OF VICHY TRENCH EFFORTS

3. MEN LEFT ARE TOUGI (2 GROUPS MUT.). HAVING LEARNT FROM EXPERIENCE, NECESSITY OF BETTER ORGANIZATION OF RESISTANCE (COMMUNICATIONS, MOVEMENTS ETC) NOW FULLY APPRECIATED AND BEING TAKEN IN HAND.

4. MOVEMENT'S PAROLE "SI RENDRE INVISIBLE" BEING FOLLOWED WELL IN SPITE OF SOME COMMUNIST FRICITION.

5. TOUGI PARACHUTE OPERATION MISSED NIGHT OF 14TH/15TH ALSO 16TH/17TH THOUGH RECEPTION COMMITTEE AND SIGNALS WERE THERE. ENDS.

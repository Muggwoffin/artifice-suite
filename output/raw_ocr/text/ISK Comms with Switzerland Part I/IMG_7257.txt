Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942.
Extracts:-
No. 65. from Switzerland.

TO: X/G.
FROM: X
2nd. January, 1942.

J. rang me up and asked whether the gist of the attached telegram ought to be given to AD/X for passing on to [redacted]. *WILLI

As matters stand (please see recent additions to our file XFG/239), I think it is undesirable that [redacted] should receive the gist of all this telegram and have told J. not to send a copy to AD/X or anyone else at the moment.

I have tried to get hold of AD/X but he will not be in until Monday.

Please handle this on the following lines:-

Paras. 1, 2 and 5: should not be passed on to WILLI at all as we have already had very similar reports from a source that we consider is almost certainly "penetrated Sopade".

It is, therefore, highly undesirable that these items should be passed to [redacted] who then passes them round the emigration and makes everybody believe that the "good Socialist Germany is being reborn".

Paras. 3, 4 and 6: could be passed on to WILLI in an untelegraphic, garbled form saying that a message has been received from his friends by a devious route and that he must not talk to anyone else about it as it might imperil the channel through which it has come.

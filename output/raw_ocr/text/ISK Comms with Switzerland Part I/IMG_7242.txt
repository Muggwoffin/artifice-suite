With reference to the attached telegrams 0957 and 0958 from Robert:-

1. X did not consider that any of this matter was suitable for publication.

2. After discussion, however, he agreed that 0958 being extracts could be passed on, provided the message was paraphrased.

No. 0957, although it starts by saying that it is a resume of the German press, it obviously gives much that could never have appeared in any German paper. Paragraphs 2, 3, 4 and 5 should certainly not be circulated or published with nothing to support the authenticity of the information.

Unglueck dagewesen.

Neulich sah ich eine Karte aus Deutschland, die mit drei verschiedenfarbigen dicken Strichen von der Zensur verschmiert worden, anscheinend fahndete man nach drei verschiedenen Chemikalien.

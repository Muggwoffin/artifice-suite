(B) GESTAPO PRESSURE INCREASING PRODUCTION SINKING.
FOREIGN LABOUR CANNOT REPLACE SPECIALISTS. FINE STEEL LACKING.
(C) WEAR AND TEAR AND SABOTAGE CAUSE INCREASING RAILWAY TROUBLE. PERSONNEL UNDER SEVERE PRESSURE.

Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942. 15B.

Extracts:-

January 26th, 1943.

To: AD/X.
From: X.

Reference J's memo. J/SW/4192 of 25.1.43, and our conversation this afternoon, I explained to you the delicate situation in which we are placed when we forward information received by telegram from Robert.

We are naturally in a much weaker position now there is no Bag traffic and no open correspondence between Robert and Willi.

This last used to be the strongest point in our defence.

As you know, we are not supposed to disseminate intelligence, especially to German emigres. When we do so, we are very liable to incur the wrath of a variety of departments, such as "C", P.W.E., the M. of I., the B.B.C., etc.

We are further at the mercy of our critics if - as is so often the case - the intelligence distributed by us to these emigres is strongly tinged either with wishful thinking or with the political views of the source.

Under the circumstances, it would be much easier for us all if Switzerland could find a way of limiting these telegrams to operational items.

However, we do not want to lose the goodwill of Willi and Robert by withholding all these communications from Willi.

We therefore ask you to pass on the gist of these telegrams to Willi on the lines of the attached draft, at the same time informing him that these reports (of which we cannot expect to receive many) have come by very delicate channels, and that it is essential in the interest of all concerned that he should not publish any

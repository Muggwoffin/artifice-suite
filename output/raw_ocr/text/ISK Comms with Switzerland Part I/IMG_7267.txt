26.10.42.
From: AD/X1

To: X/G
Copies to D/R, J & F

With reference to telegram 0546 from Berne dated 20/10/42, I have spoken to Willi on the lines of your memorandum.

Willi said he noted that it was military who assumed a threatening attitude towards the workers and he was sure the police would not have taken up this line.

Further, he said he had a letter from Robert according to which GIRAUD had approached a leading Trade Union resister (whose name he said he didn't know) asking the latter to go to N. Africa to organise resistance work there. The resister refused to lend himself to the manoeuvre.

J.D.

4th March, 1943.

TO: AD/X
FROM: X/G
XF/G/237.
8th March, 1943.

The following is the gist of a message from ROBERT for transmission to ECLAIR:-

1. Recent report from Germany describes more open discontent in spite of propaganda and Gestapo. Foreign workers, female labour and wear on machinery reducing production.

2. Annette just arrived here but interned. Germain ordered "Drueben" Jupp and wife, Kurz, Feuer, Rene and yourself are on German "Fahndungs" List.

We shall be grateful if you will try and find out from ECLAIR what is meant by the message in Para. 2.

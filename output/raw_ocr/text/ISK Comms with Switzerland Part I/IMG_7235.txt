1. ROBERTS TELEGRAMS FOR ECLAIR CAUSING YOU TOO MUCH WORK.
2. YOU SHOULD GIVE THESE MESSAGES LOW PRIORITY AND OMIT PRESS EXTRACTS AND SUMMARIES.

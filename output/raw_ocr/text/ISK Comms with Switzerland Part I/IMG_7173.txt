TO: AD/X
FROM: X/G
XF/G/237.
21st May, 1943.

A rather mutilated message from ROBERT for ECLAIR has been received as follows:-

"Comrades in FRANKFURT and KASSEL report that contact is maintained among Trade Unionists and Socialists. They say that anti-War and anti-Nazi slogans are appearing on the walls, also inscriptions approving aerial bombardments. There is a great change since last Autumn and practically no belief in victory is left. There are few Hitler salutes given. On the other hand terror has redoubled and recently many arrests were made among old comrades. The bombardments in the Rhineland are extremely effective. The workers on the whole are dulled by a sense of hopelessness, fatigue, hard trials and impotence."

ROBERT says that he has supplied them with ideas for propaganda, but he regrets that his liaison with the above mentioned comrades is too irregular and indirect to be properly exploited.

Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942. 15B.

Extracts:-

LOCAL 212
CYIPHER TELEGRAM RECD. FROM BERNE DESP. 1206 23.1.43

COPY FOR X.

TO: AD/X (copies to D/R & X).

FROM: J.

28th January, 1943.
J/IT/4205.

You will have seen from Berne telegram 0970 that J.Q. feels that it is incumbent upon him to transmit such messages as he can handle from Robert for Bolair and Co.

2. I trust that your contacts here will be ready to realise the value of this service and will give you a worth while return.

Unglueck dagewesen.

Neulich sah ich eine Karte aus Deutschland, die mit drei verschiedenfarbigen dicken Strichen von der Zensur verschmiert worden, anscheinend fahndete man nach drei verschiedenen Chemikalien.

LETTER NO. 31. FROM SWITZERLAND.
29.12.1941.

Lieber Willi!

We are glad to report that we have had a successful meeting with WILLI, and he has subscribed to the telegram which we drafted.

This will be a relief to you as well as to us, as ROBERT is just beginning to be active and it would be most unfortunate if we were to compromise everything by letting him take an unnecessary trip out of the country.

As you know, ROBERT'S letter of the 29th December, to which telegram refers, indicated that he was still anxious to come to England and did not quite understand why WILLI had suddenly started to disuade him. It was rather a naive letter and I do not think it need be taken too seriously. However, our telegram will undoubtedly do good and will clear the air.

Would you please send off the telegram as follows:-

"Following for J.Q. for ROBERT.
A) Following from ECLAIR repeat ECLAIR
B) Your letter of 29th December 1941.
C) Consider even shortest visit here would be irreparably compromising. Quite impossible conceal your identity.
D) Our British friends have evidence that persons returning to Switzerland from here are definitely marked men."

As you know, the use of the signature ECLAIR is proof that the letter comes from WILLI, but WILLI is also writing a letter to ROBERT explaining in more detail.

Copy. Letter from ROBERT to WILLI dated 8.10.1942.

Extracts:-

LOCAL TP 658.
CIPHER TELEGRAM FROM BERNE
NR. 0841.

DESP. 1452 30/12/42.
RECD. 1845 30/12/42.

FOLLOWING FROM ROBERT FOR ECLAIR.

1. RELIABLE BERLIN COMRADE REPORTS ANTI NAZI RESISTANCE GREATER THAN APPEARS OUTWARDLY FROM PINPRICKS TO SABOTAGE. SUICIDE POLICY PREMATURE BUT MANY INDIVIDUAL POSSIBILITIES WITH MINIMUM RISK. THIS REFERS TO EXTENSION OF SOCIALIST CIRCLES. MASSES CORRUPTED BY (GRP MUT.) PARCELS AND LEAVE PRESENTS FROM OCCUPIED COUNTRIES.

2. HAMBURG RAILWAYS MAN (FRIEND OF HANS) REPORTS MANY ANTI FASCISTS AMONG FOREIGN WORKERS. PRINTED STICKERS? OF HITLER'S FALL SEEN IN HAMBURG STREET. SOME UNREST IN SHIPYARDS ABOVE ALL BLOHMVOSS, SLOWING DOWN WORK AND MINOR DELAYS. BUT WERK SCHUTZ VERY ACTIVE.

3. GENERAL FOGGINESS RE POST-WAR SET-UP AND FEAR OF SECOND VERSAILLES WITH CORRESPONDING RESULTS.

4. LEFTIST ASKING WHAT FOUNDATION FOR RUMOUR CURRENT ESPECIALLY IN INFORMED CATHOLIC CIRCLES RE SEPARATION EAST AND WEST PRUSSIA, SILESIA, SOUTHERN AND WESTERN GERMANY, REST TO BE SPLIT INTO INDEPENDENT PROVINCES. BUEBERGLICHE NAMES ALREADY MENTIONED? FOR CENTRAL RAILWAY, POSTAL ETC. ORGANISATION. WORRIED THAT REAL CAUSE OF WAR BIG LANDOWNERSHIP HEAVY INDUSTRY ETC. NOT TO BE REMOVED. DECISIVE NAZI PROPAGANDA WILL MAKE USE OF THIS. THEY ASK VOGEL FOR STATEMENT. OUR OWN OPINION=APPREHENSION? EXAGGERATED BUT NEVERTHELESS SOME EXPLANATORY STATEMENT REQUESTED FOR DISSEMINATION INSIDE.

5. CONTACT BETWEEN FEINSTAETTERKOSTADT HERZOG REGULAR. REST PUT ON OWN RESPONSIBILITY, DOING EVERYTHING POSSIBLE CIRCUMSTANCES ARE RIPE FOR OUTWARD EXPOSURE. MUCH SLOW WORK BUT OFTEN HINDERED BY LOYALTY TO INDIVIDUAL EMPLOYER. WAGE QUESTIONS OF SUBSIDIARY INTEREST AS MORE MONEY THAN CONSUMER GOODS ANY WAY.. NO INTEREST IN SAVING OWING TO UNCERTAINTY RE FUTURE.

6. ALLIED PROMISES ARE NOT BELIEVED. 14 POINTS FUNDAMENTAL CURRENT ONLY "SOFORT TATEN" COULD CONVINCE.

TP AT 1155 31.12.42 JL

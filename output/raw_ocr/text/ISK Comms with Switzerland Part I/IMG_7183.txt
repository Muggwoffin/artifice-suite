TO: AD/X
FROM: X/G
4th May, 1943.

ROBERT has asked WILLI to send via Sweden a documentation on social and T.U. developments in England and U.S.A. which he could use in his work with French resisting Trade Unionists. We used to send such a documentation to ROBERT up to the time of the total occupation of France, and YVON has informed us of their usefulness in French resisting T.U. circles. We are consulting with WILLI as to the type of information which would be most effective. Since November 1942 nothing has been sent. At present such papers could only be forwarded to Switzerland through the Swedish Trade Unionists, and it would be useful if we could get them engaged in this traffic.

X Spoke to S. and warned him on the subject.
6.5.43.

CROSSLAND FOSSE,

KEMPSTON, BEDFORD

6th June, 1941.

Dear [redacted] x.2.

I have never heard of a man called Serafino Romualdi, but I have asked our New York Office to make an enquiry.

Yesterday I received a telegram sent to [redacted] from Zurich and signed [redacted]. It would, therefore, seem that [redacted] is in Zurich. Robert

The contents of the telegram are that Messrs Erhardt Konopka and August Haas and Son are on board the French steamer "WINNIPEG" which was intercepted by the Dutch Navy on her way to Martinique, or some other place.

I would ask you to inform the appropriate authorities that these three persons are all right, and that we regard them as being absolutely trustworthy and anti-Hitlerites.

I have further pleasure in enclosing a letter for our friend in Stockholm through whose intermediary we got the Berlin report. Will you please have this letter forwarded through the proper channels.

Yours sincerely,

x/G/103/A.

Encl.

P.S. Will be forwarded by later mail.

Die deutsche Regierung übergibt durch de Brinon, den General-Delegierten der französischen Regierung in Paris, die Akten der verlangten Personen. Diese Akten gehen sofort nach Empfang von Vichy an einen Zentralstelle in Clermont-Gerrand, in der Deutschland durch einen Gestapobeamten vertreten sein soll, der ein Einblicksrecht in die gesamte Korrespondenz und den ganzen Geschäftsgang hat.

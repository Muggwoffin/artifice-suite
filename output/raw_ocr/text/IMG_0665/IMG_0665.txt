Dear Hig,

A thousand thanks for the two Kaye Smiths. I haven't read them yet, but I'm running short of 'light' literature, & they are very welcome. I have only read one of her books, 'Tobiana Godden', which I liked considerably.

I am writing this in the big gilded hall of the Kremlin, at the 'Fifth World Conference of Peasants'. It's a wonderful sight - I wish I could describe it. There is of course a big contingent of Russian peasants - lovely bearded things in their long coats and high boots, smelling of the land. There are also queens from Turkestan, Korea, Bachkin, Mongolia, mostly in national dress. The Mongolian wears a lovely dressing gown which I want to tear off his back. Japan & China are also represented, & most of the European countries - there are quite big delegations for France and Germany. Even America has produced

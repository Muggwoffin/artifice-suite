August 9, 1923

Did you really think I would keep you waiting all this time. I answered your letter weeks ago, & I didn't answer your subsequent notes because when they arrived, I thought you would have received my reply by then. It's the most damnable luck that just that letter of all the letters I have written should have been delayed. I only hope & God you will have received it by the time this reaches you.

Oh bug, I hate to have to say it all over again—it was hard enough saying it the first time. But I can't bear the thought of keeping you in suspense any longer, though I feel sure that my first letter has reached you by now.

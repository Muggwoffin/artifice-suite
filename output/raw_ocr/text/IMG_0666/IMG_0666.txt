They were particularly anxious to have someone from Ireland, but they seem to be a feeble lot & there has been no tremendous success in The Conference has been a every way. There have been no serious gags in the representation (cos England doesn't really count much in this connection) with the exception of Italy, which, seriously enough, hasn't sent any one. They are going to set up an International Peasant Council, whose main work will be to promote unity between the workers' and peasants' organisations. I feel a certain thrill in having been present at the first International Peasant Conference - sort of literary one will tell one's grandchildren, of course. It's a hard life being an English stenographer here, because no-one really speaks English. It's either American, Japanese-English (a frightful jargon this - I always wake up these speeches), or broken translations of Norwegian, Swedish & Finnish speeches. Fortunately there have been few of them.

Extract for File No.: PF. 66074. Y. Box. 5164. Name: OLDAG, Arthur William.

Original in File No.: PF. 40634. Volume: 2. Serial: 100a. Dated: 11.11.44.

Extracted on: 14.1.45. by: BS. Section: R4.

Extract from O/MS report on Vero RICHARD's anarchist, banking accounts, mentioning OLDAG.

..... I have now inspected both RICHARD's private account and that in the name of SPAIN and the WORLD ORPHANS FUND at the Credit Lyonnaise for the current year....
..... I understand that H.B. GAINSBOROUGH was a printer whose business address was Angel Alley, Whitechapel High Street, From the P.O. London directory this appears to be identical with 84a, Whitechapel High Street, the address of EXPRESS PRINTERS, so that it seems that the GAINSBORO's are in some way interested in that business. At the same time it does not seem to explain why payments should be made to them direct by RICHARDS, and it may be that this is the channel through which payments have been made to OLDAG. In this connection it is worth noting that the largest item £54 was paid in March last when a new edition of cartoons was issued and other contributors appear to have been paid. There is, however the cash drawing of £30 (10.3.44.) to be remembered, but as this was taken in £1 notes, it is not possible to pursue the matter further.
..... From the above it is evident that OLDAG may very easily bee paid from the cash drawings, but you may consider that the GAINSBOROUGH angle is worth following up....

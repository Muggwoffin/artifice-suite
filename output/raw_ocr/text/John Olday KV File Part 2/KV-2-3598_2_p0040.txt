Extract for File No.: P.F. 66074 y. Box 5164 Name: OLDAY

Original in File No.: 110712 Serial: 11a Dated: 20.11.44

Original from: R.O. Reading Under Ref.: 27/14 dated 18.11.44

Extracted on: 15.12.44 by: VT Section: R4

Extract from R.O. Reading letter 27/14 dated 18.11.44 enclosing a report from the C.C. Reading re Trotskyist activities at Reading University, where John OLDAY was suggested by GAMBLE as speaker at a meeting to be held on 15th November, he did not appear.

It has been confirmed that John OLDAY @ Arthur OLDAG is circulated as a deserter from the 12th Centre Pioneer Corps, vide Police Gazette Supplement B, Vol 19 dated 31.5.43 case 211. Should it be ascertained that this man intends to visit Reading in the future every endeavour will be made to effect his arrest.

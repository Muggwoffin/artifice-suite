17th April, 1946.

Dear Major Prichard,

No. 13095115 Pte. OLDAG, A.W.

Captain Liddington informs me that he has passed you his file for OLDAG on the occasion of the man being posted to Cuddington, Cheshire. The file will, I think, give you most of the story but I recapitulate the main points in case any of them are unknown to you.

Arthur William OLDAG, alias John OLDAY, claims to be a British subject having been born in London on 10.4.05, the illegitimate son of a German mother and a Scottish father. He states that he was taken as a baby to New York and at the age of eight, to Germany where he was brought up and obtained various jobs. During the last years of his stay in Germany he was employed as a journalist and cartoonist but he claims to have been engaged in anti-Nazi activities there as a result of which he left the country in 1937.

After his arrival in England in 1937 OLDAG lived in London with Rose FYLEMAN, an American authoress. This woman translated OLDAG's book "Kingdom of Rags" which sold 500 copies and was published by Jarrolds, being written under the pseudonym of John OLDAY. In September 1938, having quarrelled with FYLEMAN, OLDAG married Hilda MIESEL, a Hungarian; the marriage is thought to have been one of convenience contracted in order that MIESEL should obtain British nationality.

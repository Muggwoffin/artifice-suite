My dearest Maikin!
Very sorry I wasn't able to answer your last letter. We haven't had much luck lately have we? Tami will have told you all the news. The last days in Brixton Good old Albert told me as much he possibly could. Wanted to see you badly and so terribly disappointed it did not come off. But never mind! We shall make up later. I do not regret my stay in Brixton. The time has not been lost. The time will come when I shall exploit my experience. The profit will be ours.

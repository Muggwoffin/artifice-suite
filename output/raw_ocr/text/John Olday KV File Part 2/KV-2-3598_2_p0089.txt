SECRET

Our Ref: R.P. 60074/P.312.

To R.S.L.Os Cardiff, Bristol and Manchester.

Arthur William O'DAG & John O'KAL.

I enclose herewith a copy of a letter which I have written to Special Branch together with a note, photograph, and specimen of handwriting.

I am afraid that this is rather a long shot, but I take a very serious view of O'DAG's case and am most anxious to trace him. I should be grateful if you would mention the matter to the Police and let me know at once if there should be any signs of him. If he is traced we shall clearly have to consider searching his premises.

For your own information we have a M.O... on the Freedom Press in London, but it has never revealed anything incoming from O'KAL. This suggests that he is in personal contact with a branch of it somewhere.

R.G. Ricklothwait.

July 1944

5 enclosures

Copies to R.S.L.Os at Bristol and Cardiff.

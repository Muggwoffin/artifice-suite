Extract from Police Report under Ref. S.B/1/4/1129 1/5/11. forwarded to R.O. Nottingham by C.C. Derby 6.11.44. ref: S.B 1/4/1129 re Arthur William OLDAG @ John OLDAY and Laurence HISLAM, anarchists evading Military Service, forwarded by: R.O. Nottingham 8.11.44

At 7 p.m. on the 3rd instant we were waiting at .. 18 Mount Street, Breaston.. when ECKER.. Charles Edwin Peter.. returned from work and we interviewed him.............

ECKER said that he knew OLDAY as a Cartoonist, but not personally, and that he had a book of his cartoons.

I respectfully suggest it is possible that ECKER's house is regarded by HISLAM and perhaps OLDAY as a place where a night or two may be safely spent.

Note.

C.C. 110, 712 (Gamble, shows that OLDAG's is one of several names suggested for a speaker at a meeting of the Liberal Group (a cover name for GAMBLE's Anarchist friends) at Reading University on 15.11.44. Although nothing may come of this I to-day handed Major Phipps a copy of the note at 71B and a note compiled by Miss Sercombe on GAMBLE's case. It was agreed that unless Major Phipps heard further from us he should take no further action.

F. 3.m.
6.11.44.

R.G. Micklethwait.

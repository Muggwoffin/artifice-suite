10.7.44. 72. To Special Branch enclosing note on OLDAG etc. 71b.

M.S.
I have only recently seen 68a, and in view of it have taken action at 70a and 71a. As the H.O.W. on the Freedom Press never reveals anything from OLDAG, it seems likely that he or someone on his behalf takes his cartoons to them by hand. If you have anyone in touch with the organisation, may I suggest that he or she should be shown OLDAG's photograph and specimen of handwriting?

F.3.m. 10.7.44.

Extract from War Commentary by FREIMANN.

F.3.m. Mr. Micklethwait

We have noted your remarks in Minute 72, and have taken account of OLDAG's photograph and specimen of handwriting. Should we have any information which may be of help in this case, we will let you know immediately.

M.S. (London)

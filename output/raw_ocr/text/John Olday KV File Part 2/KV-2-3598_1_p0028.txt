Pte. A.W. Oldag 96.
A. Coy. Room 3.
M.D.B. Sowerby Bridge, Yorks.
5th March 46.

My dear John and Peta,

It is very funny but recently my thoughts keep going back to times past and I just have to follow them. I start off thinking of, for instance the latest bit of news from Germany. The Soc. Dem. Party under the pressure of the Comm. Party having a conference in Berlin in order to come to a decision concerning the "One Party" - Reich, in Russian occupied Germany to be able to write now, to be able to address the workers in the present situation? It is impossible for me to tell you how I feel about it. I could vomit with disgust, and in the next instance laugh to tears. It is a good thing I am where I am for otherwise I would be running amok and not one of those spineless renegades would feel safe. These cowardly idiots.

What does Ernst think and Fritz? What is needed in Germany is a man in the form of Marat. A fearless daredevil who would express the peoples feelings, and if Aioskan, Washington, Longons' agents would be hunting, as did the enemies of the revolutions Marats time he would be safe as long as there is one worker left who has at heart not deserted his, and his fathers ideals of Freedom. We have never expected any thing else from the functionaries of the S.P.U. and K.P.U., and it does not matter a damn what decision they take in Berlin, they do not represent the German workers, as little as Franco represents Spain, or Stalin Russia, the eternal impulse which lives in the oppressed masses everywhere?

I keep on wondering what you would say to this and that item of news. What are our Heads saying now after this statement some time ago that they had expected a revolution and found themselves corrected by the actual events? Are their eyes opened by now? The world today is divided by three conflicting systems Capitalism, Fascism, and Bolshevism. The thesis of capitalism has provoked the challenging thesis of bolshevism. The thesis of bolshevism (Russia) produced anti-thesis facism by which reactionaries hoped to save themselves from bolshevism. If bolshevism and facism are the extremes of thesis and anti-thesis the final must be what all our revolutionary missionaries called anarcho-communism, and what we today call anarchism, I hold, and I am sure, you also do, the view, that we are approaching the end of an epoch; that we are in the midst of a revolution taking place now.

What we are witnessing everywhere (India, Egypt, America), even in this so counter-revolutionary country, strikes, riots, depression etc., are the inevitable growing pains of the coming, new Soc. order. If for one in contrast to many comrades believe in the inevitability of a violent transition (which does not mean that I would approve of) in the end self-defeating tactics of the young K.P.D. which brought about misrepresentations, falsehood, slander, vulgarity, and brawling.

If I was at present in Germany I would recreate Sparacus on a more insurgent basis, adopting the tactics of the early Russian A'sts and Soc. Rev's. Why can't our many depressed downhearts see that we are living in the greatest of all transitions in history, greater than the collapse of the Roman Empire, the passing of Slavery, and the fall of feudalism, greater than Renas'ssance, Reformation, and the French and Russian Revolutions. With the Spanish civil war a new era begun. We shall see greater changes all over the world in the next few decades than have taken place in the last thousand years, and I hope we shall take part in some of them.

In the midst of thinking "shop", I find myself washing up in your kitchen, looking in the garden. I think of the recitiles, and the wonderful meals we used to have. I can see the Heath and smell the warm air. I often think of poor old black coat, and how he used to hunt fleas, the discussions we used to have, the fun. It was grand seeing M.L. behind his face I could see your face, your bright eyes, and smiles and laughter. I hope you are well and happy, give my greetings to the two little ones.

Yours,

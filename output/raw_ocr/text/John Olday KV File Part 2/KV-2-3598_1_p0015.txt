14235557 Pte. SLOANE, McNeil

I have been talking with Colonel Glegg about the four B.l.d. cases for which you still have open files: MARSTON, OLDAG, PITTS and SLOANE.

I entirely agree that these cases should now be wound up, and accordingly suggest that MARSTON's case should be closed and his file destroyed after the usual period; G.S.O. records have already been asked to let me know when he is apprehended. The OLDAG, PITTS and SLOANE cases may also be closed, but here I should be glad if you would not destroy the files until the men are released from the Army. There is a good deal of material in each file which would be tiresome to recapitulate if the case became active again.

Yours sincerely,

C.S. Weldsmith.

Lt. Col. C.H. Gurney, O.B.E.,
A.A.G.,
Headquarters,
Western Command,
Chester.

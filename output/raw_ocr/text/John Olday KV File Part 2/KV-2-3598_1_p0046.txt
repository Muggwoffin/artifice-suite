SECRET AND PERSONAL

HEADQUARTERS,
WESTERN COMMAND,
CHESTER.

25th October 1945.

Dear Tallent,

13095115 Pte OLDAG, A.W.

Reference your PF.66074Y/F3m dated 10 Oct 45.

The attached correspondence from the Commandant, Stakehill Detention Barrack, received by me today is forwarded.

I have replied to the Commandant and asked him to keep the above-named soldier and his correspondence under observation until further instructions are issued by you.

I have also informed him that on no account must this soldier suspect that he is being watched.

Yours sincerely,

Capt. F.P.T. Tallent,
Box No. 500,
Parliament Street, B.O.
LONDON S.W.1.

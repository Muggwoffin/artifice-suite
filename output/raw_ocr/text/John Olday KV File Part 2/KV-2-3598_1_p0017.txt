SECRET AND PERSONAL

HEADQUARTERS,
WESTERN COMMAND,
CHESTER.

TO: T.S.M.
24 April 46.

Dear Miss Weldsmith,

I thank you for your letter PF.66074 of 18th April, giving me the full story of Oldag.

I visited his present O.C. this morning and understand that he is being drafted on 26 April to 276 Coy, 23 Group Pioneer Corps, Craven Arms, Salop.

I have arranged to see his new O.C. on Friday morning and will talk over the case with him prior to Oldag's arrival there. His conduct for the short while he has been at Cuddington has been good.

Yours sincerely,

[signature]

Miss C.S. Weldsmith,
Box 500,
Parliament Street B.O.,
LONDON. S.W.1.

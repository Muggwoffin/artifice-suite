URGENT MEMORANDUM.

Reference No.
From War Office, F3m
Date 11th September 1945
Subject 130 95115
Plt Oldag

To O i/c Pioneer Corps Records, Bournemouth

Reference your memo D2/cc/13095115 of 3rd September 1945 regarding the release from prison of the above-named on 24th September 1945. The War Office memo PF. 66074/F3m dated 1st April 1944 quoted by you, cannot be traced, and does not appear to have originated from F3m as stated.

It is requested that a copy be forwarded to this Office (F3m) as soon as possible, together with the attached, which is returned for easy reference.

P. Damer

12 SEP 1945

SECRET

Ref: PF.66074 (E) F.3.m.

16th May, 1945.

Dear Waddams,

We have been interested in Arthur William OLDAG & John OLDBAY, now serving a sentence of twelve months' imprisonment in Brixton Prison.

I should be very grateful if you would arrange for me to have a sight of all his in-coming and out-going correspondence.

Yours sincerely,

G.R. Mitchell.

W.H. Waddams, Esq.
Prison Commission,
62-4 Baker Street,
LONDON, W.1.

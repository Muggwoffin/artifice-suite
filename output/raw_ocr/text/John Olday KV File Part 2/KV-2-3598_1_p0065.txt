Subject: REJOINING FROM DESERTION

13095115 Private OLDAG, W.

Reference your memo. PF.66074/F.3M, dated 1 Apr 44.

It is notified for information that the above-named soldier is due to be released from H.M. Prison, Brixton on 24 Sep 45.

Private OLDAG will be escorted to No.12 Pioneer Corps Holding & Training Unit, and will be tried for deserting H.M. Service with effect from 27 Mar 43 until apprehended by Civil Power on 17 Nov 44.

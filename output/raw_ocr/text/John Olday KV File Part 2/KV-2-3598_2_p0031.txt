SECRET.

SF.455/49/F.3.b.

15th December, 1944.

Dear Sir,

It has come to our notice from a very secret source that on the 3rd December 1944 a prisoner in Brixton whose initials are 'F.A.' wrote to the Freedom Press anarchists, 27, Belsize Road, London, N.W.1. Presumably this will have been seen before it was despatched.

From my information it appears that 'F.A.' has been imprisoned in Germany and France, and was to appear at Clerkenwell Police Court on the 9th December. On the date that he wrote the letter (3rd December) he was in Brixton Prison Hospital, under observation.

I would be interested to identify 'F.A.' and wonder if you are in a position to do so.

Yours sincerely,

G.R. Mitchell.

The Governor,
Brixton Prison,
S.W.

MRS/EM.

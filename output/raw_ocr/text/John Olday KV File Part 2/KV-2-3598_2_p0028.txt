30th December, 1944.

Dear Glover,

John OLDAY @ Arthur William OLDAG.

You may be interested to hear that John OLDAY @ Arthur William OLDAG is in custody at Brixton Prison, having been caught using a false Identity Card in the name of Frederick FROSTICK. Would you please inform any police forces whom you have asked to look out for him.

Yours sincerely,

R.G. Micklethwait.

Extract from F3/1982 report re Anarchists - London Anarchist Group

The following have recently been proposed and accepted for London Anarchist Group membership:-

.....John OLDAY, with the assistance of Duncan Gilchrist, Ivor Deas, and Colin Ward, is collecting information, press cuttings and data re the German Anarchists with a view to building up files of the German position. It is also intended to publish in the German language a regular bulletin of information etc, for distribution in Germany.

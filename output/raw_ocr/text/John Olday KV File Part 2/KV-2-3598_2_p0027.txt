8. Copies in S.F. 455/49
P.F. 66074

On the morning of 30.12.44. Insp. Hoare Special Branch rang up and said that they had reason to believe that they would find what they wanted at the Petty France Address and proposed to search it and also the 'studios', on the afternoon of 30th. The studios referred to are Camden Studios, 28B Camden Street, N.W.1., believed to be frequented by Freedom Press Anarchists and/or John OLDAG. (See S.F. 455/49 Vol. 7 258a.)

F. 3.m.
30.12.44.

R.G. Micklethwait.

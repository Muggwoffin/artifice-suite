Extract for: PF.66074 - Y. Box for OLDAG.

Extract from Telephone Check on PRImrose 2333.

15.12.44.

10.30. Incoming.

A man with a foreign accent rings Marie Louise who says she has been out a great deal these last few days. M.L. says she is coming round with a friend to see his pictures. He says he has put them all in his bed-room and has decided not to look at them for some time. M.L. says her friend (male) is very interested in pictures.

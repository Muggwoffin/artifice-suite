Extract from F3/1925 report re LONDON ANARCHIST GROUP mentioning John OLDAY (Communist)

Anarchists - London Anarchist Group

.......London Anarchist Group meetings are still being held fortnightly on Tuesday evenings. Of late matters discussed have, in the main, been not of undue import.
At the latest group meeting held on April 9th there was present a comrade called John OLDAY who has recently been released detention but who is still serving in the Army.
John OLDAY intends, if available, to speak at the London Anarchist Group May Day meeting which is to be held in the small Holborn Hall on the evening of May 1st.
John OLDAY is of the opinion that he may soon be discharged from the Army.

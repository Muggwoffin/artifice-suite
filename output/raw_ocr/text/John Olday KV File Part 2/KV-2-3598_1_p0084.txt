Special Branch.

22nd May 1945.

Subject: Anarchist letters.

Reference: Io Papers 47/44/29.

Further to the recent prosecution of the Freedom Press Anarchists under Defence Regulation 39 A, the under-mentioned information has been received from a reliable source concerning a circular letter to soldiers on "Soldiers' Councils", which was being prepared by the anarchists, John OLDAY (320/EPA/517) (now serving 12 months imprisonment for a National Registration offence); Corporal George Ivor DEAS, S/1623060 R.E., T.T.Coy, Survey Training Centre, Ruabon (arrested at the Freedom Press offices on 12.12.45 for desertion); and Trooper William LEAN 7957735, R.A.C. Depot, Catterick, Yorks:-

"Shortly before OLDAY's arrest, Trooper LEAN wrote several letters to the Freedom Press, and was asked to call at the office at Belsize Road when in London. On one visit to London, he met OLDAY at 11, Eton Place, N.W.3. (the address of Vernon and Marie Louise RICHARDS) and OLDAY spoke to him about "Soldiers' Councils", saying that they could be started in discussion circles. On another visit, LEAN met OLDAY and Corporal DEAS, and the three drew up a letter to soldiers on "Soldiers' Councils, partly in OLDAY's and partly in LEAN's handwriting. LEAN believed that DEAS had this on him when arrested. The letter was to be a circular letter to the troops and was to have no issuing address on it. Some of the addresses to which it was to be sent, were to be supplied by LEAN, who was going to get them at his depot at Catterick, through which soldiers were constantly passing. The names would be picked at random so that no suspicion would attach to any particular person

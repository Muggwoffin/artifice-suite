Extract from Letter from 1451610 LAC LOCKIE, J. Andrew, Block D., Room 7 R.A.F., Watebeach, Cambs, to Freedom Press, 27 Belsize Road, London N.W.6.

Many thanks to John OLDAY for sending me the original drawing of "Defend our Heritage". I shall be collecting it in the near future from my home where it is held for me.

I agree that it is "asking for trouble" to have it in the billet, but, believing in the liberty of the individual, I ask nothing more than to defend myself and my principles, and thereby defend the liberty of mankind, even though in a small way.

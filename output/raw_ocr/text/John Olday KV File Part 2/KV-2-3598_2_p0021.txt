SECRET.

Original in:-
SF.455/49/F.3.b.

The Deputy Assistant Commissioner,
Special Branch,
New Scotland Yard.

It has come to my notice from a very secret source that Marie Louise RICHARDS was in conversation with a man with a foreign accent (nationality unknown) on the 15th December 1944. Marie Louise said that she would be coming round to see his pictures with a man friend. The foreigner said that he had put them in his bed-room and had decided not to look at them for some time.

I am afraid that I cannot identify the man with whom Marie Louise was speaking and hesitate to pass on this very vague information to you. On the other hand it seems possible that the pictures which were mentioned might refer to some of OLDAG's cartoons, and it is just possible that this might link up with information which is already in your possession.

4th January, 1945.

M.R. Sercombe.

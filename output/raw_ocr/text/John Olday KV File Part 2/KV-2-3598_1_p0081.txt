The H.O.W. on Marie Louise RICHARDS intercepted a letter from OLDAG dated 23.5.45, post-marked 18.6.45, red-stamped 20.6.45. This letter was largely personal, requesting her not to organise an exhibition of his pictures which presumably she had suggested to him. Original destroyed.

F3m. 26.6.45.

F.P.T. Tallent.

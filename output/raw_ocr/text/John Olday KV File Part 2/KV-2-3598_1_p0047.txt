Secret and Personal.

No. 15. Mil. Detn. Bk.
Stakehill, Castleton, Lancs.

23 Oct. 45.

No. 15/e/8/16

Dear Vezey

Re: No. 13095115. Pte. W. Oldag. Pioneer Corps. Prestatyn.

The above named soldier was admitted to this Mil. Detn. Bk. on the 5th Oct. 1945, to serve a sentence of 18 months Detention (awarded on 29.9.45) for the offence of WOAS Deserting H.M. Service, and is due for release on 28th June, 1946.

During the last weekend he wrote a letter, and owing to its contents it was held by my Assistant Commandant (Major Watson), and Oldag was questioned as to the meaning of the contents. During the questioning Pte. Oldag stated that he is a member of the Anarchist Federation and that the addressee is also a member. He also stated that he is of dual nationality, his father being German and that he came to this country in 1939.

In view of this a copy of the letter is passed to you for information and any action you may consider necessary.

Pte. Oldag is not aware of the action taken and the original letter has been passed on to the addressee.

Yours sincerely,

J. Harvey
Lieut. Colonel.
Commandant.

To: - Major Vezey,
D.A.A.G.
Headquarters,
Western Command.

Certified that this letter was typed by an officer (Major. W.B. Watson).

J. Harvey
Lt. Col.

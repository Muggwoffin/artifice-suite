18.12.44

Dear Sir,

Reference SF.455/49/F.3.b. of 15th December, 1944, the three prisoners as follows were sent to Clerkenwell Police Court on 9th December, 1944 -

5067 ATTOE. Richard - Stg. bedsheets. Aged 45. No fixed abode. Born. London. 5'9". Sallow. Lt. Brown Hair. Hazel eyes. Oval face. Proportionate build. Next of kin - None known. Fined at Court and did not return to Prison.

5195. GARDINER. Albert Edward. Stg. wallet. Committed in custody to Co. London Sess. 2.1.45
Age. 30. No fixed abode. Born - London
5'8". Fresh complexion. Lt. Brown hair. Blue eyes Oval face. Prop. build.
Next of kin - None known.

4957. FROSTICK. Frederick
Age - ?. Ht. 5'10". Sallow. Fair hair. Blue eyes. Prop. build. Oval face. Committed to Central Criminal Ct., in custody.

Attoe and Frostick were under mental observation. Frostick refused all information about himself and from the prison record he did not write any letters but may have sent out one surreptitiously. He refused his name on his first remand. No letters can be traced against Attoe either.

Governor

G.R. Mitchell, Esq.

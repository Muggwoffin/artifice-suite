interested that my cartoons & poems should be widely circulated. 'W. Com' has refused many contributions which I.W. or D.A. or any other left paper would have gladly printed. You know that I am against co-operation with K's group until such a time they would declare their regret for the harm done and take back their foundationless accusations. I think what they should do (at least) when reprinting anything by me is to state that the contribution is reprinted from 'F.P.' of I.W. By doing so they would not create the impression that I was in complete agreement with them and I would not be forced to make a statement in F.P. published.

Please write about the things going on. I hate telling you off, but one thing you never have done and you ought to do under all circumstances is to acknowledge letters received! How often & for how long did I use to wonder I worry in the past. Did I or did they get my last letter. I know your time is taken up with every so many things but all I ask for is a few lines, a confirmation.

Needless to say how I've been longing to hear a few words such as sweet as some in your last letter. And how we are waiting for a description of the last meeting. Don't forget comrades, news of that kind is mana for our soul. After all I haven't had much for the last 12 months.

I am dreaming a lot lately. Remember how I use to tell you my dreams & how you tried to analyze them? My dreams make me realize how very much I miss you all, for during the day there is little chance to relax & day dream. I even miss the solitude & peace of my cell in Brixton. I haven't been able to write anything at all so far. My mind is absolutely blank any way and the short time after supper wouldn't do, neither for study nor writing. The hour before turning in is used for discussion & to the listening to life stories (You bet I'm picking & preserving in my mind many good stories!) I've met quiet fine chaps, another one who's been in Spain and one who came from Glasgow. He attacked me first in mad saying the Anarchists do nothing but propagate Communism & the yes against he knew, where also opportunities. When asked it appeared he meant Jus A. & his gang. For Eddy Brown he had the greatest admiration, also for Frank. Give my love to both of them please. Phil asked for my songs. I do hope they are not lost. What is Vera going to do? I shall keep my fingers crossed for both of you. Give my love to all our friends. Give the Spanish youth a big kiss and let him have a sniff at the glass when you drink our health. I often think if you people were less optimistic you would learn the great secret of being invulnerable.

Seen any French pictures lately?

Love J.O.

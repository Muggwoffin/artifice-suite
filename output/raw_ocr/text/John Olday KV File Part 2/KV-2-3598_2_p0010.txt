It is not unlikely that this duplicating set was used at MORAVEK's flat by OLDAG - who is cartoonist to the anarchist paper "War Commentary", and a prime suspect - to produce the circular letter which has been distributed among H.M. Forces by anarchists, and which forms the subject of current enquiries which will doubtless lead to a prosecution for conspiracy to subvert H.M. Forces from their duty.

MORAVEK denies all knowledge of OLDAG and says he bought the duplicating set from a French or Belgian soldier and will use it, and the new lithographic stone (page one para.3) for his writings.

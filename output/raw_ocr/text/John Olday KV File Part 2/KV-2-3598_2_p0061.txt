Extract from Special Branch report, dated 6.9.44. - reference 79/43/3097 - subject Edouard L.T. MESENS.

"Information was received from M.1.5 that a man (believed to be Arthur William OLDAG - 320/EPA/517 - a deserter, vide Police Gazette of 31.5.43, case 211) was expected to call on Mrs. Marie Louise RICHARDS (an Anarchist responsible for the printing and publishing of 'War Commentary') at her flat, 11, Eton Place, Chalk Farm, N.W.3, at about 4 p.m. on Monday, 4.9.44.

Observation was maintained on this address and at 4.45 p.m. 4.9.44 a man was seen to approach the entrance to this block of flats."

On questioning the man, it was found that he was Edouard Leon Theodore MESENS, born 27.11.03 at Brussels, in possession of Belgian passport No. F.22169, issued Brussels on 28.2.39. Registered with police under Serial No. EZ.297071; R.C. No. 661764 issued Bow Street, 8.4.38.

Further particulars about MESENS is contained in Special Branch report dated 6.9.44, ref. 79/43/3097. His case is being dealt with on C.C. 222,239.

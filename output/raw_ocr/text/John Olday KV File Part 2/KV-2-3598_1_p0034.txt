Straining beneath the windows, seeing the morning thin and sidelong
Knit cloudy brows in silence,
And turn a sad shoulder on their former lovers.
To dream of them instead, and of running away with them.
To morning worlds guiltless of time and sickness.

I hope you are well, dear comrade. I'll write again in a few days.

Auf Wiederselin,

Iva.

Certified true copy and typed by me -

S.B. No. 1. (Flimsy)
Special Report
SUBJECT Arthur William OLDAG @ John OLDAY.
Reference to Papers 320/EPA/517.

METROPOLITAN POLICE.
SPECIAL BRANCH,
18 JAN 1945
16th day of January 1945.

With reference to Arthur William OLDAG, deserter & subject of Special Branch file No. 320/EPA/517:-

This man appeared to-day before the Recorder of London, Sir. Gerald Dodson, at Court No. 4., Central Criminal Court, Old Bailey, E.C. on the following charges:-

1. 'Did feloniously, on or about 20th day of May 1944, at Gravesend, Kent, steal a National Registration Identity Card No. DIW/659, in the name of Frederick Frostick, value is the property of the said Frederick Frostic.' Contrary to Sec. 2 of the Larceny Act, 1916.

2. 'Did feloniously, on or about the 20th day of May 1944, at Gravesend, Kent, receive the above mentioned Identity Card, knowing it to have been stolen.' Contrary to Sec. 33. of the above Statute.

3. 'Did unlawfully, on the 17th day of November, 1944, at Eversholt Street, N.W.1. use a name, to wit, Frederick Frostick, other than that by which he was ordinarily known prior to the coming into force of the Defence (General) Regulations, 1939.' Contrary to Regulation 20, of the said Regulations.

4. 'Did unlawfully, on the 17th day of November, 1944, at Eversholt Street, N.W.1. with intent to deceive, falsely represent that he was the person to whom an Identity Card related.' Contrary to Section 8 (1) (b) (1) of the National Registration Act, 1939.

OLDAG had been committed for trial on 9th December, 1944, from Clerkenwell Magistrates Court.

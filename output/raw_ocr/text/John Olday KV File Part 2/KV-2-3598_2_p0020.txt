Copies in S.F. 455/49.
P.F. 66074/Y.

The H.O.W. intercept filed in S.F. 455/49 Vol. 7 284a shows that in October 1944 the Freedom Press Group of Anarchists sent a typed statement to the Anarchist Federation Group dealing with the dispute which had arisen between the two groups. The only signature at the end was a group of initials, one being "F.A". Neither Miss Sercombe nor I can think who F.A. might be if it is not OLDAG. The other initials are easily identifiable.

P.F. 66074 contains at 98a a H.O.W. intercept of a letter written by OLDAG from Brixton Prison to the Freedom Press. This is signed 'F.A.'

F.3.m.
16.1.45.

R.G. Micklethwait.

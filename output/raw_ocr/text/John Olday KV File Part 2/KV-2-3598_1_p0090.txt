Extract from an F.3/1462 Report dated 5.4.45.

ANARCHISTS.

Referring to the circumstances of John OLDAY's arrest, the following information has recently been received:-

The fact that John OLDAY spent a few weeks in prison without being identified has afforded the 'Friends of Freedom Press' some small measure of satisfaction.

According to Phillip SANSOM, John OLDAY was warned to take a taxi whilst transporting the typewriter from one place to another. Had this warning been heeded, John OLDAY might still have been free. It was to save the few pence of a taxi fare that John OLDAY went on foot carrying the typewriter.

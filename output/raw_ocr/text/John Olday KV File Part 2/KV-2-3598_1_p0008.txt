4. In these circumstances, I do not think that this case calls for any intervention on our part at this stage. I think we should allow it to proceed on its normal course. As and when it is disposed of it will always be open to the military authorities to charge OLDAG with being a deserter, and, at the same time, the J.A.G. can give consideration to the question of what, if any, charge can be preferred against him in respect of his defeatist cartoons.

5. You will see from the Police report that OLDAG was committed for trial as man, name refused, for which reason I have been unable to find the particulars of this case in the Calendar of the forthcoming sessions. I am, therefore, not able to tell you when the case is likely to be reached. I will, however, see that we are apprised of the result.

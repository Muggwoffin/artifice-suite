2. I AM NOT PERMITTED TO RECEIVE CLOTHES, MONEY, CIGARETTES OR ANY OTHER ARTICLES, WHILST AT THE ABOVE ADDRESS.

My dear friend,

By the time this letter reach you John & Vera will be home again. Then you have your celebration please do the same we use to do for our absent friends: put something representing your us upon the mantelpiece and drink to our health, e.g. St Albert's. He is having a hard time, but doing his best to become a good Soldier, in spite of his aversion against militarism, for I have - I hope - convinced him that the coming Social Revolution will need men like ourselves - Mathers and Durrell, all of them men who come from the ranks and in the struggle against the monarchs defeated the best professional military experts of their time & developed new tactics & strategic methods & knowledge of by the masses & were educated & trained in military academies. At present Albert looks more like the good Soldier Schweik than like the coming red/black Napoleon, but certainly he is forgetting his old ways & becomes a bit drill. I doubt if even the smartest staff in Britain could suppress a smile there watching a St Albert. He certainly is a fellow. I never received - we shall have to put him on the stage when we re-open our shows. Poor his sister & they & kept me informed about events. Having no newspapers - perhaps in all cases of interest (such as the bombed bus conductors and other actions) are welcome. On the other hand I am rather worried & would like to know the position as far as our group is concerned. I understand the gay gowns are published, D.G. and some cartoons & poems, but I've told you, without any permission.

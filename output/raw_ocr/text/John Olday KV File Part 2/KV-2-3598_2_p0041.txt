A.D.F. through F. 3.H.

You said that the question of keeping on the Telephone Check on the RICHARDS (Primrose 2333) would be reconsidered this month.

I have just spoken to Mr. Himsworth on the telephone and he says that from his point of view, and I believe M. also approves, the continuance of the telephone check is essential in enabling them to keep up to date with the RICHARDS' movements.

Apart from the QLDAG angle the check is useful in supplying us with other information on the current activities of the RICHARDS and their friends which do not come to light through the H.O.W. on the Bookshop. As an example, on the 13th of this month, Vero RICHARDS sister, Vera BALFOUR, rang up Marie Louise RICHARDS to say that she had arranged to get some money to Paris for Marie Louise's sister. There is apparently an extension to their telephone on which Vero listened and hurriedly interrupted the conversation. From what was said it was gathered that a friend of Vera's (probably an American) was taking the money in a letter by air. Unfortunately it does not seem possible to take any action on this without "blowing" the telephone check.

Subject to your approval I should like the telephone check kept up for a further period.

F. 3.b.
27.11.44.

M. R. Sercombe.

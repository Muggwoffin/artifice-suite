SECRET

PF.60074/Y/F.3.m.

Deputy Assistant Commissioner,
Special Branch.

Arthur William OLDAG @ John OLDAY, the subject of Special Branch file 320/EPA/517, was enlisted in the Army in 1941 and has been a deserter since 27.3.43. His name was circulated as such in the Gazette of 31.5.43. No. 211.

I enclose herewith a note of all the particulars in our file which might help in tracing him. I do not usually interest myself in the apprehension of deserters as such, but as it is clear that this man is actively engaged in the preparation of grossly subversive propaganda I consider the case one where special efforts to trace OLDAG would be desirable.

It seems probable that wherever he is living he is in possession of subversive cartoons and writings which have not yet been published. I am anxious to obtain information about these, and should therefore be glad of an opportunity of considering searching his premises, if he should be traced, before he or any associate has time to destroy any papers there.

In view of para 8 of the enclosed note I am sending a copy of it to the R.S.L.O. at Bristol, where there is a flourishing branch of the Freedom Press, and to the R.S.L.Os at Manchester and Cardiff.

for T.M. Shelford.

RGM/PB
7.44.

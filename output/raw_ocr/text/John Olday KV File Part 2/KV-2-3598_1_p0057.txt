3) Good I don't get it. Don't be downhearted - thinkin. I understand the position perfectly. Thing will be better when all our Comrades return again. I am very sorry Vero & John's return will be delayed for some time. Give them my love when you see them. Tell them I am calm. I am expecting not less than 2 years & would it be supposed to get even more. I haven't got a rheum complex as Dr. G. seems to think. I am arraigned of a great pathology. I just happen to bear in mind what happened to Rosa & Ench. Being Ench's friend I don't expect leniency from the clan that never forgive them for his attitude.

If you have not received by now a parcel with my April clothes & 2 books, please let me know.

P.S. Not to worry. I don't. Whatever the outcome is going to be I am positive it will be for

Please do tell Tom I won't be able to write for some time as I can write only once a week. Give my best to Bill & Thomas. Read & send also please inform Mrs. Reily 21 Baroness Rd. E.2 that I am awaiting C.H. Send her a copy of the album & ask her to give my regards to Romney & the boys. I shall keep them informed. I heard that one person had got hold of your address. If he ever should turn up claim to have been sent by me you will know it is not true. I have given your address to nobody except to Mr. V.

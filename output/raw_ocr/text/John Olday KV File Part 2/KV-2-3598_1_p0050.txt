This was handed to me in the Compound Holiday Camp, Pioneer Corps, Prestatyn, N. Wales, with a request to forward it on, to your address.

(1) Frank left here—I met him after his Court Martial. He seemed very pleasant. I don't know why!

(2) Could you get one of the Spaniards to look into the case of a refugee in Brixton, named Mina. Failed to notify change of address no criminal record and due for deportation after expiration of sentence. Is a Republican. doesn't know anything about our Mob, but I think there must be some Committee he can contact. He imagines there is no one. It is obvious deportation to Spain would be far too severe a sentence.

COPY.

c/o 8 Barnesdale Rd
Reading
27/10/44.

Dear Sirs,

We all enjoyed Mr. Tom BROWN's visit and we have sold all the literature practically which you sent us; except for the pamphlet on the General Strike which we consider does not meet the needs of University members. I will enclose these back to you, and the money, in a few days, and will enclose a further order.

Concerning our next meeting on Nov. 15th, we have fixed up Mr. Alex Comfort but he cannot let us know definitely till a day or so before. Therefore, could you possibly book us Mr. George Woodcock or Mr. John Olday, or any other speaker, provisionally, and subject to last minute cancellation if Mr. Comfort can manage to come? I should be very grateful if you could.

Yours truly,

Sgd. A. Graham Gamble.

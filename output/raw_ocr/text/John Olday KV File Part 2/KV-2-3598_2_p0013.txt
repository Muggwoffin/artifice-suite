Extract from S.B. report re Anarchist matters.

2. For a time during 1944, the Freedom Press cartoonist, John OLDAY, now serving 12 months imprisonment for Nat. Reg. Offences, stayed at the boarding house of an alien called "Yosef" in London Street, Paddington. Some of OLDAY's drawings were still to be seen on the walls there and some typewriting for the anarchists was done there. Recently two young Scotsmen who were seen by police at OLDAY's address at 7, Camden Studios, Camden Street, N.W.1., when he was arrested in November 1944, stayed at the Paddington address. It is now understood that they have gone to an address in Cornwall recommended by Ronald AVERY.

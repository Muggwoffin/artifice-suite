Well, Mia-Kin, once more: I am glad for you. You were right after all: we will soon see each other again. Be my brave comrade. And don't work too hard! Give my love to V. T. & P. Write more & than.

I shall keep that last letter of yours and take it with me wherever I go & whatever happens.

I am yours for ever

J. V.

NOTE. No payment in part satisfaction of the sum adjudged to be paid by a prisoner, in order to obtain the remission of part of his imprisonment can be made on a Sunday, or on a week-day before 9 a.m. or after 4 p.m., but the total amount of fine etc., may be paid at any hour before the closing of the prison, 4.30 p.m., or on any day. No part payment can be accepted in the case of a prisoner committed by a County Court.

I think of Duranti. I think of my own friends who survived. And I think of you. And the many new comrades in this country and in America, Mexico. And I think of Spain.

They thought we were stamped out, our spirit broken! But we have grown, hardened. There are many more sufferings to be faced. There are many more of us who will share the same fate of our dead comrades. That we are willing to do so and that the number of new comrades is increasing in spite of all hardships to come, shows we are the future.

My love to all!

3 APR 1945

Dear Maria & Louisa!

Just a few lines. Bill wrote a depressed letter telling me that Tom & Ken had organized (at Holborn Hall) a meeting and advertised themselves as A.F. I have written to Bill. Ask him to show you my letter and when he comes to London explain the facts to him.

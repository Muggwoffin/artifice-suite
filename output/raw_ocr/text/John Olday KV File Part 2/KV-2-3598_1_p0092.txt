I am thinking of the mass demonstrations in the continent during the Sacco & Vanzetti case. Millions voiced their protest and Sacco & Vanzetti died just the same. I am thinking of my friend Erich Minkham, our comrade in Russia, of the many who, as Vanzetti wrote were 'killed at once by the sacrifice, or little by little by the confiscement' in spite of all protests. I remember when Emil came back from prison. The thousands of workers at the station who welcomed him, carried him off on their shoulders. Were they there when he was in the hands of the Nazis? I am also thinking of Rosa Luxemburg in prison, the words she wrote. How I now understand her feelings, doubts & desperate hopes. I wonder what kind of soul she would have taken had she experienced all that our generation had to go through and has to witness at present. Would she have turned from 'a gallant fighter of humanity, who loved justice, hated misery and was the fierce champion of the under dog and the savage attacker of the exploiter', into a renegade like Lupton Sinclair, George Grosz, Rudolf Rocker? I don't believe she would have. She was the first to fall from the hands of those who killed systematically all who stood for the Social Revolution. How many of our comrades, of men & women, who believed in the supremacy of the individual conscience over social compulsion."

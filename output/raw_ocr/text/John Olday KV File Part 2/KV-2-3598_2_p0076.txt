20th. July, 44.

Friends of the Freedom Press -
Arthur Wm. OLDAG @ John OLDAY.

I beg to report that the particulars contained in the attached letter have been noted.

Enquiries have been made in the Chinese colony in the city but so far without trace of Oldag having frequented these quarters. Steps have been taken to ensure that the Police will be informed should he turn up and appropriate action will follow.

I have also made discreet enquiries in local Freedom Press circles but Oldag does not appear to have been in this City, but arrangements have been made for further enquiries to be made amongst persons likely to come into contact with him.

I would suggest that the original photograph of Oldag and six copies be forwarded to the R.S.L.O.

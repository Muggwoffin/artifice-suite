The dead can't defend themselves from being perverted & exploited. I wish I could broadcast T.'s poems, some of his anti-militarist & anti-imperialist writings, his articles on law & courts & judges! I wish I could make Hilsam's voice heard, in order to counter the infamy of opportunist scoundrels.

Give the Glasgow comrades my fraternal greetings. I am often with them in my thoughts. Tell them I have not wasted my time and studied a lot. Tell them that I do not care whatever may happen to me. Whatever it will be, it will serve our cause. Knowing that I am content & happy, I have lost many dear comrades. I think they too had found as many new & true friends & comrades as I have found since.

The news M.L. brought me has shocked me deeply, but I am now in a better frame of mind. I wrote a long letter, still under the impression of that news. A mixture of bitterness & sentimentality. I destroyed that letter and I am glad I did. N.D. two little gifts which came by post in short intervals have helped me greatly. Bless that heart of hers.

I don't think that the election of Labour will alter much as far as we are concerned, in spite of the fact that Morrison announced in one of his election speeches, he would like to pull the London prisons down, and in spite of Lasky's declaration of solidarity.

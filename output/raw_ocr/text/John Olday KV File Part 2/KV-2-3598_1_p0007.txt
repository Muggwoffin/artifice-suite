A.D.F.

When OLDAG (@ OLDA) was committed for Trial for using a false identity card, I understand that it was not discovered that he was a deserter. Subject to any advice that S.L.B. may give, I feel that when he is brought up at the Central Criminal Court, he should be remanded and handed over to the Military authorities for the following offences:

(a) using a false identity card,
(b) publishing cartoons of a subversive nature, (if that can be proved an offence)
(c) being a deserter.

The above three charges could be made against him instead of only the one, i.e. using a false identity card, as appears to be the case at the moment.

As the Session at the Central Criminal Court opens on the 9th January, I feel that no time should be lost in arriving at a conclusion as to what should best be done.

F./MA.
3rd January 1945

SLB. Mr. Sinclair.

F.S.m, Mr. Micklethwait (through A.D.F. and F./MA.).

1. As the only information regarding the charge, or charges, to be preferred against OLDAG is contained in Mr. Micklethwait's note at 101a, I took the liberty of obtaining, through London Region, a copy of the Police report on this case which is now filed at 107a.

2. From this you will see that there are no less than four charges against this man, all of them more or less arising out of his assumption of the name of FROSTICK, and his use of the true FROSTICK's National Registration Identity Card.

3. As and when OLDAG's case comes up at the Old Bailey, the Judge who tries him will undoubtedly be informed that he is a deserter, for which reason he may be given a nominal sentence with a view to his being handed over to a military escort, although, of course, the Judge is entitled to sentence him in respect of those matters upon which he is indicted.

LEICESTERSHIRE CONSTABULARY

Head Quarters,
(Special Branch),
Leicester.
25th September, 1944.

Sir,

Reference letter from Major Glover dated 13th September, 1944, Ref. 3276, concerning James E. ODEY, of 9, Thurlaston Lane, Croft, Leicestershire. (It should be noted that the name is ODEY and not ODAY as given in Major Glover's letter).

I have made a discreet enquiry in this case, and as a result have gained the following information.

ODEY was born on the 10th September, 1901, and his N.R.No. is RGJX.195/3, and he resides with his mother, Mary ODEY, N.R.No. RGJX.195/2, at Croft. His father has died since the commencement of the war. ODEY has resided at Croft at least since September 1939 and he was there on national registration day. He lives in a good-class semi-detached house, and prior to the war was a lorry driver in the coal industry. He registered for military service on the 31st May, 1941, as a conscientious objector, national service reference being LXX.587 C.O. He came before the local Tribunal on the 19th June, 1944, when his name was allowed to remain on the register conditionally. The conditions were that he performs part-time duties as a market gardener and works 20 hours weekly on agricultural work. The Ministry of Labour had reason to believe that ODEY was not complying with these conditions. He should be doing 20 hours weekly with Mr. Bembow, farmer, of Thurlaston, but I have ascertained that he has not worked for this man since the end of June of this year. This information has been passed to the appropriate authorities.

I have not yet had an opportunity of seeing ODEY, but I am arranging to do this discreetly, and will report in due course on his description and any other useful information which I may obtain. ODEY has not previously come to notice.

From the above it will be seen that ODEY is not identical with Arthur William OLDAG alias John OLDAY, and I gave this information by telephone to Major Glover's secretary on the 18th instant.

W.J. Dodge
Det. Sergeant.

Wm. G. Bunker, Esq.,
Det. Superintendent.

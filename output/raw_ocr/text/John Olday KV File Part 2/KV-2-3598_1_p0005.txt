The name of the speaker will normally be posted on the University notice-board, and Phipps has arranged to be notified. The Vice-Chancellor is co-operative and could arrange for a reliable senior student to attend the lecture without compromising us in any way.

I said that in the circumstances it seemed extremely improbable that OLDAG would attend, but I would consult Miss Sercombe and Himsworth and let Phipps know what we wanted done. After speaking with Miss Sercombe I informed Himsworth of the position and we all agreed that if OLDAG attended it was desirable from our point of view that he should be arrested* and that if information was then obtained showing where he had been living, a search warrant should be obtained. Himsworth was anxious, however, to consult S.B. before we finally decided on action and I agreed.

It was arranged for Himsworth to let me have for the file a copy of a photograph available in London which is better than the one we have.

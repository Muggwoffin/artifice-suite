F.3.b.2. (Miss Sercombe).

Enclosed is a Special Branch report in connection with our attempts to trace John OLDAG.

We picked up on the telephone that a man, who wished to discuss the publication of a booklet, desired to see Mrs. RICHARDSON about it. I spoke to Inspector Whitehead and it was agreed that observation should be kept on Mrs. RICHARDSON's home, in order to see who the man was. It was agreed, however, that if the man was not OLDAG he should be allowed to proceed.

The man was picked up - apparently because, superficially, he had the appearance of the wanted man. I gather, however, that the Police were able to gloss it over by simply saying that it was a routine check-up on aliens, so I do not think any harm has been done with connection with our enquiries.

M.S/London.
12.9.44.

Norman Himsworth.

It will be recalled (see page 10 of report dated 1.1.45) that a search of DEAS' kit following his desertion, revealed 3 pages of pencilled notes on the manner in which soldiers should mutiny. Also during the search of 7, Camden Studios, N.W.1. (where John OLDAY had been staying prior to his arrest) on 30.12.44, manuscript and typed copies of the above-mentioned pencilled notes by DEAS were found.

Trooper LEAN's particulars were found during the searches at 27, Belsize Road and 11, Eton Place, and articles have appeared by him in "War Commentary". Exhibit No. 24 in the prosecution was a letter found at 27, Belsize Road dated 10.12.44 signed by 31 soldiers of the R.A.C. Depot, Catterick, Yorks, drawn up by LEAN, protesting about the British action in Greece.

Trooper LEAN was in London on 7.5.45 (apparently on leave from his Unit) and stayed with his parents at 58, Canton Street, E.14. He contacted members of the Freedom Press Group of Anarchists and expressed his indignation at the sentences of imprisonment. He pledged his support and said that he would assist them in furthering their cause in every possible way without regard to risks involved.

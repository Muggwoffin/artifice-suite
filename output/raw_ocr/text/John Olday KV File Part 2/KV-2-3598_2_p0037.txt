BOOK ONE

NAPOLEON'S CAPTIVITY

One would think half the British Army & Navy is on the run. I have managed to get in contact with a number of prisoners. In spite of my isolation. Everybody is fed up with the war. Most of them have committed crimes in order to escape the battlefield. Everyone of them has learned his lesson. Prison experience will deepen their hatred against the existing State and drive them to desperate action when the time comes. I have, in this short time experienced examples of comradeship towards me, which have given me the greatest pleasure and strengthened my belief & trust in the 'lower' classes. There are 3 murderers here also under observation. I prefer community with them to any social intercourse with most of the respectable people I know. I have found more kindness & unselfishness amongst the thieves here than I found amongst many who think themselves far above the level of criminals.

I am feeling o.k. so far. I miss my friends. It is hard to be without any news. I am living on memories. As fare as the future is concerned I am quiet hopeful. Every thing will turn out alright!

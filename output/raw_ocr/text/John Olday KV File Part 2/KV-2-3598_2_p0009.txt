Extract from S.B. report re Josef MORAVEK, Czech suspected of Trotskyist sympathies, when a raid was made on his flat, London Road, Paddington, where various Printed and written Material was found. Report mentions OLDAG.

Information was received that Arthur William OLDAG anarchist cartoonist (S.B. reference 320/EPA/517), now serving twelve months imprisonment for National Registration Offences, had stayed in MORAVEK's flat at 12, London Street, Paddington at a time when he (OLDAG) was a deserter from the British Army. A search warrant was therefore issued by Superintendent under Defence Regulation 88A and executed by me at that address, on 16th February 1945 (with the assistance of Inspector HO RE and Sergeant DICKINSON).

As already stated our information was that OLDAG stayed with MORAVEK at 12, London Street, W.2. and the finding of the former's drawings, (item 2 in list) when we searched, confirms this information. (According to MORAVEK he got the drawings in Hyde Park from two different men whom he forgets).

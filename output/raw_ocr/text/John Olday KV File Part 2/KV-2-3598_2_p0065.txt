Mr. Hollis, A.D.F.

Re John Oldag (alias Olday).

In connection with our inquiries to trace John Oldag you kindly imposed for us a telephone check on Freedom Press last Tuesday week (August 24th). The results of this have been examined by me. With the exception of our own conversation as part of our scheme to trace this man, and one or two unimportant incoming calls the telephone at Freedom Press has not been used.

Our inquiries, however, have led us to believe that the intermediary between Freedom Press and John Oldag is Mrs Marie Louise Richards. In fact she is known to take material for "War Commentary" herself to the printers and no doubt this includes Oldag's fortnightly cartoons.

In view of the failure of the check on Freedom Press and the facts about Mrs Richards as stated above, I wonder whether you would impose for us a telephone check on Mrs Richards. The address is 11, Eaton Place, N.W.3., and the telephone PRImrose 2333.

Incidentally, Freedom Press, through Mrs Richards, have invited us to discuss our projected book of cartoons with them on the grounds that they themselves hold Oldag's copyrights. This we propose to do, and reactions from any source, including Mrs Richards' telephone, would be useful.

If you agree do you think it would be possible to impose the check almost immediately to replace the one at Freedom Press, and keep it running for, say, a fortnight.

M.S. (London)
30/8/44

Norman Himsworth.

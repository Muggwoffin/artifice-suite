The best Revolutionaries & cats have one thing in common: they never fall to death but always land on their feet.

Do write. Even if you still are depressed. Give my love to Pete & old Phil, to the two old darlings to Mavis & write to all the comrades. My message is still the same: don't count. The efforts should be devoted to the thousands of nameless.

Love as tight. The soldiers have many a house been searching for us & a copy of our own 'Comrades' can be found in Bruno & D.B.

Many thanks for all you did - Nizhkin, for the many grand little things.

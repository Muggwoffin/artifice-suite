CONFIDENTIAL

Copy sent to London Region.

Original in:-
SP.455/42/F.3.b.

Copy for: PF.66074 - OLDAG.

Deputy Assistant Commissioner,
Special Branch,
New Scotland Yard.

You will have seen the article in "War Commentary" for the 10th February entitled "Defence of the Revolution" by Max VALE. Max VALE is not known to us and it is thought possible that this may be another alias for John OLDAG. If you have any information about the author of this article I would be most interested to hear.

March, 1945.

G.R. Mitchell

M. J. Sercombe

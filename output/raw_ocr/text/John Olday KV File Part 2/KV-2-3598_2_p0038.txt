I give you full authority to use my case for whatever propaganda you think useful. After all 'W.C.' ought to be first of all papers and don't be shy. You can't make it worse for me than it is. Here is the case: I was dragged into the army more or less. I did not become an C.O. because I didn't believe in the objectivism of tribunals & 'fair trial' in order to get aquainted with modern military science & practice & the mentality of the British people under conscription at the same time determined to propagate Anarchism in the Forces. I was for 5 months under open arrest and discriminated as an anti-national. I deserted because I found my presence in the Forces waste of time and hoped to be able to do better revolutionary work in Govt street. I do not regret my action. I only regret that time & circumstances did not allow me to do so little only. Then court martialled I shall refuse to soldier and obey any order what so ever and accept whatever punishment they will inflict under protest. I shall desert and try to escape however there is a chance, and at all times preserve my strength and be in preparation to fight the only war I consider just: the class war.

I may be a long time till, if ever, we meet again. But never mind! thanks for all your love & comradeship. My love to all of you. Carry on!

Yours fraternally F.A.

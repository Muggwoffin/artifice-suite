F.3.m. 3.1.45.

104.

A.D.F. through F.M.A.

As you know John OLDAG is in Brixton Prison; 101a shows how he got there. I understand that he is awaiting trial at the session of the Central Criminal Court which opens on 9th January for using a false identity card.

OLDAG is, of course, also a deserter from the Army and both during his Army service and since he has been systematically engaging in subversive anti-war propaganda. I have clipped inside the front cover copies of "The March to Death" (published in May 1943, a month or two after he deserted) and "The Life we Live, the Death we Die" published since. I have also put in an envelope photographs of cartoons, attributed to him which have appeared in "War Commentary" during 1944. We have no evidence that they are by him. The Freedom Press circular letter of 25.10.44. claimed him as a cartoonist but this is not evidence against him.

I have spoken with Mr. Mitchell, who agrees with me that OLDAG's case is a bad one and that he should be charged before the appropriate tribunal, with any offences that he has committed. May I suggest that S.L.B. be asked to advise whether his cartoons amount to offences in themselves or whether they could be brought in in aggravation of punishment for any further offences which OLDAG has committed.

F.3.m. 3.1.45.

R.G. Micklethwait.

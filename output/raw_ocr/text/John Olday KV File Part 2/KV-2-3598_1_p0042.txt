10th November, 1945.

Your Ref: L.S.W.C./1842
Our Ref: PF.66074Y/F.3.Mo

Dear Baker,

13095115 Pte. OLDAG, A.W.

Thank you for your letters of the 25th October and 2nd November concerning this man. I will be most interested to see copies of his correspondence if this is at all possible, though I do not feel that we are justified in putting the C.O. at Stake Hill Detention Barracks to unnecessary trouble on this point. I am grateful to you for asking this Commandant to keep OLDAG under observation, but I do not think regular reports are necessary. He should, of course, inform you if anything of particular interest comes to his attention, but otherwise there is only need to inform you when he is released, and to what unit he is posted.

I do not think OLDAG is likely to be much trouble in detention, though there is little doubt that he will come to notice immediately he is released.

Yours sincerely,

Captain F.P.T. Tallent.

Major H.E.R. Baker,
Headquarters,
Western Command,
CHESTER.

FPTT/RB

Dear Micklethwait,

With reference to your letter of 10th September, 1944, and in confirmation of our telephone conversation of 18th September, I enclose herewith copy report which I have received from the Chief Constable of Leicestershire from which you will notice that James E. ODEY of Croft, Leicestershire, does not appear to be identical with Arthur William OLDAG alias John OLDAY, in whom you are interested.

I return the photograph of the last named which accompanied your letter.

Yours sincerely,

Major Gerald Glover

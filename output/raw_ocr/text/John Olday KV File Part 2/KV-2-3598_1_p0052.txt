CONFIDENTIAL

KENsington 5151.

10th October, 1945.

Your Ref: I.S.W.C.1842.
Our Ref: P.F.66074Y/F.3.m.

Dear Baker,

13095115 Pte. OLDAG. A.W.

Many thanks for your letter of the 5th October and enclosure from the Colonel commanding No.12 Pioneer Corps Hdq. and Tng. Unit concerning this man. I am unable to get hold of the file just at the moment owing to the after-effects of our move and will probably be writing to you again concerning him later.

Yours sincerely,

Captain F.P.T. Tallent.

Major H.E.R. Baker,
Headquarters,
Western Command,
CHESTER.

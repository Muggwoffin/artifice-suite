KENSINGTON 5151

Your Ref: I.S.N.C. 2730
Our Ref: P.P. 66074/F.3.m.

30th January, 1946.

Dear Colonel,

Pte. A.W. OLDAG.

With reference to Liddington's letters of 15th and 18th January concerning this man, I think my letter to Baker of 10th November last indicates all the action that you need take for the time being. I would stress the fact that though I am most interested to see copies of his correspondence, I do not feel justified in asking for this should it put the C.O. of the Detention Barracks to much trouble.

I read the letter from Vernon RICHARDS enclosed with Liddington's of 18th January, with considerable interest. As you probably know, this man was one of the three members of the Freedom Press anarchists that were convicted under the Defence Regulations last April for subversive activities among H.M. Forces.

Yours sincerely,

Captain F.P.T. Tallent.

Lt. Col. W.M.W. Collins, M.C.,
A.A.G.,
Headquarters,
Northern Command,

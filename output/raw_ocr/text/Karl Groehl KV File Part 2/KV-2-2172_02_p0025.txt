To sum up I would mention that at the beginning of the war GROEHL was detained at the Sante prison as a particularly dangerous Communist and was only released on English intervention.

The French Deuxieme Bureau and the Surete Nationale, to whom I had recommended GROEHL, rejected his services with the comment that he was unreliable. If he then turned to the English "that was their affair."

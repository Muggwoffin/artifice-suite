Pl. P.A. 46464. (RETZLAW)

Source: Ri. 6th January 1943.

S. RETZLAW.

RETZLAW, whom I knew only slightly in connection with the arrangements of the Constructivists Club, wished to speak to me on Monday. In the course of my conversation with him RETZLAW spoke of his party (League of German Revolutionary Socialists) and remarked that this also had adherents in Germany, from whom he frequently received important information through friends in Switzerland. He placed this information at the disposal of certain English authorities. He produced telegrams and letters which he said he had received from Germany via a neutral country as evidence. These were copies; the originals were with the English authorities with whom he worked. In these copies were reports of German troop transports to the West, reports of all possible measures against civil war having been taken by the German security authorities; further they contained morale reports and reports of the enemies of Prussia in Bavaria; proposals about parachute landings were also made in them. RETZLAW asked me if I had not connections with Czech circles who might be interested in this material. He was concerned less with the question of money than with the question of political support for his work, although this naturally also required money. I told RETZLAW provisionally that I was only interested in the reports from a journalistic point of view, but that I would gladly find out if these reports were of interest to any Czech authority. Whether RETZLAW's reports from a subjective and objective point of view are genuine or not I am not able to judge; neither can I say whether he is in a position to dispose of these reports in this way if they be genuine. In any case I wanted to have informed you of his offer. Naturally I will not speak about this to any Czech authority and so I request your instructions as to how I should behave towards RETZLAW with regard to this matter.

E.5.(L).
13.3.43.

No. 410.

SOURCE: Ri. 25th July, 1942.

Information from Otto LEHMANN-RUESSBUELDT.

With reference to the Party of the German Socialist Federation recently founded by S. RETZLAW, Karl GROEHL, L-R remarked that GROEHL, with whom he is friendly, stands quite alone in this new party, for which he has won over practically no members at all.

Translated on 31.7.42.

VIEWS of THOMAS MANN on the Federation of Germany

REZLAW'S correspondence is required by ISR.

Writer encloses a copy of a reply he has received to a letter he wrote to THOMAS MANN (see LON/SE/6022/45).
MANN re-affirms his belief in the desirability of a German Federation.

Extracts:-
"Many thanks for your letter of the 22nd June which I received today. Yes, I am still here and I do not know when I shall leave. There are still many difficulties. But of course it is not really necessary to be in too great a hurry. However, I should like to start as soon as I can. I am not frightened and I do not shrink from work either. I also believe I know what I want to do and how to do it.

and for DR. S. the copy of the answer from THOMAS MANN to the letter which I enclosed to Dr. S. I know LOUD VANSTIART personally, we often write to each other."

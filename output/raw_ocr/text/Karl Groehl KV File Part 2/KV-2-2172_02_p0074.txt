German refugees who deny the possibility of revolutionary activity in Germany today do so out of ignorance. They have never engaged in revolutionary work and therefore cannot estimate the effectiveness of such work. Only the ignorant can deny the effectiveness of the SPARTACIST League during the World War.
One of the reasons for deficiency of strength in the German Opposition is the lack of whipped up hatred against the Hitler régime.
Hatred is, however, a source of energy.

M.G.B. .......... 18.12.41.

SOURCE: Ma. through Mr. Sykes. 13.2.42.

Ma. mentioned RETZLAW (GROEHL) whom he considers to be dangerous. From Berlin he knows him to be a Trotskyist. At one time he was the leader of the Trotskyist cell in the Berlin branch of the KPD. He is out for revolution and although he may have been ready to give this country some assistance in the war against Hitler, he never loses sight of his ultimate aim and might even now be engaged in subversive activities against the Government.

E.7.(S)
6.3.42.

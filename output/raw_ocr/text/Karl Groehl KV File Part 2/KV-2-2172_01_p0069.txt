In "Reynolds News" of 5.7.42, there was an article by H.N. BRAILSFORD, from which it was to be assumed that he really was informed as to the actual situation. Among other things, BRAILSFORD writes the following:

"And here too, was my friend Colonel Hans KAHLE, who commanded 3,080 Germans on the other side in Spain, of whom 1,080 were killed."

It would be very interesting is BRAILSFORD could give any information as to how many of the 1,080 Germans killed died on account of internal strife and how many his friend "Colonel" KAHLE had on his conscience. There were, namely, many enthusiastic warriors who wished to fight, but who would not parade before KAHLE and also who would not believe in the infallibility of the Communist and their THAELMANN. These men paid for their views with their death. They were simply called "Trotzkyists" or "Anarchists" - the Nazis call the same people "Jews" or "Communists" - they are the people who always and everywhere receive the blame. There are to-day not a few ex-soldiers from Spain who are of the opinion that Franco would have been defeated if they had fought more against Franco than among themselves. I take this opportunity of asking what part KAHLE is playing in this country. A little time ago KAHLE spoke at a public meeting of the "Free German League of Culture" in London and declared at the end of his speech "In the spirit of THAELMANN, for a free pan-Germany!" Are the Communists therefore in favour of the Nazi idea of a "pan-Germany"? But what is "the spirit of THAELMANN"? Under THAELMANN's leadership, the Communist Party in Germany fought more against "Versailles" and the "Young Plan" than against Hitler. "The German and Russian red army together at the Rhine against the Western Powers" was one of THAELMANN's futile favourite mottoes. Are these also "Colonel" KAHLE's mottoes?

There are also some other surprising things in this exhibition. There is a picture of Professor HABER who emigrated because Hitler would not have him in Germany because he is a Jew. In the last world war, HABER was an exponent of chemical warfare, a warmonger of the worst kind. How is it that this HABER is a martyr?

Further there is a picture of a certain Bodo UHSE. A former Nazi official who described in detail in a book how he took part in the murder and maltreatment of workers.

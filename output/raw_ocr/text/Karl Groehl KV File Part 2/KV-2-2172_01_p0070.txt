A large placard at the exhibition stated the following

"1932 7,500,000 Social Democrats, 6,500,000 Trades Unionists, 6,000,000 Communists and millions in the cooperatives and sports clubs."

What do these figures show? The Social Democrats and Communists are numbers taken from voting lists, the number for Trades Unionists includes also the pro-Hitler Trades Unions, "christian" and "yellow". In spite of this, 7,500,000 social democrats and 6,000,000 communists are together a forceful number who could at any given moment overthrow the Hitler government. Where are these people, who gave their votes to the Social Democrats and Communists? It must also be observed that the Communists and Social Democrats are for the most part also Trades Unionists and also members of the cooperatives. The figures therefore should not be added up.

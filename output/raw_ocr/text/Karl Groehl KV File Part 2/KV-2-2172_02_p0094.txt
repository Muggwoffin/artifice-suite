C.H.S. For: PF. 46464 Retzlau
11.8.41 Original filed in: P.F. 52095, Vol. 3, 223a.

TRANSLATION.

18, Steynings Way London, 23.7.41.
N.12.

Dear RETZLAW!

In the enclosure I am sending the financial scheme from Otto LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT (altogether 9 typewritten pages). LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT asked for the scheme back in his last letter. But as he told me in the same letter that he is now on good terms with you I have no hesitation in treating your letter as his last word.

My brother has been absent from London for a long time whilst carrying out a special job.

Is it possible to see you? All is well here.

With best greetings from all

Your
H. Br.

S. RETZLAW. Esq.,
24, Compayne Gardens,
London N.W.6.

The following is a translation of a note written on the letter by RETZLAW:

"From Heinz BRANN. This proves that the letters crossed. K."

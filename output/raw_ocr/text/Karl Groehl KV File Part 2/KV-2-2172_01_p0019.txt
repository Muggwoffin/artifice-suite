Subject: "SPARTAKUS"

Dear Roger:

Our representative in Germany has asked that we attempt to identify "SPARTAKUS" who put out a pamphlet entitled "German Communists", published by Hutchinson and Company, Ltd.

The pamphlet had a foreword by Alfred M. WALL, translated from the German by E. Fitzgerald. (The foreword was dated London, February 1944).

It is believed possible that the author of the pamphlet ("SPARTAKUS") may be Wilhelm NECKER, recently named Director of DANA, the American sponsored German news agency but we have no reliable means of determining whether or not this is correct.

It would be greatly appreciated if you could identify "SPARTAKUS" for us.

Sincerely,

Winston M. Scott
WINSTON H. SCOTT
Lt. Cmdr., U.S.N.R.

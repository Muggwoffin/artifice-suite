B.13. (Colonel Hinchley-Cooke)

I enclose herewith a copy of an intercepted letter from "S. RETZLAW" to S. SZYMONCZYK.

"RETZLAW" is, or was, in the employ of S.O.2. and in view of the contents of the intercepted letter I shall be glad if you will please pass the copy to them for their information.

It may be that the letter is bona fide and in view of the fact that "RETZLAW" may still be employed by S.O.2. I do not propose to take any further action upon it.

It is of course essential that it should not come to RETZLAW's knowledge that his letter has been intercepted.

I enclose a spare carbon copy of this note to facilitate your communication with S.O.2.

R. Pilkington.

B.4.c.
4.6.41.

6 JUN 1941

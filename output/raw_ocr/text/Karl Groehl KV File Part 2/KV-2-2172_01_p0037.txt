1716
15th July 1943.

Dear Senter,

I spoke to you this morning regarding RETZLAW and you said you would be good enough to ask Major Thornley to show RETZLAW the photograph of a man whom I think he may be able to identify. The man conceived is one BARTLING, alias Paul BERNAU, alias BRENDAN, alias KAUFMAN, alias FERBER, and I attach his photograph herewith. The man seems prior to the war to have moved in circles with which RETZLAW is familiar and I should be most grateful if RETZLAW could be asked if he recognises the man. If he fails to recognise him he could perhaps be asked whether he recognises any of the names under which the man has from time to time been known. If RETZLAW is able to identify the man I should be most grateful for any information which he could give us about him.

Yours sincerely,

E. B. STAMP

Commander John Senter R.N.V.R.,
S.O.E.

EBS/GP

30th July, 1942.

Kulturbund Exhibition "Allies inside Germany".

The Party of the German Socialist Federation (S. RETZLAW = Karl GROEHL) has published a paper for circulation among their more intimate friends severely criticising the Kulturbund exhibition "Allies inside Germany" which is taking place at the present time. It runs as follows:

"Allies inside Germany"

In London the so-called "Freie Deutsche Kulturbund", an organisation of German emigrants, has arranged an exhibition which it is intended to demonstrate the fight against the Hitler Regime which is going on inside Germany. The exhibition is accompanied by clever advertisements; as a result it has had a "good press" and has also been praised in radio transmissions to Germany. It is, however, necessary to put forward some criticism of this exhibition.

Does the exhibition wish to prove to the English public that there are organisations in Germany which fight actively against the Hitler Regime and against the war and which are led or influenced by the organisers of the exhibition? If they are trying to prove this, then the exhibition is a conscious and clumsy attempt to deceive the English public.

The title itself of "allies" is deceptive. Until the outbreak of war and after it the Allies of to-day were by no means regarded as Allies. It can be seen that the exhibited writings and pictures are merely propaganda literature which were circulated before the war and for the most part before 1938. The only exception is the German newspaper which has appeared in London since March 1941 "Zeitung", the front page of which is exhibited. Pamphlets which have been illegally circulated in Germany during the war and which fight against Hitler and the war and for the Allies, are nowhere to be seen in the exhibition.

The echo which the exhibition has received in various papers and in English intellectual circles, shows that its deception has to a certain extent been successful.

P.T.O.

CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: GROEHL Karl @ Stanislaus RETZLAW

Extract from B.24.G report re RAUSCHNIGG

..."MILLER" has informed me that RAUSCHNIGG has recently been in touch with the following people.

Karl GROEHL @ Stanislaus RETZLAW

GROEHL who is a friend of MILLER's and who is apparently now working for S.I.S. told him that RAUSCHNIGG was at present very disheartened about the situation generally and in particular that his views had so little influence with the authorities. GROEHL tried to persuade him to take a more active part as he is at present doing very little in the active line. RAUSCHNIGG has taken a room in London for use as an office, and MILLER is shortly going to get an introduction to him and I hope will be able to give us more information about his activities.

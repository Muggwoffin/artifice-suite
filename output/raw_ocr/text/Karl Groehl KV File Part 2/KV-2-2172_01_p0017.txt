4th February, 1947.

Subject: "SPARTAKUS".

In reply to your letter of the 20th January 1947 on the above subject, SPARTAKUS is not identical with Wilhelm NECKER, the director of DANA, but is in fact one Karl GRONIL @ Stanislas RETZLAW, a German born 18.2.1896 at Schnid. In 1918 he refused to do military service and was exiled from Germany. After the revolution of 1918 he was a member of the German Communist Party for some years. He emigrated from Germany in about 1933 and soon after went over to the Trotskyists and acted as their representative for Central Europe. At about the time of the Saar Plebiscite he broke with Trotsky and went to France, where he was active in anti-Nazi propaganda. He escaped from France to this country in 1940 and was here until early 1946, when he left for the French Zone of Germany. He played some part in the Free German Youth Movement in London and although it is not thought that he has been re-admitted to the Communist Party, his views are those of a revolutionary socialist. He came to our notice in 1946 by reason of his pamphlet on German communists referred to in your letter. This pamphlet was published by the Fight For Freedom organisation, which exists for the propagation of ultra Vansittart artist views on Germany and the Germans.

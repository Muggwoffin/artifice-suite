CONFIDENTIAL

From: S. Retzlau, 100, Belsize Park Gardens, London N.W.3.

To: Mr. Marcel Rollin, Unitarian Service Committee, 103 Rua Rodrigo da Fonseca, Lisbon.

31.8.41.

Written in German.

Dear Friend,

Yesterday evening, having sent you my first letter, I received yours of 17.8. Or rather, I received the letter when I called at my old flat to collect any post. I spoke to EADOL on the telephone yesterday, I want to see him this 9 JAN 1961 You know his address. He wired you at once.

I immediately forwarded the enclosed note to LEHMANN.

RAUSCHNING's address is: Dr. Hermann Rauschning, Bedford Hotel, Southampton Row, London W.C.1.

LEHMANN's address is:
Otto Lehmann-Russbueldt, 70 Crediton Hill, London N.W.6.
and 48 Melbourne Road, Leicester.

But LEHMANN lives in Leicester; he is actually here due to me and some publishing question. He lived with me for 3 weeks but moved last week. Give my new address to the Unitarian.

I saw STEED on FOERSTER's behalf. St. thinks that FOERSTER is in USA. LEHMANN does not know this, I saw STEED for Lehmann. Lehmann has been looking for FOERSTER for some time. We have no address. The Unitarian may perhaps know the address.

I have nothing to do with OTTEN. He always behaved in a disgusting manner, I don't want any dealings with him. Cowardly, egotistical and hysterical.

SIEVERS has suddenly disappeared completely from New York. No one has heard of him for months. I

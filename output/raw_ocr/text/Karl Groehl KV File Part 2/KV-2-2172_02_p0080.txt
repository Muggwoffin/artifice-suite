JS/KV/188.

11th October 1941.

Dear Langdon,

S. RETZLAW, @ Karl GROEHL.

With reference to the letter for the above which was sent to the B.B.C. for him and passed by them to the Home Office,

I confirm that we shall be grateful if you will obtain the letter and pass it to me.

Yours sincerely,

John Curtis

Major G.H. Langdon,
M.I.5,
London.

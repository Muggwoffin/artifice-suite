Source RI. No 479                                                                 Oct 19th, 1942

The League of German Revolutionary Socialists.

S. RETZLAW, alias Karl GROEHL, has changed his "German Socialist Federation" party into a "League of German Revolutionary Socialists", which bears the subtitle of "German Democratic Socialist Federation". He has also worked out a new programme for this League, according to which the new Germany is to be a federal league of socialist, democratic and republican states within the pre-Munich frontiers. This means that Austria and the Sudetenland are not regarded as German territory. There is to be no central authority. The individual German states will have the right to join any other federation. The programme demands complete abolition of the German army and police force. With regard to economic affairs the programme advocates the introduction of a socialist planned economy, with councils, public control of production and collective farms for agriculture.

The section entitled "The former German Parties" subjects these parties to ruthless criticism and claims that they must share the responsibility for the present regime and the war.

RETZLAW'S programme shows him to be sharply at variance with the German Communist Party, which he accuses of having imitated Nazi methods and institutions and dubs a nationalist party with a display of military pomp.

A copy of this programme has been sent to you.

E.5.L. 21.10.42

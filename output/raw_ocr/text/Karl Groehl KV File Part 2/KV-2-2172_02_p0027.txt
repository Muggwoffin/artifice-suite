MOST SECRET
================

Dear Neame,

In reply to your two letters of 27th February regarding S. RETZLAW of 10 Belsize Park Gardens, N.W.3, we always regard a recommendation from M.I.5 that a letter should be held or be released for onward transmission, as being in a somewhat different category to recommendations from other Departments. In the same way, we always regard M.I.5 as having the first call on any original letters. It might so happen that M.I.5 would have a particular reason for wishing an original letter to go on, although you would prefer that it should be stopped.

I am anxious that in fixing up an arrangement to deal with the correspondence of this individual we should make a plan which would be equally applicable to other similar cases in the future. I therefore suggest that in this and similar cases we should continue to send the original to M.I.5, but also send a photostat to I.S.R.B. If M.I.5 recommend release, but your Department want the letter stopped, then you should communicate with M.I.5 and in sending us your request for the letter to be stopped, indicate thereon that M.I.5 do not object. Will this suit you?

P.T.O.

SZYMONCZYK/LECOUETRE 227a.

TRANSLATION.
8.8.41.

Dear Stacho,

Please tell LABARTHE that a Lt. HUART, a man well known to me, would like to have a word with him. How can this be arranged? HUART is only free on Thursdays as he has leave on that day. Ask then whether HUART may come to see him on Thursday afternoon. It is important. Do not forget to ask.

Greetings Karl.

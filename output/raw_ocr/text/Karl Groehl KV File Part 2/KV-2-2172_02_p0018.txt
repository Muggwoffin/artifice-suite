To: Deputy Director, P. & T. C.
From: Mr. Grogan, M.I.5

Thank you for your minute of the 3rd March regarding RETZLAW. Before it arrived I had arranged to put Neame and Robson Scott in touch, and this morning they discussed the whole matter, and they have apparently agreed that any original letters which may be intercepted by Censorship can best be submitted in original to Neame with a photostat copy to M.I.5 for Robson Scott.

Apparently M.I.5 will in no circumstances wish to have any say in the disposal of the original, whereas this is of considerable importance to Neame's Organisation at the present stage. Anticipating your approval I am therefore explaining the situation to Pilkington who has incidentally in the meanwhile sent a note to Grogan on the subject, which indicates that he is not quite clear as to the disposal of these letters.

4th March, 1942.

R.D. Gibbs.

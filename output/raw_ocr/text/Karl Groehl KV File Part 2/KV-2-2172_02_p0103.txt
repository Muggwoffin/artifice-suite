11th June, 1941.

Dear Colonel,

I refer to your B.13/S.O.2. of 7.6.41. - S. RETZLAW.

RETZLAW has offered us this collection of tin soldiers, the price being, as far as I remember, £50.

As you know many people on the Continent collect soldiers of this description, in the same way as we collect stamps.

Yours sincerely,

C. Athelinc

Lieut.-Colonel Hinchley-Cooke, O.B.E.,
M.1.5.,
London.

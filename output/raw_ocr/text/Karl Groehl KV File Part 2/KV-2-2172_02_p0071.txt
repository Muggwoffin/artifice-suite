This extreme centralisation in a police, army and official-ruled state was the best pasturage for the Nazi system. This centralisation has produced a state-machine so lustful for power, that Hitler only had to remove a small percentage of civil and police officials to make it entirely subservient to his purposes.

In Germany the official ranks higher than the civilian. He is favoured by particular protection in court verdicts. Therefore officials must become civilian employees in their districts and parishes, liable to expulsion and subject to the penal code. The police can only be organised communally.

Germany must join a federation of Republican countries. Federalisation does not imply the loosening of economic co-operation with other countries, but solely the renunciation of central power. Federalisation does not lessen the duty of reparation which must borne by the whole population.

A European Federation of States which the federation of German countries could join, is only possible if every security is adopted that would make a resuscitation of German predomination impossible. Each separate country in the German Federation would be free to join any other Federation.

In this, Austria is not designated as a German country.

FOUNDATION.

Every party in Germany is responsible for the present Government of war and force. There never has been a strong democratic liberty movement in Germany. The middle-class parties of the Right followed a policy of revenge, after they had recovered from the collapse of imperialist Germany in 1918. Every middle-class party supported, openly or secretly, the illegal re-armament, and therefore, preparation for a new war. Everyone wanted re-armament. The various parties opposing Naziism never touched the kernel of the problem. Even the opposition parties desired revenge and the expansion of Germany. The National-Socialist Party is not solely responsible for the Government of today. All the former middle-class parties are embedded in the Nazi Party.

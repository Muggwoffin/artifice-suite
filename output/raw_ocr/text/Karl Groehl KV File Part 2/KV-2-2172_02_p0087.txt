CONFIDENTIAL

Translation of a letter (photographed. copy S.E. 32,676. E & W)

From:
S. RETZLAU.
10 (D) Belsize Gardens,
LONDON - N.W.(3)

Language: German (typed)

Mail: Air.

Dated: 30th August, 1941.

To:
MR. MARCEL ROLLIN,
Unitarian Service Comite (R.C.)
103, Rua Rodrigo da Fonseca 103.
LISBON,
Portugal.

Dear Friend:

I have just heard that you are in LISBON. I at once called up your brother who had also heard it to-day. LEHMANN - R told me about it, he had it from HEINE who was with me, and whom I had introduced to LEHMANN. HEINE took a lot of trouble about it.

You will, however, be surprised that we cannot do anything but it is so. Apparently only S.P.(A) bosses (B) got a visa. V! SCHIFF!! is a big man here. The Party Committee of the S.P.(A) is spreading calumnies about you, and writes of course, "whether to the Right" or not --- "as they say. But they have influence, we have not. And W. STEED is completely powerless.

FRITZ WOLFF telegraphs for money LEHMANN and I run around after at all day long. LEHMANN is intervening (C) (C) on your behalf; but we cannot get a single penny. We are all not doing particularly well. We are pleased to get bread, and that is bitter enough. It is impossible to do any real work, to have any interest in it A "ZEITUNG" is published here, stupidly reactionary, the editors were previously nazi-editors, they get what they want. SCHIFF and VOGEL etc. are also personally completely powerless, but they have their friends.

See that you get to U.S.A. if not to MEXICO. Your brother said that he, also, was trying his utmost to get you a visa for U.S.A. What exactly did WOLFF mean by the 300 dollars? What was he thinking of anyway? Not even three dollars can be raised, and then they cannot be sent. He should have telegraphed to NEW YORK, not here.

K. [illegible] is a swine; he has a good income and also private means, but he won't give me a shilling. I have cut myself off from him altogether, and do not wish to have anything more to do with him. He is hysterical. pitiful coward.

I am very pleased that you are out, at last. But believe me, it is a bitter thing to live like this, when we cannot work as we would willingly like to.

Write to me.

Many hearty greetings and all good wishes.

(Signed) STANISLAS RETZLAU.

(C) Dictionary EITZEN. 1922. gives meaning as "to use one's influence for"

(A) Note - letters may mean "SOCIALIST PARTY" - (B) 14 Japanese priests.

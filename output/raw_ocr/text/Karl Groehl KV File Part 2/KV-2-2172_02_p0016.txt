To: Mr. Pilkington, Special Examiners
From: Mr. Gibbs, M.I.5

Many thanks for your note to Grogan (at present on leave) and the letter to STEARN from RETZLAW, which is returned herewith.

There has been a good deal of confusion between Neame's Organisation and my own in regard to this correspondence, but having got entirely sick of it I put the parties together this morning and made them fight it out. The upshot is that both parties agree that the originals should in every case be submitted to Neame, whilst M.I.5 will be entirely happy with photostat copies, and will in no case wish to exercise any right of interference with the disposal of the original.

4th March, 1942.

R.D. Gibbs.

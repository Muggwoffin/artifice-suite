E.i.a.F. Captain Beaumont.

The note attached to this file contains all the information of any interest which we have about GROEHL. This information can be summed up as follows:-

GROEHL was, before 1933, an active Communist in Germany. He would appear then to have left the Communist Party for a time, but later returned and was working for the Comintern in November 1936. In 1937 he was in Paris and appears to have become a Trotskyist and a member of the so-called MUENZENBERG Group, which was a group of former Communists, in opposition to the official Communist policy. In 1938 and 1939 he was working for the so-called OTTEN Group, which had its headquarters in London. This was a group of non-Communist, anti-Nazi refugees who were engaged in obtaining information about German military matters which at first they put at the disposal of the Czechs. Later they were in touch with our Secret Service War Office and GROEHL was taken on by S.O.2. He was still working for S.O.2. in July 1941 and is probably employed by them at the present time, although we have not asked them about him recently. GROEHL was in France at the time of the German invasion, but succeeded in escaping and arrived here on the 9th October, 1940. Since that date he has been active politically, but does not seem to be in agreement with any of the existing refugee political parties. He is said to be of a quarrelsome disposition and to have fallen out with every political group with which he has been associated. He has recently been in touch with Walter LOEB, who has been carrying on a campaign against the present German Social Democratic leaders and who represents the Vansittart view of Germany. He has recently circulated a political programme for the German opposition to National Socialism which advocates a federal constitution for the future German State. He does not seem to have any followers. Although he was at one time associating with a person whom we suspected of acting as a Soviet espionage agent, we have no reason to regard him with suspicion at the present time.

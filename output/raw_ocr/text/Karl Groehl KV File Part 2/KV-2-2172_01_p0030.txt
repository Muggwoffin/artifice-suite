INTERNAL MEMORANDUM.

From E.5.L.                To F.2.B. Miss Bagot.

You may like to have this brochure on the German Communists though it cannot be considered an objective description of the subject. "Spartakus" is Stanislau RETZLAW @ Karl GROEHL, who, as you know, is an ex-member of the K.P.D. The Pamphlet is published by the Fight For Freedom organisation, which exists to propagate the ultra-Vasittartist view that all Germans are nationalists at heart and that all sections of the German people are responsible for the Nazi regime. Consequently the pamphlet very much exaggerates the nationalist side of German Communism.

Date. 13.4.44.                Signature. W.D. Robson-Scott.

NOTE. The Brochure mentioned above is pa'd in the library.

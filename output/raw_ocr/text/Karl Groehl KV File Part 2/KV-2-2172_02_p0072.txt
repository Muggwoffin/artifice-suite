The 'Christian Conservative' circles, of which Rauschning and Bruening speak, do not exist as an active opposition. On the contrary, men like STULPNAGEL and FALKENHAUSEN belong to these circles.

The workers' parties in Germany have not accomplished their task. The representatives of the Social-Democrats, NOSKE, SVERING, SOLLMANN, were the fore-runners of HIMMLER and HEIDRICH. The Social-Democrats in the first world war supported imperial Germany, aware that it was a war of aggression and conquest. They voted against disarmament after the war. They demanded re-armament. They supported every military move that the military class demanded in its lust for revenge. In May 1933 they gave Hitler sovereign power in foreign policy, thereby preparing for renewal of war.

The fatal split among the working-classes was not a split between Social-Democrats and Communist parties, but a split between political Labour parties and Trades Union movements.

The Trades Unions actively supported imperial Germany in the first world war. They put their organisations and their assets at the disposal of the war lords. For its members Trades Unionism became a union without any ideological link. It was put on a par with membership of an insurance society or a burial club. The Trades Unions had no constructive ideas. They were powerless against the ever-increasing unemployment of the masses in the pre-Hitler days. Members' subscriptions were used to erect palatial office buildings and for the salaries of Trades Union officials. One of the banks founded by Trades Unionists put money into land and factory speculations. When Hitler took over the supreme power the Trades Unions identified themselves with the Nazi system by signing the proclamation of May 1933. They signed their own death-warrant. The resultant transfer, without protest, of Trades Unions to Nazi-controlled industry involved the complete stamping out of the Trades Union idea.

Even the co-operative associations were no longer progressive organisations for the benefit of the working-class. They had become organisations for distribution without being able to retain their essential function of regulating prices. Today the organisation of co-operative associations must be overhauled.

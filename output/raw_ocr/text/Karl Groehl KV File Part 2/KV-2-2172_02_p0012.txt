9. In the course of a telephone conversation with M.I.5. on March 10th we were informed that the French Intelligence had asked them for their views on RETZLAW, as RETZLAW had attempted to get into touch with them.

It would clearly be disastrous if RETZLAW'S contacts in Switzerland, with whom we are at present working very satisfactorily, were handed over to the French Intelligence.

10. We therefore urge most strongly that the Security Service, on the strength of the above case, should have RETZLAW removed to a safe place immediately and held for the duration in such a way that he cannot possibly communicate with anyone except us.

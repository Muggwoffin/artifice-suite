First measures to be carried out include:-
(1) Planned economy. Expropriation of big industry, especially of heavy industry, banks, means of communication and transport. Expropriation of the great landed estates in the west for division among the peasants. Collective farming on planned agricultural economic lines in the north and east. Foundation of model peasant settlements. By these methods the power will be broken of the big industrialists in Rhenish Westphalia and of the Junkers east of the Elbe; also of the supporters of Pan-Germanism and the policy of conquest, and all future manufacture of arms will be rendered impossible. The profits from the newly organised economic system will be used for reconstruction in devastated lands.
(2) Complete re-organization of instruction in all schools from the national schools to the High schools. Education in the spirit of respect for the rights of men, nations and races. Attendance at school to be compulsory and without monetary consideration.
(3) Abolition of the Civil Service; abolition of the police, military and similar services however designated, that were the mainstays of the German policy of conquest.
(4) Disestablishment of the Church. Religion is a private matter for each individual. Protection for religious denominations.
(5) Comprehensive self-government for each district.
(6) Abolition of the Army; abolition of war funds, as also the liquidation of shares, the annulment of dividends and mortgages, and the cancellation of debts.
(7) Suppression of all military symbols.
(8) Freedom of conscience, liberty of speech and of the written word.
(9) Reorganization of essential law procedure on a federal basis.
(10) Reparation to all anti-Nazis who were driven out of Germany for political, racial or religious reasons, or thrown into prison or concentration camp.

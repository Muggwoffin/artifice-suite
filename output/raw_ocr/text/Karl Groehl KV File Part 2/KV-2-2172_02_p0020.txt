S.F.69/1/7/5

To: Deputy Director, P. & T. C.
From: Mr. Grogan, M.I.5

In Grogan's absence I am replying to your memorandum of the 28th February 28th regarding Stanislaus RETZLAW, and the stated requirements of Neame in regard to him.

The arrangement proposed in your draft letter to Neame seems admirable in every way, and by direct approach to the officer particularly concerned in our Organisation, I have also satisfied myself that he too considers that the proposal should work perfectly.

For the few similar cases that may arise in the future, it might be as well for the individual officer of M.I.5 to be ascertained so that his name can be notified to Neame. You may care to inform him on this occasion that the officer concerned is Mr. Robson Scott, or alternatively we will so advise him if you wish.

2nd March, 1942.

R. D. Gibbs.

GEN/FBAS

15th September, 1942.

Dear Senter,

With reference to your letter JS/KV/1252 of the 14th September, 1942. I am sorry I did not make the position as regards the RETZLAW correspondence quite clear in my letter to you of the 23rd July. I explained to Colonel Chambers the special difficulty with regard to the RETZLAW correspondence and he assured me that he would take special care that none of it would come to the eyes of any alien working in P.W.E.

Yours sincerely,

F.B. Aikin-Sneath.

Commander J. Senter,
S.O.E.
FBAS/PW.

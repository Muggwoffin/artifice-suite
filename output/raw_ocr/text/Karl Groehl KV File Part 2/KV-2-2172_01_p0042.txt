62, Drayton Gardens, London, S.W.10.
November 1st, 1942.

To the Editor of...

Dear Sir,
I beg to inform you that in Germany as well as in exile a new: "Union of German Revolutionary Socialists" (Bund deutscher revolutionärer Sozialisten) has been founded.
The initiators of this "Union" are members of the former German left-wing groups. (Communists, Socialists and left-wing sympathisers).
The aims of the "Union" are: To fight with all means in its power against Nazism, Facism, Prussian-German Militarism and Bureaucrazy;
to build up a Socialist Confederacy of self-administered German States;
to socialise big industrie and big estates; to disarm Germany completely, abolish Militarism and Bureaucracy as the backbone of German Imperialism and Capitalism, to repair war damages and to prosecute war criminals.
Enclosed you will find a copy in German of the "Union" Program.
The new "Union" believes that only a complete revision of German socialist theory and practice will create a good new German Socialist labour movement.
The Nazi Party - the S.A. - the S.S. - the German Army - whose barbarities shook the world, are no new types, but organisations which consist even of former members of Socialist working class organisations, as well as of capitalists, bourgeois and peasants.
The reason for the German proletarian attitude may be found in German history.
The Socialdemocratic Ebert-Noske Government with their Stampfer-Schiff supporters in the first year of their power 1919 put to death more people, mostly workers, than did the Hitler Government in the first year of their power. It should not be forgotten also that during the first World War the same Socialdemocratic Party denounced to the German Military and Police Authorities strikers and illegal fighters against the war effort. Therefore it is not surprising that the German Worker of to-day is not fighting the Hitler Regime when the Socialdemocratic Party Leaders in exile are supported, as the are, for example in England, by liberal minded reactionary socialists, many of whom may be found in the Labour Party.
In Germany to-day there is no such thing as a Socialdemocratic or Communist Party.
The few real fighters in Germany want to go new ways to new goals, and want to be sure that after the war their efforts will not end merely in a new Noske-Government. They want to prevent at all costs tens of thousand of workers again being slaughtered in order to save the necks of the war criminals. Even now already the small fry of Nazi criminals and some in more important positions are probing the possibilities of joining one of the left-wing parties in the event of the collapse of Nazi rule to save their skin in order to prepare future crimes.
The new "Union" is fighting without any support as did the "Spartacus Bund" in the first World War. The German Emigration in its mental sterility, cowardice and laziness has not supported the fight of the opposition within Germany. In the more than 9 years since Hitlers ascendancy to power it has not been able to analyse the political situation or face clearly the real meaning of Socialism.
This is the main cause of the passivity of all German opposition. Opponents within Germany expect their sympathisers outside Germany to lay out a program which will stimulate them in their fight against the Nazi Regime and the Nazi War.

Yours faithfully
(sign.): S. Retzlaw.
(Former Official of the "Spartacus-Bund", for 30 years in the German Socialist movement).

The Constructivist Club. Oct. 3rd, 1942.

On Thursday evening a discussion took place on the inaugural meeting of the above club last Sunday. (See my report No. 461.)
The persons who were responsible for the invitation to this meeting now form the Club's Committee. They consist of Rolf BADER, Walter ECKSTEIN, Hans JAEGGER, Otto LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT, Paul PLURA, S. RETZLAW (alias Karl GROEHL), Ernst WICHMANN.

No Chairman was elected, and the chair will be taken at club meetings by various committee members. Hans JAEGGER was elected secretary.

The next meeting of the club will take place on Oct. 17th when RETZLAW will give an address on "Reports on the present mood in Germany".

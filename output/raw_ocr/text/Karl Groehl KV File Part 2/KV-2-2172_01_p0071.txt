What therefore is the purpose of the exhibition "Allies inside Germany"? It wishes to create for Germany a favourable attitude which can latter be exploited by all the murderers who are to-day at loose in the conquered countries. There is proof that in already in Germany to-day in circles of lesser party and state functionaries there is talk of the fact that if the Nazi regime collapses is will be necessary to find refuge in the "Left" parties. They reckon on being accepted in these parties. All the murderers therefore who are to-day committing their crimes in Russia and the occupied countries will be blameless, they were never there, they had "only done their duty", they "only carried out orders" etc. etc. The attitude of most of the emigres abroad gives these rascals good reason to think thus.

It must, however, be said; inside Germany there is good underground work against Nazism and against the war! This is known is authoritative quarters! But it is just these German underground workers who, like the peoples of the occupied countries, welcome fierce and relentless attacks on Germany, they welcome every air bombardment, even if they have to suffer themselves. This underground work, however, is neither led nor influenced by self-important Germans in England of the U.S.A. The work is unfortunately still very weak, in comparison with the voting figures of the pre-Hitler period, the number of people carrying on the work is very small.

Not much is demanded of the Germans, no-one wishes to create martyrs, only this is required: the soldiers, the workers, the middleclasses, the women, the peasants should only be bad, unwilling, uninterested, lazy, unenthusiastic, unpunctual soldiers, workers, middleclasses, women, peasants etc. - and then the Nazis would not exist for more than a month longer! "All the wheels will stand still, if your strong arm should wish it". That is how it is. If the will were there it would become reality.

Work is going on and there should be still more done. But the stupid German emigration, from the "Zeitung" to the Communists, should not, by their futile behaviour, disturb the real work which is going on to destroy the Hitler regime and the German war machine.

E.7.
Translated on 11.8.42.

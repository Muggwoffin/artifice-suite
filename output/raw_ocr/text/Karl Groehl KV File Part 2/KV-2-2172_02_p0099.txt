B.7. Mr. Aikin-Sneath.

Source: MILLER. 2.7.41.

Karl GROEHL.

Robert NEUMANN informed me to-day that he had recently met GROEHL with some German friends. GROEHL was very interested in the union of German Socialists, but German Socialist circles do not accept him as he is known to have been a communist.

At present he is making much propaganda in German Socialist circles for Dr. RAUSCHNING. This has, however, only served to increase his unpopularity with the Socialists, as RAUSCHNING is definitely rejected by these circles.

B.24.g. 5.7.41.

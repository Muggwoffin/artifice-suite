Cincinnati, Ohio
August 11, 1941.

B.B.C.
London.
Emissions in German.

Dear Sirs,

I beg you to give the enclosed letter to Mr. Karl Groehl, who, I believe, has been working for some months on your staff. If for any reason you are unable to locate Mr. Groehl, I am sure the I.S. will be in contact with him and will be able to forward the letter to him.

*G RÖHL

Thanking you for this kindness, I remain,

Very truly yours,

(Sgd) Werner ZACHARIAS

Werner Zacharias
4537 Hector Ave.
Cincinnati, Ohio

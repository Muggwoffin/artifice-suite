1st March, 1942.

Karl GROEHL @ Stanislaus RETZLAW

Karl GROEHL was born 18.2.96. at Schneidemuehl. I got to know him through Stephen Heller-Huart in Paris towards the end of 1938.

At that time HELLER was working actively for the Legitimists and was in close touch with Claus DOHRN, the brother of Serge DOHRN. In this way GROEHL was one of the circle of Austrian Legitimists and, as he himself told me, was convinced that Otto von HABSBURG would succeed in returning to rule Austria.

From GROEHL I ascertained that in 1918 he had refused to do military service in Germany and was therefore exiled.

After the revolution of 1918 he was a member of the German Communist Party and was a radical Communist. For a short time he was in the police under the 'councils' government in Munich in November 1918. After three days he had to give up the job and broke away from his party friends. He went to Moscow, where he soon joined the opposition and became a Trotskyist. He then returned to Germany, worked for disarmament and was soon forced to retire to the Saar territory. There he worked with the German Communist Party against the Nazis and in 1936, after the re-occupation of the Saar, he went to Paris with Max and Dr. Heinz BRAUN.

During the time of his work in Germany, in the Saarland, he was apparently in touch with the English Intelligence service and from then onwards, with very sufficient means, he continued in his active underground work. In Paris he came to me and asked me to write leaflets for the South Germans and Austrians, which I did, simply because it was doing something against the Nazis.

He sent these leaflets to Germany and Austria - I put my couriers at his disposal.

Once, when I was visiting him at his house at rue Barreaus 7, in Paris, I remember that he warned us against dangerous explosives. A quantity of explosive would be sufficient to blow to pieces his house and those of his neighbours.

10.3.42

Report on Stanislaw RETZLAW

Address: C/O LANDES
10.d. Belsize Park Gardens,
N.W.3.

1. We were originally put into touch with this man and his group by the head of the former "D" organisation. RETZLAW was at that time living in Paris under his real name of Karl GROEHL. We visited him several times in Paris between May 1939 and the outbreak of war. From Sept. 1939 until the collapse of France he was controlled by "D" organisation in Paris, and we collaborated closely.

2. After the collapse of France RETZLAW hid for three months as a refugee having refused "D" Organisations offer of safe conduct to Bordeaux. He got himself Polish papers in the name of RETZLAW and eventually reached Portugal whence, with our help, he came to this country about 10.10.40. He is actually a German national (age about 45) although living here on Polish papers.

3. From October 1940 until August 1941 he lived more or less as a pensioner here, in receipt of a monthly salary and doing the best of his ability anything we could find for him to do. The scope of his usefulness was naturally limited.

4. In September 1941 we were able to send an organiser to Switzerland. Before leaving, this organiser was put into touch with RETZLAW who gave the names of his collaborators in that country. Since then our organiser has worked up what promises to be a very good service, using these people. These same people have a very high opinion of and a personal affection for RETZLAW, which makes it eminently desirable that we keep on good terms with him. It constitutes the only use we have for RETZLAW, but it is very important.

5. Psychologically RETZLAW is a mixture of a conceited Prima Donna and a dyspeptic misanthrope.

/he

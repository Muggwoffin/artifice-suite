S. Retzlaw

62, Drayton Gardens
London S.W.10
November 1st, 1942

Dear Sir,

I beg to inform you that in Germany as well as in exile there has been founded a new:

"Union of German Revolutionary Socialists"
(Bund Deutscher Revolutionerer Sozialisten)

The initiators of this "Union" are members of the former German left-wing groups (Communists, Socialists and left-wing sympathisers).

The aims of the "Union" are:
To fight with all means in its power against Nazism, Fascism, Prussian-German Militarism and Bureaucracy.
To build up a Socialist Confederacy of self-administered German States.
To socialise big industry and large landed property.
To disarm Germany completely, abolish Militarism and Bureaucracy as the backbone of German Imperialism and Capitalism.
To repair war damages and to prosecute war criminals.

I would like to send you at your request a full copy in German of the "Union" Program.

The new "Union" believes that only a complete revision of German socialist theory and practice will create a good new German Socialist labour movement.

The Nazi Party, the S.A., the S.S., the German Army, whose barbarities shook the world, are not new types, but organisations which even consist of former members of Socialist working class organisations as well as of capitalists, bourgeois and peasants.

The reason for the attitude of the German proletariat may be found in German history.

The Socialdemocratic Ebert-Noske Government with their Stampfer-Schiff supporters put to death more people, mostly workers, in 1919 the first year of their power, than did the Hitler Government in the first year of its power. Neither should it be forgotten that during the first World War the same Socialdemocratic Party denounced strikers and illegal fighters against the war effort to the German Military and Police Authorities.

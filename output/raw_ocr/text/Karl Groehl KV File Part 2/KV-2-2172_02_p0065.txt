Intercepted letter from F.S. Hess, 10E, Belsize Park Gardens, N.W.3. to Walter LOEB Esq. enclosing a letter to the Daily Telegraph from S. Retzlau, dated 10.1.42. No ref.

Precis.

To the editor of the Daily Telegraph. LONDON.

RETZLAW asks to make some remarks on an article by Herr Albert about Austria published in the Daily Telegraph of Jan. 9th. He says that the article is a model example of how the Austrian refugees have become prisoners of their own propaganda. He suggests that, contrary to the gist of the article, Austria before the Nazi occupation was even more Nazi-minded than Germany herself. He gives various examples of why Austria should not be allowed to evade retributions after the war and considers it important that Austrian refugees should work for an independent Austria. At present they do not appear to be taking much action.

The "Cut Loose from Berlin" Movement.

It appears that recently a so-called "Cut Loose from Berlin" movement has started in Germany and has already produced reactions among the political emigrants over here. The purpose of this movement is to provoke national-revolutionary movements in Germany and especially in those parts of the country which are hostile to Berlin, since there are no signs of the possibility of a social revolution in Germany at present. In my reports 410h. and 434.7b. I have already mentioned signs of this movement, which at present lacks definite organisation although it is actively at work.

Recently an article entitled "Away from Berlin" appeared in the publication known as "European Correspondents, Ltd.", which is issued by the Swiss journalist, Eugene Lennhoff, and the Czech Journalist, Walter Tschuppik, and serves the interests of a number of Swiss and American papers. I enclose this article which, so far as I can ascertain, is from the pen of Otto Lehmann-Russbuehldt, to whom I have written in the hope that he will confirm his authorship. The German territories stated to be in sympathy with this movement are Bavaria, Baden, the Free City of Hamburg in particular, also, to some extent Hanover, Hessen, Holstein and the Rhineland. According to this article, the character of this movement appears to be one which would liberate ancient anti-militarist instincts.

DRAFT OUTLINE for a PROGRAMME.

I.
The present German Government is the rule of force, of oppression, of rapine, of lawlessness, of injustice. This Government must be completely overthrown.
It is the duty of every German, at home and abroad, who opposes this Government to:-(1) join actively in its defeat. (2) To assist in building a new German order.

II. The aim of the Opposition is to found a new German political, economic, federal order on a Socialist basis.
The conceptions - 'Deutsches Reich' and 'Grossdeutschland' are bound up for life and death with the Government of today. They must disappear with the Government.
A federal league must be created by the German countries and the national life of each one must be recognised. The duty of the German Federal League in foreign affairs is complete reparation for all damage, complete expiation of all wrongs. All other claims must give place to this first duty.
The German Federal League stakes no priority claim on the restoration of former frontiers.

III.
The immediate task of German Opposition is to take part in the war against the Germany of today and against German organizations all over the world. Activities in which every German can take part are provided by the contingencies of the situation.
(a) Spreading news from Germany re facts of importance that concern the war; the political, the military, the economic situation. News about the attitude of the people, about symptoms, about Germans living abroad, about foreign German organizations of all sorts.
(b) Propaganda in Germany, in German settlements, among prisoners of war.
(c) Sabotage in Germany and in German organizations abroad.
(d) Essential preparations to join troops fighting against Germany. Where possible to form a German legion.

IV.
In Germany there are fewer opposers of the Government of today than there were in the World War of 1914/18. The main cause for this is the failure to provide a common idea for the Opposition. Therefore it is essential to strive wholeheartedly to organize German anti-Nazis. A programme must be drawn up presenting a basis on which a new life in Germany can be built.

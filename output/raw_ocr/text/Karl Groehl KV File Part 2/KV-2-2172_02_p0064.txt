Karl GROEHL @ Stanislas RETZLAW

This man first came to my notice at the time in 1938 when he was in contact with Karl OTTEN whom he supplied from Paris with military information from Germany. This information was required partly for the London office of the Czechoslovak Intelligence service and partly for an anti-Hitler book which OTTEN and LEHMANN-RUBSBUELDT were writing.

OTTEN spoke of GROEHL as a former Communist of some repute who had gone over to Trotsky and a most efficient worker against Hitler. On the other hand my contact H.J. WOLFFSCHN, who knew him, thought him rather a comically melodramatic type of revolutionary whose methods were too obvious to be effective. In this respect it is interesting to note that GROEHL is mentioned in H.J. KOEHLER's "Inside the Gestapo". KOEHLER met him in the Saar and succeeded in worming himself into his confidence. He describes GROEHL as an intelligent man whose outlook had been warped by the deafness which limited his activities considerably. OTTEN also mentioned this deafness to me as one of his handicaps.

GROEHL continued his anti-German activities in Paris until the outbreak of war when he was interned in the general internment of enemy aliens in France. OTTEN told me that he was able to induce "certain British authorities" to procure his release and allow him to come to this country, since which time I have heard nothing further of him beyond an occasional hint from OTTEN that he was working for us until asked me whether I knew anything about S. RETZLAW.

From all available evidence, I should say there is no doubt of the sincerity of GROEHL's anti-Nazi views and although as a Trotskyist he must be against all that the British Empire stands for, there seems no reason to doubt the loyalty of any cooperation he may have given. On the other hand it seems to me equally probable that his mannerisms and deafness must handicap any confidential work he undertakes.

E.3.(S)
14.1.42.

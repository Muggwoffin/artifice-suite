The "Bund Deutscher Revolutionärer Sozialisten."

According to the "Programme" given me by S. RETZLAW, the full title of this organisation is the "Bund Deutscher Revolutionärer Sozialisten der Deutschen Demokratischen Sozialistischen Föderation."
The Bund proclaims itself unreservedly on the side of the Allies and calls upon all anti-Nazi Germans, inside or outside Germany, to use their utmost efforts to encompass the defeat of Nazism.
The Bund's programme envisages a federation of Socialist republics within the pre-Munich German frontiers and stresses the obligation on this Federation to comply with all allied demands for reparations and punishments. The Federation will not be eligible to join a wider European Federation until all such demands have been met.
Jews are to have equal rights with Aryans in the German Federation. The Bund stands for a planned economy, with control of transport, banking and heavy industries in the hands or state or municipal undertakings. All shares, mortgages, etc. to be cancelled. Large landed estates to be divided up among the peasantry. Collective farming to be introduced.
The Bund calls for the abolition of the German national army, police force and civil service, the destruction of all war memorials and other symbols of militarism, the election and removeability of judges, disestablishment of the church, freedom of the press and religion, free secret ballot for single-chamber Parliaments for all federal states and the use of the referendum to settle controversial questions. Compensation to all persons who have suffered under the Nazi regime.
The explanations which form an appendix to this programme condemn the SOPADE for having supported militarism in both imperial and Weimar Germany, but believe that the spirit of socialism still prevails among the German masses. Socialism is the only means by which they can secure permanent peace and assured employment.

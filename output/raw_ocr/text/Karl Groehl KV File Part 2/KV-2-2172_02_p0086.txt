OTTEN lives: Grove End Court,
Abbey Road,
London N.W. 8.

Please ask the Unitarian whether they have Erich WOLLENBERG's address. He is in prison in Casablanca. Everything possible should be done for him. Please write to him in my name (a letter from England would be prejudicial to him!) saying that I have written to various quarters on his behalf but it all takes a very long time. PAETEL is doing everything possible for him in New York. Give the address of the Unitarian with my name and his letter can be forwarded to me by the Unitarian.

Listen, I wrote to Agen telling you to draw out the travelling money in Toulouse where there was money to cover your journey to Lisbon and that of your wife. Did this letter never reach you? Please write and tell me at once whether it was given to you. It belonged to my daughter but first it was to help you to get away. But do not write there, this might be most harmful.

Affectionate wishes and greetings to you and your wife

(sgd.) S. Retzlaw.

IM. 6.9.41.

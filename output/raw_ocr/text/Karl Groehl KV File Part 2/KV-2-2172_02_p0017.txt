B.3d.                                                                 4th March, 1942.

Dear Neame,

Following on your talk with Robson Scott this morning, I am writing to confirm that Pilkington has been asked to arrange in future that all originals shall be submitted to you, and that you alone shall exercise the decision as to their disposal.

My Organisation will be perfectly happy to receive a photostat copy, and as Robson Scott has told you do not propose to have any say in the disposal of the original.

Yours sincerely,

R.D. Gibbs.

Major Neame,
S.O.S.
RDG/PI
Pend.

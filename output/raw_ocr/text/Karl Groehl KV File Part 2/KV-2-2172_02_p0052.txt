SOURCE: 19.1.42.

S. (Stanislaus) RETZLAW, 24, Compayne Gardens, N.W.6. is really called Karl GROEHL.

During the last war, in collaboration with Willi MUENZENBERG, he founded "Freie Jugend" which was founded in opposition to the Arbeiter Jugend. He did anti-war work in South Germany, just as the Spartakusbund did later on. The German military authorities arrested him about 1917 or the beginning of 1918. The Revolution brought him his freedom; and in the Munich Raete Republic he was police president for 10 days and Minister (I think of the Interior) for one or two days.

Shortly after this he disappeared from public life. He then became the head of some kind of "camouflaged" bureau, like those which MUENZENBERG founded in large quantities. Until 1933 he was a lot in Moscow. Shortly before Hitler's seizure of power, he had returned to Germany, accidentally fell in with the Reichsbanner people - with whom however he had nothing to do - during the Ulap assault case in Berlin. He was freed through the intervention of an official of the Communist International who had an American passport, and transferred to the Police Hospital in the Scharnhorst Strasse.

Then he went to Moscow again. There he broke privately with the Communist Party, without giving this out publically. He returned voluntarily to Germany in about 1935 to do illegal work, and later went to Paris. First of all the KP thought he was working for them, although from the beginning he had been working on his own account, about the same outlook as MUENZENBERG. This was later make public together with his break with the K.P.

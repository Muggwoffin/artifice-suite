The RETZLAW Affair

The affair is now clear. There are two RETZLAV's, one is connected with the Sopade, was formerly a Communist. He is hardly active politically at all, is an extremely decent and loyal political refugee.

The other RETZLAV (initial S.) is the one who gave the document to Dr. OP(POCENSKI - official of the Ripka-Benes Secretariat)?. He was formerly a Communist and in fact during the "Raete" time (temporary Communist Republic in Munich in 1919-20?) he held a high police job in Munich. He is deaf. He left the Communist Party because of fundamental differences. I have been assured from many sides that he is absolutely reliable too, in his attitude towards England.

E.3.(S)
22.1.42.

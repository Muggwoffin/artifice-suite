With HELLER, GROEHL and myself established a chemical product in Paris at 48 rue Miromesnil, which was not a beauty product but it served for underground work. I got Fritz HOCKINGER, the former 'Handelsminister' (Minister of Trade) to join us in this enterprise and introduced him to GROEHL. On account of HELLER's laziness and fecklessness this scheme for underground work came to nothing. GROEHL then intrigued with Minister STOCKINGER and HELLER against me, when I parted from HELLER on account of his indiscretion and refusal to cooperate. In the latter days in Paris, I gained an unfavourable impression of GROEHL. He was unreliable, mixed with a number of women and I gave him up as unreliable.

I met him by chance at the end of 1940 in Notting Hill Gate.

He remained in touch with me for quite a long time and then I had to give him up once more on account of his unreliability. I also saw him once with Dr. Demuth. I have heard that GROEHL belongs to LOEB's circle.

At one time GROEHL showed me reports from Germany, but I must say that I always had the feeling that at any rate the larger part of this news was forged abroad. The news he had of Austria was definitely quite false and without foundation.

GROEHL maintained that I was only released from Pentonville Prison because he had written to the British authorities offering security on my behalf.

I regard this statement as I regard all GROEHL's utterances. He is an eccentric and also unreliable.

At the beginning of 1941 he told me that he would like to imprison any person who in the slightest way expressed themselves against England. After the Russian offensive he changed his view. I noticed how he definitely became more Communist again, was very enthusiastic about Russia, frequently advised me to join the Austrian Centre and the Austrian Communists and to come to some agreement with him. Austria would become a Soviet State. Latterly he has sent a programme to all the Allied governments in London for the future of Germany. Dr. DETKA of the Czech Foreign Office showed me this. From the style of the writing I know that this did not originate from GROEHL.

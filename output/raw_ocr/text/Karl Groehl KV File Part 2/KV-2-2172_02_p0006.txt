25th March, 1942.

Dear Pilcher,

I understand that you very kindly arranged with Thornley for the interrogation of RETZLAW and that he left with you some of our papers. I shall be grateful if you will return these direct to me.

Yours sincerely,

John Lester

G. St.C. Pilcher, Esq., K.C., M.I.5., London.

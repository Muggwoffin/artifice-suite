P.F.46464/B.1a/GPW

14th October, 1946.

Dear Philby,

Karl GROENIL & Stanislas RETZLAW.

It has been reported to us by a source in this country that the above-named German has arrived in Saarbruecken, where he intends to remain.

He was born 18.2.1896, in Schneidemehsell. In 1918 he refused to do military service and was exiled from Germany. After the revolution of 1918 he was a member of the German Communist Party. Later he went to Moscow, where he became a Trotskyist, subsequently returning to Germany. In 1936 he went to Paris, where he wrote anti-Nazi leaflets.

He escaped to this country from France in 1940 and lived here until August 1941, more or less as a pensioner. It was at this time that he was in the employment of S.O.E. and came under their displeasure owing to his indiscretion in connection with contacts in Switzerland. This lapse took place in 1942. Apparently at one time RETZLAW had said that he was in the British Intelligence Service and controlled connections in Germany and Austria.

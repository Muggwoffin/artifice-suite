For some time after the war Socialism remained the hope of the masses. Hitler linked up Socialist issues with his own aims. This was only possible because the Social-Democrats had confused the issues.
German Social-Democracy, under the leadership of EBERT-NOSKE & STAMPFER in 1914/18 actively supported the policy of Imperial Germany. The massacres among the German working classes which they caused are responsible for the rise of Hitler, the dictatorship of the military party, the big industrialists and the Junkers.
The conception of Socialism must be explained afresh. The battle-cry 'Socialism or Collapse into Barbarism' must be heard again.
Socialism is a politico-economic system which guarantees a reasonable livelihood to all branches of human society and an existence in which every member can work according to his capacities and share in all economic, cultural and technical progress.
Socialism is based on an organised production and consumption of goods which will remove the fear of starvation, guarantee liberty to each individual and peace to the community.
Socialism is the rule of peace, of labour, of justice.
Socialism stands for mutual help among men against misery, hunger and injustice.
Socialism stands for material and cultural progress.
Socialism stands for the abolition of war and of militarism.
Socialism is international. Equal rights for all nations and all peoples.
Socialism is tolerance.
Socialism is outlawry of murder and of exploitation of human beings.
Socialism eliminates hatred between nations, races, classes and dictatorships.
Socialism is rule of the people, for the people, through the people.
In the beginning Socialism was the creed of the progressive element among the oppressed working-classes. Today Socialism cannot be limited to one class, one people or one nation. Today Socialism must be promoted by all classes, all trades and all professions in the towns and villages of every nation.
Socialism is a world view, a religion without a church. Foreign countries, formerly unfavourably disposed to a Socialist liberty movement in Germany, must have been convinced by the deeds committed under Hitler's system

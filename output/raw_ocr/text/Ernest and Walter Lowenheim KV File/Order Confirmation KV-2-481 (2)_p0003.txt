The future of the Company is at present uncertain, but it seems probable that even if it were wound up, the brothers, as expert engineers, would obtain employment of a similar nature.

Enquiries have been made regarding the code found among the LOEMELHEIM papers, and mentioned in the third paragraph of Dinney's letter of 29.5.41. The brothers became secretive and evasive when questioned upon it, but maintained that the code was used in the underground activities of their "Neubeginnen" Movement. We think that this is probably true. Their secrecy and evasion can, in the circumstances, easily be understood.

The further evidence that we have now obtained confirms us in our view that the release of the brothers LOEMELHEIM on any pretence whatever is in the last degree undesirable.

As this is a case of some interest and importance, we should be very grateful if you would be good enough to let us know your decision.

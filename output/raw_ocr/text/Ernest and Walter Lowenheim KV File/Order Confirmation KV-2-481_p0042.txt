Re KARTHAEUSER, I hope the following will help to clarify the position. When KARTHAEUSER mentioned his friendship with SCHOETTLE and KLATT we were actually not talking of the present. KARTHAEUSER was telling me that he could probably have obtained his release earlier as an engineer, but that he made a point of being released as an anti-Nazi under Cat. 19. It was in this connection that he mentioned SCHOETTLE and KLATT as persons who knew him from his work as a social democrat in Germany and therefore testified in his favour. Since KARTHAEUSER did not indicate to me that later on a friction between himself and the other two had arisen, he left me under the impression that they were still his friends, though I do not believe for one moment that he was deliberately trying to mislead me.

Concerning your specific questions, I can give you the following details. KARTHAEUSER told me that before his emigration to this country he worked with one of the LOEVENHEIMS politically in Germany (presumably as a Social Democrat). The only motive for associating himself with LOEVENHEIM in business in London was according to KARTHAEUSER that such association constituted a ground for the Home Office to allow LOEVENHEIM to immigrate. The plan had been at the time - and was carried out consequently - that KARTHAEUSER and LOEVENHEIM applied to the Home Office to be allowed to establish themselves jointly in business, that KARTHAEUSER should be in charge of the technical (engineering) side and that LOEVENHEIM should look after the financial and commercial side. This proposition had been a frame-up in as far as LOEVENHEIM had never been in business before and as KARTHAEUSER was in fact running the whole show by himself. KARTHAEUSER further told me, that LOEVENHEIM had practically never been of any use to the business, but that American friends of LOEVENHEIM's had provided some funds and were now holding 10% of the shares the remainder of which were equally divided between KARTHAEUSER and LOEVENHEIM.

You can see from the above that you were perfectly right in assuming that LOEVENHEIM was picking KARTHAEUSER'S brains and KARTHAEUSER consulted my firm chiefly to discuss which steps he could take in order to free himself from LOEVENHEIM. I am expecting various documents from KARTHAEUSER in the course of the next few days and I shall then be in a better position to give you more details.

June 21st, 1941.

Dear Pilkington,

Reference your PF.49542/B.4.c. of June 14th,

I am returning you the photograph of Walter LOEWEINHEIM as Hans JAEGER is no longer in the Isle of Man.

He was released on February 27th to 39, Kensington Park Gardens, W.11, and is now working for Sykes. Captain Johnson states he was once before shown a photograph supposed to be of the man at Prague, but failed to identify it as such.

Yours sincerely,

Valentine Amittagh

I would like to preface my observations on these cases by pointing out that their real interest is the supposition that they were and are Communists and that they were and are connected with Soviet espionage. There is no evidence whatsoever, and I cannot find from a perusal of the file that it has ever been suggested, that they were at any time in their careers anything but opposed to Nazism and everything it stood for. From the Office point of view, therefore, the cases are of interest to F.2 (Communism), F.2.c. (Soviet Espionage) and B.4.a. (Espionage through Pines) and they have at various times been considered, certainly from the last two angles if not from the first. Actually the report to the First Committee was compiled by E.5, but I understand that this was done in very close co-operation with F.2.c. who was in my opinion far better qualified to handle the case.

There appear to be four main denunciations of these German-Jewish brothers. With two exceptions these denunciations are extremely vague as to dates and unfortunately this vagueness has been allowed to persist in the reports which we have sent in to the First Committee. It has resulted in one instance in Sir Cecil Burst attributing our statement that Ernst LOEVENHEIM was employed in the Russian firm RUSSEPHOTO to a period after he came to England in 1936 and, as Sir Cecil points out, it can be shown by reference to the Home Office files that Ernst LOEVENHEIM was never employed in this capacity in this country - indeed it appears clear that RUSSEPHOTO never existed in this country and I am quite satisfied after reading the files that Ernst's connection with RUSSEPHOTO occurred in Berlin and dated back to the time before he left the Communist Party which, according to his own statement was in 1928 and is nowhere suggested to have been later than 1930.

It is common ground and admitted by the LOEVENHEIM's themselves that they were both members of the Communist Party in Germany for several years up to the late 1920's. Walther claims to have parted company with the Party in 1927, his brother in 1928. One of our informants puts the date as late as 1930, but he is clearly speaking from recollection only and I do not think there is any reason to suppose that the LOEVENHEIM's are not at all events trying to tell the truth on this subject. Three of our informants allege that up to the time of their severance of connection with the Communist Party both brothers were "well-known" in Berlin to be agents for the GPU specializing in military intelligence. Ernst was certainly employed at about this period by RUSSEPHOTO Limited and this firm is supposed, and I have no doubt with some reason, to have added espionage to its other activities. This charge, however, might lie at the door of almost any person who has ever been employed by a Russian-controlled establishment, unless it was made by an expert. It is not clear to me whether or not our informants are experts.

It is beyond doubt that not later than 1930 both brothers openly severed their connection with the Communist Party. It is beyond doubt that very soon afterwards they became members of the Social Democratic Party in Germany and were co-founders of the REINHARDT Movement. I think there is no doubt that they carried on this Movement in considerable danger from the Gestapo and it appears to be accepted by everyone who has studied this case that a code which was found among their possessions after their internment related to the secret activities of the REINHARDT Group in Germany and not to any form of Soviet espionage. The brothers left Germany in 1935, as they say because it became too hot to hold them, and they were forced to flee from the Gestapo. After something less than a year spent in Prague they appear to have left the REINHARDT movement in other hands and came to this country where they set up in business in a Company known as the TILADE REINHARDT COMPANY. This Company specializes in a patent for improving boilers and consequently not unnaturally has extensive connections being places where boilers are commonly used, such as power stations, oil and like. There is in my opinion, apart from one allegation to which I shall refer in a moment, no title of evidence that since their arrival in this country either of the brothers has taken any part in Communist affairs, espionage or political activities of any sort.

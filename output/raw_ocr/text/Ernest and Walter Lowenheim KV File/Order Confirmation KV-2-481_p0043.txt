KARTHAEUSER'S plans for the future appear to be these. He intends to work as an engineer for the firm of Hitchins, Jervis and Partners of 3, Central Buildings, S.W.1. I have met Colonel Lockhart Jervis, one of the partners, some months ago when he told that his firm was in close contact with the Ministry of Supply who had entrusted them with the planning and distribution of manufacture of most important armaments. Jervis' firm has apparently taken over KARTHAEUSER'S and LOEVENHEIM'S firm while these two were interned. Certain legal questions will have to be solved in this connection and this represents one of KARTHAEUSER'S chief difficulties, as LOEVENHEIM is trying to prevent KARTHAEUSER from coming to terms with Jervis.

I shall send you further information re the LOEVENHEIMS, RIESS, HACKLAENDER, etc., in due course.

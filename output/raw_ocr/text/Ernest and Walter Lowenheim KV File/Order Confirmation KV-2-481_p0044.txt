It may be of interest to you that KARTHAEUSER who was released from the married camp this November has become my client today. I remember that AMOS was asked at Onchan to report about KARTHAEUSER and from my conversation with KARTHAEUSER I take it that it may be useful to cultivate his acquaintance. KARTHAEUSER is associated in business with one of the LOEWENHEIM brothers, he also professes to be a friend of SCHOETTLE'S and Werner KLATT'S about whom you wanted some information last summer. RIESS and HACKLAENDER are further names KARTHAEUSER mentioned; both are at the married camp, and I understand from KARTHAEUSER that RIESS was until 1939 a Storm Troop man or leader and that HACKLAENDER is a Nazi-sympathiser.

E.3.(s)
22.12.41.

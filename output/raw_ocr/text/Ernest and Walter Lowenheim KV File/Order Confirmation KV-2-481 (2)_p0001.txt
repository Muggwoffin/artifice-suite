OBSERVATIONS.

Please see our report dated 31.1.41. and correspondence ending with Binney's letter to Sir Cecil Hurst dated 29.5.41. on the above subject. We are treating the cases as one as they are so closely connected.

We have now been able to complete our enquiries, and have obtained information regarding the LOEVENHEIM brothers from three main and separate sources.

The first, whose reliability we have no reason to doubt, says that Walter was at one time "undoubtedly a G.P.U. man". He adds "once a G.P.U. man, always a G.P.U. man". Walter was a noted member of the Communist Party in Germany, and had his particular alias. This source says that Walter ran an illegal Communist group in Berlin, that he is an expert on military matters, and that his London house contained many works on strategy by military experts, and military journals, all of which were presumably destroyed when the building was bombed. The source says that Ernst was employed by RUSSPHOTO, a Russian organisation devoted nominally to photography, but used for military espionage.

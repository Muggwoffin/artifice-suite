HOME OFFICE,
INTERNED ENEMY ALIENS TRIBUNAL,
(Germans & Austrians),
ROYAL COURTS OF JUSTICE,
STRAND,
LONDON, W.C.2.

11th June 1941.

Dear Binney,

Sir Cecil Hurst has asked me to thank you for your letter of the 29th May about the Loewenheimis, and to apologise for the delay in doing so.

In view of the last paragraph in your letter Sir Cecil has arranged that no decision shall be taken until your enquiries are completed.

Yours sincerely,

Doug & Jowc

C. Binney, Esq.,

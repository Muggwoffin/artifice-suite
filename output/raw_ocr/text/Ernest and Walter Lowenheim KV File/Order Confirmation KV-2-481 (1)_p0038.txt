E.3. (Mr. Sykes)

With further reference to my note of 24.8.41 respecting the LOEVENHEIM brothers, I have now heard that the Hurst Committee are not disposed to accept our recommendations respecting the LOEVENHEIM brothers and I shall be glad, therefore, if you will please arrange to obtain from Mrs. any further information that you can respecting the activities of the LOEVENHEIM brothers in Germany.

In particular I should like to know whether Mrs. has any knowledge of the LOEVENHEIM brothers being interested in military information or whether she is able in any way to confirm the statement that they were engaged in collecting information for the U.S.S.R.

In connection with the LOEVENHEIMS we have been given the name of a woman referred to as "Vicky" and I should be interested to know whether Mrs. is able to give her full name and present address.

F.2.c. 26.8.41.

R. Pilkington.

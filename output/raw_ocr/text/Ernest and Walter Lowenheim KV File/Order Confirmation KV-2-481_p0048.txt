HOME OFFICE,
B-3 Division,
The Hydro,
Durley Gardens,
Bournemouth
13th December, 1941.

Dear Renton,

I am forwarding under separate covers two files containing chiefly newspaper cuttings which are the property of Walter Loewenheim who was released from internment in Hutchinson Camp on 31st October.

The Commandant of the Camp states that the files concerned were impounded as being unsuitable for inclusion in the effects this man was allowed to take with him upon release, and hence I send them to you. If you see no objection would you kindly return them to me for transmission to Loewenheim, who has applied for them as they provide essential material for an anti-Nazi book he has been preparing while in internment.

Yours sincerely,

W. J. Paddon

R. K. D. Renton, Esq.,
Box 500,
Parliament Street B.O.,
London, S.W. 1.

HOME OFFICE,
INTERNED ENEMY ALIENS TRIBUNAL,
(Germans & Austrians),
ROYAL COURTS OF JUSTICE,
STRAND,
LONDON, W.C.2.

August 16th, 1941.

My dear Mitchell,

You may like to see the enclosed batch of reports in the Loewenheim group of cases where we have not felt able to adopt the views of M.I.5.. Nor have we been able to do so in the case of Kurt Regner.

I am now off for a holiday and am not coming back until Tuesday, the 2nd of September.

Very sincerely yrs
Civil MB Hurst

G. R. Mitchell, Esq.,
Box 500,
G.P.C.,
OXFORD.

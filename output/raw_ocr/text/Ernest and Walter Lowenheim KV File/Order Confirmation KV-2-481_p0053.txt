H.l.a. Mr Robson-Scott.

Note for Regional Officer on Released Alien.

P.C. 49542. 13.12.41

Our ref: P.F. 49542.

Name. Walter LOEWENHEIM
Born 1896.
Address, 93, Hornsey Lane,
London N.6.

Region. 5

N.B. Also please see report on LOEWENHEIM Ernst.

After serving in the German army in the War of 1914-18 this alien came under the influence of the Radical Movement of that time and joined the Free Socialist Youth. Shortly afterwards he joined the German Communist Party. According to his own account, he was expelled from it in 1927 and became a member of the German Social Democratic Party. It was about this time that he was associated with the foundation of the Neubeginnen movement in Germany and claims that he and his brother Ernst carried on an active underground struggle against the Nazis for some years and were ultimately obliged to leave Germany in 1935. He came to this country in 1936, his intention being to participate in the exploitation of a patent belonging to Bruno KARTHAUSER relating to dampers for flues.

Our information gathered from various sources cast some doubt on the fact that LOEWENHEIM was expelled from the Party. He was a noted member of the Party in Germany and is believed to have been engaged in espionage in that country for the Russians. He is an expert on military matters and his London house contained many works of strategy by military experts and military journals all of which were presumably destroyed when the building was bombed.

Another source says that Walter LOEWENHEIM was a G.P.U. man and that both brothers paid frequent visits.

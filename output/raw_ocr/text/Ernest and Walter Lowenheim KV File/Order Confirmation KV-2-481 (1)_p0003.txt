He says that he believes that Walter LOEWENHEIM did not leave the Communist Party until 1933, when he joined the Social Democrat Party.

He says that a few years after joining the Youth Movement of the Communist Party, Walter LOEWENHEIM became a member of the Executive of the Movement and in that capacity paid a number of visits to Moscow one of which, as mentioned above, took place in 1929.

He asserts that Walter LOEWENHEIM's membership of the Executive of the Youth Movement automatically resulted in his being an Ogpu agent as every member of the Executive had to work for the G.P.U.

It was, he says, commonly known to C.P. members in Berlin between 1925 and 1933 that Walter was acting as an Ogpu agent.

He remembers that in 1930 or 1931 Walter LOEWENHEIM was very closely associated with the Rote Front Bund (R.F.B.), the slogan of which was "Together with the Red Army on the Rhine against France and England".

In his report at 65a states that Walter LOEWENHEIM "ran an illegal Communist group in Berlin". He explains that he was there referring to Walter LOEWENHEIM's membership of the Neu Beginnen movement in Berlin which was, he says, composed of Communists, members of the S.A.P. and some Social Democrats.

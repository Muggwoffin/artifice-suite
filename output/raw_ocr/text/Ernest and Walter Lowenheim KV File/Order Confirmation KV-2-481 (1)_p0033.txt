INTERNAL MEMORANDUM.

From F.3 (Mr. Aikin-Sneath)
To F.2c (Mr. Pilkington)

I was interested to see this and should be grateful if you would have it p.a.'d in the "Neubeginnin" file. There is incidentally one statement made by LOEWENHEIM which cannot be true.

In para. (5) he says that members of "Neubeginnin" were brought not before the People's Court, which dealt only with Communists, but before the "Socialists" Court. This is not possible. All offences of a political character, except the most trivial (which were dealt with by the ordinary courts) came before the People's Court. There was no distinction made between Communists and Socialists in political cases.

Date 1.9.41
Signature F.B. Aikin-Sneath.

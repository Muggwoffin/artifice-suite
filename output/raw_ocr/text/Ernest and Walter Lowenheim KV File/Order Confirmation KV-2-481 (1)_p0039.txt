Dear Sir Cecil,

LOEWEINHEIM, Walther,
LOEWEINHEIM, Ernst,

Many thanks for your letter dated 16.8.41. on the above cases.

There appears to be an honest difference of opinion here. We feel that we must adhere to ours, while you, no doubt, are equally confirmed in yours.

In the circumstances, I hope you will not object to our approaching the Home Office in the matter. I send you a copy of our memorandum on the subject.

Yours sincerely,

G.R. Mitchell.

Sir Cecil Hurst, G.C.M.G.,
Chairman of Interned Enemy Aliens Tribunal,
Royal Courts of Justice,
London, W.C.2.

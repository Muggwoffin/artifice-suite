SOURCE: 15.9.41.

"I was not very lucky during the last fortnight. I had still not chance to come in touch with Mrs. POLGAR but I doubt that she can answer your question as per your letter of 28th ultimo because she was L's secretary only while in Berlin. However, I hope to see Mrs. POLGAR soon.

But I met yesterday again Traute LOEWEINHEIM, who was very optimistic re a quick release of her relatives within a fortnight or so, as according to her, "L's files have been returned by the Military Intelligence Service to the Home Office". Therefrom she got this information I do not know but she mentioned that the Military I.S. was in possession of the papers for half a year and as she knew that her people have never done anything wrong she is now certain that they will be released.

"Hella (Walter's wife) applied many months ago for U.S.S.R. citizenship as she was born in a part of Poland being annexed by the U.S.S.R. in October 1939, whether the Citizenship was granted or refused I don't know."

E.3.(S)
24.9.41.

My dear Pilkington,

Having recently received from Major Armitage a copy of the Loewenheim Code, together with several other documents pertaining thereto, I think I might perhaps be able to make a contribution of some slight use, which I enclose.

I may add that some considerable time ago I made a report on activities. I cannot be sure whether this has been embedded in the Loewenheim file or not, but early this year I got a repetition of its contents from [illegible]. I sent a copy of this to Onchan Camp where the Loewenheims are interned. I do not know whether another copy reached the country, but if not and if you are interested, I expect we have a copy in our office.

Yours sincerely,

Claud. W. Sykes

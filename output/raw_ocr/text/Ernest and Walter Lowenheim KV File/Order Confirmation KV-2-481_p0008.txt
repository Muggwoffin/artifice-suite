A.D.F.2. (Mr. Hollis).

As spoken, I to-day discussed these cases with Lord Swinton who took the view that the most salient feature of the cases was the allegation that since the beginning of the present war Walther LOEVENHEIM had achieved contact with the Soviet Intelligence in Holland. The story appears first in 31a and is repeated on page 2 of 50a. Lord Swinton felt very strongly that the grounds for this extremely specific allegation should be discovered and the author of the story carefully cross-examined as to his means of knowledge and its truth. Lord Swinton is seeing Mr. Peake tonight and intends to tell him that he proposes to have this point cleared up and to with-hold a final decision in the case until it has been done. Will you kindly arrange accordingly, as agreed?

D.D.E.

"I shall see as suggested.
It is known to me from the camp that they (the LOWENHEIMS) have supplied U.S.S.R. authorities with military informations of all kinds while in Germany and I shall try to find out about later times.
In regard to "Vicki" please see attached statement of mine."

Hedwig (Vicki) LEIBENASCHER
last known address 15, 27th Avenue, W. 3. phone: Pri: 2398. I have never seen her but it is known to me that she is a good friend of the family of LOWENHEIMS, & a certain Mr. SCHIFF (an Austrian) and Wilhelm HERZAN who used to live at Henley on Thames for 1/2 year up to summer 1941. She was in the women's concentration camp Torgau (Saxony) for a long time and got released on my personal efforts on request of Mr. SCHIFF who paid the expenses for this purposes (obtaining a ticket to overseas which my office issued and forwarded to Germany as requested). I have, therefore, said "my personal efforts"; I have nothing to do with her entry here.
I am assuming however, that LOWENHEIM or SCHIFF or HERZAN obtained for her H.O. permission for issue of a visa to her and stay in the U.K.

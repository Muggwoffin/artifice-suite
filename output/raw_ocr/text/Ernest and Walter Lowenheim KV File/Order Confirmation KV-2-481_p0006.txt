B.4.c. Mr. Pilkington.

The controlling interest in THAMES GENERAL INVESTMENT CO., LTD. is vested in B.F. KARTHAUSER and W.M. LOEVENHEIM and if they remain in internment the Board of Trade should be in a position to vest the control of the company in the Custodian of Enemy Property if it is not wound up.

It is not worth while suggesting to the Board of Trade that they should do so at present since if KARTHAUSER and LOEVENHEIM were released the Board of Trade would rescind the vesting order.

Please, therefore, let us see the file again when that fate is decided.

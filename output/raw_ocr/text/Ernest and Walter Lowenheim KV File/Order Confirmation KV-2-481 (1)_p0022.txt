13th September 1941

Re:- THAMES FLU-TITE ENGINEERING COMPANY LIMITED.

The above company was registered on the 22nd March 1941 with a nominal capital of £10,000 divided into £1 shares.

The registered offices are at, Point Pleasant, Wandsworth, SW.18. and the objects of the company are:-

To acquire from QUICKTHO (1928) LIMITED the sole licence to make and sell Flue dampers and all rights etc vested in that company by virtue of a deed of licence dated 14.2.1941, between Bruno Fritz KARTHAUSER and Walter LOEVENHEIM of the first part, Thames General Investment Co Ltd of the second part and Quicktho (1928) Ltd of the third part.

On the 23rd April 1941 the directors were:-

Frederick W BERWICK. British.
97 Barkston Gardens, SW.5.
Engineer.

William QUICK. British.
12 Tudor Way, Ealing. W.5.
Coachbuilder.

Beresford C LOCKHART-JERVIS. British.
Bramshott Lodge, Liphook.
Director of - Notting Hill Electric Lighting Co Ltd

Henry B LUCKCHUCK. British.
12 Brookfield Ave. W.5. (Secretary)
Director of - QUICKTHO (1928) Ltd.

No share return has been issued, but 9,000 shares have been allotted to QUICKTHO (1928) LIMITED.

P.F.49542/B.4.b/JGC.  28th October, 1941.

Dear Bruce,

I must apologise for not having previously answered your letter, COS/2985/40 of 14.10.41 concerning THAMES GENERAL INVESTMENT CO. LTD.

It appears from the copies of documents enclosed in your letter of 9.8.41 that the patent rights in the Flu Tite Damper have been assigned not to QUICKTHO (1928) LTD., but by QUICKTHO (1928) LTD. to THAMES 'FLU-TITE' ENGINEERING CO. LTD.

This last named company was registered on 22.3.41 to acquire these rights and has a nominal capital of £10,000 in £1 shares of which QUICKTHO (1928) LTD. hold 9,000.

On 23.4.41 the directors of THAMES FLU-TITE ENGINEERING CO. LTD. were:-

Frederick W. BERWICK, British, of 97 Barkston Gardens, S.W.5, engineer.

William QUICK, British, of 12 Tudor Way, Ealing, W.5., coachbuilder.

Col. Beresford Clayton Lockhart JERVIS, British, of Bramshott Lodge, Liphook, Hants, Consulting engineer, Director of Notting Hill Electric Lighting Co. Ltd.

Henry Benjamin LUCKUCK, British, of 12 Brookfield Avenue, W.5, Chartered accountant.

QUICKTHO (1928) LTD. was registered on 2.3.28 and has a nominal capital of £125,000 in two shilling shares.

W. C. Bruce, Esq.,
Trading with the Enemy Section,
Finance Department,
BOARD OF TRADE.

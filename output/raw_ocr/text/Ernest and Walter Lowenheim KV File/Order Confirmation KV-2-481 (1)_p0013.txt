Source 28.9.41

There are other persons in this country who know LOEVENHEIMS from Berlin times and used to be in touch with him also in this country very frequently; these are the people of his own movement "Neu Beginnen" the representative in this country being Erwin SCHOTTLE, a former Social Democratic M.P. of Wurtemberg, an aryan of about forty-five years of age; a very straight-forward man, really pro-British; I know LOEVENHEIMS gave him in their release applications as reference and TRAUTE L. mentioned to me once that SCHOTTLE did not give a very useful answer on being asked by the Home Office; unfortunately I am not of the opinion that he would answer me exactly what I could ask him for your purpose but would there perhaps be anyone else whom you could ask to contact SCHOTTLE? Because I expect there is something to harvest.

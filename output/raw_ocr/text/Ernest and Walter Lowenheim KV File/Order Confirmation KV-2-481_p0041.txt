Ref: P.F.49542/E.5.(5).

In a letter dated 16.11.41. Ernst LOEWENHEIM, writing from 70 Anson Road, N.7., vouches most warmly for the anti-Nazi and pro-British sentiments of Ramon Helmut WINGS (L.258/124). LOEWENHEIM says he knew WINGS at Liverpool Prison Hospital "a year ago" and had many political conversations with him. The letter is in WINGS's Home Office file W.622/12.

E.5.(5).
28.1.42.

9th August, 1941

TRADING WITH THE ENEMY ACT, 1939.

Thames General Investment Co. Ltd.

Dear Craufurd,

With reference to your letter of 16th June last, I have been making enquiries on your account regarding the reported sale of the patent rights in the Flu Tite Damper, and after considerable delay have received information that they have been assigned, for a consideration of £8165, to Quicktho (1928) Ltd. In this connection you will be interested to have the enclosed copy of the letter from the company and the copy of the assignment document which they have submitted to us. This evidence should I think remove any doubts you have that officials of the Thames Engineering Company will now be able to collect information which it was undesirable that they should obtain, but I should be pleased to have your observations thereon in due course.

EXTRACT FROM S.B. REPORT RE HITCHINS, JERVIS & PARTNERS.

HITCHINS, JERVIS AND PARTNERS are consulting Engineers, with offices at Hallam House, 3, Central Buildings, Westminster. Colonel HITCHINS and Colonel LOCKHART-JERVIS are the two partners. Firm appears to derive a steady income as consultants to a number of large electricity and gas undertakings, paper manufacturing plants, etc. all over the country.

The association with Bruno KARTHAUSER commenced in 1940. It appears that KARTHAUSER and Ernst LOEWENHEIM, both German refugees, owned the patent of an apparatus to control fumes in large furnaces. In connection with validity of the patent they consulted the Hon. S. Fletcher MOULTON, the well-known barrister and expert in patent law.

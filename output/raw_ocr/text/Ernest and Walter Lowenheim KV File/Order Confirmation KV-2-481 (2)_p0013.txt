June 16th, 1941.

Dear Pilkington,

With further reference to your PF.49542/B.4.c.
I am now able to send you the results of Captain Johnson's investigations with regard to the LOEWENHEIM Code. You will find attached here:-

1. Copy of Johnson's report.
2. Copy of separate interrogations of Ernest and Walter LOEWENHEIM.
3. Copy of explanations of the names in the code as given by (a) Walter (b) Ernest.
4. Comments made by Mrs. Lambert to whom the above was sent for a quick perusal and return, Frau [KARTHAUSER] being interned at Port Erin.

No doubt you have access to a copy of Sykes' report on Walter Lowenheim, E.B. Loewenheim, and B.F. Karthaeuser, which was apparently compiled "a short while ago."

Sykes and Johnson have apparently, been in correspondence about these internees for some time, and so I am also sending him copies of the above.

Yours sincerely,

Valentine Amiltaef

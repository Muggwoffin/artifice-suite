HEADQUARTERS,
RUSHEN INTERMENT CAMP,
PORT ERIN.

JUNE 13TH, 1941.

Dear Major Armitage,

Herewith perused and returned as requested. Very interesting. If I may be allowed a comment it is this:

I don't agree with Johnson's doubts that the code refers to Social Democrat Movement and I don't find the number of references to Communist organizations etc. in the least surprising. On the contrary, they are few and necessary in the circumstances. In fact, with the possible exception of Cripps and Snell - no Communist personalities are mentioned. Anyway, not very well-known ones. Mr. Sykes may be able to confirm this or add some.

I think the LOEWENHEIMS are rather rats and what worries me is the fact that the other Neubeginnen people who are perfectly all right and the rest of Social Democrats may still be using the code to communicate with abroad, while the LOEWENHEIM's may have given it away to the Nazis, or even some Nazi in the camp may have got hold of it and been able to pass it out.

Mrs. RISCHOWSKI formerly KARTHAEUSER, divorced Karthauser and took back her maiden name. She makes no secret of her membership of the S.D. Party or of the fact that she has been a member of the Neubeginnen Group since 1931 - in fact she says so on her E.A.R.1 Form.

There seems no reason to connect her with Communism and the fact that her two children are at a Convent School seems against it. Mr. Sykes can very probably establish if she is still a bona-fide Social Democrat. Personally I am inclined to think she is all right from our point of view, but of course I don't know how much there is of the whole lot of them in connection with the Thames Engineering.

I reported on Mrs. RICHHOWSKI on 7.6.41.

Yours sincerely,

(Sgd.) Florence Lambert.

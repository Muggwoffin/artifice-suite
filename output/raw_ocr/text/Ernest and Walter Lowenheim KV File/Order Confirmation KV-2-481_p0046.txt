17th. day of December 1941

With reference to M.I.5 letter PF.49542/F.2c of the 26th. November 1941, regarding Walter and Ernest LOEWENHEIM who were released from internment on the 23rd. October last:

Walter LOEWENHEIM, who is registered with police under serial No. Q-54683, is now living with his wife Chaszke at 136 Hatherley Court, Hatherley Grove, W., a flat for which they pay an annual rent of £112. Loewenheim has no fixed income, but his wife is employed as a dressmaker at £4 per week by Messrs. Hermine Ltd., 164 New Bond Street, W. Ernest LOEWENHEIM, registered with police under serial No. Q-54275, lives with his mother at 70 Anson Road, Holloway, N.7., the address at which he resided prior to internment.

Regarding the activities of the two men, Walter LOEWENHEIM who, prior to his arrest on the 17th. May 1940 was a director of the Thames Engineering Co., 114 City Road, is now occupied in business negotiations with Messrs. Hitchins, Jervis and Partners of Hallam House, 3 Central Buildings, S.W. a firm who purchased the goodwill of Loewenheim's business for £3500 in February 1941 and obtained a licence to produce the 'Flu-Tite' damper which was formerly manufactured and installed by the Thames Engineering Company. According to the terms of the licence, LOEWENHEIM and his former co-director Bruno KARTHAUSER are entitled to 25% of the net profits on orders received up to the 30th. June 1942. The present negotiations are being conducted with a view to the recovery of the good will of the business as Loewenheim considers that unfair advantage was taken by the present holders at the time of purchase owing to the fact that the vendors were interned at the time. Further, the terms of the licence have been broken by the purchasing firm.

Meanwhile he has received £40 from Hitchins, Jervis and Partners as his share of profit due on a first account and expects about £150 on a second account due for payment on the 31st. December 1941.

Walter LOEWENHEIM, who is an economist and the author of a book on the subject of economics, is now revising the manuscript of a second book and has interested Messrs. Allen and Unwin Ltd. in the publication thereof. The book deals with economics under National Socialism in Germany and is said to be extremely anti-Nazi. LOEWENHEIM hopes to put his services as an economist at the service of this country and to this end has interested Miss Ellen Wilkinson, M.P., with a view to securing a post with the Ministry of Information or the Ministry of Economic Warfare.

My mother much better. We had an excellent weekend conference with the SOPADE about Peace and War aims, about which I will tell you later. HEINI promised to write you about it, I doubt it if he will do so. Pity you were not there.

JERVIS can't see me, but I am going to see HARDEN today and ask for an interview.

[HELFERMAN] was very nice and friendly, promised all sorts of hope he will keep it. He is with [M. BRAUN] the only opposition, I think he is crazy but honest and, so to say, the symptom of a great past. If he really wants to help he could, but he is so crazy that he might forget all about it. He likes me personally which might be a help.

I am rather shaky, the silly business with my mother got on my nerves a lot. Hope you will be here soon, to do the dirty work.

NB writes about her peace and war aims in GOLLANCZ Left News which I will try to send you. This is interesting because ACLAND is connected with the Left News as well. But this does not matter, have not read the stuff yet, heard about it via GOTTFURCHT.

Both GOTTFURCHT and SANDER tell me that they send their paper via Commandant. I keep them for you, so don't worry, you don't lose much. New membership regulations for SOPADE not issued.

Be cheerful and confident, what a silly life.

Yours,

R.

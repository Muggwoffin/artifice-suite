Note on Evidence against Walter and Ernst LOEWENHEIM.

1. (whose name should not be communicated to the Hurst Committee but who may be referred to as "a source whose reliability we have no reason to doubt") says that he knows both the LOEWENHEIM brothers personally and that Walter, the elder, was at one time "undoubtedly a G.P.U. man". adds "once a G.P.U. man - always a G.P.U. man."

says that Walter LOEWENHEIM ran an illegal Communist group in Berlin, that he is an expert on military matters and that his London house contained many works on strategy by military experts and military journals which were presumably destroyed when the building was bombed.

Ernst LOEWENHEIM, says, was employed by RUSSPHOTO, a Russian organisation devoted nominally to photography but used for military espionage.

Walter LOEWENHEIM was a noted member of the Communist Party in Germany and his Party name was "Dr. BURGER."

2. (whose name also must not be disclosed to the Hurst Committee, but may be referred to as a "Very reliable source") also knows both the LOEWENHEIM brothers and says:-

(a) that both brothers were members of the K.P.D.;

(b) that although ostensibly carrying on a normal business, they were in fact interesting themselves in matters of a military nature;

(c) that it was an "open secret" in Berlin that Walter was a G.P.U. man;

(d) that both brothers paid frequent visits to Russia;

(e) that Walter used the alias of "Dr. BURGER"; and

(f) that Ernst LOEWENHEIM, purporting to act for RUSSPHOTO, had as his main interest and topic of conversation the obtaining of military information.

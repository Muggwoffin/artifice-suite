E. I. A. Mr Robson-Scott
Note for Regional Officer on Released Alien.

Our ref. P.F.49542

Name. Ernst LOWENHEIM
Born 21-10-98
70, Anson Road, N.7.

Region. 5

The story of this man follows very closely that of his brother Walter LOWENHEIM. There, in fact, are only two points of difference. (One) is that Ernst was expelled from the Communist Party in 1928 a year later than his brother and (two), that Ernst was employed, while in Germany, by an organisation known as Russphoto.

Russphoto was supposed to be a firm for the supply of photographs etc. relating to the Soviet Union. It was generally known that this organisation was a part of the Soviet espionage system. The fact that Ernst LOWENHEIM worked with Russphoto up till 1930 is rather strange in view of his alleged dismissal from the Communist Party in 1928. However that came about it confirms the impression that Ernst like his brother Walter was employed while in Germany by the Russians.

Of the two Walter is said to take the leading part. The case of Ernst was treated by the Hurst Tribunal on similar lines to that of his brother Walter.

15th June, 1941.

Dear Prestige,

Please refer to your letter dated 4.6.41 addressed to Renton on the subject of Ernst and Walter EINHEIM.

I am answering it as I now deal direct with the Hurst Tribunal and not through Renton.

We much regret the delay in completing this case. The enquiries have been particularly difficult, but are now nearing their close and we hope to be able to report finally within a short time. We should like to assure you that no unnecessary delay is occurring.

Yours sincerely,

W.C. Edwards.

H.H.C. Prestige, Esq.,
Aliens' Department,
Home Office.

29.6.41

A german political friend was living with Löwenheims for a long time but later-on expelled from this country. He was the man who was visited by Walter in Holland.

Ernst has asked his internment the Home Office for permission to re-marry his divorced wife.

While in this country through years Walter used to write long (3 to 5 pages) political letters, reports, essays etc. to a friend of him in France. In order not to interrupt this connection Traute is now writing to him from time to time as he is at present in the unoccupied part of France.

Löwenheime and Karthäuser made various applications to the Home Office to get a permission to re-enter this country for their expelled friend while in Holland, but were refused.

Political differences with the other leaders of "Neu Beginnen" are the reason given by Traute why Walter left the movement.

Schöttle, a full aryan, at present representative of the "Neu Beginnen" in this country on being asked by the Aliens Tribunal about Löwenheims has not given a 100% clear and straightforward answer.

Traute thinks it may be that the (British) Labour Party or one of their leading members have made representations to the authorities of this country as Löwenheims movement meant a split of the (German) Labour Party.

She tells that Ernst used to be a member of the Left Book Club as well as of the Labour Party for many years under a wrong name. The reason being that it was not allowed at that time that Germans are joining these two institutions.

Her opinion is that the brothers are in danger of their life in Nazi Germany as well as in Soviet Russia.

Walter wrote various books and pamphlets while in this country but none of them were ever published.

Up to a certain point Traute is really disappointed with Walter, due to his cleverness and some of his theories.

She thinks that the authorities here are believing that the "Thames Engineering" had the only purpose or was used for the purpose to cover certain political activities of her people, an assumption which is wrong.

(Traute was the absolutely dominating part of this couple).

19th September 1941.

Dear Prestige,

Reference our telephone conversation this morning.

I much regret that the copies of these letters were omitted from the papers I sent to you yesterday. They are, as you see, not of great importance, but they explain the first paragraph of our final report to the Burst Tribunal dated 23.6.41.

Yours sincerely,

W.C. Edwards.

H.H.L. Prestige Esq.,
Home Office,
Bournemouth.

Personally have a rotten time, my mother is nuisance, she bothers me a lot, I feel rotten as a whole and need you, badly. Had no time to do anything for the book, I am ashamed, but even I cannot split myself into halves.

What about HESS? If the Nazis were in a military defeat I would regard this a symptom but so I think it is a personal problem of this so-called "honest Nazi" or "sane refugee" as the B.B.C. called him.

Yours,

R.

enclosures.

B.8.c.
11.6.41.

Letter written to LOEWENHEIM, House 32, Onchan Camp, I.O.M.

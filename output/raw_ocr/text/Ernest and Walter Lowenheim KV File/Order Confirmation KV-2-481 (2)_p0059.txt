COPY LETTER.

3-6, Northwood Hall,
London,
N.6.

12.5.41.

Dear Ernst and Walter,

Since I left Onchan I had no news from you except the circular which indicated the new way to write your address. I hope you got the letter Wilkinson I sent via registered letter. I had written to the Secretary which promised me the interview and heard that she works now in another capacity - the fact, that the H.O. has not got your recommendations may have the following reasons:-

1. SCHOETTLE and KLATT, who wrote to you the fact that the 19 Tribunal recommended the release, may have been wrong. This, I think, is unlikely (new address SCHOETTLE-KLATT, Neu Beginnen 10 Devon Rise, N.2.) although SANDER does not tell me anything one way or the other. I have at once written to SCHOETTLE, got no answer have again written and got no answer either. But still, I am sure that his informations are correct. I would ask BRUNO to ask SCHOETTLE to get into touch with me, as SCHOETTLE did not do that I could not find out about the KLATT letter which BRUNO did not receive.

2. WILKINSONS asked for your files at a date when the recommendations was not decided yet, i.e. before her letter to you and she just tells me this old information. This, I think, is likely to be the case.

3. H.O. filed things the wrong way.

My answer to WILKINSON must be in your hands, I think that you should write the same, but stress the fact that you are social democrats and interned now for one year, facts which I have already stressed in my letter of the 6th. Let me know what you did. BRUNO should write to SCHOETTLE and ask for explanation (to me as well) and help.

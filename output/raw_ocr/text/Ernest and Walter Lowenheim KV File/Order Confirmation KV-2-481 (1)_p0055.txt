3/6 Northwood Hall, London, N.6.

Dear Ernst (LOEWENHEIM)

Although there are no news I must write. The files are still in circulation and so I don't know a thing. What a life. Still lots of excitement about politics, but people clam down. You by the way, misunderstood what Heini (i.e. YAKUBOWITZ) wrote about Herz, the man does not want to negotiate with Hitler, but after the destruction of Hitlerism he is prepared to be the "heir" and is prepared for a "Wiedergutmachung", as if this would be possible. Heinies still in the country, I still with Cromwells, which does me a lot of good. Jenny moves into own flat in Kingston next week, and will take lodgers, because she could not get on on the long run with WEILS.

Could not get through to the man who wrote about the NEP in Tribune, it was nobody of NB anyway. Keep the number and we will do something about it when you are back. Made enquiries about your man in the Zeitung, I think he is a friend of Schaefers, definitely an old Frankfurter Zeitung bloke.

This waiting gets on my nerves -- and on yours I presume.

All love,

Ruth LOEWENHEIM

6/65/42

My dear,

Many thanks for yours of the 4th. So sorry to hear you have had a touch of flu, but trust you have fully recovered.

What particularly interests me about your report on L. is K's statement that he became an honorary member of the Red Military Academy. This presumably would indicate some interest on L's part in military affairs and some particular military knowledge as qualifications. If you should happen to see K again soon, can you make enquiries in this direction?

Yours sincerely,

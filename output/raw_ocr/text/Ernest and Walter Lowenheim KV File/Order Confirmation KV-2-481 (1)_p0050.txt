THIS ASSIGNMENT is made the twenty-fifth day of April 1941 BETWEEN QUICKTHO LTD whose registered office is situate at Point Pleasant Wandsworth in the County of Surrey (hereinafter called "the Licensees") of the one part and THAMES FLU-TITE ENGINEERING COMPANY LTD, whose registered office is situate at Points Pleasant Wandsworth aforesaid (hereinafter called "the Assignees") of the other part

WHEREAS by a Deed dated 14th day of February 1941 and made between Bruno Fritz Karthauser and Walter Loewenheim (thereinafter called "the Patentees" of the first part The Thames General Investment Co. Ltd. of the second part and Licensees of the third part the Patentees granted to the Licensees an exclusive Licence to make use exercise and vend Flue Dampers under and in accordance with an invention protected by Letters Patent No. 453477 and in addition to or improvement thereof for the residue or the term comprised in the said Letters Patent AND WHEREAS the Licensees have agreed to sell to the Assignees the said Licence for the sum of £8,165 NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH as follows:-

1. In pursuance of the said agreement and in consideration of the sum of £8,165 now paid by the Assignees to the Licensees as beneficial owners hereby Assign and Convey unto the Assignees ALL THAT the said Licence and the full and exclusive benefit thereof TO HOLD unto the Assignees and their assigns subject to the covenants agreements and conditions therein contained and on the Licensees part to be observed and performed.

2. THE Assignees hereby covenant with the Licensees that they will pay to the Patentees all sums payable in pursuance of the said Licence and will perform and observe all the covenants agreements and conditions on the Licensees part therein contained and that they will indemnify and keep indemnified the Licensees from and against all actions, proceedings claims and demands in respect of the said moneys convenants agreements and conditions respectively.

3.

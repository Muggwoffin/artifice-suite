In the illegal period underground trade union circles, in which GOTTFURCHT played a leading part, were in contact until about the beginning of 1935 with the Neu Beginnen Group, whose representative at that time was a certain JABLONSKI @ BUETTNER. But this arrangement proved unworkable in the long run, because Neu Beginnen had a very weak underground organization, but nevertheless claimed the leading roles for themselves and tried to misuse the Trade Union Groups, which did genuine underground work, for their own political ambitions. Above all Neu Beginnen began to employ a romantic and adventurous type of underground tactics, which involved many of its contacts in serious risks and caused a number of them to fall into the hands of the Gestapo. The flight of Willi SNELL (now in the U.S.A.) led to the arrest of his wife, GOTTFURCHT and others by the Gestapo, and from the details of the statements shown to them it was perfectly obvious that they had been betrayed by the malice or negligence of the afore-said JABLONSKI @ BUETTNER. There is also a well known story of the Gestapo's discovery at Muggelsee of a trunk or suitcase containing complete lists of illegal workers, who then had to pay the penalty of the adventurous romanticism of the Neu Beginnen leaders, which was almost criminal. It was only due to the skill and unscrupulousness of FRANK-MUELLER (who was an adept at cultivating valuable connections, as e.g. with Edo FIMMEN) that the movement could maintain itself.

At present the two LOEVENHEIMS belong to the SOPADE, but they remain politically inactive and are chiefly interested in technical matters. They seldom appear at SOPADE meetings and are very reserved in their attitude because they do not approve of the SOPADE's association with Neu Beginnen in the Socialist Union. As far as we can tell they are anti-Communist; at all events they have no contacts in this direction.

2nd September 1941.

Re:- QUICKTHO (1928) LIMITED.

The above company was registered on the 2nd March 1928 with a nominal capital of £50,000 which has since been increased to £125,000 divided into 1,250,000 two shilling shares.

The registered offices are at, Point Pleasant, Wandsworth, SW.18. and the objects of the company are:-

To acquire and take over as a going concern all the undertaking, property and assets and all the debts, liabilities and engagements of QUICKTHO LIMITED.
and to carry on the business of motor and mechanical engineers, in all its branches.

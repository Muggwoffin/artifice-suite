The third source is a woman and we believe her to be reliable. She described Walter LOEWENHEIM as being "a red hot Communist". When asked - "What do you mean by a red hot Communist? Do you mean that he parades about in demonstrations and waves a red flag?" She replied quite spontaneously - "No, I do not mean that. Walter LOEWENHEIM is a very clever man, and if he is active in Communist matters, he is active in the background".

It may be noted how closely these independent sources corroborate one another.

As stated in my report on 31.1.41., in 1929 we had information that the LOEWENHEIM's firm in Berlin had amalgamated with another German firm to form the Metropolitan Trading Company, which was understood to handle Soviet funds in Shanghai. This suspicion was partially confirmed.

After their arrival in England, the LOEWENHEIMs formed a company known as the THAMES GENERAL INVESTMENT COMPANY, Ltd., which carried on business under the name of the THAMES ENGINEERING COMPANY.

There is no evidence that this Company has been used for espionage purposes, but the background of the case bears a striking similarity to other cases in which Soviet espionage has been established.

It is probable that much convincing evidence has been placed before the Tribunal regarding the LOEWENHEIM's anti-Nazi activities, but this is by no means inconsistent with the evidence of their having been engaged in espionage on behalf of some other foreign power. Moreover, in view of the shifting nature of Russo-German relations, it is never possible to say what will be the final destination of information thus obtained.

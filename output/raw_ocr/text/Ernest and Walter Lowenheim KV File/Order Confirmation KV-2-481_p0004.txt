17.6.41. (B.8.c. forwarding H.H. report dated 6.6.41. re LOEWENHEIMS)

19.6.41. Note on evidence against W. and E. LOEWENHEIM.

20.6.41. Report from B.8.c. re their investigations on the LOEWENHEIMS.

B.8.t. (Mr. Edwards)

I have not yet heard the result of the interrogation of the LOEWENHEIMS upon their code but my other enquiries are completed and as the Hurst Committee are pressing for a decision a report, I think, might be sent to them.

I have summarized the result of my enquiries at 69b and if you agree you will perhaps write to Sir Cecil Hurst accordingly.

B.4.c. 21.6.41.

R. Pilkington.

B.8.t. (Mr. Edwards)

Since Minute 70 was dictated the report from Armitage of the interrogation of the LOEWENHEIMS (now filed at 69a) has been brought to my notice. You will see that when questioned upon the code both the LOEWENHEIMS became secretive and evasive and affected loss of memory.

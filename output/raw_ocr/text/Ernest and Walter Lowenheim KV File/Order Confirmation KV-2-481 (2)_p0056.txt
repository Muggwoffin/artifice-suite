COPY LETTER.

3-6, Northwood Hall,
London,
N.G.

14.5.41.

Dear Walter and Ernst,

Still no letters from you since I left the Isle. Hope you are well.

1. SCHOETTLE. As SCHOETTLE did not answer my letters I asked SANDER to send him to me or HEINI and asked HEINI to write again to him (during the days when I was stuck up with my mother) HEINI wrote a very rude letter which I disapproved, but which had gone off already (you remember the sort of letters HEINI used to write.) It had, after all the effect that SCHOETTLE appeared at his Office and told him (in GLEISBERG'S presence) that the information about recommendation was correct and dated from SEATON to KLATT. He did not tell if KLATT had written to BRUNO. SCHOETTLE is still furious about HEINI (who, by the way, apologised) and so I think BRUNO should if you think it advisable go on writing to SCHOETTLE and ask for his help, but need certainly not know anything about the things here. I am really sure that things will be all right soon, I got letter from WILKINSON'S Secretary that she will look into the matter again (dated 12th May) but she does not give me an interview. It is a pity that the old secretary left, but if necessary WATKINS, who returns to London next week, will do something. Other efforts not worth mentioning enough that I go round "roaring" again, so much that I have not even time to go to school.

2. Business HARDEN asked me to see him and told me that QUICKTHO want to do give a licence to a new founded Company "James Flu-Tite Engineering Co. Ltd." but that they need your consent to the agreement. See both enclosures. I wanted to give me the impression that this was purely formal and that if the solicitor agrees things are all right.

I don't think that we should give this consent without remuneration. I want, as a tactical move, delay everything as long as possible and wait your instructions. Another set of enclosures

B.8.c. (Mr. Sykes)

Many thanks for your letter of 20.6.41 enclosing a memorandum on the LOEVENHEIMS.

I came to the conclusion that the code had been used by the LOEVENHEIMS for the purposes of their Neubeginnen activities and not for espionage and am glad to know that you agree.

I have myself seen upon this case and have also obtained a report from and I have just arranged for a report of the result of my various enquiries to be sent to Sir Cecil Hurst.

I have pressed as strongly as I can that the LOEVENHEIMS should not be released and although my enquiries have not established any evidence against [KARTHAUSER] I have suggested that his case should be dealt with as one with the LOEVENHEIMS and that his application for release should also be refused.

I have also received your note of 23.6.41 respecting the attempt by one of the LOEVENHEIMS to smuggle a message out of the Camp. When LOEVENHEIM said that he was one of the founders of the German Democratic Party he was, I should imagine, referring to the fact that he founded the Neubeginnen movement.

B.4.c. 25.6.41.

R. Pilkington.

26th November, 1941.

Deputy Assistant Commissioner,
Special Branch.

I should be most grateful if you would kindly let me know the present address of Ernest LOEWENHEIM Registration Card number 585192 and Walter LOEWENHEIM Registration Card number 601734. These men were the subject of your report reference GR-79/K/1050; SB-520/SPN/1235; of 31st May 1940.

The two LOEWENHEIMS were released under Category 19 from internment on the 23rd October 1941, and it is understood that they have returned to London.

I should also be very interested to have any information which you may be able to obtain regarding their present activities.

F.M. Small.

FMS/AD.

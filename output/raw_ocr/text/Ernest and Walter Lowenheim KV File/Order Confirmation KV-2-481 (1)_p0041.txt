LOEWENHEIM, Walter, A.C. 424.
LOEWENHEIM, Ernst, A.C. 425.

The cases of the above brothers have been considered by the Hurst Tribunal, and the Tribunal's recommendation dated 16.8.41. has been forwarded to you. A copy has also been sent to us, as we regarded the cases as unusually important.

We find that the Tribunal has formed a view which differs widely from ours, and, in short, favours the release of the brothers, an event which we still regard as extremely undesirable.

Should you feel disposed to implement the recommendation of the Tribunal, we should be grateful if we could be given the opportunity of expressing our views before you make your final decision.

A copy of this memorandum has been sent to Mr Cecil Hurst.

W.C. Edwards.

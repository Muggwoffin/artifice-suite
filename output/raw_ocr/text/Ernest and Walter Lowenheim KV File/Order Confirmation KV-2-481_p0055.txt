INTERNAL MEMORANDUM.

From: E.3.(S)
To: F.3.c. (Mr. Pilkington)

Please see the attached. tells us he has seen Mrs. Traute Williams (former Mrs. L.) again and learnt from her that both L's and Mr. & Mrs. KARTHAEUSER returned to London a week ago. He would glad to know whether we are interested that he should see them.

As the Lowenheim's have now been released, you will perhaps reply to this note.

F.3.c. R. Pilkington
16/11/41

Date: 13.11.41
Signature: For C.W. Sykes.

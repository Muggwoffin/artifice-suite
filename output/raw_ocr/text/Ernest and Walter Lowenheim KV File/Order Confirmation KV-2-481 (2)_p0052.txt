ECW/KV/1096.

10th June, 1941.

Dear Colonel,

I refer to your B.13/S.O.2. of the 20th May.

reports as follows on the brothers LOEVENHEIM:

He knows both brothers personally. Walter, the elder was at one time undoubtedly a G.P.U. man, whether by inclination or because he had no choice in the matter cannot say, but he remarks however: "Once a G.P.U. always a G.P.U."

Walter LOEVENHEIM ran an illegal Communist group in Berlin and, when the Nazis came into power, was so careless in destroying his records that he cost the lives of about a 100 people. It is not known whether he was really working for the Nazis and double crossing the G.P.U.

Both brothers were at one time in Russia.

has heard that in internment both brothers claim to be "MILES" of the "Neubeginnen". This is not true, as MILES is really Dr. Karl FRANK.

claims that both brothers are equally unpleasant. He regrets that he cannot throw any light on the identity of "Vicky".

The two photographs of the Loewenheim brothers are returned herewith.

I apologise for the delay in letting you have this report.

Yours ever,

G. C. Chetwood

Lieut.-Colonel Hinchley-Cooke, O.B.E.,
M. 5,
London.

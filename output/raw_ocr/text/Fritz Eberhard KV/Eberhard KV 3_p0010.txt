T.C.10223/M.L.9/B.10.m.  13th June, 1940

Dear Colonel Chaytor,

I should be glad if you would let me have any information as to the names and activities of the tenants of "Duona Vista", a house in Sleights Village, near Whitby.

It has been reported that a member of the household recently posted a registered letter of suspicious bulk, thought to contain photographs, and addressed to:

Dr. Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT,
6, Brunswick Square,
London, W.C.1.

We have the following information concerning Dr. Rauschenplat. He arrived in England on the 4th January, 1938, stated that he was a Doctor of Political Economy, and is said to be engaged in the preparation of a book entitled "State Intervention in Economics".

He is said to be a Socialist, but has no Communistic sympathies. In 1939 he joined the Central European Joint Committee, which compiles information from various continental sources, makes comments and puts the results at the disposal of the Ministry of Information.

From the above, it can be seen that Dr. Rauschenplat seems to be of irreproachable integrity, but it would be interesting to know, if possible the contents of the above mentioned registered letter. I think it would do no harm to approach directly, in this case, the people concerned.

Yours sincerely,

Major General Sir Vernon Kell,

Lieut-Col. J.C. Chaytor, D.S.O., M.C.,
Chief Constable,
County Constabulary,
Northallerton, Yorks.

22.12.38 Application form for Certificate of Identity sent to RAUSCHENPLAT at 30, Doughty Street, W.C.1.

30.12.38 Letter from RAUSCHENPLAT enclosing:-
Certificate of Registration
2 Certified photographs
Postal Order for 7s.6d.
Application for Certificate of Identity

Certificate needed for the purpose of foreign travel. Hopes to meet friend of his who will soon be released from concentration camp.

22.11.39 Exempted from internment and placed in Category "C"

13.7.40. Letter from RAUSCHENPLAT to Home Office applying for permission to reconnect his wireless receiving set.

States he is a German refugee exempted from internment. Exemption granted on account of work of national importance which is largely connected with the B.B.C. and the Planning Committee of the Ministry of Information. For this work he says it is essential that he has at all times access to a wireless set.

Mr. Gibbs Smith, Electra House, W.C.2 is prepared to sponsor this request.

This committee utilises the knowledge of aliens in this country and distributes propaganda periodicals in various countries.

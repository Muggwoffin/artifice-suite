Dear Harker,

With reference to your letter of the 13th instant, numbered as above. I have to inform you that my Divisional Superintendent visited the house "Beunavista", Sleights, the home of Johann and Therese HAMMERSCHMIDT who are enemy aliens and found that they had staying with them a friend named Hilda Oldag, an Hungarian by birth, born on the 31st July, 1914, and who is married to a British Subject, John Aldag of 54, Lillington St. London. S.W. 1. a writer. Mrs. Oldag explained to my Officer that she is also a writer and came to Sleights in November 1939 as the peacefulness of the village would assist her in her work. She stated that about a fortnight ago she sent a registered letter addressed to Dr. Helmut von RAUSCHENFLAT of 6, Brunswick Square, London. W.C.1. and state that the letter contained manuscript for a new book entitled "How Fascism got hold of the German People".

She further stated that in conjunction with Dr. RAUSCHENFLAT she wrote a book entitled "How to conquer Hitler" and this work was published by Messrs Jarrods (Publishers, London) Ltd, of Paternoster Row, London. E.C.4. She also wrote an article published in the Contemporary Review No. 894 dated June 1940, on "Government and People in Germany". She uses the pen name of "Hilda Monte". She intends returning to her home in London some time next week.

At the present I have no reason for doubting the loyalty to this country of Mrs. Oldag or of Mr. and Mrs. HAMMERSCHMIDT.

For your information I would add that HAMMERSCHMIDT is an invalid and his wife was placed in Category "C" by the District Tribunal.

Yours sincerely,

J. G. Hayles

Colonel O.A. Harker C.B.E.

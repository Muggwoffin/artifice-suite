R.9753

Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT

German Dr. of Political Economy.

Born Dresden 2.10.96.

Passport R.138 Magdeburg 18.11.33 to 18.11.38.

Visa London 27/5/38 valid until 17.11.38.

8.9.38. RAUSCHENPLAT left Germany where he had been earning his living by writing newspaper articles, last Xmas, and arrived in U.K. on 4.1.38 on a N/E condition. Until 10.6.38 he studies privately and he now wishes to stay indefinitely in order to continue preparation of a book on "State Intervention in Economics" which he hopes to have published in Switzerland. The reason for his present visit is to obtain a further knowledge of English economic affairs, and he intends taking a course of English and Political Economy at the London School of Economics. He stated that he does not wish to go back to Germany because he is not in agreement with the present regime, and is virtually a political refugee. He was in possession of £5 only but stated that during his previous visit to this country he received £3 p.w. from a Miss OATTLLEY (Swiss) an Assistant Newspaper Editor in Zurich. When the book is finished he intends to return to Switzerland where he feels certain of obtaining journalistic work. He does not anticipate any difficulty in having his passport extended.

Alien landed for 1 month, proceeding to Brighton Hall Hotel, Cartwright Gdns, W.C.1.

20.9.38. Application for extension. Alien at 30 Doughty St. WC.1. Has £14 p.m. References: - Dr. Otto KAHN-FREUND, Barrister at Law, formerly a Judge in Berlin, now assistant lecturer at the London School of Economics. Add. 1 Fawley Road, N.W.6.

Professor Adolf LOEWE, formerly Professor of Economics at Kiel and Frankfort, now Hon. Research Fellow at Manchester University. Add. 10 Wilbraham Rd. Manchester

30.9.38. To M.I.5. Has he come under notice, please?

10.10.38. H.O. file returned minuted: We have nothing against this man"

British Consulate-General,
Sophienstrasse 24B,
STUTTGART.

January 24, 1958.

Dear Office,

We have been asked officially by the Landesamt fuer die Wiedergutmachung, Stuttgart, for help in connexion with the restitution claim of Dr. Fritz EBERHARD who is Intendant of the Sueddeutscher Rundfunk.

They have to establish whether Dr. Eberhard was in Germany before or after the date of capitulation - May 10, 1945. He is known to have left England on April 16, 1945 but he did not register with the Stuttgart authorities until July 1945. They are inquiring whether British authorities can throw any light on his movements after leaving England which would indicate his actual date of arrival in Germany.

They inform us that Dr. Eberhard was working with the Department of Psychological Warfare in London. He held a British Certificate of Identification No. R.9753 and SHAEF permit No.3891.

We would be grateful if you would pass the inquiry to the War Office provided, of course, that you see no objection.

Yours ever,

(Sgd.) G.F.
Consulate-General.

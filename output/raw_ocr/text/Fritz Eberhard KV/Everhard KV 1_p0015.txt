FOREIGN OFFICE
S.W.1.
March 14, 1958.

Sir,

I am directed by Mr. Secretary Lloyd to transmit to you a copy of a letter which has been sent to this office by the British Embassy at Bonn, and to enquire whether the War Office has any record of Doctor Eberhard's service.

I am,
Sir,
Your obedient Servant,

(Signed) Illegible.

The Under Secretary of State,
War Office.

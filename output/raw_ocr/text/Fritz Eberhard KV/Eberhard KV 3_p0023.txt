CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: - V. RAUSCHENPLAT

Source: - Report from BOGEY, dated 8.1.40.

Johann REINHOLZ said that through his work at Gordon Square, he comes into touch with many different groups, and describes the individuals as follows:

Dr. FRANK (Pseudonym MILES), leader of the Neu Beginen group. Periodical: Deutsche Inlands-Berichte. His Deputy is a certain LOEWENTAL. Colleagues in the Demuth Committee are Dr. KLATT and V. RAUSCHENPLATT. Irreproachable, sound, desirable as a collaborator.

Original in P.F. 48709 REINHOLZ 7a dated as above. D.B. 14.3.40.

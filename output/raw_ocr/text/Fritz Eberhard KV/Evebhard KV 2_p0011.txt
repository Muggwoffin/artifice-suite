Note.

RAUSCHENPLAT was, until recently, a member of the ISK, a small group of moderate Socialist intellectuals. In Germany he was primarily connected with the Trades Union side of the movement. Since he came to this country in September 1938, he has worked for both the Demuth Committee and P.W.E. The latter did not renew his contact in May 1942 "not because they had anything definite against him but there was a feeling of uneasiness about him and they concluded that it would be better if the Department got rid of him".

We know of nothing against him from the security point of view.

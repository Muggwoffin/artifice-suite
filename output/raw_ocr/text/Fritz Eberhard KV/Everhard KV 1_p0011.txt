REPORT

Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT @ EBERHARD.

1. The Press Service of the Christian Democratic Union has issued a warning about Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT @ EBERHARD, who was a war-time refugee in the U.K.

2. It is said that RAUSCHENPLAT has established contacts in Eastern Germany, that he has recently visited the Soviet zone and that he was planning to produce a film about the so-called German Democratic Republic.

3. RAUSCHENPLAT lives in Stuttgart, Wurttemberg, where he, until recently, was the Intendant of the South German Radio.

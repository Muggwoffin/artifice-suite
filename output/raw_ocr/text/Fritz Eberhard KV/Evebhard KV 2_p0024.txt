SECRET.

Box No. 100,
W.D.O.
London W.1.

4th January, 1941.

Dear Hollis,

I am enclosing a copy of a memorandum received by me today from Mr. Murray of our Dept. who, as I think you know, is concerned with the management of some of our secret houses. I am wondering if you could make enquiries about Mr. LEONHARDT, mentioned therein. I think it probable that he is known to M.I.5., as also Mr. JAKSCH.

Mr. RAUSCHENPLAT, as I think you know, is employed in one of our German secret houses.

If you would like to have a talk with me on this subject, or if there are any other points on which you think we could give you some more information, will you please let me know.

Yours sincerely,

(Sgd.) Walter Stewart Roberts.

R. Hollis
Box 500,
Oxford.

No action without reference to Mr. Hollis.

M.C.
25.4.41.

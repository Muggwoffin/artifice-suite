1st January, 1945.

Dear von RAUSCHENPLAT, Holmuth,

In reply to your letter of the 28th December, 1944, regarding von RAUSCHENPLAT, there is nothing in our records to substantiate the information that he has Communist leanings.

Until recently he was a member of the ISK, a small group of moderate Socialist intellectuals. In Germany he was primarily connected with the Trades Union side of the movement. Since he came to England in September 1938 he has worked for both the Central European Joint Committee and P.W.R. P.W.R. did not renew his contract in May 1942 for the reason, to use their own words "not because they had anything definite against him but there was a feeling of uneasiness about him and concluded that it would be better if the Department got rid of him".

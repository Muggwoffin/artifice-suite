SECRET

E.4(5) October 29th, 1942.

Dear Stewart Roberts,

I refer to your letter of October 8th, 1942, regarding Hulimut von Rauschenplat.

According to our records the grant of the A.P.A. certificate and the A.W.S. permit was made to Rauschenplat on 1st July, 1940, subsequently I understand that he joined your organisation complete with certificate and permit. He left your staff on 26th May, 1942, I believe.

There therefore appears to be no reason why he should now be in possession of an A.P.A. certificate.

I return his Registration Certificate.

Yours sincerely,

W. Stewart Roberts, Esq.,
Box No. 2.,
Western Central District,
P.O. New Oxford St.,
London, W.C.1.

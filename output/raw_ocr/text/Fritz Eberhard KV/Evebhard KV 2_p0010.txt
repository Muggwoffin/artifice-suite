28th December, 1944.

Dear Stone,

Von RAUSCHENPLAT, Hellmut.

Our O.S.S. contacts have been advised by P.W.E. that the a/n man has Communist leanings and they have asked me whether you are able to substantiate or amplify this information.

I am afraid they have provided me with no further details regarding him except that he was vetted through their X.2. branch, which they tell me has direct contact with you.

Yours sincerely,

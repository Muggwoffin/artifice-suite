R/C from S.R. alien has now left employment, request for cancellation of A.P.A.

R/C to S.R. A.P.A. cert. cancelled. 30 MAY 1942

The new style A.P.A cert. was inevitably left uncancelled S.R sends R.C. & draws attention.

A.P.A cert. (N.C.) cancelled. Receiving R.C. in lieu apology. 4 JUN 1942

S-R writes. Says alien held Certif. D Permit before he went to his staff. Will we please restore?

This man was a 'B' case to begin with and got his permit etc as such. Looks as if he were trying to pull a pick me up on us.

Not on your life!

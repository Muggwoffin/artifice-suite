June 1st, 1942.

Dear Osborne,

Thank you for your letter of May 29th enclosing the Certificate of Registration of Mr. H. von Rauschenplat.

I notice that the A.P.A. certificate on page 9 has been cancelled but not the A.P.A. certificate of a later date on Page 11 and I am returning the Registration Certificate herewith, as you will no doubt wish to cancel the second A.P.A. certificate as well.

Yours sincerely,

[signature]

F.P. Osborne Esq.

SECRET

In our file for GERKE, we have a report from L.L.6. dated March 1947, which alleged that a Karl J. GERKE was strongly suspected of being in close contact with the Russians who were reported to have offered him a position in the future German Government. It may be that you are already aware of this report. It is difficult to say, however, whether it refers to the Karl Johannes GERKE in whom we are interested. The latter was presumably in the United States in April 1947 as that was the month in which he was naturalised, and in the autumn of 1947 as he applied in New York on 22 September for a U.K. visa, which was subsequently refused on 20 November. We do not know whether this deterred him from visiting Europe but as the L.L.6. report was dated March 1947 and GERKE was still in the United States in the autumn of that year, it appears rather unlikely that the information does relate to him.

B. Russell Jones.

Reference: L.158/1424

Extract from Home Office file R.9753 - Hellmut von RAUSCHENPLAT - received on 28th April, 1958.

Letter from H.M. Immigration Office, Bovingdon. Dated 16.4.1945.

H.M. Chief Inspector.

Hellmut von RAUSCHENPLAT. Ge.
H.O.: R 9753

At the request of the S.C.O. under instructions from Central Security, the above named was permitted to embark to-day for France in a Liberator aircraft under U.S. military escort.

Alien was in possession of an I.B. 113 No. R 9753 of 7.1.39 valid to 28.12.1946.

I.B. 29 to T.I. and CNRO.

(Signed) G.L. Harbottle.

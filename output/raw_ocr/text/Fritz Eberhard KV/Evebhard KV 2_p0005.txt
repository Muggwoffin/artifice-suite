CONFIDENTIAL

To: Captain G.E. Hogg
From: Lt. Col. H.B. Holt

In connection with the re-education of German Ps/W, it is desired that the under-mentioned be permitted to enter a Camp to lecture. I regret I have no further detail and would be grateful if you could let me know if there is any security objections.

Dr. Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT, 2 Clifton Gardens, N.W.11.
(formerly employed by P.I.D.)

Nationality: German.
Subject: Modern History: Economics.

Lieutenant Colonel,
Deputy Security Officer.

3rd April 1945.

CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-
RAUSCHENPLAT.

From M.E.W. with reference to list of German refugees (doctors)

7.6.40.

Dr. F. Demuth in a letter to Dr. Ingrams makes an appeal that certain German doctors should be exempted from the wireless restriction; and allowed to listen to broadcasts. Among the names is that of Dr. H. von Rauschenplat, 6, Brunswick Square, W.C.1.

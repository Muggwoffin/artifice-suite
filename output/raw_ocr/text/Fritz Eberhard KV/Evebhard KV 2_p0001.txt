American Embassy
71 Grosvenor Street
London, W.1
12 January 1950

Reference No: 1584

TO: Major R. A. A. Badham,
M.I.-5

SUBJECT: Dr. Fritz EBERHARD, formerly Baron Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT

We are attaching herewith forms requesting a check on the above-named individual. This check has been requested by the American Consulate at Stuttgart, Germany who have asked for any available information concerning subject's activities, associates, etc., during his stay in England.

According to our records, subject was vetted in December 1944 for employment by O.S.S. and your organization advised at that time that there was nothing against subject from a security point of view.

We should appreciate being advised whether subject has come to notice since the original check was made.

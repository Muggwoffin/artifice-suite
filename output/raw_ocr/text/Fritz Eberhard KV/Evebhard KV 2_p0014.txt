Dear Hollis,

Dr. Helmut Von RAUSCHENPLAT.

Reference your number L.158/1424/A.D.F. of the 16th June.

Rauschenplat left this Department on the 26th May, 1942. He worked in one of our R.U. houses which was running a Social Democrat line and included some members of the Neubefreienen Party. When the house was closed down, it was suggested that he should remain in the Department and work in our German region. This was turned down, not because we had anything definite against him but there was a feeling of uneasiness about him and we concluded that it would be better if the Department got rid of him.

As to whether he continued to work with the International Transport Workers Federation whilst he was with us, I have no information that he did so but I should not be very surprised if he did.

The R.U. house that Rauschenplat worked in was in the charge of Mr. Crossman. It would be interesting to know whether Mr. Crossman had arranged for Rauschenplat to be taken on by the Americans. Mr. Crossman is now working in P.W.D. (Psychological Warfare Division, S.J.A.E.F.) P.W.D. have a large number of people who are partly O.W.I. as well as P.W.D. and as Crossman is in great favour with the Americans in fact, does everything he can for them, I should not be very surprised if he was not roping in some of his late friends.

Yours Sincerely,

P.R. Chambers

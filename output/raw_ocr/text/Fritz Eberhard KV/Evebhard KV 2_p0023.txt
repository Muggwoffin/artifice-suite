NO ACTION WITHOUT REFERENCE TO MR. HOLLIS.

Box No. 100, W.D.O.
London W.1.

9th April 1941.

Dear Hollis,

Our liaison officer with the S.I.S. informs me that an enquiry has been received through the Censorship of foreign mail regarding "Box No. 100, W.D.O. London W.1.", whether this is a Government Box, and consequently its letters should not be interfered with (it does not at the present moment appear on their list).

As I think you know, this Box Number is the official box of our Department, and I should be grateful if you would let the Censorship know.

In addition to this Box No. 100, to which the bulk of our Department's correspondence is delivered, we have a number of other boxes reserved for the past of certain members and domestic staff at various secret houses. I think it might be as well for you to have a list of these latter, and I am therefore enclosing it. We should raise no objection to censorship of post going to any of these boxes, except Box, No. 100, though I think it improbable that any of the persons mentioned in the list herewith will be receiving, for the present, any mail from abroad. They have, of course, all been vetted by your Department.

Yours sincerely,

Walter Stewart Roberts,

R.H. Hollis Esq.

No action without reference to Mr. HOLLIS.

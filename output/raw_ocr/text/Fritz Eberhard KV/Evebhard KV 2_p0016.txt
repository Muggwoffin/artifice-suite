The following information comes from an F3/1013 report dated 3.6.44.
No action to be taken without reference to F.3.

Von RAUCHENPLAT

It is reported that von RAUCHENPLAT and Hilde OLDING were living together until about a year ago. Von RAUCHENPLAT is also stated to have worked for some time in connection with some secret B.R.C. radio station at Oxford. He is stated to have left there to do some work in connection with the special United States broadcasting station in this country.

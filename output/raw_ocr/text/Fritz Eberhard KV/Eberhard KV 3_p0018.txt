In reply to your memorandum of 26.2.40 we have no traces of [Hilda MONTE] P.F. 45000 neither is there any record of her with the Home Office.

Helmut VON RAUSCHENPLAT arrived in the U.K. 4.1.38 on an N/E condition. The object of his visit was to obtain further knowledge of English economic affairs. He intended taking a course and writing a book.

In September, 1939 we received an anonymous letter suggesting that VON RAUSCHENPLAT was qualified to be of assistance to the Intelligence in view of his economic knowledge and his information and contacts in Germany. This letter stated that between 1933 and 1937 he had helped to organise activities in Germany subversive to the Nazi Government. This information was passed to M.I.6.

We have nothing recorded to his detriment and have, in fact, heard indirectly on another occasion that he is a reliable individual. He was exempted from all restrictions by an Aliens Tribunal.

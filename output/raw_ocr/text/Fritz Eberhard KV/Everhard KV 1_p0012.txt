First European Congress for Nuclear Disarmament, held in London on 16th, 17th & 18th January 1959.

Further to our letter, OF.1383/E.1./JHL, of 14th January 1959, we now attach a list of 89 foreign delegates who are known to have attended this Conference. You will see that a number of individuals who had previously been expected to attend did not in the event do so. The list was obtained by checking against traffic index the names of those people whom the organisers of the Congress had invited to attend. It is possible that other foreign delegates came to the meeting, but if they did so it has not been possible to identify them.

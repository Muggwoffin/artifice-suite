CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: RAUSCHENPLAT Helmut Von.

Extract from B.4.b.2 Notes re individuals employed by P.I.D.

German, born Dresden, 2.10.96. According to an anonymous informant, RAUSCHENPLAT undertook administrative work in French occupied territory during the last war, and was later employed at various dates in municipal administration in business, as a teacher in a ballet school, and as a journalist. According to the same informant, between 1933 and 1937 he lived illegally in Germany, organising underground activities throughout the country. He arrived in the U.K. on 4.1.38 as a journalist and at first stated that he intended writing a book on state intervention in economics, which he hoped to publish in Switzerland. He is said to be a Socialist with no Communist sympathies, and according to an anonymous informant, RAUSCHENPLAT was extremely well informed in advance of the political events of 1939. At the end of 1939 RAUSCHENPLAT was a member of the Central European Joint Committee, which was engaged on the compilation of information from various continental sources on behalf of the Ministry of Information and other Government departments.

In Feb. 1940 RAUSCHENPLAT came to notice as a member of the propaganda section of the Demuth Committee.

Mr. H. RUSSELL is Hellmut von RAUSCHENPLATT of 6, Brunswick Sq. W.C.

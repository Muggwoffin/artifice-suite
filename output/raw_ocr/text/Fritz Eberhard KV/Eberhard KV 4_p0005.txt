London, September 9th, 1939.

War Ministry,
Intelligence Service,

Re: Germany.

Dear Sir,

Please, don't put this letter in your waste paper basket, although it is not signed. This letter is like an anonymous denunciation, in order to help liberty against dictatorship. My aims in this case are quite sober and clean ones. For I "denounce" to you not an enemy, but a friend of yours.

To be brief, I know a gentleman, whom you will find extremely valuable for helping you. You can't buy him. Now, in war time, he will probably be ready to cooperate with you.

His record: born about 1900, son of a family, which belongs to the old German nobility.

War service: mostly administrative work in French occupied territory.
Economic studies.
Employed in municipal administration and commerce, where he seems to be very efficient.
After becoming a socialist, he has broken with his family. Teacher in a school for adults. Journalist.
1933-37 or 38 he lived illegally in Germany, travelling over the country, organizing underground activities. He is very reluctant in telling details. But from to items I can conclude that he was the leader of important illegal organisations: I know his true name: by chance, I have read this name as that of the escaped leader in a detailed review of a trial at the Volksgerichtshof, which was given in the "Voelkischer Beobachter" in 1938.

He is a bachelor. As an adherent of the German philosophy Nelson, he is a liberal socialist and shows no communist tendencies.

Also 1939, he was excellently informed about the situation and the spirit of the German people. Obviously he is in close contact with friends in Germany. Thus he could tell me beforehand the reaction of the German people upon the Roosevelt message some months ago, and he foretold me the Nazi-Soviet approachment.

WHITBY,
YORKSHIRE.

Dr Hellimet von Ranichenplet.
London. W.C.1.

The Personnel Dept.
(I.A.)

The Lt. Postmaster has drawn my attention to the above points as stated to have been fairly bulky and may have contained photographs. He states that it was posted by what are believed to be refugees living at "Bueno Vista", a house in Sleights village, one of whom, (a lame man) it is alleged, has been observed taking photographs of a main road bridge at Sleights.

27 May 1940.

J. Hockis
Head Postmaster.

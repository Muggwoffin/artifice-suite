12th May, 1945.

Dear

Dr. Helmut von RAUSCHENPLAT.
Address: 2, Clifton Gardens, N.W.11.

Dr. RAUSCHENPLAT is the subject of your vetting form H.H.52 of 7.9.40.

Until recently he was a member of the ISK, a small group of moderate Socialist intellectuals. In Germany he was primarily connected with the Trades Union side of the movement. Since he came to England he has worked for both the Central European Joint Committee and P.W.K.

Nothing is known to his detriment from the security point of view.

Yours sincerely,

(sgd) Henry C.M. Stone.

Lt.-Colonel H.B. Holt,
P.I.D.,
Centre Block, Bush House,
Aldwych, W.C.

SECRET

L.158/1424/A.D.P.  16th June, 1944.

Dear Chambers,

I corresponded with Stewart Roberts, ending with his letter of 4th January, 1941, about an employee of yours named Dr. Helmut Von RAUSCHENPLAT. It has just been reported to me that Von RAUSCHENPLAT has taken up new work with the Americans, and I should be grateful if you could let me know if this is true, in order that I may keep my records up to date and check the reliability of the information.

As far as I can see at present there is no reason to view Von RAUSCHENPLAT with any suspicion, but I should like to have your opinion of him. I believe that he was working for the International Transport Workers Federation before he was taken on by you, and I should be most grateful if you would let me know whether he continued to work for them after becoming a member of your staff.

Yours sincerely,

R. H. Hollis.

Colonel P. R. Chambers,
P. W. K.

RUII/APB

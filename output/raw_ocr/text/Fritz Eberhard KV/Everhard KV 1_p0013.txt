Extract from S.B. Report on the Congress for Nuclear Disarmament. (Non-Communist) Memb: EBERHARD.

With reference to my report dated 19th January, 1959, on the first European Congress for Nuclear Disarmament, in which it was stated that enquiries were in hand to identify the delegates from abroad who attended the conference:-

The official list of foreign delegates, as prepared by the Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament, 143, Fleet Street, E.C.4, contained the names of eighty Europeans, all of whom were invited to attend either as delegates from similar organisations, or, in view of their high standing in their own countries, as personalities. Of these, all but seven attended, and in the event, others made the total up to eighty seven. They were as follows:-

EBERHARD, Fritz, councillor, born Dresden 2.10.96. German passport 3774419 Stuttgart 23.3.56. Arrived Dover 14.1. and left Gatwick 18.1.59. Stayed Tavistock Hotel.

Copy of B.A.O.R.19 Security Services report re Dr Wilhelm Wolfgang SCHUETZ. ment. RAUSCHENPLATT and GERMER.

Dr. Wilhelm Wolfgang SCHUETZ.

Please refer to our letter of even reference dated 30 Sep 54.

1. We have received a report, the source of which we believe is one of the anti-communist organisations in BONN. The report contains the following points:-

(a) SCHUTZ had tried to obtain the position of Geschäftsführer in "Unteilbares Deutschland" for a journalist named SCHUSTER who works for the "Suddeutsche Zeitung". SCHUSTER (who is known to us) is described in the report as being known for his neutralist articles and attitude and, further, to be a "stooge" of SCHUTZ.

(b) SCHUTZ collaborates closely with EBERHARD of the Suddeutsche Rundfunk, both men having known each other while emigres in England. It appears that EBERHARD is also known as Baron von RASCHENPLATT. He is stated to be "in close contact" with PFLEIDERER of the FDP and with Klaus MEHRENT of "Christ und Welt".

We have in our records a Helmuth con RAUSCHENPLATT, who, according to Gestapo records, was born on 2 Oct 96 in DRESDEN, was a member of the KFD and, in 1937, was reported to have fled abroad.

(c) Finally, SCHUTZ is stated to be closely connected with GERMER, described as the representative of the KAISER Ministry in BERLIN. GERMER is stated to have recommended in a recent memorandum that suitable West German personalities take the initiative sponsoring East/West talks.

A Karl GERMER, known to us from Gestapo records, was born in ELBERFELD on 22 Jan 85. He was allegedly a member of the KFD and, in 1937, was reported to have fled abroad.

2. The report concludes by drawing attention to "constant contact" between KAISER, the former Reich Chancellors BRUNING and LUTHER, and RAUSCHENING, three personalities who have attracted attention by their neutralistic attitude.

3. We should be glad to know whether you may have any trace of a von RAUSCHENPLATT (alias EBERHARD) or of a Karl GERMER having spent the war years in England.

Rose Cohen (Moscow) to Hig. - 30.10.23:

Dear Hig,

Your letter of the 21st, which arrived this morning, makes it easier for me to answer the earlier one. Though God knows, it's hard enough. What can I say? I loved getting your long chatty letters, full of news & your soul-stirring, & I should hate to do anything to stop these letters. If, however, it would be easier for you if you didn't hear from me, & if you find it impossible to write, then I suppose we had better both stop. I'm most frightfully sorry, Hig, but at the moment I don't feel I can respond to you in the way you want. I suppose I am what you would call an unfeeling cow. I think I must be. Of course, it's quite irrational & unreasonable. You & I are interested in the same things, we both seem to admire each other, & we obviously liked being together.

c. I do not know whether disproportion in realisation arises in our work. I could not work for a long time. You have been galling me slowly but surely for a long time now. Once again, you are responsible for Sonja and myself. I am prepared, indeed delighted to work. I am only staying here for your sake, and for your sake only.

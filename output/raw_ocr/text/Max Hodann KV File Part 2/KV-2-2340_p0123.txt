Max Lichtenstein

Reference your PF.43817, dated 17.4.35.:

According to the Max Lichtenstein gave his date of birth as 13.3.1889. and place Nordhausen, Germany.

He arrived in Copenhagen from Germany on 16.1.35. and was granted "permis de sejour" No.39219 valid till 30.5.35. He is living at Nørre Farimagsgade 68, Copenhagen, and has applied for an extension of his "permis de sejour". Max Lichtenstein has not come to unfavourable notice.

It is perhaps more than a coincidence that the Max Lichtenstein, mentioned in my CX/6561 of 13.10.34. was born on the 13th April and that the Max Lichtenstein, at present in Copenhagen, was born on the 13th March, 1889. It would seem possible that the two are identical and that the pseudonymous informant made an error over the month of birth.

Miss Weeks.

Translation of Int. V 269K.

Dear HODANN,

We must presume, from the length in time you have been in writing, that you are still busy with laboratory work. As we do not willingly impute (?) that, we congratulate you on your recovery. Moreover, how are things going? Everything is proceeding happily with us. Every day now brings rising security in handwork.

A reply has just come from DANG. He does not go into detail, but says that one must set off shortly, as 'his' country confers no guarantees, nor for anything previous. He gives us every hope, for everyone who arrives will receive help at all events. The people are doing a great deal, but only for those who try to swim. Thus swimming, us! when! ! ?

In the meantime Germania has allowed herself the joke of making you a non-resident citizen. Hold on tight to your passport, and do not make too much fuss about this circumstance, as otherwise the journey in without a passport would create unnecessarily great difficulties.

At all events send for a copy of your birth certificate from the registrar of your birth-place and also your certificate of good conduct. All this has nothing to do with the non-resident citizenship and even 'serious criminals' like you can get them.

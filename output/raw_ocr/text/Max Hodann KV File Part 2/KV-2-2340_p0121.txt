98a Mill Road
12th June 1925

Dear Dr. Finkelstein,

Edge and I would like to come on Friday evening, but if this is not convenient we can manage Saturday. You can either write to me or telephone Pembroke College (Cambridge 2000) and ask the porter to take down a message for me and give it to me when I come into college. In either case, Friday or Saturday, what time shall we come?

Yours sincerely,

(signed) Lionel Pendlebury.

(PP 65384)
(Pembroke 1931-35)

Fres. John Edge
(PF 69378)
(Pembroke 1933-36)

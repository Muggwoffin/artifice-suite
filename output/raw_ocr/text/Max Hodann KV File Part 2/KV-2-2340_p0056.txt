From  ?, Berlin.

To  Alexis FINKELSTEIN, 13, Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter  -

Date of Postmark  8.7.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges invitation to stay which she cannot accept as she has just received a cable from Rupert saying that her visit is impossible.

Hopes one day to have the opportunity of making addressee's acquaintance.

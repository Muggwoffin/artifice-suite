From Mrs. Dora RUSSELL, Beacon Hill School, Boyles Court, South Weald, Nr. Brentwood, Essex.

To Dr. Max HODANN, 63, Haverstock Hill, N.W.3.

Date of Letter 20.6.35.

Date of Postmark 21.6.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Writer was glad to hear from addressee "and to learn that you both are well".

She will be able "to take the three of you at the terms you mention, i.e. £5 per week". Would like to know when to expect them, this will help her in making arrangements for others who wish to come during the summer.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

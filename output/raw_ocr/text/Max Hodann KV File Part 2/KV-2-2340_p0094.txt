June 20th, 1935.

Dear Max Hodann,

Mrs. Russell was glad to get your letter and to learn that you both are well. She will be able to take the three of you at the terms you mention, i.e. £5 per week. Will you be so good as to let her know at the earliest possible moment, when she can expect you and how long you will want to stay. This will help her in making arrangements for others who wish to come during the summer.

With best wishes,
Yours sincerely,
P.P. [Mrs. Dora Russell]
Secretary

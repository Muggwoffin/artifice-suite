P.F. 43817. HODANN.

CROSS-REFERENCE.

Subject:— HODANN. Max

Int: letter from Molla ORDING, Copenhagen to Lise LINDBAEK-NILSSON dated 11.7.35. W.629.B.

Molla ORDING states that Max HODANN's permit for staying in Norway will very soon run out "so had he not better try to get it made a permanency? A lot of emigrants have managed that. In that way the government is fairly all right."

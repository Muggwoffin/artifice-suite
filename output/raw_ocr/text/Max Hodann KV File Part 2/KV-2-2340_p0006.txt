Dear Major Vivian,

Will you please refer to your CX.1446 of 11.7.35 concerning the arrival in Norway of Leon TROTSKY.

In this connection and also in connection with previous correspondence regarding Dr Arne ORDING, you may like to have the attached translation of a letter addressed to ORDING from an individual in Oslo named Hans VOGT, "Universitetsstipendiat", of Parkveien 13. We have no trace of him.

For easy reference I attach a copy of your CX.5079 of 21.10.20 and CX.7196 of 20.12.20 which formed part of a series of reports from you about that date concerning Olaf SCHEFLO who is mentioned in the body of the attached letter.

PFR 1571

It is interesting to note from a report of yours CX.4404 of 20.0.20 that Emil STANG, Olaf SCHEFLO and Martin TRANMAEL (your latest report CX.5004 of 5.11.31) were all professors at a Bolshevik Propaganda School opened in Christiania about that date.

We have not previously heard of TROTSKY's courier, HELD.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E., S.I.S.

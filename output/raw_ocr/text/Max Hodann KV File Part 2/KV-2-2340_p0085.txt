Extract from H.O. file R.689 [O.L. RUSSBULDT] P.F.42816.

19.5.35. Gertrude EATON, 48, Hampstead Way, N.W.11, wrote to the Home Office on behalf of several Germans:

"Then another friend brought an Austrian - an older man - who wishes to study chemistry in England. His name is Fritz KILLIAN, 14, Calthorpe Street, Grays Inn Road, W.C.1.

"I understand they have all been told their time is up this month - Fritz KILLIAN was a member of the Austrian Guard (Schutzbund) under the last Government, so is not in sympathy with the present regime, but I do not think he has been in any trouble beyond not agreeing politically with the present regime. He is a friend of Mr. Lebomann Russbildt the writer - who brought him to see me."

Lehmann Russbildt

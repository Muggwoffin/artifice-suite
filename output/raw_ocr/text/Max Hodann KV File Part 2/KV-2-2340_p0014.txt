From Traute NEUMANN, Gennemløbet 17, Vanlose, Copenhagen.

To Max HODANN, 63, Haverstock Hill, N.W.3.

Date of Letter 9.7.35
Date of Postmark 9.7.35

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Has had an accident but has recovered to a certain extent.

Refers to Sonja (his daughter).

Understands that addressee is living in a better way than she. "If you leave me in the lurch and if I cannot carry on any longer (as in this month) I shall not hesitate to come to you."

States that she can only go to the Argentine if the return journey is assured as she does not want to leave Germany for ever.

"The S.U. - I have not to station myself there! On the contrary I have to report truthfully to them over there, why I want to go there. These matters are settled by comrades............"

Reminds addressee again that he is responsible for her and Sonja.

For translation see attached sheet.

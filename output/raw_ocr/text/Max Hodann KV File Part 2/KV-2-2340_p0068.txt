From SANDELMANN, 8, Chemin des Clochettes, Geneva.

To Dr. M. HODANN, 63, Haverstock Hill, N.W.3.

Date of Letter 1.7.35.

Date of Postmark -

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Will probably come to London at the beginning of August for 6 weeks. Will stay in the house of American friends who will then be visiting the Continent. "The address is Barons Court, Barons Court Road, W.14."

"I am hoping to be able to discuss many fundamental questions with you."

States that he will be going alone to the Argentine in November where he will help in the work for the refugees.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

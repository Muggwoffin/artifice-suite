Translation of Int. W535C.

To Herr Max HODANN.

Dear Sir,

Some time ago we sent you the call of the "Counter-Attack", "One must do something", with the request as to the attitude you take towards the important problem of a German united front against the Hitler Terror and for the victims of the Hitler Fascism.

Up till now, we have unfortunately not had any statement of your opinion on this matter. We beg you to let us know by return of post your attitude.

With anti-fascist greetings,

AW. 12/7.

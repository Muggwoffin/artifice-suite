From RICHTER, Hans & Ella, 3, Claremont Gardens, Surbiton

To Arne ORDING, 1, Britannia Street, W.C.1.

Date of Letter 6.6.35.
Date of Postmark 6.6.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Asks addressee to come down for Whit Sunday or Monday.

"Fritz will send me further numbers of the "New Front". I would ask you to pass these to Max Ho. You know that up to now I have refrained from sending anything to him direct as I do not under what circumstances he lives here. If you are going to send these on by post please send them in a closed envelope............"

Asks addressee, if he see HODANN, to arrange a meeting sometime.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT
(a) No action required. P.A.

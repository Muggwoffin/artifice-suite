How are your studies going in so far as OUR aim is concerned?

Have you had any success with your first inquiries?

What else can we do for you?

What is the position with Geneva?    Place of help for academicians.

Could you please do my trifling messages at Rolls Razor.

Everything, but also all outlay will be promptly compensated.

Please do not write too like a telegram.

With comradely greetings,

Your Katy and Gutse(?) REITH.

AW. 19/6/35.

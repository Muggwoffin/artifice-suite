X/12650/2082, dated 13.6.35.

I have no record of Traute NEUMANN, isbeth BROCH, or Max HODANN, nor has Professor Arne ORDING previously come to notice. "Tavastehus" is the Finnish town in which Mrs. MART is in prison.

In general from the letters passing between Molla ORDING, Mrs. MARTIN and Lisa LINDBAEK, the have gained the impression that the Stang-Ording-Lindbaek-Hodann group is more closely linked up with the Soviet Intelligence than was at first imagined. They draw attention to the fact that Professor Arne ORDING arrived in the U.K. to study, and that JACOBSEN of the Martin case visited inland under the pretext of studying for the Helsingfors University.

My personal view is that the association of these persons is suspicious, particularly in view of Max HODANN's previous connection with the Soviet Military Intelligence in Berlin, but the two letters are hardly conspirative.

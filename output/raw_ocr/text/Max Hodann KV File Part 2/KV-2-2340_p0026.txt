Translation of Int. W505M.

To Max HODANN,

Dear Max,

I had an accident. I have recovered to a certain extent, but I can't write with a machine yet.

K.St. wrote you in detail I believe about the money. I have received up to to-day 80 Kr.

Sonja is spending her holidays in Birnholm. She sent you the closed letter to-day. Her address is S.Heo hrs Philipsborn, Holshavn-Birnholm, Post Srancke. I cannot say how long she can stay.

I have made enquiries about the cost. I am glad that she has the opportunity of being at the sea. She needs it. (Long account of a school report).

In answer to your letter: I must treat your proposals with caution, not with opposition, as you say. You know my reasons for that.

1.) I don't think I am over-estimating your capabilities, which I have always admired, when I assume that you, like Helmut G. for example must succeed in carrying on without assistance. Once again, Max, I am not here for my own pleasure, and I am not here to rob you. I am not prepared to conduct a household for us three, and I tell you quite seriously, if you leave me in the lurch and if I cannot carry on any longer (as in this month), I shall not hesitate to come to you. I wrote you this once before and you did not pay any attention. I have found out that you live in a much better way than I do.

2) Yes, I have written to DANG. I asked him to wait till October. I am not letting myself be hired out like a worn boat (the further the better.) I have to discuss these things first with Kurt. I shan't be able to do that until August, and only then if he gets a tourist's marks. I cannot invite him. And there are no more concessions for July. This step depends upon the discussion with him.

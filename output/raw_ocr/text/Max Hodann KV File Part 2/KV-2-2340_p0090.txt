Translation of Int. V 600 K.

Dear HODANN,

Many thanks for your card. I am looking forward very much to seeing you and hope therefore that it will be possible for you to come here until Wednesday. I have to go away to a conference in the South on Thursday and will be away for a week. My wife is here, but if possible, I would like to see you myself. Please let me know.

We are well known in the Settlement as my wife works there. Are you staying there? We can easily put you up.

Best wishes,

Yours,

Yesterday we all connected with the Conference. We went to the Agricultural Exhibition, & received a tremendous ovation from thousands who had gathered there. It was a great sight looking down on the sea of heads. God, they are a wonderful people. They stood for hours & hours listening to speeches in strange tongues - all the time I was there, about 3 hours, hardly a soul stirred - and every speech was translated. They love talking here. In the evening we were entertained by the Village Co-ops, who gave us an enormous dinner, & still they went on talking, saying the same old thing over & over again. Tonight there will be more speeches in a grand final build-up at the Bolshoi Theatre, ending up with Boris Godonov. I'm going late. I feel I couldn't bear another speech.

I feel very / worry indeed about the Dept. now - Robin's shoulders don't seem to be broad enough to bear it alone. Tell me, what is happening to Rath? I hear no word of him. Clemens too, has he been fixed up yet? Sorry to ploy you with so many questions, but I hunger for news.

By the way, could you make enquiries about my 'w.w.' I haven't received it since Sept. 15. I thought that No. was particularly good. The T.U.C. makes a splendid kick - H6 for a Left-wing opposition. I hear you are now staying with H.C., which strikes me as a damn good idea, particularly as he's given to early rising and is, I understand, a man of sober habits! Give him my love and tell him to guard against double chins.

Please write to me again when you have a spare moment.

Thanks again for the books. They have already begun to go around of the Lune, although they have just arrived.

Ever Rose.

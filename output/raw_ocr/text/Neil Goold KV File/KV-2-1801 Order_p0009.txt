Ref.: G2/C/109.

I attach copy of a report which appeared in the Dublin "Evening Herald" of the 14th ultimo in which it is alleged that a young Irishman, Brian Goold-Verschoyle, was spirited out of Spain in 1937 by a Russian agent and that nothing has been heard of him since.

I have no knowledge of this man but I do know something of the activities of his brother, Niall Goold-Verschoyle, some particulars of which I am attaching for your information. (Yr. Ref. PF.49182/B.9a of 30/3/'41.)

While I am rather anxious to get some information on Brian Goold-Verschoyle, especially in regard to the authenticity or otherwise of the "Evening Herald" story, I do not, at the same time, want you to put any inquiries in train which would be likely to cause you any trouble or inconvenience.

21st April, 1949.

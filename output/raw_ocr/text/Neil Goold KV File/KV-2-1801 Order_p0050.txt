RHQ St. Alketa Road
Cabra
Dublin
Ireland
19.4.41

To
Olga Ivanovna Dolrova
Moscow 21
Post Restante

Dear Madam,

I am instructed by your husband to write to you & tell you that he is in an internment camp and will be unable to write to you for some time. We worked very closely together and I miss him very much. We were both members of the C.P.I. here & our work placed us very much together. He was often in our home & was loved by all our family and everybody that knew him to work with. We often joked about you coming over here for a holiday even but now this is impossible. He often showed me your photo & the children's. I am sure he would like some fresh ones. Could you send some?

He allowed some reading books, papers. If a kind passed by cancer. You may write through me to him if you wish too.

We expect to go to Moscow some time in the future. Any questions about him that you would like to know I will do my best to answer. My best regards from him to you & myself also to the boys. Greetings to R.S.S.R.

Thomas Reddett Kelly
Tim Walsh

P.F.41426/B.9a

Mrs. Archer
S.IS.

Thank you for your QK/897 of 15.5.41 giving the news of the arrest of 15 Communists, including Niall GOULD.

I think this must be your old friend, Hamilton Neil Stuart GOULD-VERSCHOYLE who goes by the name of Neil GOULD in the Communist circles of Dublin where he is active. According to information given to Kimpton of this office (who knows the whole family), by Neil's sister, Neil GOULD-VERSCHOYLE, went to Russia where he had married and been engaged as a state servant in education and broadcasting. He had then been expelled from Russia without being allowed to take his wife and child with him. Apparently he took the view that his expulsion was entirely correct because the Russians had doubts of his bona-fides, and Russia was so permeated with foreign agents that they could not afford to take any risks. He hoped, however, that his work for the Communist Party in Ireland would convince them that he was an ardent and bona-fide Communist and fit to be allowed to return to Russia. According to an article in the January, 1941 issue of "Irish Freedom", Frank Aiken attacked the Dublin workers' leader associated with the unemployed and tenants movement in Dublin "for being here under several aliases". The article goes on to say that since Neil had become associated with the workers' movement in Dublin he had played a leading part in the unemployed demonstrations, and had been thrown into prison many times. The police had singled him out for special attention and even tried to have him certified as insane to stop his activities. The article closes:

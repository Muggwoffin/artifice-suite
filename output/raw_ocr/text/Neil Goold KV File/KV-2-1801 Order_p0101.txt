1st March 1933

Dear Comrade,

Thanks for your letter of the 27th. I am afraid I am not likely to be in London in the near future.

The above will be my address for some time, my permanent address being The Manor House, Dunkin-eely, Co Donegal, Ireland.

The name and address of the student in question who is a friend of mine in Moscow is as follows:-

R. Novitzki,
MOSCOW
Moskvoretskaya Ulitza, House 32/5 Room 1.

He writes for Moscow Economical Periodicals especially on problems connected with the British Isles, and would therefore like to get into touch with a worker who could let him know something about conditions first hand. He is also anxious, I think, to improve his English, which I hope is sufficient to start a correspondence. As I suggested in my first letter someone who is anxious to learn Russian might write to him, mentioning my name, and fix up some kind of exchange.

Yours sincerely,

Neil Gould Verschafle

GARDA SIOCHANA

Dublin Metropolitan Division.
Assistant Commissioner's Office,
Dublin Castle.

6th April 1939.

The Commissioner of Police,
of the Metropolis,
New Scotland Yard,
LONDON, S.W.1.

Sir,

Hamilton N.S. Gould Verschoyle charged with loitering at Dublin - in possession of Communist Literature.

I beg to forward for your information the annexed copy of report relative to above subject, and to request that you kindly cause the suggested action to be taken and favour me with a report as soon as convenient.

I am, Sir,
Your obedient Servant,

----- Chief Superintendent.
for ASSISTANT COMMISSIONER.

Extract from S.I.S. report on a lecture given at S.I.N.N FEIN H.Q. in Dublin on 30th October, 1944.

...... Neil GOULD, recently released I.R.A. prisoner, asked the chairman, Mrs Margaret BUCKLEY, if he could propose a resolution about Charles KERINS, found guilty by the Special Military Court of the murder of Det. Sergt. O'Brien...... The resolution was to the effect that that this assembly congratulates KERINS on his resolute stand for the Irish Republic before the Military Court, demands the abolition of the Special Military Court, protests against the proposed murder of an Irish Republican soldier, and demands his immediate release.............

Neil GOULD suggested the resolution be sent to the Press and to the Minister for Justice.....................

This is the first intimation that Neil GOULD had been released. (vide your report 92/IRA/HO/B.I.H. of 1.8.44.)

Extract from list of MOOS' contacts mentioning GOOLD-VERSCHOYLE

MOOS said that Brian GOOLD VERSCHOYLE originally went to Russia in 1933 and it was then that he was recruited by the Ogpu. He was recruited through Loyla, the wife of Neil GOOLD VERSCHOYLE. Loyla was an Ogpu agent. She introduced him to three people. They told him to go back home sever his connection with the British C.P. and to go nothing until he received instructions.

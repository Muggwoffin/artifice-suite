PASSPORT OFFICE,
Dartmouth Street,
London, S.W.1.

S/F. 2nd March, 1936.

CONFIDENTIAL.

Dear Miss Sissmore,

With reference to your letter, P.F.41426/D.S.10 of the 27th February, I write to say that I am including the name of Brian Goold Verschoyle in the next circular to Consuls containing the list of individuals from whose passports the British Empire endorsement should be deleted.

As regards Hamilton Neil Goold Verschoyle, it is, as you say, probable that this individual is the same person as the Niall de Gold Vershoyle whose name was included in the list supplied by the Irish Free State authorities in 1933. As he now holds an Irish Free State passport, it will be necessary to refer to the High Commissioner for the Irish Free State in the event of his making any further application for passport facilities to us in the future.

Yours sincerely,

[hand signed]

Miss K.M.M. Sissmore,
Box 500.
Parliament Street B.O.,
S.W.1.

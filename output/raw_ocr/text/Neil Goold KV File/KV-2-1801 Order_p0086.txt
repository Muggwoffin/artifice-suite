GARDA SIOCHANA.

D.B. 1046/39.

D.M.D. 4579/39.

6th April 1939.

Assistant Commissioner,
Dublin Castle.

Hamilton N.S. Gould Verschoyle charged with loitering at Dublin - in possession of Communist literature.

I beg to report that the above named was arrested in Dublin at 1.5 a.m., on 1.4.39., and charged with loitering and being in possession of housebreaking implements. He was remanded on bail to 8.4.39.

When searched he had in his possession a quantity of Communist literature and some private correspondence. He is known to be actively associated with the Communist Movement both here and in England, having first come under notice here in April 1938, when he arrived from London.

There is nothing known here regarding this man's antecedents, activities or general character prior to his arrival in this City and I respectfully request that a copy of this report be forwarded to the Commissioner of Police at New Scotland Yard for favour of a report as to what is known regarding him there.

I also respectfully request that a copy of this report, together with copies of the following correspondence:-

(a). Letter written from No. 22, The Square, Winchester, signed 'Sheila' and addressed to H.E. Smith, Summer House, Fisher's Pond, Fair Owl, Eastleigh, Hants.

(b). Letter dated 29th March, Wed. bearing the address, The Square, Winchester, and signed 'Sheila'.

(c). Post card addressed to 'Mrs. Fitzgerald, 22, The Square, Winchester' from 10 Upland Park Road, and signed M.F.

be forwarded to the Police at Winchester for favour of enquiries at the address shown and for report as to what is known of Verschoyle to the Police there.

(sgd). T. Maher. Supt.

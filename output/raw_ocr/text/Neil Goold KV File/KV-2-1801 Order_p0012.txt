Subsequent to this incident, Goold appears to have ceased all activities and has not since come under unfavourable notice. He is at present living by himself in a hut or cottage in Co. Waterford and earns a meagre living by helping local farmers.

Although a beneficiary to a considerable sum of money left by his father, he declines to have anything to do with it. This is in keeping with his attitude during his years of Communist activity when, although reputedly wealthy, he lived in an excess of poverty to demonstrate his oneness with the Proletariat.

He is described as quite selfless and fanatical.

13th April, 1949.

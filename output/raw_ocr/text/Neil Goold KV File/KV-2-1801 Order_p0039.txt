Inspector General's Office,
Royal Ulster Constabulary,
Belfast.
13th October, 1942.

Dear Stephenson,

Your reference P.F.41426/B.I.H. of 5th instant concerning GOOLD-VERSCHOYLE, I think you might be interested in the attached copy of an intercepted letter from The Cardinal to De Valera.

The gentlemen concerned may "be nearly in the purple" but any GOOLDS that I ever knew had extremely "Parrot-like Noses" and held their Sundays on a different day to the majority of the population.

Yours sincerely,

Morgan Lewis

John Stephenson, Esq.,
Box No. 500,
Parliament Street B.O.,
London, S.W.1.

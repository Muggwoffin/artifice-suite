P.F.41426/B.I.H.

Mrs. Archer
S.I.S.

I attach a copy of a letter from the Cardinal to the Taoiseach, which I beg of you to treat as most secret.

It shows that the Cardinal is not concerned with releasing GOOLD as stated in my P.F.41426/B.I.H., dated 5.10.42, but in removing him from his fellow prisoners who are of the Cardinal's persuasion.

John Stephenson

21st October, 1942
JFES/VS

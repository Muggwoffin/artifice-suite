IRISH PRESS
21.12.45

Trial Date Fixed For Two Men
Nial Goold, Iveagh Buildings, and Joseph O'Connor, 94D Lower Dorset Street, appeared before the Special Criminal Court in Dublin yesterday, charged with membership of an unlawful association—the Republican Unity Committee—and with attempting to usurp the functions of the Government by advocating the formation and maintenance of an armed force not authorised by the Constitution.

The Court fixed January 2 as the date of trial. Mr. L. J. Lysaght (Chief State Solicitor's Office) said he would not oppose an application for bail.

Goold said: "I have no undertaking to give."
O'Connor said: "I refuse to give any undertaking."
Both were remanded in custody.

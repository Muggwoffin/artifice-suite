One of Brian's brothers is Hamilton Neil Stuart GOOLD-VERSCHOYLE, who is known to be a very active Communist in Ireland. Between 1930 and 1934 Neil was employed in Russia as a translator. He was subsequently expelled from Russia and forced to leave without his Russian wife and children. He maintained that Moscow's action in expelling him was only reasonable, since doubts had been entertained about his bona fides. He hoped to convince Moscow of his loyalty by his subsequent Communist activities.

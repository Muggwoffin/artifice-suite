25-2-41.

Dear Bagenal,

This is just a further personal note in reply to yours received six days after dispatch.

I am terribly sorry to hear that you have been so dangerously ill and hope that you will be on the active list by the time this letter reaches you.

I went to Belfast and saw our old friend Tommie, he has begun to work in an aircraft factory and although I tried hard to get him to come down here he found himself unable to do this in view of a whole number of circumstances arising.

This means that I will have to do a lot myself. The N.C. agrees with our view of the situation here, and we have developed that line in the course of discussions which are now proceeding.

The tendencies to economism which Watters raised in relation to Belfast are also strongly noticeable in Dublin, too—in fact our old friend Gould opposes our main line and in all his work is carrying a line opposite to that of the Party. I am enclosing a number of copies of a Bulletin which he is issuing on behalf of a tenants Committee which should be carefully read. Pass on to Jimmie. I have arranged a meeting of the N.C. for this weekend in which decisions will be arrived at affecting the future of our leadership. I will see to it that there is a thorough review of the works position etc., I have established my position as a consultative member of the N.C. and am attending all leading Committees.

Here it is impossible to elaborate, but the immediate aim in Dublin and Belfast is to get Conferences of T.U. and Labour militants to endorse a line for a Rank and File Convention to break down the isolation of the Left on the economic and political issues facing the people as a result of the invasion threat, blockade, gag of the 'political truce' etc., steps are now being taken to carry out a drive all round for the summoning of such Conferences.

However I will send you the fullest possible report next week and will you explain to the boys that all the initial difficulties one meets in the first few weeks have prevented me writing at length. Will you press for the immediate operation of that levy Harry proposed? Also tell Tramm I have heard from her twice—for which many thanks.

I sent you an order for lites. Will be glad to hear when it is dispatched. We have had no stuff for a week now.

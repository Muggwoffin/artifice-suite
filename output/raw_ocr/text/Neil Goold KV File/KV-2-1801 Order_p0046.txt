Copy for 411/ Ireland

P.F. 41426/F.2a/E.I.C. 23rd August, 1941.

Dear Jane,

Reference your QK897 of 18th May and QK 1163 of 14th July, 1941 I am not quite clear whether all fifteen of the arrested Communists were subsequently interned or only Neil Goold VERSCHOYLE.

I do not know whether you are in a position to probe this matter further, but any details and the names of the arrested men would naturally be of great interest to us here.

Yours sincerely,

Mrs. Archer,
S.I.S.

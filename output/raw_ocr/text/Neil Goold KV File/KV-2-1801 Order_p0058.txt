SECRET

12th May 1941.

ARRESTS IN DUBLIN.

[Redacted] reports that 15 known Communists were arrested in Dublin on Friday, 3.5.41, and are at present in Bridewell Gaol. The reason for these arrests was the attempted distribution of pamphlets advocating non-payment of rent by certain Dublin Corporation tenants. One of the arrested men is Niall GOULD, a well known Communist.

This report has been confirmed by an occasional source who thought that the arrested men belonged to the I.R.A., but [redacted] version seems the more authentic.

NO ACTION TO BE TAKEN WITHOUT REFERENCE TO B.I.H.

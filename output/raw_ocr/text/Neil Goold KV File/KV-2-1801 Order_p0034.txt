"....The only development of any interest outside No.1. Internment Camp has been the formation of a new I.R.A.-Communist Group under the leadership of Niall Gould, whose activities in this direction can be traced back to his sojourn in No.1. Internment Camp where he succeeded in getting a small following. Gould has devoted considerable energy to this project by keeping in contact with internees and ex-internees and supplying them with the immediate task of this body is to work for the unity of the Irish Labour Party and to recruit new blood for the Communist Party of Ireland from the younger members of the I.R.A. who would ultimately coalesce with the Labour Youth Movement to form the nucleus of a militant section of the Communist Party. The total strength of this group at the moment is 35 members, and there is no reason to regard it at this stage as a serious menace...."

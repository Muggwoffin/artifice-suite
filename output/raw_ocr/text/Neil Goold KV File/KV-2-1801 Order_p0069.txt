Mr. Godwin of the Passport Office asked me if we had any recent information about Brian Goold VERSCHOYLE. He said that VERSCHOYLE's mother had been making enquiries through the Foreign Office as he had been missing in Spain since April, 1937.

She herself believed that he had been abducted and taken to Russia.

I told him that our last information was that VERSCHOYLE had left this country in February, 1936.

Extract from letter from Royal Ulster Constabulary, Belfast re Communist activities in Dublin and mentioning GOULD.

"I have received information that the Communist Party in Dublin is at present unofficially divided into two sections, and the other under Neill GOULD, Iveagh House, Bride Street, Dublin.

It appears that Gould is a clever and well-educated young man, and while interned in the Curragh a few years ago, owing to his political activities, succeeded in recruiting most of the internees with whom he came in contact to his aid. He and his followers are at present arranging that a small delegation be sent to Belfast in the near future to gain the support of the Belfast Group with a view to re-organising the Party in Dublin, with himself in full control of the Party's Bookshop at 16a, Pearse Street, Dublin.

PTO.

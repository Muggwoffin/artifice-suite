COMMENT.

Letter to Russia from Political Prisoner interned in Eire.

Translation by Examiner 4252.

"Dear Olga",

As they have already written you, I have been sent for an indefinite period to a Camp for political prisoners, for taking part in a worker's movement. I have already been here for 2 1/2 months. We watch with great pride the feats of the Red Army and the Soviet population.

Greetings to you, the children and the unconquerable country.

Your Neil.

P.S. I cannot write often here."

S.M.M.

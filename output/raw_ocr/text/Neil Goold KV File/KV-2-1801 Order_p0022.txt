TO-MORROW NIGHT'S STADIUM BILL

FOUR Birkenhead boxers, one of whom—V. Harrison—has boxed in Dublin twice already this season, figure on the bill for the Terenure-Rathfarnham Boxing Club's tourney at the Stadium, Dublin, to-morrow night. Following is the full programme:—
Cruiser—M. Behan (Myra) v. J. Kennedy (St. Canice's). Welter—V. Harrison (Birkenhead) v. T. Kavanagh (Scouts), J. Kealy (Terenure) v. M. O'Neill (Guinness).
Light—Mark Clark (Birkenhead) v. P. Fitzsimons (Terenure), J. Kershaw (Terenure) v. J. Ryan (St. Agnes), J. O'Brien (Terenure) v. V. Harris (Mount Street).
Featherweight—P. Maguire (Pollkoff) v. D. Grant (Birkenhead). Bantam—P. Maguire (Terenure) v. T. Dennis (St. Vincents), T. Mardy (Terenure) v. Tom Thomas (Birkenhead). Juvenile—M. Hyland (Rathfarnham) v. T. Kiernan (Terenure); L. Kelly (Rathfarnham) v. J. Ramsey (Terenure).

Amateur Boxing Affairs

IT was stated at the meeting of the Standing Committee of the Irish Amateur Boxing Association that enquiries were being made into the reasons for the non-appearance of three boxers billed to appear in the Sandymount tourney bill last Friday.

The International team to meet Scotland in Dublin on January 25 will not be selected until after the Internationals with Wales on January 11 and 13 in Dublin and Limerick, respectively.

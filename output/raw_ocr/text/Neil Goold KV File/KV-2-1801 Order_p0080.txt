He never heard from these people but received letters from Irina and Loila. His brother Neil helped him to find a job at the B.B.C. When one year was over, Brian went again to Russia. He was reprimanded for having come and was told to go back. He stayed again with his brother and with Irina H. (who had divorced her husband) and was very enthusiastic. In May/June 1935 he went again. This time he was almost arrested for not staying in the Intourist Hotel, he could not see the two people, but met somebody else, a person called Felix (I am not sure about that) and an engineer (Porcupine). He was asked about his recordings at the B.B.C. and whether he had many aristocratic connections. (Loila made a lot of the family's aristocratic connections to enhance B's value. But B. as a matter of fact, had none.)

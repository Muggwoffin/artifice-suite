I would very much like to know if Mac is still in existence. I wrote to him but have heard naught.

If Celia still happens to be hanging around (I would not be surprised) tell her I still think she is a charming girl, but she is really the only woman I know who regards a return home as something in the nature of a Polar Expedition— I suggest that she should get a complete technical, scientific and commercial staff around her it might help to hurry her arrangements to arrive in about six months time!

I have not written to any of your friends yet in the provinces but will do so as soon as the air here is cleared a bit.

Incidentally it is extremely important to send O.P.S.U. historys here— send a couple of dozen I will see to it that you get paid for them.

Keep writing to me in a personal way, Bagenal, and let me know all you can about what the boys feel about things, advice etc.

I hope the Spring misses you as the Autumn did us both.

All the best and regards to [illegible].

Jim Hendren

P.S. Got three copies Irish P. from Bill Scott. Send more.

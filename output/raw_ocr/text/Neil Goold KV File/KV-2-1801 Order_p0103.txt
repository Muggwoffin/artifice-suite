PORT of HARWICH.

SPECIAL BRANCH
Ref. 336/MP/2014
Date 4/1/33
To M.I.5.

3rd. January, 3

Arrived here this morning from Hook of Holland, Hamilton Nie Stuart GOODVERSCHOYLE, in possession of Irish Free State Passport No. A 11399, in which he is stated to be a Student.

It was ascertained that this man had been in Moscow for about three years, and his occupation whilst there was a Translator.

His baggage was searched by Customs but it failed to reveal anything of interest to Police.

He proceeded to London.

R. Glasscock
Sergeant.

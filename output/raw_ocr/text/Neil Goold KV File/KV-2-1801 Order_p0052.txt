COMMENT.
The writer, an Irishman in Dublin, is in touch with a Russian now in an internment camp and writes to his wife in Moscow.
The Irishman and the Russian worked together and belonged to the "Communist Party of Ireland".
Extracts:-
"I am instructed by your husband to write to you and tell you that he is in an internment camp and will be unable to write to you for some time. We worked very closely together and I miss him very much. We were both members of the G.P.I. here and our work placed us very much together."
"You may write through me to him if you wish too. We expect to go to Moscow sometime in the future. Any questions about him that you would like to know I will do my best to answer. Boy's Greetings to C.S.R."

INTERNAL MEMORANDUM.

From... B.4b(2)............................. To.............B.9.............549

With reference to the attached Censor's Comment and copy of letter, I do not understand the reference to the internment of DOLROVA's husband. Surely the Irish have not interned a Russian subject! Of course he may be her husband by Russian law only and is not himself necessarily a Soviet citizen.

Date.............25.7.41.

Signature.............

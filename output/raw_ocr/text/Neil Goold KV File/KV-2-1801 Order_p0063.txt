INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

From: B.9.a.        To: B.4.a.2.

It appears to me that Comrade N. GOOLD, who is mentioned at the bottom of the first column of Nolan's article, on the last page of this Irish Workers' Weekly, may be identical with H.N.S. (Neil) GOOLD-VERSCHOYLE, the subject of P.F. 41426.

Neil GOOLD-VERSCHOYLE is believed, by Kimpton, who knows the family, to be still in Eire; and it seems possible that he is conducting his Communist activities there, under the less aristocratic of his full names.

20.1.41.        John Stephenson.

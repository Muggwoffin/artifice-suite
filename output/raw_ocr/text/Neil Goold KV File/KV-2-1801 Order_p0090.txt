P.F. 41426. GOULD-VERS-CHOYLE.

CROSS-REFERENCE.

Subject:— GOULD-VERSCHOYLE, H.S.

11.8.32. The undermentioned British subjects arrived at Hay's Wharf to-day per the s.s. Dzerjinsky from Leningrad:—

GOULD-VERSCHOYLE, Hamilton Stuart. Free State Passport No. A. 11399.

As these passengers did not fill in landing cards it was impossible to obtain their addresses.

S.B. 11.8.1932.

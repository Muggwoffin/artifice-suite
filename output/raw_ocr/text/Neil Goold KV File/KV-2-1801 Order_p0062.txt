MR. AIKEN AND NEIL GOOLD
The Minister also used the same occasion for an attack on Dublin workers' leader associated with the unemployed and tenants movement whom he accused of being "here under several aliases." The man in question is Hamilton Neil Stuart Goold-Verscoyle and comes from a well-known Donegal family. Since Neil became associated with the workers' movement in Dublin he has played a leading part in unemployed demonstrations. His activity has singled him out for special attention from the police. He has been thrown into prison many times, and once the police authorities tried to have him certified as insane to stop his activities.
His modesty led him to drop his high-sounding name, and he is known simply as Neil Goold. In a recent issue of the "Irish Workers Weekly" he writes a letter asking Aiken by what law he has no right to use his own name. How about it, Mr. Aiken—why not admit that sometimes you speak in the approved Goebbels fashion?

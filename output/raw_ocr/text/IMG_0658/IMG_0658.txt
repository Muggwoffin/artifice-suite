About Brother. I had a letter from him a few days after yours. I disliked the tone of it so much that I showed it to Reginald, and he disliked it still more. So much so, that we both agreed it would be undesirable to approach anyone here about his coming here. It is difficult to describe why he created this opposition in us, but his letter was supercilious, he sounded cranky, & he seemed to suggest that he had a lot to offer Russia—and that anyhow he might just as well try his pet themes there as anywhere else. We both felt that we couldn't make out a good enough case for him, & that if we showed anyone his letter it would condemn him right away, & also wouldn't do us any good by in recommending him.

Well, Dick. I suppose you thought I had given up writing to you. I feel very much ashamed of myself for not having written all this long while. I've written God I'm out now many letters. I can't even think of an adequate excuse except to one fortnight when I was knocked out with flu. But in one way you've been terribly busy—mostly learning Russian! I'm writing you offered to you (your own) in 'Pravda!' but I find it awfully hard to remember the words. Still, I mean to pay away of it. My German on the other hand is really improving. Yesterday I started to attend a Berlin class. This is your like being heavily occupied. So I look I have so many things to say that I don't know where to begin.

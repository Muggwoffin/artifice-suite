By the way, could you make enquiries about my 'W.W.' I haven't received it since Sept. 15. I thought that No. 1 was particularly good. The T.U.C. makes a splendid kick-off for a left-wing opposition. I hear you are now staying with H.P., which strikes me as a damn good idea, particularly as he's given to early rising & is, I understand, a man of sober habits! Give him my love & tell him to guard against double chins.

Please write to me again when you have a spare moment.

Thanks again for the books. They have already begun to go the round of the Luce, altho' they have just arrived.

Ever,
Rose

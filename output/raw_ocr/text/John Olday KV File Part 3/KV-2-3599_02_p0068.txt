T. Carver
256 Harrow Rd
W. 2.

15 Orsett Terrace
London. W2
27 July '48

Dear Comrade,

You were not present at the last meeting called in by Spartacus? Tomorrow Wed. 28th there will be a second meeting at above address, 1900 o'clock. We expect some Comrades from different movements i.e.: I.L.P., S.W.L., L.P., 1948 Research Group, Contemporary Press Group, and Anarchist Comrades.

There will be a final discussion on: setting up an International Information Office publishing a Engl/German bulletin, etc.

May we invite you also to come?

Tranquilly Yours
John alway

Copy of report re HOLLAND/U.K. - Communism - Relationships of the "SPARTACUS" Movement. date of inf. 19.7.49.

1. It is reported that the leaders of the Communist Federation "SPARTACUS" in Amsterdam, have recently been communicating regularly by letter with sympathisers in England.

2. It is reported also that the English centre carrying on this correspondence with the "Spartacus" leaders of Amsterdam, is to be found at the house of John OLDAY, 15 Orsett Terrace, London W.2. This address has been mentioned in connection with the paper "Freie Sozialistische Blätter".

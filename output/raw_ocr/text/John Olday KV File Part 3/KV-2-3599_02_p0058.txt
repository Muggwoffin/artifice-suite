To: R. Thistlethwaite, Esq.,
British Embassy, Washington.

From: Director-General.

The following information has been received from a reliable source and you may consider that it is worth while passing it to the F.B.I. and the Canadians.

In June 1948 we learned that the Revolutionary Communist Party in London had been informed that a "SPARTAKUSBUND" had been founded in Germany, and that its London representative was one John OLDAY (alias Arthur William OLDAG) of 15 Orsett Terrace, London, W.2. In July, we learned that a meeting organised by this SPARTAKUSBUND had been held in London, and that another was to be held later in the month at which a number of widely differing groups, including the Revolutionary Communist Party, the Independent Labour Party and the Anarchists were expected to be present. OLDAY was apparently the prime mover of these meetings.

Amritsar, 1919.—"Heaps of bodies lay here—some on their backs and some with their faces upturned. A number of them were poor innocent children. I shall never forget the sight. I saw heaps of dead bodies and I began to search for my husband. After passing through the heaps I found the dead body of my husband. The way towards it was full of blood and dead bodies."
Madam Ratan Devi in her report to Congress.

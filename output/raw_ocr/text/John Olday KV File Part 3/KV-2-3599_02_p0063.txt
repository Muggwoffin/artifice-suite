Dear Miss Bagot,

I enclose one copy of a letter sent by Willy FRITZENKOETER of Suppertal-Barmen to Arthur KRULL, former K.P.D. functionary in Brunswick, who resigned from the party in autumn 1947.

This letter was intercepted in Germany and has been forwarded to us by [redacted] there. Intelligence Division is informed.

John OLDAY is well known to us both from correspondence ending with your L.271(124) of 19 December 1939 and our [redacted] 21 October 1939.

We have no other information on this Spartabund alleged to be rapidly developing in countries abroad.

We are leaving it to you whether you should inform the Canadians, and you will wish to consider the remaining Empire angles. We are proposing to inform the F.B.I. through our Washington link about the American aspect. Before we do so, we should very much appreciate receiving your opinion whether this letter is a Trotskyist product and any recent information which you may have on OLDAY and on this Spartabund.

Yours sincerely,

Miss J.E. Bagot,
M.I. 5.

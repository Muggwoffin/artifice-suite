SECRET

21 September, 1950.

In reply to your letter of 14 September, 1950, and with reference to our telephone conversation yesterday, I will write to you again to let you know if I have been able to obtain any names and addresses of people in Belgium with whom John OLDAY, alias William Arthur OLDAG, is corresponding.

OLDAG was born in London on 10.4.1905; his National Registration Identity Card No. is A.K.A.7062995. He was issued with a passport in 1937 by the British Consulate in Hamburg, No. C.34071, and I am making inquiries as to whether he has been issued with a subsequent one. I will let you know the result.

Yours sincerely,

M. Sercombe.

S.I.S.

MRS/JVD.

SECRET

FF.66074/B.1.c/MRS.

27 September 1950.

Further to my letter of 21 September 1950 about John OLDAY, alias William Arthur OLDAG, I have been informed that 15, Orsett Terrace, W.2. is now empty, and it appears that OLDAY must have moved. I am trying to obtain his present address and thought you would like to know what is happening as it will take a little longer to try to obtain the information you wanted about his correspondents in Belgium.

This man has been issued with a new passport, No. 881660, dated 29 April 1948 in the name of Arthur William OLDAG, for the purpose of travelling to Paris on holiday.

Yours sincerely,

M. Sercombe.

S.I.S.

MRS/AGB.

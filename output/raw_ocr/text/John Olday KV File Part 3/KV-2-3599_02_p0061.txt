SECRET.

26th October, 1948.

O.F. 348/3/BIB/PS.

Please refer to your of 24th September, 1948.

The "Spartabund" referred to in the intercepted letter must be the German equivalent of the "Spartacus" group in Holland about which you last wrote under reference 24th September, 1948. We learnt in June, 1948, that the Revolutionary Communist Party in London has been informed that the "Spartakusbund" had just been founded in Germany and that its London representative was John OLDAY, 15 Orsett Terrace, London, W.2. At the same time the R.C.P. were given the address of the Paris Spartakus Group as:- JOULIN, 145 Quai Valmi, Paris.

In July we learnt that there had already been one meeting organised by "Spartacus" in London, and that a second one was to be held on 28th July at which representatives from such different movements as R.C.P., I.L.P., S.W.L., 1948 Research Group, Contemporary Press Group and Anarchists were expected to attend. John OLDAY was evidently the prize mover of these meetings.

John OLDAY, otherwise known to us as Arthur William OLDAG, has had a somewhat stormy career since 1939. He served during the earlier part of the war in the Pioneer Corps, but deserted in 1943. He was arrested by the Police in 1944, brought to trial for using a false identity card and sentenced to twelve months imprisonment. On his release he was promptly taken over by the Army authorities and sentenced to two years detention for desertion. During his period of desertion he made several contributions to the Anarchist journal, Freedom Press, and upon his release from detention in 1946 he is reported to have resumed his relationship with the London Anarchist Group.

P.T.O.

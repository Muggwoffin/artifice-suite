PASSPORT APPLICATION FORM "A"

INSTRUCTIONS

Important.—Please read instructions carefully before completing the form.

The charge for a passport is 15s., which must be paid in cash or by postal order at the time of application. Where the husband and wife are included on a joint passport the charge covers both.

Note.—A Passport cannot be issued or renewed by the Passport Office on behalf of a person already abroad; such a person should apply in a foreign country, to the nearest British Station of Consular, or, if residing in any part of the British Commonwealth outside the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, should apply to the nearest local Passport issuing authority.

HOW TO COMPLETE FORM

(A) Males (married and single) and women who have NOT reached the age of 16 years of age and British Commonwealth 1, 2, 5 and 6, if appropriate to their own application, Sections 4 and 7. A married man who wishes to include his wife in his passport should complete also Section 6, his wife signing that Section in the space provided. A passport which includes particulars of the holder's wife is not available for the wife's use when she is travelling alone.

(B) Married women of any age (including widows and women whose marriage has been dissolved or annulled). If a separate passport is required, complete Sections 1, 2, 5 and 6 and if appropriate to their own application, Sections 4 and 7.

(C) Children under 16 years of age will normally be included (without extra charge) on the passport of the father or other legal guardian. In completing the application form, also enter particulars of the children under Section 4. Inclusion on any other person's passport requires the consent of the father or legal guardian.

Application for the addition of children to an existing passport should be made by completing a Child Addition Form.

Should a separate passport be required for a child under 16 to reside or travel abroad, application by his father or other legal guardian should be made on Form "B".

(D) Personal Description. The description of the applicant and of the wife, if to be included on the passport, should be entered in block capitals on the "Personal description" form included with this application form. The "Personal description" form should also be signed at the foot.

(E) Signing the form. The application form must be signed in Section 3 by the applicant (and in Section 6 by the wife if to be included in the passport) in the presence of the examining officer at one of the Passport Offices or at any Local or Residential Advice Office of the Ministry of Labour and National Service. Note.—If the applicant cannot attend at any of these Offices, he or she can have his or her signature witnessed by a Member of Parliament, Justice of the Peace, Minister of Agriculture, or a member of the Police, Ministry of Health, or a member of the Civil Service, who should complete Section 10 and certify on the reverse side of one of the photographs that it is a true likeness of the applicant. (Wife's photograph to be certified similarly if she is to be included in the passport).

DOCUMENTS TO BE PRODUCED

(F) Note.—Any applicant (other than a naturalised British subject) who surrenders with this application previous passport(s) establishing his/her (and wife's) identity and nationality will be required to produce for examination other documents (excepting National Registration Identity Card when personal application is made) unless the name or status has been changed since the issue of the previous passport(s).

(G) Males (married or single) and women who have not been married should produce Birth Certificate (or Certificate of Registration of Birth), Certificate of Naturalisation when applicable, National Registration Identity Card or current Armed Forces Identity document. If a married man has to include his wife, his passport should be signed by his wife, her birth certificate and National Registration Identity Card should be produced.

(H) Married women (including widows and women whose marriages have been dissolved or annulled) applying for a separate passport should produce Birth Certificate (or Certificate of Registration of birth), Certificate of Naturalisation when applicable, Marriage Certificate, National Registration Identity Card or current British Armed Forces Birth Certificate. In cases in which the applicant's marriage has been dissolved, annulled or declared void, the final Decree of Dissolution or annulment must be produced.

Under 16 to be included in the passport. The children's birth certificates. If the applicant is not the children's father, proof of legal guardianship or a letter of consent from the father or other legal guardian is required.

(v) Persons born abroad. Applicants who were born abroad will be required to produce documentary evidence in support of the statements in Section 4.

PHOTOGRAPHS

(6) Two copies of a recent photograph of the applicant (and also of the photograph of the applicant's wife if there is a joint passport application). The photograph must be taken with this application. These photographs must be taken full face without hat, and the photographs must not be mounted. The size of the photographs must not be more than 2 1/2 inches by 2 inches or less than 2 inches by 1 1/2 inches. The photograph must be printed on a white background and the applicant's name must be written on the reverse side. The examining officer (or witness) is also required to endorse the reverse side of one copy of the photograph with the words "I certify that this is a true likeness of the applicant Mr. (Mrs. or Miss) ______."

Where a joint passport is applied for one copy of the wife's photograph should be similarly certified.

HOW TO MAKE APPLICATION

(H) Personal application
To facilitate the issue of passports applicants are recommended to make personal application to any one of the Passport Offices (addresses given below) or at any Local or Residential Advice Office of Ministry of Labour and National Service.

London.—Queen Anne's Gate Buildings, Dartmouth Street, Westminster, S.W.1. (Hours: 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., Saturdays 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.)

Liverpool.—Exchange Place, Liverpool. (Hours: 9.30 a.m. to 4 p.m., Saturdays 9.30 a.m. to 1 p.m.)

Glasgow.—10 Bothwell Street, Glasgow, G.1. (Hours: 9.30 a.m. to 4 p.m., Saturdays 9.30 a.m. to 1 p.m.)

MINISTRY OF LABOUR LOCAL OFFICE. (Hours: 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturdays 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.)

The Passport Offices are also open for cases of special emergency only, between 4 p.m. and 6 p.m. (Saturdays 1 p.m. and 4 p.m.) and in London on Sundays and Public Holidays between 10 a.m. and noon.

The relevant sections of the application form should be completed and this form and the Personal Description form, together with the necessary documents, taken personally to one of these Offices. The fee of 15s. should also be paid at any of these Offices.

If an applicant wishes to obtain his passport through a Travel Agency, the documents can still be examined and the signature and photographs witnessed at any of the Passport or Ministry of Labour and National Service Offices (see above). The applicant would then take the form to his agent with whom arrangements for payment for the passport would be made.

(i) Postal application
If it is not possible to make personal application at one of the Passport Offices or Ministry of Labour Local Office, the completed, signed and witnessed form and the photographs (and other necessary documents, National Registration Identity Card) should be forwarded, together with a postal order for 15s., to one of the three Passport Offices whose address is given above. (N.B.—Cheques cannot be accepted.) Applicants resident in Northern Ireland should apply by post to the Passport Office, Exchange Place, Liverpool.

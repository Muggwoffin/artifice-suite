WUPPERTAL 3 August '48.

Dear Comrade KRULL,

Our Spartabund is rapidly developing in countries abroad and is well on the way to achieving international status. That should encourage us before everything to bring the Bund to maturity here in Germany. In England the Bund has already been formed and will be developed further throughout all the British Colonies - CANADA, AUSTRALIA, SOUTH AFRICA, etc., and also of course in the U.S.A. The connections are already made. This of course, demands a lot of work and you Dear Comrade are going to assist us in it by writing articles for the new newspaper which will shortly be appearing in England and which will from there be sent to all the countries listed above. If you have not a typewriter then write your contribution by hand. The Editor in England understands German as well as you or I. Write reports three or four pages long and send them to the Editor in London (a letter containing three sheets of paper costs 50 pfennig postage to England). Send the reports to the following address -

Mr. John OLDAY,
15 Orsett Terrace,
LONDON, W.2.
ENGLAND.

Concerning the contents of reports which will be required, on no account are articles on theories or opinions on the class struggle required. These theories are known throughout the world and are by no means new. What is required are detailed Stimmungsberichte and articles on the opinions of the man-in-the-street and how he is re-acting to present day conditions. Very important are reports emanating from Works' Councils etc., reports on strikes, workers' connections with the Party and TU's, what the worker in the factory is talking about and what action they think should be taken. Other points of interest are - what is happening in the legal groups? What are the Nazis doing? What is KPD Opposition doing, and so on. These reports will be translated and expanded in England.

So Dear Comrade I hope to read something from you very shortly, and should it be the case that you are not accustomed to writing articles, now you have the chance to practice.

With comradely greetings.

Willy FRITZENKÖTER

If you have newspapers cuttings send them also to England. For printed matter the postage to England is 10 pfennig for each 50 gms.

P.S. Many thanks for your letter which I received. The reports should be written as indicated above and I should like them to be sent off within a week. If you know of a revolutionary group which is opposing the present Party and TU lines then send the address also to London when the information will be "worked on" there.

Please show this letter also to comrade BERGERT from whom I would also like reports - from EILFRILD as well. We have promised ourselves that we will beat the best newspapers in England with our reports - "The Manchester Guardian" and "The Times".

PARIS is at the moment not so important. LONDON is the important thing.

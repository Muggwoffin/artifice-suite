"In the last war we did not sacrifice comfort except under duress: we earned income by service for which the labourer was worthy of his hire; we made large profits and we won the war. Victory was ours. Victory for 'vested interests' together with every interest for which comfort, income, profits, and life could be preserved. Only those who sacrificed income, profits, and life went down. Only those went under. Only those lost the war."
Sir Oswald Stoll, 'The Stage', 21st Nov. 1940.

Reference Karl Friedrich GRÖHL's application for permission to visit the U.K., enclosed in your file S.60210, returned herewith:

Karl GRÖHL first came to our notice in December 1936, when he was reported as a well known German Communist, then living in Paris, who had played a prominent part in the Communist movement in Munich and who was a personal friend of Willi MÜNZENBERG and a member of the Comintern (Münzenberg) Committee in Paris. He had, it was reported, been commissioned by the Comintern to write propaganda booklets and pamphlets in the German language for distribution in German speaking countries in Europe. Subsequent information obtained about the man did not confirm that he was a member of the Comintern Committee in Paris, but seemed to show that he was of the Trotskyist persuasion; he was also understood to be intensely anti-Nazi. The latest information obtained about him was to the effect that he was concerned with a forthcoming work on German Air Armaments.

M.I.5. may be able to supply you with some additional details regarding GRÖHL's activities, and we should like to hear in due course what decision is arrived at in regard to this man's application.

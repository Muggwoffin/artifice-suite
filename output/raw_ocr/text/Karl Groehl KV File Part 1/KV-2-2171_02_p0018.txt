CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl Groehl

On 3.5.39 S.I.S* forwarded a report on various Anti Nazi organisations under CX. 12535. It was stated that members of the various parties and groups which formerly played a leading role in Germany and Austria have set up committees and bureaux abroad with the object of undermining the Nazi regime and regaining the power for themselves.

The different groups frequently exchange information and unite in fighting against Nazi Germany.

In addition to these groups there are "independent" emigrants who have succeeded in establishing good international.

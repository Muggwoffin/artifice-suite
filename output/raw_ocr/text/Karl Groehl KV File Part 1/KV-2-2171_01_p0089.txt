From Karl GROEHL
Paris.

To Karl OTTEN
London.

Date of letter 9.11.39.

Summary of Contents

Has just received OTTEN's letter of 2.11.39. The work is not progressing. "I have really said often enough that I need some cover. I really must get the permit to stay: I still haven't got it, and I must go there again on 22nd. On Friday, an official came to the house and asked the landlady if I was still receiving much post from abroad. The day before yesterday I had to go to the local police station, where I had to fill in a form stating what I was living on, what work I was doing, etc. Yesterday the police were in the printing rooms, wanting to know who I was. Why doesn't PROBST speak to the Police? Is England the chief enemy, or Germany? Please speak to Madame, so that I can get a visa to come over. Here I am lost. The police authorities would be quite prepared to deliver me up to Germany. Do grasp that. How can I possibly get on with the job, if I have yet more difficulties than the few who are in Germany?

RAUSCHNIG has gone to London again; so has FUCHS. They journey to and fro as they please. Why can't you get hold of a visa for me? I am not even allowed to leave Paris.

It is even impossible to get into Belgium. It has all taken so long that the whole thing has no object, but still if we have to prepare ourselves for four, five or six years, something may yet come out of it. This answers any questions you may have had. I can't ask anything by letter. I must get down there..."

Again implores OTTEN to do everything he can to get him over to London.

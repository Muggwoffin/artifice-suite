15.3.41.        P.P. 52095.

This intercept was not received by B.4.c. until 18.3.41 when it was too late to arrange for the proposed meeting on Monday (17.3.41) to be covered.

Below is a translation of the letter:-

My dear,

To-day I am in bed as I have poisoned myself.

On Monday I have an appointment at 1 o'clock and cannot therefore come.

Can you please come to me on Monday at 6 o'clock in the evening? I have ordered the typist for 6 o'clock.

What exactly did you say to URIEWITSCH?? He will now give you as reference! You have told him that you are with France Libre. He has already passed this on and another acquaintance has already told me of it.

Be more cautious!

Best wishes Karl.

Greetings to Madame.

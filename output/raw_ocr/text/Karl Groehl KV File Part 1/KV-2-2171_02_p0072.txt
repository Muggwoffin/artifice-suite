PF 46464.

CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl Groehl.

Extract from M/S report on Otten Group, 22.7.38.

....It appears that GROEHL has postponed his visit to London, because he will have to "go on tour" about the end of the month. I elicited that this meant a visit to Denmark, which Otten says is a safer place to receive agents having to report outside Germany than any other neighbouring country. Nevertheless, I understand that GROEHL met one of his agents in Switzerland about a fortnight ago, and the report was sent direct to Otten.

....I told Otten I should like to meet Groehl when he came over, and he said I should.

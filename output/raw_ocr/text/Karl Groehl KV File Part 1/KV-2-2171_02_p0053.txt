EXTRACT.

Relating to Karl GROEHL.

Extracted from P.F. 45807 (OTTEN) Vol. 2... No... 87a.

Author of original B.5b... M/S... Place and date of origin ... 23.9.38.

Extract made by M.J.E.B... on (date) ... 7.10.38.

re. OTTEN Group:

The following report has been received from M/S.:

"I took tea with OTTEN on 21.9.38 but found that both his wife and LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT were present, which reduced the chances of getting anything interesting. Also they were so dazed by what seemed to them to be the collapse of their world that they were hardly in a position to discuss these matters.

GROEHL has once more postponed his visit - not because of any refusal by the British Consulate, but because he declared that in the present situation he could not afford to leave Paris for even a single day. It is not known when he will visit London.

In a few minutes conversation that I had alone with OTTEN he told me that they intended to continue their work. They think that if CHAMBERLAIN settles with HITLER, war will be postponed for about two years, during which period of respite it is incumbent upon them to work for the overthrow of Hitler from within. This, they declare is simply a matter of finance. GROEHL, or someone else, has informed that there are about 50-60,000 active underground workers in the Communist Party of Germany, who are in very close touch with Moscow, and that a quick reliable line of communication has been established between them and Russia. These men can be relied upon to initiate sabotage work in all Germany's factories; they are already doing so in a quiet way, but would be ready to sacrifice their lives, if need be, when ordered to do so. At present GROEHL needs about ten subordinates who could organise both sabotage and propaganda in Germany for him."

I submit that all information received from GROEHL concerning aviation should be checked with extra special care, as WOLFFSOHN told me he thought they got some rather wild statements on it. He also thinks that both RUSSBUELDT and OTTEN have lived in such a state of intense excitement during the last weeks that their powers of judgment are invalidated and that they are not capable of drawing correct inferences from accurate information they receive. But it is more and more obvious that they for their part do not trust him and exclude him from their most intimate councils."

R.3527.

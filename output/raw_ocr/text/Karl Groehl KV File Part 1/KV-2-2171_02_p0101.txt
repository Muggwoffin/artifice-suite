GROSS - REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-
Karl GROEHL.

29.5.36. In an intercepted letter to Otto LEHMANN RUESSBUELDT, dated 29.5.36, Karl OTTEN says that he would have liked to know more about "this Conference on 12th June". Could they invite his friend Karl GROEHL, of 13 rue Barraoult, Paris? OTTEN says GROEHL is one of the cleverest men he has met in Germany; Leo TR. thinks a lot of him, but GROEHL just goes his own way. OTTEN has apparently been working a great deal with GROEHL over the Saar question.

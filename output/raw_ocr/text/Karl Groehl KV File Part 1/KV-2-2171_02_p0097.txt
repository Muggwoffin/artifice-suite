GROSS - REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl Groehl.

4.10.37. Karl Groehl wrote to K. Otten from Paris giving him news of friends and political events. He also enclosed a letter from a German doctor containing gossip against the Nazi regime. His wife is trying to get a permit for England and also money for the journey. His child, aged 9, is well. The letter from Grumbel is useless as the Committee is Jewish, they have refused to give money to his wife for her journey.

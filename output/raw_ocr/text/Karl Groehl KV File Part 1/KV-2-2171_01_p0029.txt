P.F. 52095 - copy in P.F. 46464.

Saw Major Whetmore, Mrs Holmes and Miss Stamper of S.O.2. upon RETZLAW's association with SZYMONCZYK with the following result:-

1) There is no doubt that the "RETZLAW" with whom SZYMONCZYK is associating is Karl GROEHL, the subject of P.F. 46464.

2) From their knowledge of GROEHL extending over a number of years, S.O.2. have every reason to believe him to be honest.

3) The work on which GROEHL is now engaged is not such as to involve an association with SZYMONCZYK and Mrs Holmes thinks it quite probable that GROEHL is engaging in private journalistic enterprises with SZYMONCZYK.

4) GROEHL has previously reported to Mrs Holmes any interesting contacts he may have made (for example, he reported his association with URLEVIITCH) and Mrs Holmes is perturbed that GROEHL has at no time mentioned the fact that he is in touch with SZYMONCZYK.

5) It was thought well that Mrs Holmes should not mention to GROEHL that we are interested in SZYMONCZYK as this would almost certainly come to SZYMONCZYK's ears.

6) S.O.2. have no objection to our taking any steps that we may think fit in connection with the SZYMONCZYK case even though this might disclose that GROEHL's associating with SZYMONCZYK was not an innocent one.

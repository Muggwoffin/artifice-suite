B.2a (Mr. White)

re Carl OTTEN group.

M/S called on Carl OTTEN on the evening of 20th October and obtained the following information:

1. In the past OTTEN has definitely done work for the publishing firm of ULLSTEIN. He has also been employed by FISCHER Verlag and by the "Frankfurter Zeitung" and "Berliner Tageblatt".
2. OTTEN knows nothing of Ralph PARKER who was reported recently to be doing intelligence work for the Czechs.
3. Erich WOLLENBURG has escaped from Prague by air and he is now in Paris, lodging with Carl GROEHL.
4. Re the paper "Notre Prestige": OTTEN received his copy of the paper through GROEHL but actually from the author of one of the articles, a certain Berthold JACOB, whom OTTEN describes as a first class military expert. This JACOB is the man who was kidnapped from Switzerland by the Nazis sometime ago. OTTEN says that the Gestapo tried to get out of JACOB the names of the German officers who had given him information, but claims that they were unsuccessful. The second copy of "Notre Prestige" which OTTEN obtained was not got from, Paris, but was bought by WOLFFSOHN in London, probably from Hachettes. This was the copy which had certain items censored out. OTTEN is of opinion that "Prestige" is subsidised by one of the extreme Right Wing bodies in France.

Berthold JACOB is not a member of the "Primrose League" but is an intermittent contact of OTTEN. OTTEN considers that JACOB has a store of extremely valuable information which OTTEN and his group are not able to use as fully as they would like as they have not the money to pay for it.

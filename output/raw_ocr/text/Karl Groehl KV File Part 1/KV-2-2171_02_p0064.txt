From K. Groehl

To K. Otten.

Date of letter 17.8.38.

Summary of Contents

Why has he not heard from Otten since he wrote of Erich's arrival?

The invitation is not enough and they will certainly enquire who Sykes is. He has stated that he is an editor and that Sykes is translator for novels etc.

He is expecting a heavy blow as he thinks that his enterprise on which account he is coming to England will go broke. Sykes is no good at all.

He can do the Berlin and the southern Germany affairs, as he touch with both places.

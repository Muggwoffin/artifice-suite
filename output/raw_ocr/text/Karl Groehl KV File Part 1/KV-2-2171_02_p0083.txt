Summary.

It was lucky I received the registered letter before I saw Bio. I have the impression, that Bio is trying to get another job. I already told you this in letter 6. I also think that the information he obtains, he offers to someone else at the same time as us. He was so excited about the handwritten letter he practically stormed at me. The records can be fetched any time. The method does not please me. Bio always does them. He sent money in the registered letter. After receiving it H. will travel to H.

Enclosure to say that 400,000 pamphlets be distributed in Munchen. Note to say that 400 or 4000 small pamphlets must be meant.

Letter from K. Groehl to K. Otten.

Copy. Original in P.F 45807 K. Otten - 66B.

(SOURCE: Intercepted letter from GROEHL (postmark Paris) to OTTEN, dated 27.4.39., in PF.45807, 162a.)

SECRET.
PF.45807/B4b. 1st May 1939.

Dear Major Vivian,

I have just seen a letter passing from GROEHL to OTTEN, in which GROEHL says that the French authorities have now given him a permit for a month's residence which has enable him to acquire a "Titre de Voyage".

He is now proposing to approach the English Consulate, if he can get a suitable invitation to England, and hopes to be in London in the course of the next week. I am not proposing to take any action on this information.

Yours sincerely,

(sd.) K.G.Y.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.
S.I.S.

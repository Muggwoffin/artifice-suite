SECRET

PF 45807/B.4b. 30th June, 1939

Dear Major Vivian,

The following is a translation of an extract from a letter from GROEHL to OTTEN.

"LOWENHEIMS. They have a Russian espionage bureau!!! They work for the three letters. (drei Buchstaben). ie G.P.U. Their last heroic deed in Germany was to sink the minutes of their secret sittings (they keep minutes !!!) in the Muggelsee, and they were fished up again. Result, many Arrests and heavy sentences. When they left Prague they had no money. Their business is the usual Russian disguise (Booksellers, Radio businesses are their favourite covers, naturally they also form other firms). Do what you think right. They are swine and not decent people."

I do not know of any anti-Nazi organisations under the name of LOWENHEIM. We do, however, know of one German Jew refugee called Walther LOWENHEIM, who is now in business in this country having previously been in business in Prague. We have nothing to show that he is concerned in this organisation I imagine that the "Three letters" referred to are probably K.P. D.

Yours sincerely,

Sgd. K.G.Y.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.
S.I.S.

B.2a (Mr. White)

re Carl OTTEN Group:

I had an interview with M/S yesterday on the subject of the publication "Notre Prestige"; he was able to give me the following information:

It appears that OTTEN received this from Carl GROEHL and OTTEN passed it on to M/S as he wished as soon as possible to check up the service details therein. At the same time OTTEN endeavoured to get a duplicate copy which, however, had all the important parts blocked out when he received it. M/S was going to see OTTEN last night when he would endeavour to get further information about the publication itself and also OTTEN's views as to the censorship. The typed slip which is inserted in the first page of the publication was actually inserted by GROEHL as M/S recognised the typewriter.

I also questioned M/S tactfully on the subject of the "Major." He had never heard of any "Major" actually being mentioned in the course of his talks with OTTEN but OTTEN has several times referred to an individual coming across from Germany and bringing material in person. If M/S gets an opportunity of pumping OTTEN about this he will do so very discreetly.

21.10.38.

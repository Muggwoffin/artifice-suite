Original in PF 45708 v.3. 123a.

From Karl Groehl
To Karl Otten
Dated 27.1.39.

Summary.

Writer said that he had received from his Swiss friend to the effect that there was a great movement of traffic going from Germany to Italy, via Basel. He would write again. He enclosed also a report on the personnel of the German army.

Information sent to S.I.S* on 6.1.39. (report sent, copy not kept).

GROSS - REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl Groehl.

On 20.11.37., Groehl wrote to Karl Otten giving information about their literary colleagues, different editions etc. Sievers is going to finance the german translation and edition of Souvarines "Stalin". How much does Otten think should be put aside for translation? and who could undertake it? Willi M., Braun and Heinrich Mann wish to separate their party from the C.P.

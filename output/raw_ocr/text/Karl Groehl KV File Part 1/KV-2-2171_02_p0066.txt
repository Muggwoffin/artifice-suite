32 Souberie Avenue
Letchworth

11.8.38.

My dear Groehl,

I hope you are coming over to see me this week-end as you promised, because our literary work cannot go on unless I have an immediate consultation with you. But I think I have told you that before, and I feel sure you will put off any engagement you have in Paris and come along. I can put you up as usual.

Yours sincerely,

(s) Claud W. SYKES

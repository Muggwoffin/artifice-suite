CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl GROEHL.

On 9.9.39, Karl GROEHL wrote to Karl OTTEN, saying that he was imprisoned in the Prison de la Santé, Paris, and had been there since 1.9.39. He asked OTTEN to intervene on his behalf, and to talk with Mr. PRABSCH; he suggests he might be able to come to England, even if he would be interned there.

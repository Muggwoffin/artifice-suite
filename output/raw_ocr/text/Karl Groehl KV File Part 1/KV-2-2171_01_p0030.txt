Extract from report on Samuel URIEWICZ (Suspected Comintern Agent) and his contacts mentioning RETZLAW

Another acquaintance (of URIEWICZ) is Stanislaw RETZLAW, Pole, born 2nd June, 1898 at Inowaratzlow, living at 11 Peters Court, Porchester Road, W.2. mentioned in Special Branch report of 9th January, 1941 (405/40/1434) who is said to have been acting as an agent for the British Government. URIEWICZ has said that this is not the man's correct name but he affirms that RETZLAW is a British agent.

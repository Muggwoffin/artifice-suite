From. Karl Groehl
To. Karl Otten.
dated. 9.1.39.

Summary.

I have received your letters of 7th and 8th.
I am trying to deal with the translation. The publishers must make the final decision as to whether they will sell the translation. I am trying everything and have sent the book to a Spanish official. Where are the reviews? I have not seen them in the Paris or Basle papers.

Yes, Bornstein is the chief and B.J. is military collaborator. Olden is not a certain correspondent. He is paid quite enough.

Rauschn. is coming again to London (do not talk about it). I gave him your address and he will come and see you. Since Christmas I have received no money. B.J. was here yesterday and had got the 300,00 I had obtained for him. He has received nothing from the Daily paper. I had many notices on the first page. I am so ill through personal troubles that I cannot work and am going to Nice for 8 days. On my return I will be with Marcu, but not living with him.

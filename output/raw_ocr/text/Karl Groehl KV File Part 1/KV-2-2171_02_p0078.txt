12.6.38.

My Dear,

I have just received your letter of the 10th. Enclosed is the news from BIO, who has given up another job here.

The notes are worth very little - anyone there could get the information, certainly they have been known for a long time. I feel they are small stones in a big mosaic; one thing in a thousand, but the thousand makes up the picture. For example -

1. The news that BORSIG are building works in Greece has been for a long time in the commercial papers, and KRUPP and BORSIG are also building works elsewhere.

2. The Barracks and factories on Page 1. These are found on the town plans, and are marked as barracks.

3. Everywhere soldiers and S.S. men are on the aerodromes and barrack-squares. That is why they are there (the soldiers).

4. All civil aerodromes will be used by the Air Force in an emergency. "Geschickte Tarnung" is important (Wichtigtuerei).

5. Everyone knows where the "Persil" works are, and the fact that they can make other things besides Persil. It was done in the last war.

6. It is important that the "Jaberg" will be used for military purposes, where the flag is; that is where the munition sheds stand.

7. One can buy Ordinance Maps legally as "Travel maps", and can get them here in Paris from a bookshop.

8. It is possible that the "friend" went to Berlin, but he probably went privately. The enormous "Saureverbrach" one can confirm from commercial sources. The Berlin journey is nonsense.

The exercises on the Dutch frontier are important, although every paper had a better account, but all the same important.

I am interested how the job goes with BIO. He was so important and anxious to give the enclosed report, I was quite horrified with their insufficiency when I read them. After the news from H., I find him a braggart and exaggerator, like BIO. One must have such people, they are only little stones in the mosaic. (Also one must form the right method).

I am meeting BIO tomorrow and will give him your letter and news. I will be restrained in my criticism. These people have no sense for really important work, and can only be used as hacks.

I will try to raise some money and speak to H. personally. I have already told BIO that I wish to know H. He agrees.

K. Groehl.

Filed on 14.6.38.

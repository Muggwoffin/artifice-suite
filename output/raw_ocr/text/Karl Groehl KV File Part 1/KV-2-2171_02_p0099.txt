GROSS --REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-- Karl GROEHL.

17.12.36. The following is an extract from a letter to S.I:S., dated 17.12.36:-

"....It was somewhat surprising to us to find the names of Carl GROEHL and Karl OTTEN among your list of new Comintern appointments for the Paris Secretariat. We have records of a Karl GROEHL who was formerly a member of the German Communist Party, but who quarreled with that organisation and whose beliefs appeared nearer to those of Trotsky than to those of Stalin. We have one trace of him in which he is urging publication of a newspaper or magazine which should "above all be independent of Moscow". In May, 1936 GROEHL's address was Paris 13, rue Barrault 7............."

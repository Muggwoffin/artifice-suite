CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:- Karl Groehl..

According to M/S information of 13.6.38 most of the people in Germany who send their information in to Groehl are members of the German Social Democratic Party. They are mainly intellectuals, a number of doctors, a good many in important positions in the police (which is honeycombed with the S.D.P.) good representation in the Inland Revenue Office, and numerous engineers, technicians electricians etc. They are mostly domiciled in the Rhineland and Westphalia, with a fair sprinkling in Hanover. All the reports are made verbally outside the frontier, which there is no difficulty in crossing by legitimate means. Aachen and Saarbrucken are two favourite places for crossing the frontier for this purpose, but the Danish frontier is also used. The great difficulty is finance.

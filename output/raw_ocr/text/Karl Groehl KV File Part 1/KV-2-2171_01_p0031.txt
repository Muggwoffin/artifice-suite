P.P. 52095/B.A.C. 28th March, 1941.

Dear Shetmore,

I have received through Colonel Hinchley-Cooke your SCW/KV/637 of 25.3.41 respecting SYMONCZYK.

You do not, however, deal with the portions of my letter of 18.3.41 relating to Stanislas RETZLAV and I should be grateful if you would do so at your earliest convenience.

I particularly want to know whether the man associating with SYMONCZYK is identical with the RETZLAV whom you know and if so the significance of this regular association. It may be that you are satisfied that RETZLAV's activities are entirely innocent, but I should be glad to know your views of RETZLAV's reliability from the security point of view.

I am writing to you direct with the approval of Colonel Hinchley-Cooke in view of the urgency of the matter and of the fact that the address of Colonel Hinchley-Cooke's office is in process of being changed. I am sending Colonel Hinchley-Cooke a copy of this letter.

Yours sincerely,

R. Pilkington.

Major Shetmore,
Room 055A,
War Office.
RP/BB.

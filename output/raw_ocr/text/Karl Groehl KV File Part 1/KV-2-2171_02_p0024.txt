CX.12650/4199, 6.4.39. V.

Dear Younger,

Reference your P.F. 45807/B.4h, dated 10th March:

This is only an ad interim reply to tell you that we have ascertained a considerable amount about OTTEN, GROEHL and half-a-dozen other refugee organisations, which are supplying information, and their connexions one with another. The whole matter has assumed great importance in view of tendentious information which is being purveyed, often, we have no doubt, originally emanating from German official sources.

We hope to have a conference of officers here on the matter shortly after Easter, and we will ask you if you will, to be present.

Yours sincerely, G.S.

K.G. Younger, Esq.,
M.I.5.

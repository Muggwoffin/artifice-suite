Secret. PF 45807/B.4b 24th November, 1937.

Dear Major Vivian,

With reference to Carl OTTEN and Karl GROEHL, mentioned in your CX/4380, dated 14.12.36, as members of the Propaganda Section of the Munzenberg Committee, you may be interested in the following extract, translated from a letter sent to OTTEN from GROEHL in Paris -

WilliM., Braun and Heinrich Mann are trying to extract their organisation from the Communist Party. The Communist Party has already an inkling of this and has quickly formed a new organisation - "Union fur Recht und Freiheit". This is comic, such a fine name for such rabble.

I should add that in my check on OTTEN's correspondence, I have come across no indications that he is working in with Munzenberg, or the new action committee in Paris, in fact, he seems to be more of Trotskyist than anything else. His literary interests and film connections appear to occupy most of his time, and he is still contributing articles to Social Democratic papers.

Yours sincerely,

D.G.W.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.
S.I.S.

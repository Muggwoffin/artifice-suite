Translation

From: Peter,
Hotel Bodan,
Romanshorn,
Switzerland.

To: Mr. Stanislaw Reszlaw,
11 Peters Court,
Porchester Road,
London, W.2.

19.3.41.

"I have been working a great deal, more than ever, and with more success than ever. You can't imagine what possibilities I have today, and I am continually sorry that you can't or shouldn't take part in my work. I sought contact with you through relations, cousins of yours with your name... Dear friend, please try to get into contact with your relatives on the basis of my work, with the cousins bearing your name. There are many possibilities for mutual help. You must only get into contact. Your cousins who bear your name are of no use as it is. I am awaiting the expected connection with Wales. I knew French very well, so you mustn't be afraid. You must take care of yourself, we need you. Out there in Germany, all over the continent, the new democratic order of things is already being prepared for. If only England holds out, and here we have no doubt of that! The Germans do not consider the future bombing attacks likely to have the desired effect. That is sure. Democracy has no basis for despair and the liberty of the people will again be secured on the continent - even if the Russians again go with the Germans, and they will, the non-aggression pact will be made with Japan and the position in the Balkans handed over to the accursed Nazis. They (the Russians) will, too, go even further with them. I am very sceptical. It (Communism) is just a reddish Fascism. Silone saw that two years ago, and here (in the Balkans) they are making a united front in sabotage with the Nazis. Swine! Dear friend! I have great hopes that you will really be able to rise to your former height.... Your Peter.

Konni S. is a soldier. Lucy's address is of no more use; she is ill. Vera L's address is unobtainable. She is perhaps in Hamburg. Let it alone, we can see later. Greetings from Heinrich. I was at H.O's, something is going forward. Greetings.

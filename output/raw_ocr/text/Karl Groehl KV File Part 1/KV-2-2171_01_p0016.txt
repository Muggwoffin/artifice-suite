Original in P.F. 52095 (SZYMONCZYK)

Translation of Letter from RETZLAW (i.e. Karl GROEHL P.F. 46464) to SZYMONCZYK (P.F. 52095)

23.5.41.

My dear,

I am removing on Monday. New address herewith. Please give a card to Mme. ORLOW. I again have direct connection by 31 bus from me to Kensington High Street.

With regard to a typist I will let you know.

My new address is very nice and it is cheaper.

But I am frightfully hard up; can you please for the last time give me a little help.

Best wishes and also for Madame ORLOV.

S.R.

Enclosed with this letter was a visiting card for "S. RETZLAW" giving his address as:

24, Compayne Gardens.

London, N.W.6.

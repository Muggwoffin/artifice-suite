From GROEHL

To OTTEN.

Date of letter 28.7.38.

Summary of Contents Letter 8.

Many thanks for the book which arrived yesterday and is very well made. You have misunderstood me about my London visit, I only wish to come for 2 days to talk over things which I cannot write.

A long letter has come from BJ, I sent him 100,00 and Erich 200

Letter Regarding Anti-Nazi propaganda in Canada and here.

10.5.41.

Translation from German.

Dear KOP,

I have read in a newspaper that O. STRASSER is in CANADA and that he has published an appeal. Have you any contact with him? If so, please give him my address. Please inform him at once that he writes my address correctly.

I am of opinion that we require just now some clearness in the future shaping of D. (Germany) and it would be advisable to talk to all people of energy, as we have now to clear the whole meaning of "Socialism". HITLER uses or misuses the meaning at present rightly or wrongly like a NOSKE or some other big pot.

Are there any papers over there, which do serious work? the "Zeitung" published here has 4 Editors, of whom 3 have worked for the NAZIS (until 1938!!!) the fourth was a "Steel Helmet man". Also good. However, one can do little against that.

Tell this to SIEVERS.

B.C.
28.5.41.

SECRET

8th February 1939.

Dear Major Vivian,

Attached are two reports which Karl OTTEN has received from GROEHL.

GROEHL considers the letter from Karl LANGE to be of great importance and is anxious to have OTTEN's opinion on it. We have no record here of LANGE.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

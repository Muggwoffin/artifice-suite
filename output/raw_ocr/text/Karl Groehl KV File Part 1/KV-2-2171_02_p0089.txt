PF. 42816/B.4b.

12th May, 1938.

Dear Major Vivian,

I am sending you the attached document for what it is worth. We picked it up passing from Karl GROEHL, mentioned in your dated 9.5.37., to Karl OTTEN in this country, and I understand that the information is to be used in a forthcoming work on German Air Armaments which will probably appear over the name of LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT.

OTTEN, GROEHL and LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT are, so far as we know, intensely anti-Nazi. OTTEN is a Trotskyist and GROEHL appears to be of the same persuasion. LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT is rather more liberal in view, but a great scaremonger as to Germany's war preparations. All three have Jewish blood in them, which in part accounts for their exile.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

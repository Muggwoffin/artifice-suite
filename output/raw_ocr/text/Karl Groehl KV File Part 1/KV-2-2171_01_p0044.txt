They entered at 4.0 p.m. and at 4.45 p.m. they all left again in a taxi for Piccadilly, W. where SZYMONCZYK alighted and walked to Brewer Street where he made some purchases and then returned to the Circus and took a bus from there to Hyde Park Corner where he alighted, entered the Park and walked to Queensway from where he took the bus to Holland Park Avenue, alighted and walked home.

On 19th March the young lady previously mentioned was seen to enter at 11.50 a.m. At 1.40 p.m. SZYMONCZYK and Mrs ORLOW left by taxi and were seen to return by taxi at 2.50 p.m. Mrs ORLOW had parcels of shopping and she entered the block containing No. 216, the address of Henry Carl SCHNUR, whilst SZYMONCZYK returned to No. 106. At 3.0 p.m. he left with the above mentioned young lady and entered the block containing No. 216 for 10 minutes when he left and walked to Lyons Teashop, Earls Court Road, where they purchased a loaf of bread. They then proceeded to Kensington Gardens and Hyde Park where they fed the birds on the Round Pond and the Serpentine with the recently purchased loaf. They then walked to Hyde Park Corner where the young lady left SZYMONCZYK. She ran across the road and joined a young man at 5.0 p.m. They returned to the Park and both passed SZYMONCZYK without any sign of recognition on the part of any of them. SZYMONCZYK was then walking towards home and the young woman and man were followed to Marble Arch where they took the train to Liverpool Street, and from there they took train for Chingford at 6.0 p.m. and finally walked to and entered No. 58 Station Road, Chingford. These premises show the sign of H.E. GILLET, jeweller, and have living accommodation above the shop. The young lady entered with a key and appeared to reside there and at 7.30 p.m. two middle aged persons, a man and a woman left for a short walk. The woman appeared to be the mother of the young lady, the man might be her father and the young man her brother - both were conversing in fluent French on the way home. Her description is:-
age 22-25 years; height 5'6"; slim build; dark hair; features plain looking, might be French. Dress: beige coat, brown frock, flat heeled shoes; hatless. May be a typist or clerk.

Her companion's description is:-
age 32-34 years; height 6'6"; medium build; dark hair; dark moustache; fresh complexion. Dress: blue suit; dark blue overcoat, black Homburg hat, carries document case. He might also be French.

On 20th March the above young lady entered the flats at 12.10 p.m. and at 5.0 p.m. she left with SZYMONCZYK and they walked to Kensington High Street where she boarded a bus and he made a few purchases and returned at 5.20 p.m. to Oakwood Court.

The observation is being continued.

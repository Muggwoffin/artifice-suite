Original in P.F. 52095 (SZYMONCZYK)

Translation of intercepted letter from S. RETZLAW to S. SZYMONCZYK Esq., 106 Oakwood Court, W.14.

30.V.41.

Dear Friend,

I have an interesting thing and am looking for somebody who will be interested in it.

An acquaintance of mine has a collection of lead soldiers of the new German Army. There are several thousand figures and all kinds of arms are represented:-

Infantry.
Artillery - light and heavy.
Anti-aircraft artillery and appurtenances.
Cavalry.
Aerodrome troops, searchlights, etc.
Sanitation, with coaches and appurtenances.
Panzer arms.
Motorised infantry with automatics.
Machine gun troops with appurtenances.
Railway troops with coaches, lines, stations, etc.

The whole collection cost more than 18,000 marks and it is all made to scale.

My acquaintance will take £50 but I can get the price brought down by half. The troops are very good for amateurs or schools or officers for practice in tactics.

It is a complete replica of the German Army.

Please ask around and perhaps find someone who will buy the collection.

Best wishes, I shall call at mid-day tomorrow. Greetings to Madame.

(sgd) S. RETZLAW.

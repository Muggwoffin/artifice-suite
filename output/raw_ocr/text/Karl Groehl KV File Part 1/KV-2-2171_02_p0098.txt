"...The Munzenberg Committee consists of three different sections, as follows:

a) The political section, which is definitely Communist........

b) An active section, under Dr. FREISTADT, which puts into practical effect the directions given by Section 'a' above.

c) An idealistic Section, under the leadership of Hans STUCKENBERGER, a well-known German communist, which seeks to enlist the sympathies of all persons of Left ideals or of persons who have reason to dislike Nazism, Fascism or any form of Imperialism, and who, as such, have no other organisation to turn to....It was under this category that such persons as Karl OTTEN ... Karl GROEHL, and Erwin Egon KISCH have been roped in......

With regard to Karl GROEHL, this individual, some time ago, returned to Stalin Communism. In November 1936, he was commissioned by the Comintern to write several propaganda booklets and pamphlets in the German language for distribution in the German speaking countries in Europe. This commission, which was worth 20,000 francs to him, was obtained for him through the intermediary of the well-known German communist Fritz ERPENBECK, Chief of the Press Bureau of the Communist Propaganda organisation in Czecho-Slovakia.

If it is correct that GROEHL still poses as a Trotskyist, it is probable that he is doing so under the instructions of Moscow...."

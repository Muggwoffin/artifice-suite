P.F.45807/B4b. 28th September 1939

Dear Colonel Vivian,

In a letter which we have seen passing from Karl GROBUL to OTTEN, the former states that he has been in the La Sante Prison in Paris since 1.9.58. He wants OTTEN to help him if possible to come to England and ends his letter by asking OTTEN to speak to Mr. PRALESH.

Yours sincerely,

Lieut. Colonel V. Vivian, C.B.E., S.I.S.

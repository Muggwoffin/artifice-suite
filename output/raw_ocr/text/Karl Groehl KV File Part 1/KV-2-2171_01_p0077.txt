CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-

15.3.40. Note from B.2. to B.4b:-

"Lord Bearsted called to see me this morning on the advice of Colonel VIVIAN. He tells me that his section is in touch with Karl OTTEN and that material that Karl OTTEN gets from his contact Karl GROEHL should, by arrangement, be handed to him. He now has the impression that OTTEN is getting stuff from this source from Scandinavian countries, and he wishes to know whether we can do anything to check up on this, because if we have evidence of it or can obtain such evidence, it will be sufficient for them to sever connection with Karl OTTEN and advise his immediate internement.

For the sake of speed, I agreed with Lord Bearsted that if anything urgent arrived on the check I would telephone him and he could then send a D.R. to gather the material. Otherwise I would send it addressed to him at Room 055A, War Office. He would like to see all the material occurring on this check, at any rate for the next few weeks..."

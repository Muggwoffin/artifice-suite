SECRET.

CX/12650/4199, dated 26.4.39.

Dear Younger,

Reference your PF. 45807/B.4b. dated the 20th March, enclosing certain material seen in transit from GROEHL to OTTEN. We consider that most of the Air and Military information is concocted from published matter and re-edited.

As regards this OTTEN material in general, we already received his output through several sources and do not really need it. We should, however, be grateful for copies of any letters which will help to show his connections and sources. We are, as you know, interested in taking stock of the ramifications of all this refugee information which is flooding the country and this may help us in doing so.

Yours sincerely,

The Hon. Kenneth Younger,
M.I.5.

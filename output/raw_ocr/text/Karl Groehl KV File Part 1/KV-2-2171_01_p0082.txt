Summarised translation of two int. letters from GROEHL to OTTEN.

Paris, 27.11.39.

(1). From Karl GROEHL.

Dear Karl,

I am sending you a letter from Belgium, the Aachen frontier. You will see from it that the People have turned to Churchill, which is rather naive of them but it shows their impatience. Actually, they are quite right. One can do nothing without personal relationships.

The remark that the writer wants to leave there connects with the new preparations on the frontier.

I am now preparing a new association of German Officers. I hope something comes of it.

(2). Enclosure, from Belgium.

Discusses letter sent to Churchill. Hopes it will be mentioned on the English wireless, which would encourage the workers to persevere in their effort.

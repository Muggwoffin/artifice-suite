Copy of Translation. Original and Photostat filed in P.F. 45807 OTTEN, volume 5, 221c

19.11.39.

L.K. Yesterday evening I got your letter of the 16th and to-day an important letter from Aachen. This is to be sent on correctly like DREIR's of the day before yesterday. There is a lot in this new explanation. It should be pretty good. (Pretty good too against the idiots who made the picture of Hitler and Stalin.)

I will answer your letter but must first translate it correctly. (I can read it but always make a careful translation.)

Yes, I think our reporting must be put right. This will soon be done.

BUT IT IS NO GOOD WITHOUT TRAVELLING!!!!

I heard from Brussels that yesterday, Friday, there had been an action against SEPP. My informant also said that SEPP had been shot (or defeated - "geschlagen")!! So that is English "protection". The Germans don't let their people get shot. What actually has happened to SEPP???

Yours,

KARL

If I can get a press-card I shall be allowed to send my letters off the same day!!!

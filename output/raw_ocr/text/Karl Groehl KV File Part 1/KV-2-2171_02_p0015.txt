My dear Younger,

Reference your P.F. 45807/B4b of 10.3.39. regarding correspondence between GROEHL in Paris and Karl OTTEN in London.

Our Air Section states that the lists of German personnel are received periodically direct from OTTEN by Air Ministry. They are not copied from any official lists, but are mostly taken from the press and sources in Germany.

who recently drew up the chart of emigre organisations sent you under my CX/12535/V of 3.5.39., comments that these letters prove what earlier indications led him to believe, i.e. that MUNZENBERG, GROEHL, OTTEN, TREVIRANUS, etc., are all cooperating. He has, however, no idea who "Der Propelm". can be, but considers that "B.J." is probably JAKOB. He adds that there is every indication that MUNZENBERG is being financed by the I.T.F.

Another Section in this office comments as follows on this correspondence:

As regards the statement in letter A. 328 K. with reference to MUNZENBERG, I know it is the belief of some of his friends that he gets his money from London, but from where nobody knows. It might even come via London from Aschberg.

The last paragraph of this letter with regard to TREVIRANUS interests me. Assuming this to be Gottfried, I do not think he maintains any very active contacts in London now, though possibly still with Mr. Reginald McKenna. He is living up at Lancaster, managing a small factory in rather straitened circumstances. If it refers to his brother Alexandre, it is rubbish. The man was in Paris two weeks ago absolutely down and out. Moreover, he seemed to be in extremely bad health and unlikely to live long.

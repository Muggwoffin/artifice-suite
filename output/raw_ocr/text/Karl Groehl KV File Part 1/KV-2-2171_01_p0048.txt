P.F. 52095/B.4.c. 18th March, 1941.

Dear Whetmore,

Confirming our telephone conversation this afternoon, I am investigating in connection with suspected Soviet Espionage the activities of a man named Stanislas SZYMONCZYK who is living at 106, Oakwood Court, London, W.14.

I find that SZYMONCZYK is associating regularly with a man whom I believe to be identical with Stanislas RETZLAW who is known to you.

My informant states that the man associating with SZYMONCZYK is a German, but has a Polish passport; that he was in Spain and later in Portugal during the Spanish War and that he was ultimately conveyed by plane from Lisbon to London.

He is a middle-aged man and is so deaf that he uses a microphone attachment.

He visits SZYMONCZYK regularly and it is reported that he claims to have some means of transmitting correspondence abroad in such a way as to avoid the censor.

I should be very grateful if you could kindly ascertain and let me know whether the man associating with SZYMONCZYK is identical with RETZLAW and if so I should be interested to know why he is associating with SZYMONCZYK and to receive any information respecting SZYMONCZYK's activities

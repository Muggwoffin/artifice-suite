In the course of the enquiries, a number of associates of Szymonczyk have come to light, and the position as regards the more important of them may shortly be stated as follows:-

1. Andre Labarthe.
   Adverse reports upon this man's history prior to his arrival in England have been received, but were discredited when it was found that they emanated from Sergeant Collin. The enquiries in this case have not disclosed that Labarthe is engaged in any activities savouring of espionage.

2. Mrs. Therese Orlow and her son Alexander Orlow. I am reasonably satisfied that these two persons are not consciously engaged in any improper activities (PP.54176)

3. Alexander Zussman Orkin.
   This man was seen to make contact with Szymonczyk on one occasion in circumstances which were somewhat mysterious, but it has since been explained that Orkin at the time was endeavouring to interest Szymonczyk in his patents. Orkin is now in the Royal Engineers and is subject to supervision by his Commanding Officer.

4. I have discovered that Szymonczyk is an associate of Samuel Urievitch who is the subject of separate enquiries which are still continuing. (RE.451/362)

By reason of his association with "La France Libre", Szymonczyk has come into contact with a number of other aliens, e.g. Miss Lehner, and Miss Gompertz, who have previously come to notice, but it has not been possible to establish that the association has been concerned with espionage. It is obviously impossible, within the scope of this memorandum, to particularise the many contacts that have come to light, but I can if thought fit explain the position in discussion.

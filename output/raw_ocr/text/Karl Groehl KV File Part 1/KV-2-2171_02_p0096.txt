GROSS - REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: K. Groehl.

On 11.10.37., K. Groehl wrote to K. Otten on notepaper headed "Editions Asra". The financial position is still bad but he still is hopeful. A great deal of dissension is going on between Seehof, Sievers and Jacob; some articles are good, some are bad etc. His wife is trying to get a position in England and he wants to send the child to school there.

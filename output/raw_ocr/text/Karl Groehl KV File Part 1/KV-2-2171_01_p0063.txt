METROPOLITAN POLICE.
SPECIAL BRANCH,
10 JAN 1941

9th day of January, 1941.

With reference to M.I.5 letter W.5a/JS/L.I237 of 15.12.40, asking that Karl OTTEN of 58, Hampstead Way, N.W.11, be interviewed regarding a letter addressed to him from Portugal:-

On 8th January, 1941, I saw Karl OTTEN, German, registered under serial No.E.Z.260737, at the above address and he informed me that the attached letter was written by a Pole named Stanislaw (Stan) RETZLAW who now lives at 11, Peters Court, Porchester Road, W.2, and is registered with police under serial No.Q.X.1360.

RETZLAW, born at Inoworatzlow, Poland, on 2.6.98, formerly lived at 11, Rue Loopole Bellan, Paris and according to OTTEN acted as an agent for the British Government. He received items of information by various means from the person referred to in the letter as 'Ernst', who is stationed in an anti-aircraft unit in southern Germany and is an anti-Hitlerite.

Some time after the capitulation of France, RETZLAW fled to Spain and when Ernst received news of the coming aerial attack on London, he was unable to contact him, with the result that the information was passed to an agent in Switzerland, who made an unsuccessful attempt to telephone London from Basle (referred to as B.)

RETZLAW suffered considerable privation after his flight from France, a fact which OTTEN considers was responsible for the rather indiscreet contents of his letter. On 11th October, 1940, RETZLAW was conveyed by plane from Lisbon to London.

OTTEN further gave me to understand that he and RETZLAW both work for M.I.5 and he mentioned the names of Col. Douglas and Major Longmore.

Submitted.

C. Ludgros
P.S.

Inspector.

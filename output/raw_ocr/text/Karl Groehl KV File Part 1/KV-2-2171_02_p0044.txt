Summary.

From Karl Groehl. Paris 26.11.38
To. Karl Otten.

I have just received your letter and the enclosed £2. The journal has been sent off to-day. What you say about the Soviet has been confirmed by the news of the Danish spy affair.

I hear from C.B.R. that the Communist Party are excluding all the good old people. All this stupid boasting of Woroshilow and the paid men like Werner-Schifrin (real name)

I hear that a book is being published by "Pont de l'Europe".

I am sending 2 numbers of F.D. to press. The advertisement is in both numbers. The big advertisement is not yet in. Sievers is coming here on Monday and will do the number himself. I will send the number out with Sievers.

I know Sanders, he is in touch with B.J.

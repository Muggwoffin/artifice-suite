6.8.41.

My dear,

I have to-day received a letter from Marseille by messenger. Tell Aaron please that the writer sends the following information about public opinion there:— The Germans are very active in the South of France as well as in occupied territory, they make use of every difficulty by means of propaganda against England. But in spite of this the opinion is universally spreading and the people are continuously saying secretly that they will attack the Germans in the rear if they should make an invasion of England which fails. Everything is waiting for that. But the people speak much more about Weygand than about de Gaulle. It is said that Weygand is preparing a pretended revolt against Germany.

2) Provide me with two pounds. The article is in fact worth twenty, but I send you the others.

3) Denicke is still in Marseille.

4) First of all give a greeting to Mad. O. (? Madame ORLOW).

5) I left my umbrella behind at your house.

Greetings Ka.

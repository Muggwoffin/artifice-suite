With reference to telephone message of 4.40 p.m. this day from Colonel Venn, Command Security Officer, London area, regarding a man named OLDAG, who had just arrived from Germany, and who had suggested that he could give some information regarding conditions in Germany:-

OLDAG left England on 18th February 1939, returning to Amsterdam, and later toured Belgium, France and Switzerland, returning to Paris where he met a number of German political refugees and a nameless organisation was formed, the sole object of which would appear to be the destruction, by means of sabotage, of points of military importance in Germany. The principal of this group, he stated, was a German named Karl GROEHL, or FRIEDBERG of 7 rue Barrault, Paris XIII, which address is also the headquarters of the organisation.

OLDAG stated that he had spent a great deal of time working on behalf of the above mentioned group, particularly during the last three months, when he had been travelling to and from Switzerland, Germany, and France, meeting the various contacts in Basle, Geneva, Freiberg and other border towns on the Swiss-German frontier. He was now in England to propose to the Military Authorities here that a number of travelling trunks, packed with explosives - made up in the style adopted by the I.R.A. - should be sent to high German officials. OLDAG stated that his organisation would undertake to convey the trunks over the frontiers and see that 7 trunks so composed should find their mark, on payment by the British of 7,000 Belgian francs. With this object in view he had arrived in London from Geneva on a special refugee train.......

From Karl Groehl
To Karl Otten
Date of letter 14.12.38.

Summary of Contents

Why do you not write? Have you received the book? Has Erich written back to the Czech (?)? What is the American doing? I suppose that you have read that America is about re-armament. Have you any means of getting in touch with them? That would be valuable. Think about Poland -......Klatz is working for Poland (also for ?). He has a great deal of money, a motor car etc. Please write.

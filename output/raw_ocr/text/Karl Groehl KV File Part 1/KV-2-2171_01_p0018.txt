B.13. (Colonel Hinchley Cooke)

I am making enquiries into the case of Walter and Ernst LOEWENHEIM who are at present interned and applying for release under Category 19 of the Home Office White Paper.

I understand that the LOEWENHEIMS are well known to Stanislas RETZLAW who is the subject of P.F. 46464 and is now employed by S.O.2.

I should be grateful if you would obtain through S.O.2. a report from RETZLAW upon the LOEWENHEIMS. The report should be as full as possible, specifying what particulars are in RETZLAW's own knowledge and which are derived from other sources specifying the sources if possible.

I enclose photographs of the LOEWENHEIM brothers and should be glad if you would ask S.O.2. to produce these to RETZLAW for identification purposes. The photographs should then please be returned.

Will you please at the same time ask S.O.2. to enquire from RETZLAW whether he knows the surname and present address of a woman whose Christian name is "Vicky" and who is known to have been an associate of the LOEWENHEIM brothers.

I enclose a spare carbon copy of this note to facilitate your enquiry.

B.4.c.
17.5.41.

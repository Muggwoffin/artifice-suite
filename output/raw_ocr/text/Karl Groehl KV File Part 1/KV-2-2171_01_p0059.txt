CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:
GROEHL

Extract from Note of Interview with Bedford. Re SZYMONCZYK and LECOUTRE. B.4.c. 27.2.41.

Bedford mentioned that Mrs. ORLOW had told him that SZYMONCZYK regularly met a German whose Christian name was Karl who had represented that he was acting for the British Intelligence Service. Bedford believed that SZYMONCZYK and Karl often met at the Jewish restaurant at 55(?) Berwick Street.

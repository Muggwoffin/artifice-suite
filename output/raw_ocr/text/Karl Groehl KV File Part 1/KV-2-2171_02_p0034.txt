B.4b

OTTEN Group.

The following information has been received from M/S and was obtained by him in the course of a conversation he had with Carl OTTEN on the evening of 25th January.

1. OTTEN was sounded on the subject of Czech refugees. According to OTTEN, there is no definite C.P. organising centre yet, but he thinks one is bound to be formed sooner or later, for already the Czech Communist refugees are quarrelling violently with the others. Some of the Communists have been given hospitality with private persons and the rest have been sent to various youth hostels.

2. OTTEN was in a very excited state over the fall of Barcelone, and in the course of conversation it emerged that OTTEN has recently been in close touch with the CHURCHILLS. On 24th January he had a long interview with young Randolph CHURCHILL. It appears that OTTEN is going to try and use the CHURCHILLS as a medium for explaining to the British people what Germany really is and what she means to do.

3. With reference to a recent enquiry from B.4b regarding the identity of "The professor" mentioned by Carl GROEHL, M/S says that he has heard the title applied jokingly to LEHMANN several times.

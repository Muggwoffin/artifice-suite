GROSS - REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl Groehl.

Groehl wrote 2 letters to Otten dated 17th and 18th of April 1937. The first was mainly about French politics. At the end Groehl said that Herr S. had not let him know whether he was coming to Paris and Groehl assumed that he did not wish to see him after his attitude over the Students Fee which was still owing and had not been sent. The same had happened at Christmas which showed exactly how things stood.

In the second letter Groehl said he had visited the H.S. Chemist, he was very good, politically he knows next to nothing but will be of great use to them. He has many interesting things of which could nothing could be written. He (Chemist) is going to meet his friend who is still in D and will get some more material.

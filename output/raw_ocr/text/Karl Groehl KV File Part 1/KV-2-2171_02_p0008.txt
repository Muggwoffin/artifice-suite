Copy from. P.F. 45-807. 182a.

P.F. 45807/B4b. 11th July 1939

Dear I.P.I.,

We have for some time had a warrant in operation on correspondence passing to a German refugee in London called Karl OTTEN.

OTTEN co-operates with another refugee, Karl GROEBHL, living in Paris, in running an intelligence and propaganda service against the Nazi regime in Germany. GROEBHL and OTTEN have both been reported in the past as having connections with the Comintern, but they are now strongly opposed to it.

The following are extracts from two letters written by GROEBHL to OTTEN apropos of agents which they are employing for their work.

3.7.39. "There were three NEHRUS, father and two sons. This one is the eldest son. The father is dead. He knew LENIN and was at the Eastern Congress etc. I have a woman friend from Munich whom I have known for 20 years. She was a friend of NEHRU for 10 years and was with him in India. Her sister was the wife of ROY. You will certainly have heard of both these sisters, Lu and Eva. NEHRU's name is A.C. NEHRU NAMBIAR. He has an English passport and spent six days in London eight weeks ago. I wrote to him yesterday to make an appointment. There is no possibility of mistake, he is 100% Fascist".

4.7.39. "Frightful mistake! My NEHRU is not the right NEHRU. My man, A.S. N. NAMBIAR, is a friend of NEHRU and corresponds with him regularly. NAMBIAR is a sort of correspondent of the group and thus acquired the nickname of NEHRU. I got to know him as NEGUS-NEHRU through the sisters, without knowing that it was a nickname. Nevertheless the man is important. He knows everything the Nazis do there. He carries a good deal of weight among Indians."

We have no up-to-date information about NAMBIAR. I should be interested to know whether you have anything to confirm the statement that he knows all about Nazi activities.

Yours sincerely,

CROSS REFERENCE TO SZYMONCZYK AND LECOUTRE.

Note of Interview with Bedford.

2. Bedford has not yet been able to ascertain the identity of Karl, but Mrs. ORLOW reported to him that she had received a letter from her husband and as Bedford had not received any letters from Holland respecting his mother or the state of his business Mrs. ORLOW said that she understood that Karl had told SZYMONCZYK that he, Karl, had an agent who would take the letters to Tangiers and would there meet a Dutchman who would take the letters to Holland and similarly bring back the replies. In the hope of ascertaining Karl's identity Bedford wrote an innocuous letter of enquiry to his secretary and handed it to Mrs. ORLOW for transmission to Holland via Karl, but it was found that neither Karl nor the agent called during the course of the next few days and Bedford later learned that Mrs. ORLOW had destroyed his letter as well as her own as she thought it dangerous for them to be left about. It has, however, been arranged that Mrs. ORLOW will let Bedford know when there is an opportunity of sending a further letter. Bedford says that Karl calls fairly regularly at Oakwood Court for lunch or dinner. Bedford believes that Karl sometimes uses the name BERG, but it is very probable that this is an alias. Karl has, Bedford believes, known SZYMONCZYK for twenty years. Mrs. ORLOW says that she does not know Karl's proper name.

4. Bedford does not know who Stani R. is, nor does he know anyone bearing the name RETZLAW.

6. Bedford does not know anyone of the name of DENICKE.

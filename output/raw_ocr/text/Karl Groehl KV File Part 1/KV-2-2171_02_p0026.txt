SECRET.

PF 45807/B.4b

20th March, 1939.

Dear Major Vivian,

I enclose the following material which we have seen passing from GROEHL to OTTEN:-

1) Report from Berlin dated 10.3.39., A slip dated 7.3.39. which probably belongs to the report on the personnel of the Air Force previously sent to you.

2) A rough translation of some of the points of interest contained in a letter from GROEHL to OTTEN, dated 7.3.39., together with a statement of GROEHL's expenditure, which gives some idea of the extent of his activities. I think this statement refers only to the sending out of the letters referred to in the attached translation.

3) Letter from KURT INAGE, Dortmund. This letter was presumably sent to GROEHL, who forwarded it to OTTEN. I attach a summary in translation of this letter but have enclosed the original as well, as it seems to be of more interest to you than to us.

I would be interested to hear if you can throw any light on the question of ASRA which appears to be used as a cover address by the OTTEN organisation. Perhaps you can also say what is meant by the sentence - "The silly asses call themselves I.S."

It would be useful to me if you could at the same time express an opinion on the material which we have been sending you on this check. There is a considerable quantity of it and I do not want to trouble you with it if you consider it of little value. I have been trying to limit the material which I send you to reports containing specific military information and details of Otten's own organisation for obtaining information in Germany.

Yours sincerely,

Signed K.G.Y.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

P.S. I also enclose a report on the Nationalsozialistche Fleiger-Korps which has just come in. Enclosed in the same package was a letter from an agent of GROEHL's in Basle who stated that "a high influential politician, Regierungsrat, in Basle, told him that whatever official statement might be made it was quite certain that the Swiss would not resist a German occupation", and that this information was confirmed by another excellent source. The Regierungsrat had already sent his daughter out of the Basle Area.

CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: Karl Groehl.

According to a M/S report dated 23.5.38, it was arranged by Lehmann-Russbueldt that M/S should act "as Mr. Smith's letter box" as the mail of 19 Oppidans Road was already large enough. M/S was to take all the material from Groehl which would be entirely about the book he is producing.

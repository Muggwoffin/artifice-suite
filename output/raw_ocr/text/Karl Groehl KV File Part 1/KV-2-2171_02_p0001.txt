P.F.45807/24b.  25th July 1939

Dear Major Vivian,

With reference to your CX/12650/4199 of 26.4.39, concerning OTTEN's connections and sources, in a letter from GROEBEL to OTTEN dated 4.7.39, the former mentioned a certain A.S.N. NAMBIAR, an Indian who is a friend of NEHRU.

GROEBEL considers him to be an important man, as according to his (GROEBEL's) words "he knows everything the Nazis do and carries a good deal of weight among Indians".

We have learnt since from I.P.I. that NAMBIAR was born at Tellicherry, Southern India, on 15.6.96. He was formerly married to a sister of the well-known communist Virendranath CHATTOPADHYAYA. He went to Berlin in 1921 where he joined the communist group run by his brother-in-law and which was also largely concerned in the formation of the League against Imperialism. He remained in Germany until the Nazis came to power in 1933 when he was obliged to flee to Prague. He was in touch with S. bhas BOSE when the latter was in Europe and through his influence he was permitted to pay one visit to Berlin after he had settled in Prague.

After the September crisis he left Prague for Paris where he stayed for a short time and was in touch with two women, Lu SCHULER and Eva GEISSLER-SCHULER. He is now living at the Hotel Vermont, 11 bis, rue du Bois de Boulogne, Paris.

NAMBIAR is a very old friend of Jawaharlal NEHRU. When the latter founded his newspaper "The National Herald" of Lucknow, he made NAMBIAR his correspondent for Central Europe. NAMBIAR visited London some weeks back and was in touch with V.K. Krishna MENON, the London correspondent of the "National Herald". NAMBIAR is a communist and according to GROEBEL 100% anti-Fascist.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

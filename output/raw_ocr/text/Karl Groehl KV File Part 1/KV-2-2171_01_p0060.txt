Translation of letter to SZYMONCZYK, dated 18.1.41.

My dear,

I hope it will not be inconvenient if I call at 1 o'clock on Monday. I think I have so far copied the French matters which are, however, interesting.

Will you tell A. that it is very difficult for me to come away during the month of January. (Because I received my pay well before Christmas and spent half during the holidays).

But A. will surely be able to make a case of it.

All the best

Stani. R.

NOTE.

This intercepted letter was not received by B.4.c. until 22.1.41 when it was too late to arrange for B.6. to watch the arrival of "Stani R." at 106, Oakwood Court.

The "A." referred to may be Alta LECOUTRE, Andre LABARTHRE or Alexander ORLOW.

It will be noted that SZYMONCZYK's initials are not stated on the envelope, although the letter is addressed to him familiarly as "My dear"

R. Pickering
23/1/41. B.4.c.

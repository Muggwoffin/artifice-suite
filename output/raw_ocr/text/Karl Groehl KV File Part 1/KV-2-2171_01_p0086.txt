TRANSLATION OF INT. LETTER FROM KARL GROEHL TO KARL OTTEN.

Paris, 15.11.39.

LK. I got your letter of the 9th on 13.11. and the same evening PROBST came round and brought the letters of the 24.9. and the book.

In my opinion the letters take such a long time not because they are kept, but just through slackness. WE are no bureaucrats and manage to get everything done faster.

Re the handwritten B(ook?) per post: PROBST told me you were feeling depressed. But as he always talks of you with the greatest respect (that is a fact!!) I guessed it must be only a momentary depression. As I have more or less the last word in the matter of what is to be printed, I had it printed as I thought it came from you.

The following is essential: about a fortnight ago I sent a sort of circular to our friends in which I said we could not work as individuals for the duration but must in the end start some kind of organisation. The organisation must have a name. LEX is to remain the password, but it can't be the name of the organisation. The name must be that of a party or movement. As a movement we must have a managing committee (the work being done at present has nothing to do with it!). The Committee will have to communicate the formation of the movement to all parties and governments. A "work" must never appear to come from abroad. EVERYTHING, whatever it is, is to be the work of the organisation.

In this connection one naturally thinks of MAX etc., although he always wants to take on the leadership, is inclined to be unsociable, and above all can't bear being criticised. He is rather an old fool, but I prefer him to 100 WILLIS. Don't let's have anything to do with WILLI, he will frighten too many people away.

By the way, PROBST was rather annoyed the day before yesterday when I asked him what was wrong (your letter had just come and I was in a bad temper, that's why I was rude); I said I didn't want a quarrel, was no servant etc., He tried to soothe me but I was pretty tough. He is sure to make excuses to you: "It was all a misunderstanding."

I noticed that even Madame hasn't seen the papers you refer to. But you are still pretty low in her estimation though our good friend goes his way.

My dear man, I know the "Thanks from the House of Hapsburg" very well. OTTO is more than a fool. But he has managed to buy DEUTSCH, Julius, in exchange for a promise to mention him to the General and the Minister of War. But no Austrian "Sozi" knows anything about it. DEUTSCH is corrupt. Please tell everybody that. DEUTSCH must be settled. Moreover he used to do the paper "KRIEG UND FRIEDEN" ("WAR AND PEACE") for the Russians. I know that for certain.

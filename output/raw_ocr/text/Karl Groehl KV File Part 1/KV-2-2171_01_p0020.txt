Translation of a letter from V. Marcu to Mr. Stan RETZLAW of 11 Peters Court, Porchester Road, London, W.2.

27.3.41.

Dear Friend,

I have been here a few days and should be leaving for New York in a few days time. I should love to be in England in order to help you. If you should have a real job of work for me to do, some political work, write to me in New York. I shall leave my wife and child and make haste to come to you. My address will be,
V. Marcu,
c/o Hermann Kesten,
Hotel Park Plaza,
50, West 77th St.
New York.

Yours,
V.M.

Information on Maps.

1. Map of Dusseldorf.

No. 1 = Airmen's barracks. For one squadron who have no number on their uniforms, so cannot identify them. In (1) is the 2nd Squadron, "Jagdgeschwader", No. 234...... No. 1a = "Rollfeld". The airport for the civil aviation serves as a cover. At the back are the munition dumps. The map itself is of the "Horst", guarded by soldiers and S.S. men.

2. = Infantry Barracks. I.R. 39 near the town.
3. = Artillery Barracks. A.R. 26 outside the town.
4. = Factories "Rheinmetall and Mannesman", both working for the Army.

The approaches are for No. 1, Tramway No. 11 to the Airport; further on greatest precautions against danger of arrest must be taken.

For No. 2, Line 7 is less dangerous.
For No. 3, to the Honigheim Station; further back the greatest care is necessary.
For No. 4, Line 12, the way is forbidden; in all investigations no cameras should be taken.

2. Mettmann's Map.

No. 1 = big manoeuvre grounds.
No. 1a. In the enclosure in the wood there are munition dumps (shown with ...) W.T. means Water Tower, which will be used as a Wireless Station. From here a signalling arrangement to Jaberg can be seen on the Hilden Map.

The red line through the map indicates the planned road.

3. Hilden Map.

The red spot is Jaberg, a good watch tower. As already stated, a signalling arrangement has been set up from here to the water tower in Mettmann. Because of the "Schwere Flak" situated at Mettmann, which lights Dusseldorf nearly every evening, an anti-air search light has been erected.

4. Approaches.

Electric train from Hilden to Waldschenke Station. From here a little path goes into the wood. I cannot explain this to a stranger as the way is very complicated. Here one must be very careful not to be noticed, as a careless word can have unpleasant consequences. Here every soldier is told to report anyone who asks any questions to the Commander. It might help the stranger if he asked for Jaberg. I have the necessary information (?). The whole land is in a "secret state of mobilisation in peace", if I can so describe it.
No. 2 are the well-known Henkel Persil works, that can manufacture poison in wartime.

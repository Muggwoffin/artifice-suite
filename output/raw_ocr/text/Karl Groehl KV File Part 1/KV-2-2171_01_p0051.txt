CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT: GROEHL.

Extract from Telephone Check on WESTern 4930. 17.3.41.

17.3.41. 11.5. a.m.

In call for Mr. SZYMONCZYK from "Karl" who wants to see him very urgently. The latter asks him if he has not received his letter. SZYMONCZYK seems to be very evasive and excuses himself on the grounds that he is very busy. Tells "KARL" to ring him next week. "KARL" is somewhat disappointed.

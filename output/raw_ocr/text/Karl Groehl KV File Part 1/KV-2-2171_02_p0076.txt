7.6.38.

Dear Herr B.

Now after endless trouble and with risks that in less favourable circumstances would have cost me my life, I have finished my task. I had the impression from H. that you rated my task too slightly and underestimated the dangers. You will realise this when I describe everything to you.

First the obtaining of the maps:

This can only be done through contact with people in high places which is a big expense. You know the demands are not small. I have sent the maps by suitable people across the frontier as they would have never come through the post. The notes could only be made abroad, and so I travelled with my observer to - . Think of the cost and the danger! etc.

I must say that many of the risks were taken because I am an idealist and wish to make an end to the present conditions in Germany, but unfortunately I am not in a financial position to do everything for idealism. I told you this in H., so please could you send me some compensation etc. I have dedicated myself to your service, but must live. You can check my work. But be careful whom you choose for this work, as dangers lurk in every corner. I can send you a detailed account. I must remind you that I could not work alone, but with my new friend.

Enclosed is the note.

M.I.3.b.

We have recently seen a summary of an article on the German fortifications on their Western frontier, which appeared in the military periodical "Die Wehrmacht", No. 21, dated 1938.

No doubt you already have seen this periodical. If not, however, and you think it would be of any interest to you to see the summary, I could let you have it.

Prima facie, it does not appear to contain much information which would be of use to you.

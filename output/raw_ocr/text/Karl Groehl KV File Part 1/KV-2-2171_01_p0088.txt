COPY of Translation. Original and Photostat filed in P.F. 45807 OTTEN, volume 5, 220a.

13.11.39.

Dear Otten,

I have just got your letter of 6th. It would be much better if we could have a talk as we have so much to tell each other. But to turn to the important matter; you will be interested to know what effect the Munich affair had among the German people. I can assure you, in the light of all my experiences, that it is disastrous for the Nazis! There are certainly many people who believe that the Nazis did it themselves - that England had a finger in the pie is the opinion of a few, but the greater part of the people find it absurd, impossible and ridiculous. This affair has revealed such a vulnerable spot in the Nazi organisation that anywhone who has had any experience cannot possibly believe that they did it themselves! As yet we cannot judge of the effect in comparison with that of the Reichstag fire, either in its historical perspective or its immediate consequences. This is roughly the effect in South Germany; it is for them the first blow, even if it didn't quite succeed, and it has been struck by the Opposition, no matter exactly whom, against the Tyrant. This is what a number of individuals think, and it is they who build up the growing mass of those who oppose the Regime. Internal collapse in Germany is far nearer than the outside world imagines - the only thing to be realised is, there can be no other outward expression of it than that people are discussing the official versions of the story. And do they discuss it! According to the reports I receive I have had every reason to believe (this is well-founded) that the affair was perpetrated by a definite group - not by the Nazis themselves. I am sure you will be interested to hear this.

I am very glad to be able to discuss the question of propaganda with you. I shall be able to start something here and will let you know about it. I have certain friends with whom I shall be able to arrange things. This I will tell you about by word of mouth - only see that I am able to speak to you soon. Destructive propaganda; such as we have had up till now, couldn't be better. Positive propaganda, very moderate in its expression but all the more powerful in its content. These two qualities make an excellent combination.

I am in perfect agreement with the group! And it is certainly high time! But we have still got time and all will come right. We shall even manage to get things settled as regards our programme if we are clever enough to make the fundamental principal of its propaganda the linking up of Peace with Hitler's downfall. I can see definite possibilities in various ways. But we must have a really good talk about all this. Do get it arranged as soon as possible. If there weren't the technical difficulties I would come at once. I hope it will soon be arranged. I have a definite position here now and good reason not to be quite so pessimistic about the future. The work is darned hard, but I'm managing, and with future possibilities in mind, I must not abate one jot of my effort.

Copy sent to S.I.S. on 28.11.39. (See PF.45807 OTTEN, v.5. 225a.)

SECRET.

PF.45807/B.4b

9th August, 1938.

Dear Vivian,

With further reference to my PF.42816/B.4b, dated 12.5.38, I now enclose a copy of a letter passing from Karl GROEHL in Paris to Karl OTTEN in London. I am unable to suggest the source of the report on an underground barracks in S. Germany, or to assess its reliability.

I am unable to identify H. von ZWEHL, but am making some enquiries about him. It is just possible he may be identical with a certain Hans W. von ZWEHL who wrote, from the address, Wein IX, Thurngasse 11/22, in March 1936, to LEHMANN-RUSSBUELDT, about obtaining English contacts for a propaganda organisation to combat the official Nazi propaganda.

It is interesting to have this confirmation of the origin of the lecture by General von REICHENAU, about which we spoke on the telephone.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

CROSS REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-

15.3.40. Translation of an int. letter from Karl GROEHL to Karl OTTEN, dated Paris, 12.3.40:

No. 18.

"L.K. I have received your letter (of the 14th. number 14.
Well, that doesn't matter. But I would like to come. Only for a short time though.

It would be a good thing if you could return the Munich letter. ??Kiv P. makes nothing of reports - not much - only -?.

I haven't had the 15 yet, but will see to-day.

Yours,

Karl."

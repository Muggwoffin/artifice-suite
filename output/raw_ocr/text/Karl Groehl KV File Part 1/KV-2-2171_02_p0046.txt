PF.45807/B.4b 2nd November, 1938.

Dear Major Vivian,

With further reference to our PF.42816/B.4b of 12.5.38, I am enclosing photostat copies of two documents about German air armaments, picked up passing from Karl GROEBL to Carl OTTEN. I am unable to say who is the author of the report with sketches attached. The author of the one entitled Allgemeines Uber Lufttrustungen is Berthold JACOB.

JACOB is believed by OTTEN to be a first class military expert and is a fairly recent emigrant from Germany. I do not know what his experience in Germany was. Our contact, however, in the OTTEN group has been given to understand that he knows a good deal about the administrative side of the German Air Force, but is not to be taken too seriously on technical matters.

I also attach a copy of another memorandum on the state of the German Air Force, which was written by WOLFFSOHN. WOLFFSOHN is a Jew who has been in this country for a considerable time, and hopes shortly to apply for naturalisation. In the opinion of our contact, he is a clever man and entirely honest and reliable. The enclosed memorandum is believed to have been written for Lord Robert CECIL, through whom presumably it would go to the British authorities.

I should be interested to have your opinion of the memorandum. If you think it would be of any use to you to be put in touch with WOLFFSOHN, I think this could easily be arranged by a member of this office. WOLFFSOHN was a pilot in the Air Branch of the German Navy during the War, and is said to have been associated with General MILCH in the building of Germany's camouflaged Air Force before the Nazi regime came into power. He worked for some time as a journalist for Wolff's Telegraphen Bureau, Berlin, and had to leave Germany in 1933.

A copy of WOLFFSOHN's memorandum has been sent to the Air Ministry. I shall not have the others translated and sent to them unless you consider they are of importance. I have not kept copies of the letters myself.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

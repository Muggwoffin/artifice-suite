From Karl Groehl

To Karl Otten.

Date of letter 15.9.38.

Summary of Contents

I read parts of your letter of the 13th aloud to some journalists, they were in full agreement. Everywhere fury is directed against England. In a letter I have received from Prague they say "We have two enemies, England and Germany". Sykes seems to be very light-headed, through his stupidity I have no visa. Wol. says that the American edition has been refused because of the bad translation. Sykes knows no German. I have to meet 2 men from Germany it will be very difficult. Czecho-Slovakia will be a second Spain unless they are freed from Germany. Be J agrees with me that there will be a bath of blood in Germany when things are let loose. Follows particulars about the "Heeresaufstellung" (Distribution of Troops)

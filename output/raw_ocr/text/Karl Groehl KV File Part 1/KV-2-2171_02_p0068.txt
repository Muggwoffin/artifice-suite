Regarding the Intelligence work of the group, this has been done by an inner ring of the group. WOLFFSOHN himself is only a co-opted member; he does not attend all their meetings.

While on the subject of the group, WOLFFSOHN referred to Carl GROEHL. WOLFFSOHN describes him as the old-fashioned type of revolutionary who cannot be happy without a revolver in his pocket and a scheme for blowing someone up. Apparently GROEHL had a hectic career before quitting his native country. He was thrown out of every Left Wing party in turn. He is a quarrelsome fellow and is utterly impossible in any team work. WOLFFSOHN considers that the only reason why he has not quarrelled with the "Primrose League" is the fact that they operate in London and he is in Paris. GROEHL, however, is the centre of their Continental Intelligence service.

WOLFFSOHN also mentioned GLAS. GLAS is definitely a member of the Intelligence group and appears to be an extremely unpleasant character. He is a Trieste Jew by birth and considered tough by even Trieste's standards. He has been associated with many shady transactions and is also known as a heroin smuggler. WOLFFSOHN has several times warned OTTEN and RUSSBUELDT about GLAS, but they refuse to listen to anything against him.

The visit of Carl GROEHL to OTTEN is likely to take place some time between the 9th and 14th August, and it is clear that Carl OTTEN attaches the greatest importance to this visit.

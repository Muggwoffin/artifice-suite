Copy. Original in 45807 Otten v.2. 106a.

From Groehl
To Otten
Dated 9,12,38.

Writer hopes that Otten is settled in.

Enclosed letter from BJ. Groehl said that during the recent visit of Ribbentrop to Paris a certain number of German communists had been detained at the request of the German Government. Although the principals of the party had not affected BJ had been confined for a couple of days. Groehl thinks a pact with Poland is very important. He had nothing much of importance to tell about his journey to Switzerland, but it was necessary to go there now and again, otherwise his agents became slack. Otten's book costs 9.40 in Switzerland which is much too expensive. Groehl saw the girl he hopes to marry while in Switzerland, she wants to wait.

The enclosed letter from BJ. comments on his detention. His wife is working with him.

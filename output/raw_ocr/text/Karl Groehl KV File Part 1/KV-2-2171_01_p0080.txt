COPY OF TRANSLATION OF INT. LETTER FROM GROEHL TO OTTEN.
Original in P.F.45807 vol.5. 228b.

Paris, 30.11.39.

In answer to your letter of the 25.11.39. from England, I must tell you No, I have no travelling permit and as a journalist could only travel if I was employed by a paper. On the contrary I must return one of the letters from a paper. Someone must have written without the knowledge of the paper. In war time I cannot risk it.

Two of my best people in Jugoslavia have gone to Po as I cannot come to them and give them any papers. Soon all the work will be finished. Then I shall go to Polen where I shall get the papers immediately. They have asked him not to work for the British but for them.

Concerning the West Front, I have written to all the Western posts and told them to take care, but if such a letter is read either in Belgium or in Switzerland the receiver would be arrested.

I enclose the Savings Book I have made.

I have not yet seen the Christmas Cards.

Enclosure

A printed "Savings Book" in which particulars are given of the "savings" of GOEBBELS, GOERING, RIBBENTROP, etc., designed for propaganda.

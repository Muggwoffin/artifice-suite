OTTEN has recently been in correspondence with a certain FISCHER or VISCHER in Holland, who was formerly on the staff of the D.N.B. in Paris, although nominally only a refugee. He was expelled from France last year and is now in Holland. It seems possible that FISCHER is identical with a Dr. R. FISCHER, who came to the notice of one of our sources in 1930, when the following particulars were supplied:

Dr. R. FISCHER, Press and Propaganda chief of the newly-formed "Volkskonservativen-Partei", of which Herr TREVIRANUS is the leader. Now with the Volkspartei. FISCHER does not give "information", but under the influence of a good dinner can be induced to talk sufficiently freely to obtain line of enquiry.

In March last a Dr. Fischer was reported to us as in touch with an ex-agent of ours in Holland and as being engaged from Holland in the dissemination of anti-Nazi propaganda inside Germany, in which he was said to have had a very considerable success. It seems possible that this Dr. FISCHER is identical with the Dr. R. FISCHER who came to our notice in 1930, and also with the FISCHER stated to have been recently in touch with Karl OTTEN.

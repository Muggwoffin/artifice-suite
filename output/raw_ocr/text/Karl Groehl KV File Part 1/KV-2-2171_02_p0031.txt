CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-
Groehl.

On the 8th and 10th of February, K. Groehl wrote to to Otten enclosing information on the German Army and Air Force. He also sent a letter from a friend who had been in Switzerland, and who had obtained information regarding the German-Swiss and the German-Italian relations.

Information at 129a sent to S.I.S. on 27.2.39.

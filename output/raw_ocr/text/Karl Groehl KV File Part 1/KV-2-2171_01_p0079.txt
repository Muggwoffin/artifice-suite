B.4b

I was informed by telephone by M/S at 4.30 p.m. to-day that Karl GROEHL would be "leaving for France" at about 6 p.m. M/S was not clear whether this meant leaving London or sailing from a Channel port. As with whom GROEHL has been staying, will be with him until 6 p.m., GROEHL probably leaves London at that time.

GROEHL was to have come from France to stay with OTTEN for a week, but is understood to have been taken ill whilst with OTTEN, so may have been here longer.

M/S thought that GROEHL should be thoroughly searched at the port, as he is regarded with some suspicion.

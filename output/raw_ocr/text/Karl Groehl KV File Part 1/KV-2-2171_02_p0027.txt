CROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:- Groehl.

On 20.3.39 Karl Groehl wrote to Karl Otten from Paris. He said that they must prepare to protect themselves against the possibility of the whole gang one day turning against anything on the left. He added that the "Könenleute" were the biggest dolts and cowards on earth. One must be careful of them. 1000 times rather the people round Rau.

B4B note stated that "Könenleute" might refer to people round Wilhelm Koenen, in which case Koenen was doing something of which nothing was known. Rau might refer to Rauschning.

28.

With reference to 82a (in P.F.45807), phoned the War Office to ask if any papers had been received from Mr. Wickham Steed for consideration by W.O. technicians. Discovered that Major Benfield, M.I.3.B. was dealing with them at that very moment. I therefore rang him up and mentioned that with regard to the material he had before him we knew the source of the information and if it would be of any help to him I could tell him something about it.

Major Benfield then said the material was very interesting and from various technical reports previously received appeared to be fairly accurate. He said he had decided from internal evidence that it was produced by some group, possibly financed by Jews, with fairly wide ramifications.

I then gave him some account of the intelligence service worked by Carl OTTEN, and based upon Karl GROEHL in Paris. He was very interested to hear that GROEHL was in touch with certain German technical men who went in and out of Germany. I then told Major Benfield that as we knew a fair amount about it he might like to consult us further if the material warranted it, and that I could come over to the W.O. to see him and discuss the matter on some future occasion.

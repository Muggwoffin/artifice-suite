GROSS-REFERENCE.

SUBJECT:-- Groehl.

According to a M/S report dated 9.12.38, Wollenberg (author of the "Red Army") is staying incognito on Groehl's premises; having been refused permission by the French authorities to stay in France he has no alternative but to hide there.

Original in PF. 45807 Otten v.2. 107a. dated as above. MGH. 13.12.38.

S. Form 81.

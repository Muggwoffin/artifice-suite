Original in PF. 45807 Otten vol. 3. 136a.

From Groehl, Paris.
To. Otten, London.
Dated 21.2.39.

Summary.

My friend, Alfred Futran is doing the African article. He was nearly my brother-in-law, I lived with his sister. The father is the well known councillor who was killed in the Kapp putsch.

I have got some details on the Ukraine; everything that goes on there is most important.

Yes I can always employ Mark. Don't be angry or jealous about Rausch. I must think about Holland.

I know Richard Katz, he is the man who wrote the furious article against Erich. Details about the Propelmann follow, Groehl does not want him to know his name. Olden and Willy do not think about the "Zukunft".

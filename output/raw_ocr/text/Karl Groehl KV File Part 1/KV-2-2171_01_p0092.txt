EXTRACT.

Relating To Karl GROEHL.

Extracted from PF. 45000. MEISEL/OLDAG....... No.......28a.....................
Author of origin S.I.B.. REPORT............. Place and date of origin 19.9.39........
Extract made by D.G........................... on (date) 23.9.39.....................

With further reference to Arthur William OLDAG, who arrived from the Continent on the 16th September:-

OLDAG has again been seen, and he adheres to his story previously told, with the following amendments:-

In February 1939 he returned to Amsterdam, and later to Antwerp, where he states he made contact with German refugees who hate the Nazi regime as much as he does. He declines to give the names or other particulars of these refugees, but was referred by them to Karl GROEHL @ FRIEDBERG, of Rue Barrault 7, Paris XIII, who he described as a member of the central committee of the Communist Party. GROEHL was approached by OLDAG to work for the overthrow of Hitlerism, and OLDAG states that he was prepared to commit acts of sabotage for this purpose. He states that GROEHL promised him supplied of a colourless liquid with strong acid properties for sabotage in munition factories, docks and warehouses; a supply of fountain pens which explode when unscrewed, and a quantity of suitcases which explode upon opening. OLDAG states that whenever he pressed GROEHL for these explosives, the latter always put him off with some excuse such as "that the time was not opportune". GROEHL ultimately sent OLDAG to Switzerland with the promise to supply him with a quantity of the acid. This he failed to do, and after repeated requests he stated that he would forward the formula. This also GROEHL refused to do, and OLDAG then decided to return to Paris, but upon arriving at Geneva after the outbreak of war he was entrained for London as a refugee.

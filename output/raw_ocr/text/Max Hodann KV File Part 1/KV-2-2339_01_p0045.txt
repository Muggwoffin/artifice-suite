PF.43817/D.S.9.a.

23rd April, 1935.

Dear Mr. Cooper,

With reference to the cases of Max HODANN and Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON, who were landed on 18.3.35. for six weeks, I should be most grateful if you could arrange for us to be consulted if and when the question of extensions arises.

Yours sincerely,

(SD) K.W.W.S.

N. Cooper Esq., O.B.E.,
Home Office,
S.W.1.

From Edward R. ELKAN, 14a, Manchester Square, W.1.

To Max HODANN, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

Date of Letter 5.4.35.

Date of Postmark 3.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

No covering note - merely encloses letter of introduction to H.J. LASKI, 5, Addison Bridge Place, W.14.

Enclosure:

"Dr. HODANN is an old friend of mine and I should be very pleased if you could find time to see him and give him some advice how to get on in England. I am sure you would find him a most interesting person."

States that HODANN was formerly municipal physician, Berlin.

For translation see attached sheet.

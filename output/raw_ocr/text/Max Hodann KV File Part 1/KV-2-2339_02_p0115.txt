particulars of refusal were telephoned to H.O. on latter's request.

Since his refusal, alien has decided that he wishes to visit his daughter Renate. She is 14 and is studying at the Dora Russell School. Alien's wife from whom he is divorced is living in London and has the custody of the child. As the ship in which he arrived is not due to sail until tonight (19th instant) alien was permitted to remain - instead of returning to Esbjerg on the ship which sailed on 18th instant & in order that further representations might be made to the H.O.

Departure will be notified in due course.

(sgd) R. McKenna.

19th March 1935.

PF.43817/D.S.9.a.

16th April, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

With reference to my telephone conversation with [redacted] this morning in connection with Max HODANN, the following CX. references of the earlier history of this individual are noted on our files: - CX/12906/V. of 11.9.28., 2.10.28., 13.11.28., 28.1.29., CX/6561/V. of 29.4.31. and CX/9955/V. of 4.3.35.

Several points of distinct interest have emerged since his arrival here of which I gave you a brief note in our PF.43817/D.S.9.a. of 11.4.35.

The following further details you may like to have on your records.

Max Julius Alexander HODANN was born at Neisse on 30.8.94. He holds German passport No.48/Rev/28/31 issued in Berlin on 24.2.31. His description is as follows: - Face oval; eyes brown; hair darkish blonde; build medium. I also enclose a copy of his photograph.

HODANN is understood to have spent some time during 1934 in Palestine collaborating with [Mrs. LINDBAEK-NELSON] in writing a book - 'Jew are Coming Home'.

He is a Doctor of Medicine but apparently no longer practises but obtains a living by writing articles for newspapers on social questions. His sympathies, however, appear to be communist rather than socialist. He is a member of the Danish P.E.N. Club and is an authorised correspondent of the Danish newspaper "Arbeiderbladet".

Since his arrival in this country he has been living at Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, with Mrs. LINDBAEK-NELSON with whom he is supposed to be collaborating in writing the life of Dr. Nansen. From observation on his movements, however, it does not appear that he is spending very much of his time in this connection.

He has a divorced wife - Maria HODANN née SARAN - living in London, whom he visits occasionally. We have seen certain correspondence from one [redacted] who urges HODANN to return to Copenhagen where she, he and Sonja can live more cheaply together as she cannot manage on the money he sends her. She also refers to the question of separation.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

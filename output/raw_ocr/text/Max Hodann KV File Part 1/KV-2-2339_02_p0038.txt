Edward R. Elkan, M.D. L.R.C.P.

14A MANCHESTER SQUARE
LONDON, W.1

2nd April,
1935.

Dear Mr. Laski,

This is to introduce Dr. Max Hodann, formerly municipal physician, from Berlin.

He was one of the best known personalities who fought for sex reform in pre-Hitler Germany. He has also written an interesting book about Germany's eastern neighbours and recently one about Palestine, although he is not Jewish.

Dr. Hodann is an old friend of mine and I should be very pleased if you could find time to see him and give him some advice how to get on in England. I am sure you would find him a most interesting person.

With friendly greetings to Mrs. Laski,

I am,

Yours sincerely,

Edward R. Elkan

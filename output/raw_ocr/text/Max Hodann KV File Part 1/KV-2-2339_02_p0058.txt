To Hodann, London
From Trunte Kierkegaard, Copenhagen
2-4-35

Acknowledge registered letter with 100 Kr.

1. Separation. Having plagued writer writer for months on the separation question, Hodann now refuses to sign. She is convinced this is not a new decision.

2. Money question. Agrees that emigration is not a luxury. They cannot afford two households. Advise Hodann to return to Copenhagen where he & she & Sonja can live more cheaply together. She cannot manage on the money he sends her.

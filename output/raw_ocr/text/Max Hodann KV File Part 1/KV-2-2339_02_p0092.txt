2. Dr. Eagon OBERMER was reported on by M. in August 1934. He was said to be very friendly with the intelligentsia of the Communist Party and extreme Left Wing Group; to be very well off and a brilliant man. It was suggested that he might have been connected with the C.P. in Germany.

At this time he was reported to be residing at 5, Frogna Gardens, N.W.3. On 11.8.34. he left Dover for Boulogne(?) his address on leaving being 14a, Manchester Square, W.1.

Note. Dr. Eagon OBERMER is obviously identical with the Dr. Edgar OBERMER of 12a and in view of the Lausanne connection it seems possible that the son of Seymour OBERMER may also be identical.

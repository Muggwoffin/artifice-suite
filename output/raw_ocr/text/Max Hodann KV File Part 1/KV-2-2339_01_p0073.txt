PF.43817/D.S.9.a.

Most Secret & Personal.

17th April, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

In continuation of my PF.43817/D.S.9.a. of 16.4.35. regarding Max HODANN, you may like to have the following two additional items of interest.

We have seen a letter to HODANN from one Max LICHTENSTEIN in Copenhagen in which he says "you can thank your stars and Lisa that you have been allowed to enter England. My emigration was terribly difficult."

The writer mentions one Maurice (?)RAPAPORT of 131, Regent Street, W.1. (of whom we have no record) and adds - "If you should speak to (?)RAPAPORT don't mention my political views nor my present miserable lack of money. He need not know either."

The letter continues: "That lazy fellow HEINZ should get busy about the Englishman. If he has got money he might start business in Holland and make a lot of money."

It seems possible that LICHTENSTEIN may be identical with a man of the same name about whom we wrote to you in September 1934 - your CX/6561/V. of 13.10.34. refers.

The other point of interest which I omitted from my letter of yesterday's date is the fact that during observation on HODANN he was seen to call at Flat 37, Hartington Court, Lansdowne Road, which is the address of [Alick WEST] whose name figures in your Most Secret messages and about whom you have corresponded with Miss Saunders under your CX/12650/3043/V.

Yours sincerely,

(SD) K.H.H.S.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

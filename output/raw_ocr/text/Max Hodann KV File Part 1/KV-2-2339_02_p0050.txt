From Traute NEUMANN Gennemlobet 17, Vanlose-Copenhagen.
To Max HODANN, Kynance Court, 6, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

Date of Letter 1.4.35.
Date of Postmark 2.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges letter with 100 Kr.

Refers to separation question which she states addressee now refuses to sign.

Advises HODANN to return to Copenhagen where he, she and Sonja can live more cheaply together.

References to Specht and Kuddell. The latter has asked whether HODANN got the manuscript of "Igel" which she sent at Christmas.

"K. is coming at Easter and asks for 25 Kr. of his money as he can't bring any out."

For translation see attached sheet.

Note on fps at

Norman HAIRE.

On 1.8.29. HAIRE wrote to the Home Office supporting the applications of Dr. A. GENSS and Dr. BATKIS who wished to come to the U.K. to attend a conference on Sexual Reform.

In September 1929 enquiries were made as to the occupants of 127, Harley Street as this was given as the address of Felix HALLE. Dr. HAIRE was stated to employ an Austrian Secretary and one or two German menservants.

Under the heading "Programme for Action for London" obtained from Gerald HAMILTON in May 1932 the following appeared:

"B. Tasks with reference to the W.I.R.

"If the Committee is given a certain autonomy, an endeavour is to be made to bring the well known sexual reformer Norman HAIRE and similar persons within the sphere of the W.I.R."

In June 1932 HAIRE was one of the signatories to a manifesto supporting the International Anti-War Conference being called by Romain ROLLAND and Henri BARBUSSE.

At a meeting of the Provisional Committee of the Anti-War Congress held on 13.7.32. it was agreed that STRACHEY would discuss with HAIRE the proposition of uniting the physical delegates who might attend the Anti-War Congress into an International Physicians Conference.

On 23.1.33., under the heading "Medical" was one of the signatories to an appeal to send delegates to the National Congress Against War to be held under the auspices of the British Anti-War Committee on 4/5.3.33.

In January 1933 his name appeared on a list of persons whom John STRACHEY proposed to approach for financial assistance for the British Anti-War Movement.

On 5.3.33. at the afternoon session of a meeting of the National Anti-War Congress a letter of greetings was read from Norman HAIRE.

On 4.6.33. HAIRE wrote to John STRACHEY that he doubted whether he was altogether in sympathy with the Anti-War Movement since the rise of Hitler with the attendant consequences "I find that I would support a war which had as its object the crushing of Germany."

In October 1933 the Secretary of the S.C.R. wrote to VOKS in Moscow stating that as requested a copy of Dr. E. Conus' book "Protestion of Motherhood and Childhood" had been sent to Mrs. Dora RUSSELL, Eden PAUL and Norman HAIRE.

April, 1935.

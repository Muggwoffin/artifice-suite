Note. A woman named "Saran" occupies the basement flat at 8, Blenheim Road, but no alien of that name appears to be registered at Bow Street.

The occupants of Flat 37, Hartington Court are said to be a British subject named WEST and his wife. They are said to have been there about 6 weeks and to have arrived from Vienna where he is stated to have held a good position as Excise Officer.

WEST's furniture was apparently brought to England under the supervision of the Custom and Excise. His wife is a native of Austria.

Friday, 5th April:

HODANN and NILSSON visited Chatham House at 2.30 p.m. the latter leaving at 4.15. and went to Norway House for 40 minutes and then returned to Chatham House where she and HODANN remained until 7 p.m. when they went back to Cornwall Gardens.

Saturday, 6th April:

HODANN at 12.50. posted letters, bought newspaper and returned to his lodgings. Woman out shopping. Nothing important seen.

10th April 1935.

re Max Julius HODANN and LINDBACK-NILSSON.

Result of further observation in this case.

Monday 8th April.

HODANN, carrying attache case, visited Hartington Court from 1.55 to 3.40 p.m. Then at 4.10 to Chatham House where NILSSON entered at 5.25. Both left latter address at 7 p.m. and after taking refreshments at the Criterion Brasserie visited the News Theatre, Shaftesbury Avenue, at 8.35.

Tuesday 9th April.

HODANN and NILSSON left 6 Cornwall Gardens at 1.25 p.m. and after visiting Imperial Institute, Victoria and Albert Museum and Kardomah Exhibition Cafe, entered at 3.25 the League of Nationa Union, 15 Grosvenor Crescent, where they remained until 5.30 after which they did some shopping and returned to their lodgings.

Observation now withdrawn.

J.O.

S.11.

BEACON HILL SCHOOL

BOYLE'S COURT,
SOUTH WEALD,
Nr. BRENTWOOD,
ESSEX.
April 18th 193

Dear Max Hedann,

I am sorry if I misunderstood your letter. I thought that you wrote that Frau Lindback could pay "nur" 2½ pounds a week. I have not got the letter here with me. As you will understand, it would be very difficult for me to carry on the school; if I took all children for very little. I do not mind doing it in this case, since it will not be over a long period. Please ask Frau Lindback to let us know if we are to expect Janka next term.

By address here is

Carn Veel
Porthcurno
Cornwall.

Yours sincerely,

Dr. Russell

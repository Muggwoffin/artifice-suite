CROSS-REFERENCE.

Max HODANN

Aliens Dept., H.O. forwarded copy of report from I.O., Harwich on Elise Neander LINDBAEK-NILSSON.

She arrived from Esbjerg on 17.3.35. She was accompanied by Max HODANN, who is, on his own admission, a political refugee from Germany. He stated that he was divorced from his wife in 1926. During the past year he has been assisting LINDBAEK-NILSSON in her literary work, and their passports show that they were both in Palestine in May 1934, when they received permission to remain for six months. Jointly they have written a book (copy produced) entitled "The Jews are Coming Home.", which they have had published in Norway in Norwegian.

Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON expects to obtain a divorce in May next. In the event of her obtaining a divorce she has no intention, she states, of marrying Mr. HODANN. Although Mr. HODANN collaborates with her in her work, she claims to be entirely independent of him.

Together they had nine pieces of baggage, which appeared superfluous for Mr. HODANN's stay of three weeks, and Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON's for two months. It is quite possible that Mr. HODANN intends to settle in the U.K. and that Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON has decided to follow his example.

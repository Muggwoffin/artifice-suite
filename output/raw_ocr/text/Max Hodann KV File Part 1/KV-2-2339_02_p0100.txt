At 7.25 p.m. HODANN left the latter address and visited 127, Harley Street where he remained from 8.0. to 10.10 p.m. and then went back to Kynance Court. Whilst journeying from Harley Street to Gloucester Road he wrote a post card and posted same.

Friday, 22nd March.

Car GE-312 arrived about 9 a.m. at Kynance Court where the driver, supposed ELKAN, entered. At 10.15. the latter, HODANN, NILSSON, with another man and woman, who are also residing at 6, Cornwall Gardens, left the address and drove towards High Street, Kensington. At 3.45. the two latter returned on foot, but nothing more was seen of the others although watch was kept until 11.15 p.m.

Saturday 23rd March.

At 11.15 a.m. NILSSON came from her address, did some shopping and returned. At 12.45. she and HODANN left and visited 14a Manchester Square, where the said car was seen outside. At 1.30. HODANN, NILSSON and ELKAN left, entered car and drove away. At 2.40. the car arrived at 6, Cornwall Gardens with the three named together with a woman about 27 years of age and a girl age about 5 years and luggage. Later ELKAN and the woman age 27 drove away.

Observation continuing.

The descriptions of the man and woman associates of HODANN and NILSSON and who reside at 6, Cornwall Gardens, are as follows:-

Man, age 40, height 5 ft 8"; complexion sallow; hair brown; clean shaven; snub nose; medium build; dresses well and usually carries small camera.

Woman, 30 to 35 years, pale complexion; hair dark and straight; thin face; slim build.

The two are believed to be husband and wife and of German nationality.

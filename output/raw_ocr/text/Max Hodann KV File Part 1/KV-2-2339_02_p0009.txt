180, Regent Park Rd. N.W.
April 8, 1935.

My dear Jim,

This is to introduce a socialist friend, M. Hodaun, M.D., a German refugee and writer. He wants information on the work of the Labor Corps (inert under Labour). I suppose your Research Department can give him all he wants.

I have been ill up to a last nine week and am still weak and depressed. There is something wrong either with the liver or gall, but I am slowly pulling up, though it is a hard pull and a long pull. Well, age tells. I hope, however, to live long enough to witness the end of the Hitler regime.

Ever yours,

M. Beer.

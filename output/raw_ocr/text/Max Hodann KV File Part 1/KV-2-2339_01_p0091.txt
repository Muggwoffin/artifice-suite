From Alexis S. FINKELSTEIN, 13, Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

To Dr. M. HODANN, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

Date of Letter 14.4.35.

Date of Postmark 14.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Was disappointed not to see HOFANN.

"I shall be in town Thursday afternoon. I want very much to see you and [Mrs. LINDBAEK]. Will you give me a sure appointment? My last train leaves 8.22. Liverpool Street."

For translation see attached sheet.

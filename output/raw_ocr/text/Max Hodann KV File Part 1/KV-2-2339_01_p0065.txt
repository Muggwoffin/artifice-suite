From Dora RUSSELL, Beacon Hill School, Boyles Court, South Weald, Nr. Brentwood, Essex.

To M. HODANN, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road.

Date of Letter 18.4.35.

Date of Postmark 18.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that she thought addressee said (Frau LINDBAEK) could pay £2.10.0. a week. Does not mind making a reduction as it will only be for a short period. Asks if she is to expect Janka next term at £1.10.0. a week.

For translation see attached sheet.

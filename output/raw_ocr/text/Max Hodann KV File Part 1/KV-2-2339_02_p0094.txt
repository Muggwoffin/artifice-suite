From Hugo HOGDAHL, for H. ASCHEHOUG & Co., Oslo.

To Lise LINDBAEK, Kynance Court, 6, Gloucester Road, S.W.7. (Re-directed from Norwegian Legation.)

Date of letter 25.3.35.

Summary of Contents.

Encloses some notices which addressee may not have seen.

Notices:

3. Sv. Arnvig. The real intention of the authors (one of whom is the German well known writer on sexual matters, Max HODANN - of doubtful reputation) is to show that sexual difficulties matter more than anything else in the life of the Palestine immigrants. An exaggerated attitude which deprives the book of much of its value."

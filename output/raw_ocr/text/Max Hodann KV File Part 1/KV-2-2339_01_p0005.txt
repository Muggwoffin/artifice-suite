27.3.35.

Mr. Cooper, Home Office, rang up to say that HODANN would be calling at the Home Office between 3.0. and 5.0. to-day and asked whether we would like him detained to enable us to pick him up when he left.

Mr. Cumming informed Mr. Cooper that we had already managed to pick HODANN up and were keeping a check on his movements.

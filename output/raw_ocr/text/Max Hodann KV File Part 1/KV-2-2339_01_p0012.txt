From K. Ceul ROUNG ? KEUNZ? Kusnetzki Most 9 Quartier 9 Moscow.

To Dr. D. FINKELSTEIN, 14, Shelford Park, Great Shelford.

Date of Letter 22.4.35.

Date of Postmark 25.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that if Karin is a member of the Party she must get permission from the latter before she goes to Moscow.

She will have to work as a Russian and will not be given the privileges accorded to foreign specialists. They will do what they can to assist her.

Asks if addressee has the address of Professor NATHANSON.

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

29.4.31. S.I.S. reported that Max HODANN in 1928 arranged a meeting in Berlin between the chief of the Russian Espionage service and Dr. GUMBEL of Heidelberg, who was then offered the post of Russian agent in Germany and the neighbouring States which he accepted. (Summary of information re HODANN mentions that he was present on 4.11.28 at a Reichs Foundation Congress of the F.O.S.U. in Berlin.)

6.6.32. Press cutting from the "Daily Worker" mentions the fact that Dr. Marx HODANN is one of the members of a preparatory committee formed in Germany to assist in the organisation of an International Congress against War to take place in Geneva in July.

(The same information is contained in intercepted correspondence from Berlin W.I.R. H.Q., addressed to Isabel BROWN.) dated 8.6.32.

16.8.33. At a meeting of the German Relief Committee held by BRIDGEMAN, Isabel BROWN, Ellen WILKINSON, etc., the question of the Doctors' Committee was brought forward, as Dr. Louis, who was in charge of this was getting up a strong protest campaign against the punishment of solitary confinement imposed by the Nazis. The name of one of two eminent doctors suggested as being included in this campaign was apparently that of HODANN.

4.2.35. S.I.S. reported that Dr. Max HODANN was one of a group of German immigrants in Palestine, known to have belonged to 'some radical party or other' before they left Germany. The following information about HODANN is appended:
"Was a member of the Social Democratic Party of Berlin, and played a leading part in politics. Has written a number of books, including one on travel in Soviet Russia. Following his travels in Russia, he took an active part in Communist organisations such as the Red Aid Association, the League against Imperialism, etc. Has recently published articles on travel in Syria and Egypt in the German immigrant press."

With regard to FINKELSTEIN, the writer of one of the letters forwarded to you on 11.4.35., we have ascertained that this man is a German Chemist, born 25.1.77. at Leipzig. He arrived in this country on 21.8.58. We are hoping to obtain further information regarding him and have taken out a warrant on his correspondence.

With regard to HARYOLL or HARE, also referred to in our letter of 11.4.35., we have received a further letter from this individual in which he states that he has asked Karen to come to Oslo which he hopes will be a refuge for her. He asks HODANN to keep him informed if he hears anything.

Particularly interesting I think may be his connection with a Professor Arne ORDING who arrived in this country on 7.3.35. to study for three months. You will remember that Miss ORDING was the name of the governess to whom Mrs. HARTIN's son George was handed over.

I wonder if you have come across this Professor ORDING in the course of any investigations, or whether they have any further details of Miss ORDING's family which would help us to connect the two.

Yours sincerely,

G. H. L.

20th March, 1935.

Dear Mr. Cooper,

With reference to your enquiry yesterday as to whether we had any record of Max HODANN, I write to confirm what I told you verbally, that this individual has been known to us as a prominent member of the International Committee of the Friends of the Soviet Union in Berlin since 1928. In December 1928 he actually became International President of the Friends of the Soviet Union and was present at the formation of the Belgian Section.

In 1932 he was active in preparing for an anti-War Congress.

In 1933 it was suggested that he should form one of the Doctors' Committee of the German Relief Committee.

More recently, in a review of the Communist situation in Palestine up to the end of 1934, his name occurs in a list of persons engaged in political activity there.

The following note of his activities appears in the summary referred to above:-

"Was a member of the Social Democratic Party of Berlin and played a leading part in politics. Has written a number of books, including one on travel in Soviet Russia. Following his travels in Russia, he took an active part in Communist organisations, such as the Red Aid Association, the League against Imperialism, etc. He recently published articles on travel in Syria and Egypt in the German immigrant press."

For reasons which I explained to you verbally we are very interested in this man and I should be most grateful if you would let us have the names of any persons who have interested themselves in his case and any further information which you may be able to obtain about him.

We should also be most grateful if you would kindly let us see the Immigration Officer's reports on both Max HODANN and Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON.

Yours sincerely,

(K.W.H.S.)

Mr. Cooper Esq., O.B.E., Home Office, S.W.1.

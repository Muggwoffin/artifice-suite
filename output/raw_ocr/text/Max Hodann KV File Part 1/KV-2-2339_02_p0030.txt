3.4.35.

Dear Dr. Hodann,

Thanks for your postcard. I have been terribly busy, not only with professional work, but with some literary work that I am trying to get finished, and with meetings about an appeal for German refugees, which is taking up far too much of my time. This accounts for my not having yet made an appointment for you to come and see me. But I should be pleased if you and your friend would come to tea about four o'clock next Sunday afternoon, if you are not otherwise engaged. Will you let me know if this time suits you?

Yours sincerely,

Yehuda Halevi

Dr. Hodann.

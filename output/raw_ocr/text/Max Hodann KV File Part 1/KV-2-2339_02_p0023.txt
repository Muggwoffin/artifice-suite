From M. (in German)

To Dr. M. HODANN, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

Date of Letter 3.4.35.

Date of Postmark -

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Writer has to help at the Veg. Rest (aurant?) unexpectedly the next day between 12.0. and 2.0. Asks addressee to come there at 2.20. "we could have peace and quiet to eat and to speak to Renate. In any case ring me up to-morrow at 9.15. so that we can appoint another time. Renate arrives to-morrow at 10.13."

For translation see attached sheet.

COMMENT

(a) No action required. P.A.

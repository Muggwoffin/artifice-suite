From Jacques GOLDSTEIN, President of Association "Prokor", Brussels.

To Dr. Max HODANN, c/o Skuse, 63, Haverstock Hill, N.W.3.

Date of Letter 19.4.35.

Date of Postmark 19.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Refers to letter send to addressee by SONNTAG and the correspondence exchanged between KINN and himself, and invites HODANN on behalf of the "Prokor" organisation to a conference to be held in Brussels and Antwerp.

Suggests that addressee's lectures should be given in German and the fee 1000 frs. for the two.

States that the "Prokor" is a Belgian organisation equivalent to the French "Geserd" and closely allied to the 'Ozet' of Moscow.

The principal aims of "Prokor" are as follows:

1. To popularise the Soviets and the results of Jewish colonisation in U.S.S.R.

2. To deter the return of the Jews to Palestine which is considered as Imperialist views.

3. To improve the moral conditions of the Jews in the manner of the U.S.S.R.

For translation see attached sheet.

Int. post-card dated 3.4.35.

Dear M.

I have to help at the Veg. Rest (aurant?) unexpectedly tomorrow between 12 and 2. Could you perhaps come there at 2.20, we would have peace and quiet to eat and to speak to Renate. In any case, ring me up tomorrow morning at 9.15, so that we can appoint another time. Renate arrives tomorrow at 10.13 - Greetings M.

+ Corner of Panton and Whitcomb street, near Haymarket.

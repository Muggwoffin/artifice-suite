SECRET.

PB.43817/D.S.9.a.  11th April, 1935.

To THE POSTMASTER-GENERAL, and all others whom it may concern,

I hereby authorize and require you to detain, open and produce for my inspection all postal packets and telegrams addressed to:—

Alexis S. FINKELSTEIN,
13, Shelford Park,
Great Shelford,
Cambridgeshire.

or to any name at that or any other address if there is reasonable ground to believe that they are intended for the said Alexis S. FINKELSTEIN.

and for so doing this shall be your sufficient Warrant.

One of His Majesty's
Principal Secretaries of State

The H.O.W. imposed on Max HODANN and Mrs. LINDBAEK-NILSSON - suspected of associating with Soviet military espionage activities - has produced suspicious correspondence emanating from FINKELSTEIN. It is desired to ascertain further information with regard to his activities.

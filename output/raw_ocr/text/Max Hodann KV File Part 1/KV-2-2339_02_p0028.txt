From Norman HAIRE, 127, Harley Street, W.l.

To Max HODANN, Kynance Court, S.W.7.

Date of Letter 3.4.35.

Date of Postmark 4.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Acknowledges card.

States that he has been busy with professional and literary work and meetings about an appeal for German refugees.

"I should be pleased if you and your friend would come to tea about four o'clock next Sunday afternoon........."

For translation see attached sheet.

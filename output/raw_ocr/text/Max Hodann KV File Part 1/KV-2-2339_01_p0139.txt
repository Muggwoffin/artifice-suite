DECARDED
From Alexis S. FINKELSTEIN, 13, Shelford Park, Great Shelford.
To Dr. M. HODANN, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.
Date of Letter 9.4.35.
Date of Postmark 9.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

Has written to Oslo, Paris and Moscow.

"We are the sons of defeat,
Children of the sinking sun,
We are as sparks in the anvil,
Like the spray in foaming waterfalls,
We wander over and around the earth,
Harried, scattered and few."

That is the fate of my children -
Are you coming to Cambridge next Sunday or can I see you again next week.

For translation see attached sheet.

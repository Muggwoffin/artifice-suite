2nd April, 1935.

re Max Julius HODANN and [LINDBAEK-NILSSON]

Further to my report of 23rd ult. the following is result of continued observation:-

Monday 25th March.

At 10.25 a.m. HODANN and NILSSON left 6, Cornwall Gardens (the former carrying despatch case) and visited Norway House, 24, Cockspur Street where they are believed to have entered Den. Norski Klub Ltd., they remained at this address for 30 minutes and on leaving NILSSON was seen to hand HODANN several re-addressed letters. Both then went to Friends Meeting House, Euston Road for an hour and left with the other man who is also residing at 6, Cornwall Gardens to which address the three returned together at 1.40 p.m.

During the afternoon NILSSON did some shopping.

Tuesday 26th March.

Twice during morning HODANN left his lodgings, posted letters and returned. NILSSON left at 10.50 a.m., returned at 1.35 p.m. carrying several parcels.

1.45. HODANN, NILSSON and child left Cornwall Gardens, lunched at Lyons Café, Gloucester Road and returned.

At 2.50. the three latter visited Kensington Gardens and returned home at 3.45. They did not again leave up to 8.15. when observation ceased.

Wednesday 27th March.

1.30 p.m. HODANN left his lodgings, went to Vauxhall Bridge Road, boarded a Camberwell Tram but was shortly afterwards missed and he did not return home till 5.45. Nothing more was seen of him up to 9 p.m. when observation ceased.

HODANN, Max Julius Alexander

31.8.28.
Scotland House, writing to S.I.S. on the subject of Leopold MARESCH and the "International Committee of the Friends of Soviet Russia", says that their letters are signed "fraternally Dr. Max HODANN" on behalf of L. MARESCH, but that elsewhere, Dr. Max HODANN is called Hon. Sec. or signs "for the Committee".

11.9.28.
S.I.S. say that Dr Max HODANN appears to be identical with the Berlin city doctor, whose books, "Geschlecht und Liebe" and "Bringt uns Wirklich der Klapperstorch?" were banned by the Public Prosecutor at Rudolstadt as being against public morals. HODANN won an appeal.

2.10.28.
S.I.S. reported HODANN to be a Berlin city councillor (Stadtrat).

1.10.28.
In letter to I.P.I. Dr. Max HODANN is described as Secretary of the International Committee of the Friends of Soviet Russia, Friedrichstrasse 24, Berlin, and "a medical man whose works have incurred censorship on sexual grounds."

3.1.28.
The Reichskongress of the Society of Friends of Russia took place on 4.11.28 in the rooms of the former Prussian Upper House, Berlin. Dr. HODANN spoke of the difficulties which Russia had to overcome after the Revolution and said that it was the duty of all workers to defend the Soviet Union, now the home of Socialism.

8.1.29.
Sent a report on the Congress of the Friends of Soviet Russia held in Brussels on 2.12.28 at the Café Cornet, 16, rue du Marché aux Fromages, to discuss forming a Belgian section. The meeting was sponsored, among others, by Dr. HODANN, Secretary of the German Section and International President of the Society. Report of the proceedings appeared in the "Drapeau Rouge" of 4.12.28, in which articles were written by HODANN, etc. in issues of 5 & 6.12.28. in SZ/98/3.

From Bertold HAWOLFF c/o Carl Pande, Tostrupsgate 22, Oslo.

To Max HODANN, Kynance Court, Gloucester Road, S.W.7.

Date of Letter 12.4.35.

Date of Postmark 13.4.35.

SUMMARY OF CONTENTS

States that he has written to Karen asking her to come to Oslo which he hopes will be a refuge for her.

Asks HODANN to keep him informed if he hears anything.

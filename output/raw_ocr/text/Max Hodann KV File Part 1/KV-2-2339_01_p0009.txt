S.9.a.
23.4.35.

The H.O.W. on Max HODANN and Lisa LINDBAEK has produced an interesting letter from one Molla ORDING in Oslo.

We know that the governess who looked after Mrs. MARTIN's son was a Miss ORDING and the intercept at 52a shows pretty clearly that this was Molla ORDING and also that she knows more about the Martin case than was at first thought.

Reference is made in the intercept to one Arne who is Professor Arne ORDING of 1, Britannia Street, W.C.1. who is also in correspondence with HODANN.

If you agree I think a H.O.W. on this man's correspondence is justified. H.O.W. submitted herewith for signature if approved.

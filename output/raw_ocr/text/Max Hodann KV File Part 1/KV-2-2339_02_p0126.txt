Telephone message from Chief Immigration Officer, Harwich, 3.35. 8.0 p.m.

DANN, Max Julius Alexander.

Born. 30.8.94. at Neisse.

Description. Build medium; height 5ft 6"; hair fair, almost bald; eyes brown; clean shaven.

Special marks. Four moles on left cheek, two of which are small and two fairly large, one of them close to the corner of the mouth.

Press. Grey-brown overcoat - large herring-bone pattern; half belt at back, two buttons on back; grey-brown trilby with brown band; grey-blue suit; brown shoes; light fawn socks; blue-grey tie with grey or black stripes; faint blue shirt.

Address. [c/o Max BEER, J] 180, Regents Park Road, London.

Will stay there only one night; will seek Hotel accommodation to-morrow, do not know what hotel.

Luggage. 1 Typewriter in black case.
1 Brown Despatch case.
1 Large expanding suit case made of brown fibre.
1 Brown leather suit case.
1 Small expanding leather case with two straps.
1 Small cloth case with four straps.

Trains. Left Parkestone Station on 7.45 p.m. train, changing at Mannington at 8.30 p.m. leaving Mannington Station at 10.10 p.m.

Note. The above telephone message was telephoned to Miss Sissmore as instructed, by the Nightwatchman.

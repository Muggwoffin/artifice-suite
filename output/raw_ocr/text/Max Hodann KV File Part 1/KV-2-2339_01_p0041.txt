General Post Office,
London. E.C. 1.

April 22nd. 1935.

Secret.

Miss Sissamore,

Re your check on HODANN etc.

A redirection notice has been received from these people, giving the new address as

c/o Skuse,
63, Haverstock Hill, N.W. 3.

The check on the South Kensington address has therefore been cancelled, and a check on the Hampstead address instituted.

C. J. Powell

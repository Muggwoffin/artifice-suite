21.
Mr. Boxall, G.P.O., rang up to say that letters for HODANN were being subjected to a double delay owing to the fact that they were being re-directed from 24, Cockspur Street - an address added to the check on 27.3.35.

Spoke Mr. Ottaway and explained this to him. He said that HODANN had been seen coming from 24, Cockspur Street with letters on one occasion but he had not been observed to call there regularly. He agreed that as the letters were being re-directed to Kynance Court it would be safe to cancel the 24, Cockspur Street address.

Spoke Mr. Cumming who will advise G.P.O. accordingly.

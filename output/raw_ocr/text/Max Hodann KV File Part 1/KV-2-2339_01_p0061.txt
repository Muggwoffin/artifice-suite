Copy Original in PF. 43824 LINDBAEK-NILSSON

PF. 43824/D.S. 9.a.

23rd April, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

With reference to my telephone conversation with Mr. Mills on Thursday and in continuation of my PF. 43817/D.S. 9.a. of 16.4.35., I now enclose a translation of a letter in Norwegian from Molla ORDING to Lisa LINDBAEK.

I think from the contents that we can be satisfied that Molla ORDING is actually the governess who had charge of George MARTIN.

This letter, on top of what we already know of Max HODANN and Lisa LINDBAEK, makes one think that both these individuals probably know a great deal more about the Martin case than we at first suspected.

"Traute" is presumably Traute NEUMANN who is referred to in my letter of 16.4.35.

"George" must be George MARTIN and "STANG" - Emil STANG, Mrs. MARTIN's lawyer.

"Arne" is Professor Arne ORDING, now living at 1, Britannia Street, W.C.1.

According to the Traffic Index Lisbeth BROCH has not yet arrived in this country but I am taking steps to ensure that we shall be informed if and when she arrives.

With regard to GJESDAHL who is mentioned in the enclosed translation, we have a record of one Nils Severin GJESDAL, a Norwegian ship's fireman, who was deported from this country in January 1931 after a conviction of failing to register with the police. I am obtaining the Home Office papers for this man with a view to ascertaining further particulars.

Yours sincerely,

(©) K.H. H.S.

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E.,
S.I.S.

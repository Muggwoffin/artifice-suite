PF.43817/D.S.9.a.

11th April, 1935.

Dear Major Vivian,

With reference to my telephone conversation yesterday afternoon with Mr. Mills, I enclose literal translations of the two letters to HODANN which we have recently intercepted.

I was wondering whether in the letter from Oslo there would be sufficient circumstantial evidence for the Norwegian Police to identify Karen. In FINKELSTEIN's letter you will notice the curious last sentence referring to Uncle Martin Hansen.

I think I previously told you on the telephone that Max HODANN and Lise LINDBAEK arrived together at Harwich on 17.3.35. The latter gave her full name as Elise Neander LINDBANK-NILSON. They said they had been collaborating on a book and they were now to collaborate in writing the biography of Dr. Nansen.

Lise LINDBAEK was landed owing to the intervention of the Norwegian Minister but HODANN was at first refused leave to land on the technical grounds of lack of means. He was, however, eventually landed, since when we have had both these individuals under observation. The enclosed two letters are the first items of interest which have transpired.

I will let you have further particulars regarding HODANN and LINDBAEK but am sending this over at once as I understand your Norwegian Bag goes to-day.

Yours sincerely,

Major V. Vivian, C.B.E., S.I.S.

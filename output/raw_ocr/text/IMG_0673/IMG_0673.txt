I was having tea with one Toronto (Correspondent of the New York Times - but not an American) when someone who had just been elected at the Moscow Soviet came in - you may know him - George Andraichin. Telephone rings - ever - wants to answer - comes back in bursts, withdraws to answer - comes back in great excitement and demands to know how G.A. (who works there) why the Prov'baule was closed today, and a sentry posted at the door to keep people away. Hoot it failed, or he? Oh! calmly replied S.A., we shut down because all the employes marched down with their duty notes & the Moscow Soviet! Isn't it nice. In what other country could this sort of thing happen but in Russia?

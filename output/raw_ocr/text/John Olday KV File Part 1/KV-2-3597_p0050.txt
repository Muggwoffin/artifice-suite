Captain L. E. Rutherford,
J. A. G. Branch,
Headquarters,
Western Command, Chester.

SECRET AND PERSONAL.
Your Ref:- I.S.W.C.702

Dear Rutherford,

Re:- 13095115 - Private Oldag. A.W.

With further reference to your letter of the 26th November last under reference No. quoted above, asking for information in connection with the above-mentioned soldier, I have to report that his behaviour since he has been at this Centre has been beyond reproach.

He is industrious and quiet, does his work well, and his Company Commander, who has been keeping an eye on him has no complaint whatever to make concerning him.

Furthermore, Private Oldag is not popular with a few of the remainder of the foreign element here, as apparently they got to know that at some time or other he has been helping people to escape from the Nazi rule in Germany and get to this country, which seems to point to the fact that he is certainly Anti Nazi, and if anything Pro British. However, we have nothing but good to say about him here, so presumably his case can now be written off.

Yours sincerely,

[signature]

Pheasey Farm Estate,
Great Barr,
Birmingham.
23rd December 1941.
HG.

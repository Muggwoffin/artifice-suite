H.O. File No. M. 11683 - MEISEL, Hilde.
Minutes.
This alien was landed on 8.12.37 on the N/E condn. It is for consideration whether this should be varied or a circular issued to I.O.'s asking them should they allow Meisel to land when she next returns from abroad, to place her on a short time condn:
15.1.38. W.N.P.
Study was apparently not her prime consideration in applying for permission to land on 8.12.37, although she uses the British Museum and London School of Economics libraries. She is an associate of extremists and the view that she is acting as an intermediary between members of the M.S.I. and extremist organisations abroad is probably correct. It may be left to S.B. to keep an eye on her.
22.1.38 J.B.
In spite of her associations she may be acting as a Gestapo agent but in any case her activities are worth watching.
As proposed.
22.1.38 E.N.C.
Circular is being issued. Name entered in Suspect Index.
26.1.38 J.B.N.
In view of alien's known activities it would seem that she might have been invited to take her departure when we had the opportunity. It would prove awkward however at this stage, unless a further Police enquiry reveals any fresh developments.
To Comm. of Police for further enquiry and report on alien's activities. The proposed date of her departure from U.K. should be ascertained if possible.
22.10.38 J.W.
The police report that she married a British subject A.W. Oldag, on 13.9.38. There is no proof that this marriage is one of convenience but little doubt that she is still in sympathy with the M.S.I. and is still working for them. A British passport was issued to her 6 days after the marriage for the purpose of visiting the Continent, and in the circumstances it seems we must regard her as a British subject.
28.12.38 J.W.
File returned "Seen, thank you" on 3.1.39.

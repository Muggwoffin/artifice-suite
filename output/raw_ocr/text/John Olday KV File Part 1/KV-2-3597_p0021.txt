You will find attached a book of cartoons published by the Freedom Press which was purchased on the 22nd April at Smiths Book Stall at Cambridge Station by the Rev. J.B. JOYCE, the Vicar of Duxford, Cambridgeshire. The Rev. JOYCE sent this publication to the Chief Constable of Cambridge Borough who has forwarded it to us asking our views upon it. Although I have no doubt that you have seen it before, I should be grateful for some information. The only reference which we have to the Freedom Press is that contained in Special French Security Work Summary issue No. 74 paragraph 5 (a).

Yours sincerely,

(Captain N.G.K. Burgess.)
Major C.M. Hughes.

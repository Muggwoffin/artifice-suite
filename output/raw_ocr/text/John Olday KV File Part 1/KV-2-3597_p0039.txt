Extract from R.M. A.G.14.A.5416 re DUAL NATIONALS AT NO. 12 P.C.

Memorandum from O.C. No. 12 Pioneer Corps Training Centre to A.G.14.A. dated 19.3.42.

Subject: Disposal of Dual nationals: Re:- 13095115 Pte. Oldag W.

It will be remembered that Lieut. Colonel C.C. Adams M.C. interviewed at this Centre some three months ago, the above-mentioned men of Dual Nationality, concerning the advisability or otherwise of their serving in H.M. Forces. He was accompanied by a civilian representative of the War Office in London.

As a result of this interview, and of further correspondence and telephonic conversations, it was decided to send these men, who had originally been in the Pioneer Corps and then transferred to the Buffs Regiment, out from here with drafts of normal Pioneer Corps personnel.

On being informed of this decision, by their Company Commander, they preferred a request to have an interview with me, which I granted this morning. They all stated to me that they did not wish to undertake any further service with H.M. Forces, as their parents were living either in Germany or Italy, and in most cases they had brothers or relatives serving in the German or Italian Forces, and they did not wish to be put in the position of possibly having to fight against them.

I have informed these men that if they persisted in their attitude the result would probably be that they would find themselves in an Internment Camp, but although not in any way insubordinate they still stated that they did not wish to undertake further service with H.M. Forces. From what I know of them, however, and of the views they expressed to me, I am of the opinion that they would not be unwilling to serve in any civilian capacity such as the A.P.S., A.R.P. or other work of a like nature.

My considered opinion however with regard to these men and their circumstances is that they should be discharged from H.M. Forces and sent to an Internment Camp.

In the meantime these men are being posted out to Normal Pioneer Corps Companies.

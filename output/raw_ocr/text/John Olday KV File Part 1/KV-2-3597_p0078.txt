Special Branch,

LONDON, S.W.1.

5th October 1939.

320/EPA/517.

M.I.5.

With reference to your L.183(608) DS/DyD regarding Arthur William OLDAG with which you forwarded copy report from Security Officer London Area and asked what action was subsequently taken:

I would refer you to Special Branch reports dated the 16th and 18th September 1939. OLDAG attended Cannon Row Police Station on the 25th September 1939, was released from his recognizances and allowed to go.

CHIEF CONSTABLE.

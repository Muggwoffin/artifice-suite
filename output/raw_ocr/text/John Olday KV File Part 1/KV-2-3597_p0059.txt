G.2/D. (C.& D.)

D. of O. wishes to place the onus of final decision regarding the discharge of these men on our shoulders.

It seems clear that the only cases in which we can recommend discharge are those where we are able to add, or confirm, the adverse information submitted by the C.O. These cases are as follows: MUTZKE, STEIN-BICHLER and ARMANI.

As for the remainder, without further information, the question of their discharge must rest with D. of O. It seems quite clear that on the information given the following should be retained in the Service: CURA, DITTRICH, RETTNER, LANZER and OLDAG.

As we have no trace of STRAUSS we are unable to make any recommendations. If what the C.O. says can be substantiated, he would appear to be unsuitable for retention. This applies equally to the cases of QUASNITSCHKA and WINTER.

When a decision has been reached that any of these men should be discharged, the procedure which should be followed is clearly stated in paragraph 5 of minute 5.

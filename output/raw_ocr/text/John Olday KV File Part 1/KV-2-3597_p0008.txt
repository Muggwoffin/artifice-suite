MINUTE SHEET.

Copy of Minute 75 in PF. 45000

F.3

Please see 70a (52a in this file) and Regional Control's letter at 71a (53a). I am now proposing to re-open this case and have drawn OLDAG's Army papers to ascertain his present location.

I showed the booklet, "The March of Death" to Colonel Alexander, and he is of the opinion that as our Allies are frequently caricatures, the book is of a thoroughly virulent nature. He would also direct your attention to the cartoon entitled "Liberty: Will it strangle me", which appears to be of a very blasphemous and offensive nature. It is remarkable that a book of this nature should be sold by Messrs. W.H. SMITH & Son (see 52a), and it seems probable that they did not examine it very closely.

Perhaps you would like to reply to Major Hordern's letter at 70a(52a) rather more fully than I have done. I have simply given him an outline of the case from the Internal Security angle.

F.1.c. 14.8.43. (Sgd.) G.A. Minto,

To R.O. Leeds re the FREEDOM PRESS and OLDAG.

Extract from Army Papers re A.W. OLDAG.

To Pioneer Corps Records re OLDAG.

M/S through F.3.b.2.

As arranged with Himsworth, I am passing this file to you through F.3.b.2. I was not aware that OLDAG is a deserter from the Pioneer Corps, and have written to Records with a view to locating him. I should be interested in any further information that you may be able to supply.

F.3.m. 1.4.44.

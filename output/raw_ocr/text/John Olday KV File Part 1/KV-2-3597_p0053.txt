The War Office,
CHELTENHAM.

18th December, 1941.

Dear Binney,

I am enclosing a copy of the minute which I am sending in regarding the men whom we interviewed on 16th December. You will notice that 11A is stated to give summaries of the previous reports, but I am not bothering with these as you already have them. I am attaching only copies of our recommendations.

It was very kind of you to come with me to Great Barr, and I can assure you that we are most grateful for your help.

Yours sincerely,

C.C. Arthur

Bimney, Esq.,
The War Office (M.I.5.),
London,
S.W.1.

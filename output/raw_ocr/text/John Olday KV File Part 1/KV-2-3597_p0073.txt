ARRAM FAIRFIELD & Co.

11, Queen Street,
Bank,
London, E.C.4.

1st July, 1940.

The Secretary,
Special Branch,
New Scotland Yard,
S.W.1.

Dear Sir,

Information has reached us that some interesting information may be obtained by your Department if a representative of yours saw Mr. John Olday of 54, Lillington Street, S.W.

Our information is that he was connected with an organisation called the International Friendship League and that he holds a British passport issued in Hamburg, but that he himself does not speak ordinary English. We gather that he lives together with some Irish boys and teaches them German folk songs and goes camping in the neighbourhood of Aldershot.

Yours faithfully,

(sgd)
Arram Fairfield & Co.

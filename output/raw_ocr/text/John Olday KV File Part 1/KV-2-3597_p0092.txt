Special Branch,
23rd November, 1938.

With further reference to Hilde MEISEL, formerly of Hungarian nationality, subject of Home Office circular S.C.10176/M.11883/H.O. of 27.1.38:

It has now been ascertained that this woman was married to Arthur William OLDAG, age 33, a British subject by birth, on 13th September, 1938, at Westminster Register Office.

She now resides with her husband at 57, Greek Street, W.1, where they occupy rooms on the top floor.

The husband is described as an author, and I am informed that he writes short stories for Messrs. Hutchinson & Co. (Publishers) Ltd., of 34, Paternoster Row, E.C. His wife is said to be engaged in commercial sketching at their home address.

So far as this woman's political activities are concerned, I have been unable to obtain any information which indicates that she has any connection with the International Publishing Co., of 7, Dean House, 116, Great Titchfield Street, W.1, which firm made application for the grant of a Ministry of Labour Permit to employ her as a translator, in July, 1938. In view of the fact, however, that she remains friendly with Mrs. FLANIER (known as SARAN), there is little doubt that she is still in sympathy with the objects of the Militant Socialist International.

Hilda (not Hilde) OLDAG was issued with British passport No.157926 on 19th September, 1938 (6 days after her marriage), it being stated on the application form that this was required for a holiday visit to the Continent. Arthur William OLDAG holds British passport No.0.34071 issued at Hamburg on 12.4.37. Mrs. OLDAG's description is as follows: born 31.7.14; height 5'4"; eyes brown, hair brown, sometimes wears horn-rimmed glasses, large face, fresh complexion, prominent nose, well built.

I respectfully suggest that this woman be included in S.B. suspect book, as it is not improbable that she made the marriage for the purpose of obtaining a British passport.

F. Cox
Sergeant.

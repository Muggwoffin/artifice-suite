MOST SECRET

P.F.45000/IS/F.1c.  14th August, 1943.

Dear Major,

Regional Control has passed your SF.19/3/1 of 7th August, 1943, relating to the book of cartoons, entitled "The March of Death" to me, as the author is a serving soldier. He is No.13095115 Pte. A. W. OLDAG of the Pioneer Corps, who has been known to us for a very considerable time. In November, 1941 we learned that he had been an active member of the Young Communist League in Germany, and that he was serving in the Army. He was a British-born subject of German-Hungarian extraction, and he held violently anti-Nazi views. He was placed under observation in his unit, but as satisfactory reports were received on his conduct, his case was finally closed on 10th January, 1942.

In view, however, of his present activities we are re-opening the case and will obtain further reports from his Commanding Officer.

The Freedom Press publishes a large quantity of anarchist literature, and we have considerable records of its activities in this office. It is, however, dealt with by F.3. of this office, and no doubt they will write to you more fully on this subject than I am competent to do. I have passed the file to them to-day.

Yours sincerely,

Captain G. A. Minto.

Major C. W. Hordern,
Box No. 161,
Leeds.

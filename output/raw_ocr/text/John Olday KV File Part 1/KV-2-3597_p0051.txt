The War Office,
Cheltenham, Glos.
23 December, 1941.

SECRET.

Dear Binney,

Many thanks for your S.F.96/Brit/9(5)/E.3., dated 20th December, 1941.

I am interested to hear what you have to say, particularly regarding Oldag. He is an interesting character and if it is not too much trouble I should like to see a summary of the information about him, which is in your possession.

Best wishes for Christmas and the New Year.

Yours sincerely,

C. E. Atand.

C. Binney, Esq.,
Room 055,
The War Office,
Whitehall,
London, S.W.1.

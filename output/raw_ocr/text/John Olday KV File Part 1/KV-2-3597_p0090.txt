From S.B. Report re Hilda OLDAG nee MEISEL.

Hilda OLDAG, nee MEISEL of 1, Calthorpe Street, W.C.1, arrived at this port from Dieppe at 4.a.m. today, travelling on the return half of week-end non-passport ticket from London to Paris. She proved to be identical with Hilde MEISEL, subject of Home Office suspect book No. SC. 10176, in which she is described as an associate of extremists, particularly with the Militant Socialist International.

OLDAG produced a marriage certificate showing that she married Arthur William OLDAG, aged 33, an author, on 13.9.38 at Westminster Register Office. On the certificate she is shown to have been born in Vienna on 31.7.14......She had no proof of her husband's nationality but claimed that she was British, and that, by virtue of his nationality, she had been issued with a British passport in September 1938, but had failed to carry it with her on this occasion. Her husband is now resident in Paris.

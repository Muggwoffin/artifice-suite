To F362.
From: F.3.b.2.
To: F.2.a. Mr. Clark.

I see from the attached note from M/S that F.2.a. are dealing with John OLDAG. I am interested to see this second publication of his which has been printed by the Freedom Press. You may like to have it for his file - on the other hand it might be rather convenient if it could be kept in the Library as it will presumably be distributed by the Freedom Press anarchists and I may have enquiries about it.

You may have seen from OLDAG's file that I had an enquiry from Cambridge. I gave them a non-committal reply as it had not been decided who was going to deal with him, and I felt that at that stage it would be better not to pass on the information obtained from M/S as it did not appear to be my case.

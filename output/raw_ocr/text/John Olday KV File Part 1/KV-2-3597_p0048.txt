Shortly before this there was a suggestion that OLDAG might be used by the British Authorities, but he showed a lack of discretion, and eventually he was not employed. He returned to the Continent where he states that he engaged in anti-Nazi activities, but that his associates would not give him the necessary assistance to carry out the acts of violence he contemplated. If this is true it may be that they gave him some encouragement but did not take him seriously.

On 16th September 1939 he returned to England and put forward a scheme for acts of sabotage against the Nazis by sending trunks to the Nazi Leaders which would explode when opened. He felt rather hurt that Scotland Yard did not show any enthusiasm for this project.

OLDAG has not in this country associated with Communists, and it may be that he has adopted the opinions of Hilde MEISEL.

It is difficult to know exactly what to make of this young man. It was at one time suspected that he might have been sent here as a Nazi agent. Though this is not impossible, there is absolutely no evidence to support it and it appears to me highly improbable, especially in view of the fact that persons such as Hilde MEISEL accepted him as genuine. Equally, there is no reason to suppose that he is a Communist agent. He has never expressed anything but anti-Nazi Left Wing views, but it is difficult to know how seriously his anti-Nazi activities are to be taken. On the whole I think the most likely explanation is that he is a somewhat vain imaginative person whose great desire is to dramatize himself and appear more romantic than he actually is. It may be thought that he is rather too old for this kind of thing, and indeed the fact that he is not like a man of 37 in appearance or behaviour is the only reason for suspecting his statement; that he was born in London in 1905 is untrue. But it seems more probable that he is a man who is rather juvenile for his age, than that he has assumed the character of some person whose birth certificate had come into his possession.

7.1.42

C. Binney.

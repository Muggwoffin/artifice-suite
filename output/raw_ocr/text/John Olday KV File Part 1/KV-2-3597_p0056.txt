OLDAG is a man of superior intellect and education, who quite obviously should be doing something other than serving in the Pioneer Corps. He stated that he had taken no part in politics in England and there was no suggestion that he had adopted a subversive attitude at the depot. I think there is no doubt that he is genuinely anti-Nazi.

QUASHNITSCHKA is only nominally an Englishman, but he is not much more of a German. He was born in London in 1912, as his father had a tailor's shop here which had prospered. The father was interned in the last war and repatriated against his wish in 1919, and was never allowed to return to this country. His brother came here before him and established a tailor's shop; he himself came in 1936 and established a shoe shop. He gave evasive answers when asked why he came, saying that it was to visit his brother and to see the world. It became clear, however, that the position was that, his father having prospered here and been unable to return and his brother having succeeded in coming here and doubtless prospering, he himself came here as it was a better opening than remaining where he was. He is married to a Scotchwoman and his sister is also married to a man in this country. These are his only ties with this country. He speaks English adequately, but is very foreign in appearance. He remarked that wars are not in his line and this is no doubt true; he is a central European business man. He calls himself a Sudeten German; from his name, one would suppose he is originally of Czech origin and from his appearance that he is partly Jewish. Nevertheless, since his father, who originated in Czechoslovakia, chose after the war to become stateless rather than become a Czech national and went to reside in Vienna, it is clear that the father's sympathies were with Germany, or at least with German Austria. This young man lived in Vienna till he came to this country, and therefore never had to serve in the German Army; he has never actually lived under German rule. It would seem that his sympathies in the present war are really with Germany, but like many persons from Vienna, his sympathy, like his race, is something of a mixture.

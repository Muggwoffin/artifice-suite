With further reference to the case of Arthur William (John OLDAG), Hilda OLDAG and Helmuth von RAUCHENPLAT, the following information has been received from M/H:

(1) With reference to Christine SHADDICK, previously reported, further observation in connection with this woman seems to show quite definitely that she is a well-educated person politically and that she has definite contacts with the Communist Party.

(2) According to an informant (of M/H's) who would seem to have no reason to mislead, von RAUCHENPLAT and Hilda OLDAG were living together until about a year ago. Von RAUCHENPLAT is also stated to have worked for some time in connection with some secret B.B.C. radio station at Oxford. He is stated to have left there to do women's work in connection with the special United States broadcasting station in this country.

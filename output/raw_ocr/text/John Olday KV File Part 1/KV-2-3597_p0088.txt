Saturday night, 16th September, 1939.

8.20 p.m.

Inspector Newton, Special Branch, telephoned to say that a British subject named OLDAG arrived this evening at Waterloo and was interviewed.

He stated that he was the illegitimate son of a German mother born in London 1905. He married a year ago a German woman named Hilda MEISEL.

Subject to Special Branch report No. 5258.

OLDAG stated that he left London in February last for Amsterdam and later made his way to Geneva. He borrowed money from the Consul at Basle and reached this country yesterday. His British passport was issued in Hamburg. When he was in Geneva he received a wire from his wife saying that she was in Brussels.

On his arrival at Waterloo he said that he was completely broke and without any fixed abode. He said that he had come back to this country to investigate "I.R.A. activities" of high German officials in Germany.

I asked Inspector Newton to detain him for the night and he will be taken to Cannon Row police station. I promised to get in touch with him in the morning.

RHS.

Spoke Inspector Henson, S.B., told him we had no reason to hold Oldag. I asked him to get particulars of the 'I.R.A. activities' which he planned. We will have a report.

Bua. 17.9.39.

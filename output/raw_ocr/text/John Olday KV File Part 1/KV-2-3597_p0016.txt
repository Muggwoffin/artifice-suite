24th May, 1944.

Dear Miss Sercombe,

Re the case of John & Mrs. OLDAG

I expect by now you will have received from A.D.F. a long report from us regarding this couple, together with A.D.F.'s opinion and appreciation of the situation. I spoke to A.D.F. yesterday and we agreed that the information contained in M/H's report was probably quite correct, but that the interpretations which were at first put on this information were not correct.

However, you may like to have the attached additional information for your files, and I would be most grateful if you would let me know what steps you are taking in order that we can line up our activities with yours. A.D.F. agrees with me that it is of some importance to try and locate OLDAG himself in order that he may eventually be arrested by the appropriate authorities, and that it would also be of value if M/H met Mrs. OLDAG with a view to giving us his opinion of her present political outlook.

Yours sincerely,

Maxwell Knight,
Major.

Miss Sercombe,
F.3.

Report on:-

No. 13095115 Private OLDAG, W.

Notes by Commanding Officer.

Age: 36
Born: 10.4.1905. London.

Nationality of Parents: Father: "Dont know anything about my father."
Mother: Was German but for about 20 - 25 years has been a naturalised American.
Self: Believe I am a British subject. I had a National Registration Card.

Mother living: In U.S.A.

Occupation:-
Writer - Journalism, free lance.

Languages:-
German (Can read only - Scandinavian and Dutch)
Very good English.

Abroad: Germany from 9 years old to 32 years old.

Passport: Possesses a British passport made out by consul in Hamburg.

Sympathies: Mother: For us.
Self: Has been working underground against Hitler for years. Had to escape.

Passport particulars.
C 34071 issued Hamburg 12.4.37.

Notes by Security.

This man's case is under active consideration, in accordance with Internal Security procedure. It is not considered that his case is a serious one. He was an active member of the Young Communist League in Germany, and as such was anti-Nazi. It is suggested that his case should continue to be pursued as an Internal Security case.

P.T.O.

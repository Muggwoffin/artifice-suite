PF 45000/RC  12th August 1943.

R.S.L.O. Leeds

Thank you for your SF. 19/3/1 of the 7th August 1943, enclosing a book of caricatures entitled "The March to Death" by John OLDAY. We have a file for this man, whose real name is Arthur William OLDAG. He is now in the Pioneer Corps and is of interest to F.1. (Captain Deacon), to whom I am passing the file.

We also have a file for THE FREEDOM PRESS (S.F.455/49) and I am asking F.1. to pass on your minute to F.3. in this connection.

Lt-Colonel A.S. MacIver.
Regional Control.

/RL

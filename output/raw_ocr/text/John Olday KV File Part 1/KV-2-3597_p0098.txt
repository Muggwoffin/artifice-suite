Extract from note in connection with S.I.S. letter of 25.7.3

"Informant thinks he has several contacts who might be useful to them (S.I.S.), the principal one being a certain John OLDAG (no trace in this office) - John OLDAG is British born of German parents, and holds a British passport. He is a writer and cartoonist, and is an intellectual communist. He has several friends still in Germany, who are violently anti-Nazi and who are prepared to go to any lengths to 'damage the Nazi regime'.

"As an example of what informant considered these people could do, he quoted the case of one of John OLDAG's friends who was actually working on what was described as 'one of Hitler's secret war factories'. This is apparently in some wooded region near Hamburg, and is one of the districts which has been specially 'enclosed'."

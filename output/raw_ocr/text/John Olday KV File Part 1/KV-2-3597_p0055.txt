When asked if his father was a Nazi, he made the significant answer that he was not one when he himself left Germany. This was in 1931 before the Nazis came to power, and I regard this as a tacit admission that his father is now a Nazi. In any event there can be no reason for supposing that his father is anything but a patriotic German who happened to be in this country for a short time before the last war. MUTZKE himself appeared to be a typical young German and a typical hotelier. He can have no reason for loyalty to this country except the fact that he married a British subject.

OLDAG is an interesting character. As far as one can judge, he is the illegitimate child of a German woman, probably of fairly good class, by a Scotchman whom she met while living in England shortly before the last war. Possibly this origin partly accounts for OLDAG always having had extreme Left Wing opinions. His mother was a journalist, or something of the kind, and took him to America at an early age. She is now residing there and appears to be an American citizen. OLDAG himself speaks perfect English, but he has spent many years in Germany. He was a journalist there; he was a communist but joined the opposition to Stalin. He worked for a paper which was not communist but democrat and when the Nazis came into power and took control of the Hamburg paper for which he wrote, he refused to continue to do so. Having a birth certificate, he was able to obtain a British passport from our consul in Hamburg and came to this country in 1937. He then went back to the Continent and did underground work in France, Holland and Belgium against the Nazis. One of his reasons for going to the Continent seems to have been that in this country the police would not countenance his organising violent sabotage in Germany. He said that they adopted the same attitude when in September 1939 he came to this country in order to help in the war. He admitted to me that he had disagreed with persons who wanted to send leaflets to Germany since he believed in more violent means. I did not clearly gather to what party he belongs but this is doubtless due to my ignorance of German politics.

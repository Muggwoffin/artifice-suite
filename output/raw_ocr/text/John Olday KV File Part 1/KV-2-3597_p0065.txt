SECRET

PF.45000/IS/F.1.c.  11th. November, 1941.

My attention has been called to No.13095115 Pte. Arthur William OLDAG, Pioneer Corps, said to be serving with the Royal Warwickshire Regiment, I.T.C., stationed at Warwick. OLDAG, who is a British born subject of German/Hungarian extraction, is reported to have been educated in Germany where he was an active member of the Young Communist League. He is further said to be violently anti-Nazi and at present I have no information to show that he is in any way connected with the C.P.G.B., so that I am not inclined to look upon the case as being at all a serious one.

In the circumstances, I think that it will be sufficient if his Commanding Officer is warned of his past associations, asked to keep a quiet eye on him and to report to you in the event of his having any reason to suspect this soldier of attempting to engage in subversive propaganda or activities. Provided, however, he is able to send you a satisfactory report after a month's quiet observation, I do not think that any further action will be necessary.

Yours sincerely,

M. Johnstone
(Captain M. Johnstone).

Captain L.E. Rutherford,
Headquarters,
Western Command,
CHESTER.

MJ/RC.

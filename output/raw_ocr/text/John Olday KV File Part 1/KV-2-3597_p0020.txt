Your Ref. M.A. 25.

Major C.M. Hughes,
Box No. 500, Cambridge.

I am sorry that I have not replied to your letter of the 30th April sooner but I have only just been able to collect the traces required.

The author of the publication "The March to Death", John OLDAY, was born on 10.4.05. in London. His address is given as 49 Upper Montague Street, London, W.1. He enlisted in the I.T.C. on 17.7.41. was transferred to the Buffs on 10.10.41. and then transferred to the Pioneer Corps on 4.11.41. He has, however, deserted from the Pioneer Corps and, so far as I know, has not yet been apprehended.

It is very difficult to discover whether OLDAY is a Trotskyist or an Anarchist but I think he is more a Trotskyist. According to our records the only connection which OLDAY has had with the Freedom Press is the publication of "The March to Death" by them. This, I am sure you will agree, is a very subversive booklet. Please let me know if you wish me to return it. I have got another copy, but it always useful to have a spare.

I enclose a note on the Freedom Press anarchists which I compiled a few months ago. Since it was written the Freedom Press anarchists have been making a drive to procure more members and to start more Freedom Press groups. They have been writing round to all the people whose names appear on their books asking them if they could start a group in their district. Up to date the only places where there is much activity are London, Glasgow, Newcastle, Bristol and Liverpool. Except for London and Glasgow other groups only consist of three or four members.

17.5.44.
MRS/PMW.

1 Encl.

M. R. Sercombe.

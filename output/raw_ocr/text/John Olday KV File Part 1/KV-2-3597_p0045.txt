PF. 45000/IS/Flc. 10th January, 1942.

Rutherford,

Many thanks for your I.S.W.C. 702 of 26.12.41. enclosing a report on No. 1309515 Pte. A.W. OLDAG which I am glad to see is satisfactory. In view of the above, I am now prepared to write this case off as cleared of suspicion and would be glad if you would inform his C.O. accordingly.

Yours sincerely,

Captain M. Johnstone.

Captain L. E. Rutherford,
Headquarters,
Western Command,
CHESTER.

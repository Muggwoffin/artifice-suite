7th August, 1943.

I attach a copy of a letter which I have received from the Chief Constable of Hull and the booklet of caricatures referred to. I entirely agree with the Chief Constable that the booklet is highly undesirable but as I have no record of the Freedom Press, I am not sure which section of the office will be interested.

I shall, however, be glad to receive some information about this as soon as possible to enable me to reply to the Chief Constable.

Major C.W. Hordern

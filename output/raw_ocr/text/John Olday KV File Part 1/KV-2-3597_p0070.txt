REPORT on Black Brief Case recovered from 54, Lillington St. S.W.1.

Above house was destroyed by enemy action in April 1941. It's occupants were - Philipp Israel URBACH & Arthur Willaim ORDAG (subject of Special Branch file No. 320/EMP/517). They are now both living at - 49, Upper Montagu Street, W.1.

URBACH met OLDAG at the Fortes Milk Bar, Leicester Square, W.C. where both are employed at biscuit makers.

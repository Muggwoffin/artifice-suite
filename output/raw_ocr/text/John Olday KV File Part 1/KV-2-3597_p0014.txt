He is very keen to associate with Chinamen, eats at Chinese restaurants, has many contacts in the Chinese quarter in London, and has taken dope which he has obtained from Chinamen. In the days when OLDAG was a Communist in Hamburg he has stated that he was once smuggled across and out of Hamburg with the assistance of members of the Chinese community in Hamburg. In the opinion of M/H, if John OLDAG is being hidden anywhere in London it is more than possible that he is being hidden by some Chinese or in some Chinese quarter. Failing that, he might well be in some other seaport where there is a strong Chinese quarter, say Liverpool or Cardiff.

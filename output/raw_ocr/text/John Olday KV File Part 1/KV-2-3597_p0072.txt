She also spends some of her time writing political articles of an anti-Nazi nature for periodicals. She has published one book entitled "How to Conquer Hitler" which she wrote under the name of Hilda MONTI.

She states that personal reasons have caused she and her husband to live apart but there seems every possibility that their marriage was contracted for convenience sake.

There is no doubt that she still maintains her extremist contacts. Sharing her flat is Hanna MANDELBAUM, German, Serial No. E.Z.252881, the wife of KURT MANDELBAUM, Serial No. E.Z.250140, who was interned by "E" Division on 6.7.40 as a Category "C" Enemy Alien. MANDELBAUM had previously lived at the same address as OLDA. He has previously come under the notice of Special Branch as being in close contact with extreme left wing elements in Paris and as an associate of Hilda RIGAUDIAS, a German communist.

Similar to her husband, Mrs. OLDA is unlikely to work for any pro-Nazi aims. The place of abode of both these persons is crowded with literature of a left wing nature, some written in English and some in German. The man also has some sticky back labels with German captions belittling the Nazi Government which were used during his political activities in Germany and which he is keeping as souvenirs. Both disclaim any connection with the Communist Party in this country but he admits he approached that party with a view to anti-Nazi work but did not join them because he considered their campaign against fascism was half-hearted.

There is no reference to these two persons in Special Branch records except as mentioned above.

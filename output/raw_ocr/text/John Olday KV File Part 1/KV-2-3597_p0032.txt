6th August, 1943.

Dear Major Hordern,

Undesirable booklet of caricatures.

I enclose herewith for your information a copy of the publication 'The March to Death' (price 2s6d), which was purchased recently from the Paragon Station, Hull, bookstall of W.H. Smith and Son, Ltd.

Enquiries reveal that twelve copies were ordered from the representative of the Freedom Press on the 21st June, 1943. All these copies were disposed of at that bookstall and there are no more obtainable in the City.

After having examined the contents, I consider it is a highly undesirable publication and I should be pleased to receive your comments on the matter at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely,

Thos Wells
J.

Major C.W. Hordern,

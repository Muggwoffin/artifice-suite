F.2.b. Mrs Bosanquet.

John OLDAG.

It has been reported to us that John OLDAG went to the Continent before the outbreak of war and did work for the Communist Party all over the place - including, so we are informed, Germany. It has also been stated that OLDAG is now in the British Army where he is continuing this sort of work.

If this man should not come under your section - (we are not certain of his real nationality) - perhaps you would be good enough to pass on this note to the appropriate section?

12.4.43.

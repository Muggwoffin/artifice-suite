Re the case of John and Mrs. OLDAG

(1) Miss SHADDICK. Although it is unlikely that SHADDICK at the moment takes any very active part in Communist Party activities, there is no doubt whatever that she is fundamentally a Communist in her views. Her great passion is India, and on her own admission she has always studied and worked on this subject from the Communist angle. She did a certain amount of work for Nehru when he was in this country and she still maintains definite contact with the India League and she still visits the League and members when she is in London.

(2) M/H has obtained some further information from another source, a Czech. According to this source, John OLDAG and Mrs. OLDAG have parted both personally and politically. According to M/H's informant, Mrs. OLDAG officially deserted the Trotskyists between 6 months and a year ago, when she identified herself with the Militant Socialists.

(3) Von RAUCHENPLAT. This man is stated to have been a member of I.S.K. for sometime before the war, and according to the informant previously mentioned he was kicked out of this organisation for some political deviation which the informant did not or would not disclose.

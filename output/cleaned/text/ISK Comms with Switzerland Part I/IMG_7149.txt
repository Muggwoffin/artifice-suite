MINUTE SHEET

1. Letter from [redacted] received on 20.5.41. Copies sent to D/T, D/F and L. (Letter No.3. See XF/G/136/28) Enc: 1A-B

2. Two reports received from Robert for Willi received on 23.5.41. Copies sent to D/T, D/F and L. (Letter Nos. 4 & 6. See XF/G/136/28) Enc: 2A-C

3. Letter received from Robert on 20.5.41. Copies sent to H, D/JG, D/F, L and D/T. (Letter No.5. See XF/G/136/28) Enc: 3A-B

4. Letter from X/G/103/A to X.2. dated 6.6.41. giving the contents of a telegram to Willi from Robert. Enc: 4A

5. D/T1. The letter at Encl: 4A from the I.T.F. is self-explanatory. Apparently the friends named in the third para. are friends of the I.T.F. and reported to be trustworthy and anti-Hitler.

We have no personal knowledge of these people, but as you know we have a certain amount of confidence in the I.T.F. and hope that you can inform the requisite authorities so that the men do not get too raw a deal. X/PA 9.6.41. R.T.

6. N. Reference enclosure 4a, can you do anything in the direction of Dutch authorities. D/T1. 13.6.41. 6.C.W.

7. D/T1. I am seeing the Dutch Minister for Foreign Affairs tomorrow, and will inform him about these men and see what can be done. N. 16.6.41. R.N.

8. D/T1. N. has made enquiries and informed me that these men are no longer in Dutch hands, but are in Trinidad, which I believe is one of our possession.

If there is any particular authority which could be informed of our Minute 5., we should be grateful if you could do this for us. X/PA 19.6.41.

9. D/T. I am passing this to you in case "C" can effect the necessary contact. D/T1. 23.6.41. 6.C.W.

10. X Please see copy of minute to G.2.A. at encl.10A. D/T 26.6.41. P.T.O.

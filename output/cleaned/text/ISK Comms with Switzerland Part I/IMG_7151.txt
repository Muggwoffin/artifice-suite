MINUTE SHEET

23.11.41. CXG 686 from Geneva (apparently crossed with our telegram under Minute 23A) stating that X/G/116/A requests definite decision regarding trip to London. Enc:24A.

24.11.41. X/U discussed with the I.T.F. the question of X/G/116's correspondence with Switzerland. They said that they were quite agreeable for X/G/116 to conduct this correspondence direct, without them acting as an intermediary and declared themselves satisfied with this arrangement.

Memorandum to J. asking him to inform J.Q. that the question of X/G/116/A's direct contact with X/G/116 has now been settled. Enc:26A.

Letter No. 20A. from Switzerland: ROBERT to WILLI, with memo by ROBERT. Enc:27A-B.

11.12.41. Memorandum to J. confirming having handed him the sum of £300 in bank notes, to be credited to J.Q.'s account and the counter-value in Swiss Francs to be paid out to X/G/116/A, in accordance with his letter of 24.10.41. Enc:28A.

Letter No. 21 from Switzerland: X/G/116/A to Eva Lewinski. Enc:29A.

11.12.41. CXG 386 to Geneva instructing them to pay out 5190 Swiss Francs to ROBERT, being the equivalent of the £300 collected by WILLI. Enc:30A.

16.12.41. Letter No. 42 to Switzerland - ROBERT from WILLI. Enc:31A.

18.12.41. CXG 812 from Geneva stating that the money will be paid out to ROBERT. Enc:32A.

Extract from J.Q.'s report received 27.11.41. confirming ROBERT's desire to have his correspondence passed direct to WILLI, without being seen by the I.T.F. Enc:33A.

Letter from ROBERT to WILLI. Letter No. 26 from Switz. Enc:34A.

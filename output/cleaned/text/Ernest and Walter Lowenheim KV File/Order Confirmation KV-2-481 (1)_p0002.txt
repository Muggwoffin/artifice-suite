NOTE OF INTERVIEW WITH [redacted] AND [redacted] (ALIAS GROEHL)

IN LONDON ON 13.10.41.

I questioned [redacted] at some length upon his knowledge of the LOEWEHHEIM brothers in the hope of obtaining more detailed information of their activities in amplification of the report from at 65a, but without success. [redacted] has had no contact with the LOEWEHHEIMS since they came to England and the only information which he is able to give relates to their activities on the continent. Even in Germany, however, [redacted] met the LOEWEHHEIMS very rarely and his knowledge of their activities was usually obtained through third parties. [redacted] was himself a member of the K.P.D. and it was through his Communist Party associates that he heard of the doings of the LOEWEHHEIM brothers.

[redacted] was in prison in Germany from 1925 to 1928 (as a result of his Communist Party activities) and from 1928 until he left Germany in 1933 he was employed by a publishing firm associated with Willi MUNZENBURG.

[redacted] says that Walter LOEWEHHEIM was of greater importance than his brother Ernst LOEWEHHEIM, but that neither was of very great importance. He agrees that both the LOEWEHHEIMS have always been anti-Nazi.

[redacted] says that he believes that Walter LOEWEHHEIM joined the Youth Movement of the Communist Party in Germany (corresponding to the Y.C.L. here) in 1920 and it was not until four or five years later that he became a member of the K.P.D. proper.

[redacted] does not accept Walter LOEWEHHEIM's statement that he was expelled from the Communist Party.
